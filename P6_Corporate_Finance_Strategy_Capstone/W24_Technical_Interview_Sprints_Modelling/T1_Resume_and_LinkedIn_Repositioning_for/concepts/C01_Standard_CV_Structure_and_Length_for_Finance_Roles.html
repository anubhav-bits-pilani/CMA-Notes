Key concept

Standard CV Structure and Length for Finance Roles

Concept: CV structure: summary (3 lines), core skills, experience (reverse chronological), projects, certifications, education; 1–2 pages

Statement

A finance CV for reporting, FP&A, valuation or controllership roles in India follows a fixed section order and length:

Order Section Content standard
0 Header Name, headline (s01 formula), city, phone, email, LinkedIn URL, portfolio link
1 Summary Exactly 3 lines: credential and years, core capability with standards/tools, target role and one proof point
2 Core skills Grouped hard skills (Accounting & Reporting; FP&A & Modelling; Tools & ERP)
3 Experience Reverse chronological; employer, title, dates, location; 2–6 XYZ bullets per role
4 Projects Up to six portfolio projects, one line each with outcome and link
5 Certifications CMA (ICMAI) first, then add-ons
6 Education Degree, institution, year; omit school details beyond Class XII unless asked

Length: one page for under about 5 years of experience; two pages maximum beyond that.

Explanation

The order reflects how a reader scans. The top third of page one decides whether the rest is read, so the header and summary must carry the positioning and the core skills block must carry the ATS keywords. Experience comes next because employers price you on paid experience; reverse chronological order puts the most recent (usually most relevant) role first and is what Indian ATS parsers and recruiters expect. Functional or skills-based CVs that hide dates are viewed with suspicion and parse poorly.

Projects follow experience for most candidates. For a career switcher with limited relevant paid experience, moving projects above experience is acceptable, but keep the section clearly labelled so that portfolio work is not confused with employment.

Certifications sit after projects: the CMA is already in the headline and summary, so this section confirms it with membership details and lists add-ons (see c04). Education is last because, for qualified professionals, the degree matters less than the qualification and experience.

The 3-line summary is a discipline: line 1 who you are ("CMA (ICMAI) with 3 years of statutory and cost audit across 16 companies"), line 2 what you can do ("Ind AS 115/116/109 reporting, consolidation, 3-statement/DCF modelling, Power BI and Power Query automation"), line 3 what you want and why you fit ("Seeking financial reporting/FP&A roles; built six portfolio projects including an Ind AS close pack and a DCF of an NSE-listed company").

Items normally excluded from Indian finance CVs for these roles: photograph (unless requested), date of birth, marital status, father's name, religion, "declaration" paragraphs, hobbies (unless distinctive and relevant), and a references list (provide on request).

Illustration

A candidate with 3 years of audit and a 2-page CV is asked to cut to one page. A typical allocation:

Section Lines
Header 3
Summary 3
Core skills 4
Experience (one firm, two promotions) 16 (6 + 5 bullets plus headers)
Projects 7
Certifications 2
Education 2
Spacing and headings ~10

Total about 47 lines at 10.5 pt with 0.6-inch margins, fitting one A4 page. Cuts come from removing a "Personal details" block (6 lines), a "Declaration" (2 lines), duplicated duty bullets (8 lines) and an "Objective" paragraph (4 lines).

Practice Questions

Q1. Restructure a CV [L1]

A CV has sections in this order: Objective, Personal Details, Education (with Class X and XII marks), Skills ("MS Office, Tally, Hardworking"), Experience (oldest first), Hobbies, Declaration. Restructure it to the standard order, listing what you would delete, move and add.

Q2. Write the 3-line summary [L2]

Write a 3-line summary for a CMA with 5 years in a listed company's plant accounts team (inventory costing, cost audit coordination, monthly MIS for a ₹900 crore plant) who targets FP&A Manager roles. Then write a variant for a controllership role.

💡 Hint: Keep line 1 and line 3 structure the same; change only the capability emphasis in line 2 and the proof point.

Q3. Two-page decision [L2]

A CMA with 9 years across audit (4 years), a listed manufacturer's financial reporting team (3 years) and a GCC R2R team (2 years) has a 3-page CV. Explain how you would cut it to two pages: what you would compress, what you would keep in full and how many bullets each role should get.
