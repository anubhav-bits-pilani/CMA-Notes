Targeting Big 4 Accounting Advisory (FAAS/CMAAS) Teams

Scope: Big 4 accounting advisory (FAAS/CMAAS-type teams): Ind AS conversion, IPO readiness, technical accounting memos, PPA and consolidation support

Why it matters

Big 4 accounting advisory practices — branded variously as Financial Accounting Advisory Services (FAAS), Capital Markets and Accounting Advisory Services (CMAAS), Accounting Advisory or Accounting & Reporting Advisory — are among the largest recruiters of qualified accountants with Ind AS depth in India. They are separate from statutory audit (to manage independence) and serve non-audit clients on Ind AS transitions, IPOs, technical accounting, deal accounting and financial reporting transformation. For a CMA with audit experience, these teams are a natural bridge: they value audit-grade documentation and Ind AS knowledge, and they offer exposure to many companies quickly. Understanding the service lines lets you tailor your CV, pitch and interview stories to what the team actually delivers.

Core notes

Other adjacent work: financial reporting outsourcing/co-sourcing for clients with weak finance teams, ICFR/IFC documentation (Sec 134(5)(e) and 143(3)(i) of the Companies Act 2013), Ind AS 117 implementation for insurers (notified, effective date set by IRDAI — date-sensitive), ESG/BRSR reporting support (SEBI LODR Reg 34(2)(f)), and IFRS/US GAAP conversions for Indian subsidiaries.

Hiring profile and levels

  • Entry: Consultant/Associate (0–2 years post-qualification), Senior Consultant (2–4 years), Assistant Manager/Manager (4–7 years). Lateral hires from audit typically enter at senior or AM level depending on Ind AS exposure.
  • Preferred qualifications: CA is the default filter, but CMA candidates with demonstrable Ind AS technical work, IFRS certifications (e.g., ACCA DipIFR) or strong portfolio evidence are considered — frame the CMA plus audit background as "controls and costing depth plus Ind AS technical capability".
  • Interview pattern: technical round (Ind AS fact patterns, linkages, Schedule III), case or memo exercise (write a short position paper in 60 minutes), manager/partner fit round (client handling, deadlines, travel).
  • Work pattern: project-based, seasonal peaks around quarter ends and IPO filing windows, travel to client sites (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Ahmedabad).

Tailoring your CV and pitch

  • Lead with Ind AS in headline and summary: "CMA with statutory audit experience; Ind AS 115/116/109 technical memos; consolidation and Power Query automation."
  • Convert audit bullets to advisory outcomes: "Reviewed Ind AS 116 transition for 40 leases (₹85 crore liability), identifying a ₹6 crore lease-term error" rather than "Conducted audit of leases".
  • Include a memo sample (anonymised, two pages) in your portfolio and offer it in outreach.
  • Mention independence awareness: you understand why advisory cannot audit the same client and why documentation quality matters.
  • Highlight tool exposure: consolidation tools, SAP/Oracle, Power BI for reporting packs — FAAS increasingly sells finance transformation.

How work flows on a typical engagement

An IPO readiness engagement for a mid-sized manufacturer: (1) diagnostic of current Indian GAAP/Ind AS reporting and closing process; (2) restatement adjustments for three years (prior-period errors, policy changes); (3) Ind AS technical memos on revenue and leases; (4) support for the restated financial information and KPIs in the DRHP; (5) quarterly reporting readiness (LODR Reg 33). The consultant's day: building adjustment schedules, drafting memo sections, preparing audit-ready working papers, client calls with the finance team, and review by the manager.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Audit vs advisory move from the same firm: internal mobility is possible but independence rules may restrict client assignments for a period.
  • CA preference: some teams formally require CA; target teams and partners who have hired CMAs/ACCAs before (use alumni and LinkedIn searches).
  • Mid-tier and network firms (Grant Thornton Bharat, BDO India, Nangia, others) run similar accounting advisory practices, often with faster responsibility — include them in your target list.
  • Contract vs permanent roles: IPO season can create contract roles; weigh learning against stability.
  • Compensation: Big 4 advisory fixed pay is often below industry for the same experience but brands the CV strongly for later GCC or corporate moves.

Key takeaways

  • FAAS/CMAAS teams deliver Ind AS conversion, IPO readiness, technical memos, PPA and consolidation support — map each to evidence you hold.
  • Lead with Ind AS technical capability; reframe audit as documentation, controls and technical depth.
  • Carry a two-page anonymised technical memo and a consolidation or PPA workbook in your portfolio.
  • Expect a technical round, a memo or case exercise, and a client-fit round.
  • Include mid-tier firms in the target list; target teams with a track record of hiring non-CA professionals.

Practice Questions

Q1. Map evidence to service lines [L1]

Build a two-column table listing each of the five FAAS service lines in this note and one specific, quantified piece of evidence from your experience or portfolio for each. Identify the service line where your evidence is weakest and a two-week plan to close the gap.

💡 Hint: Use the Week 23 dossier summaries as the source of quantified evidence.

Q2. Rewrite audit bullets for advisory [L2]

Rewrite these three audit CV bullets into FAAS-oriented, quantified bullets: (a) "Performed statutory audit of manufacturing clients"; (b) "Checked compliance with CARO 2020"; (c) "Assisted in Ind AS transition audit for a listed company". Assume plausible numbers and state them.

Q3. Memo exercise under time [L3]

In 60 minutes, write a two-page technical memo for a client that issued compulsorily convertible preference shares to a PE investor with a conversion ratio that varies with the IPO valuation, and an investor put option if the IPO does not occur by March 2028. Cover issue, facts, guidance (Ind AS 32/109), analysis of alternatives, conclusion, journal entries and disclosure.

Q4. Partner-round preparation [L2]

Prepare answers to three partner-round questions: "Why advisory and not audit?", "Why should we hire a CMA rather than a CA?", and "How do you handle a client CFO who disagrees with your technical conclusion?" Each answer must be under 90 seconds and include one specific example.
