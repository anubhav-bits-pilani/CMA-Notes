Key concept

The Three-Line Outreach Message

Concept: Outreach message: 3 lines — who you are (CMA + specialism), proof (portfolio link/result), specific ask (15-minute call or referral)

Statement

An effective cold or warm outreach message has exactly three components, one line each:

  1. Who you are: credential plus specialism, framed for the recipient's team — "CMA (ICMAI) with four years of audit, now focused on FP&A modelling."
  2. Proof: one quantified result or a link to a relevant portfolio item — "Here is my Power BI variance pack bridging ₹420 crore EBITDA by price, volume and mix: [link]."
  3. Specific ask: a small, time-bound, easy-to-accept request — "Would you have 15 minutes next week to share how your Hyderabad FP&A team works, or could you refer me for job ID 12345?"

Target length: 50–80 words, readable on a phone without scrolling.

Explanation

Busy hiring managers and employees decide in seconds whether to reply. Each line answers one of their silent questions: Who is this and are they relevant? (identity), Are they credible? (proof), What do they want and is it easy? (ask).

Principles:

  • Personalise one element: reference the recipient's team, a recent post, a shared ICMAI chapter or alumni link. One genuine personal line beats a long generic paragraph.
  • Proof over adjectives: "built a 3-statement DCF in 75 minutes in timed drills" outperforms "passionate and hard-working".
  • Small ask: a 15-minute call or a referral to a specific job ID is easy to accept; "any opportunities in your company?" is vague and burdensome.
  • Make referral effortless: offer a two-line summary and the tailored CV they can forward.
  • Follow-up: one polite nudge after 5–7 days with an added piece of value, and a second after another week; then stop.
  • Platform constraints: LinkedIn connection notes have a short character limit (around 200–300 characters depending on account), so send a compressed version first and the full three lines after acceptance.
  • Tone: professional, concise, no excessive flattery; check names and company spellings.

Illustration

To an FP&A Manager at a Pune GCC (after connecting):

"Hi Priya — I'm a CMA (ICMAI) with four years in audit and cost audit, now moving into FP&A. I built a driver-based forecast and Power BI pack for a ₹1,200 crore manufacturer, including a PVM bridge that explained a 180 bp margin drop: [link]. Would you be open to a 15-minute call next week about your team, or to referring me for the Senior FP&A Analyst role (job ID 45821)?"

Word count: about 70. Personalisation could add: "I enjoyed your post on rolling forecasts."

To an alumni contact at a Big 4 FAAS team:

"Hi Arjun — fellow member of the ICMAI Pune chapter here. I'm a CMA specialising in Ind AS technical accounting; my two-page memo on Ind AS 116 lease-term judgements is here: [link]. Could I send you my CV for the Assistant Manager – Accounting Advisory opening, or would a 15-minute call be easier first?"

Connections

The message operationalises the role-specific 30-second pitch in writing, draws its proof from the role-fit matrix's strongest evidence and the Week 23 portfolio dossier, and is the first stage in the pipeline tracker and funnel metrics. It must stay consistent with the CV variant and LinkedIn headline (Week 24 t1) — the recipient will click through to your profile.

Practice Questions

Q1. Write three messages [L1]

Write three-line outreach messages to (a) a KPO equity research team lead, (b) a controller at a Chennai GCC, and (c) an HR business partner at an NSE-listed company, each with a different proof point and a specific ask.

💡 Hint: Match the proof point to the recipient's daily work, not to your favourite project.

Q2. Critique and rewrite [L2]

Critique this message and rewrite it in three lines: "Dear Sir, I am a CMA with 5 years experience. I am looking for job change in finance domain. I have good knowledge of Ind AS, Excel, Power BI, SAP. Kindly let me know if any opportunity is there in your esteemed organisation. PFA my CV."

Q3. A/B test design [L2]

Design a simple A/B test for your outreach over 40 messages: define two variants (e.g., portfolio link vs quantified result as proof), the metric, sample split, tracking columns in your pipeline tracker and how you will decide the winner.
