Key concept

Registered Valuer (SFA) Route via IBBI and ICMAI RVO

Concept: Registered Valuer route (SFA class) via IBBI exam and ICMAI RVO as a long-term valuation credential

Statement

Under Section 247 of the Companies Act 2013 and the Companies (Registered Valuers and Valuation) Rules, 2017, valuations required under the Act (and, by reference, under the IBC 2016 and several SEBI regulations) must be performed by a Registered Valuer (RV) registered with the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI), the authority designated under the Rules. Registration is by asset class: Land and Building, Plant and Machinery, and Securities or Financial Assets (SFA) — the class covering shares, businesses, financial instruments and intangibles in a business context.

The route for an ICMAI member:

  1. Eligibility: membership of ICMAI (or ICAI/ICSI), or a finance post-graduate qualification, plus the prescribed post-qualification experience in the discipline (the Rules' annexure sets the qualifications and experience by asset class — verify current requirements).
  2. Enrol with a Registered Valuers Organisation (RVO) — e.g., the ICMAI Registered Valuers Organisation — as a valuer member.
  3. Complete the RVO's educational course (a 50-hour programme for the asset class).
  4. Pass the IBBI valuation examination for SFA (online, MCQ-based; check the current syllabus, pattern and validity period on the IBBI website).
  5. Apply to IBBI for a certificate of registration (through the prescribed form and fee); maintain RVO membership, continuing professional education and the Code of Conduct.

Explanation

Why it matters as a long-term credential: many Indian valuation mandates are legally reserved for RVs — preferential allotments and other share issues for non-cash consideration, mergers and schemes under Sec 230–232, IBC fair value and liquidation value (two RVs per asset class), and various SEBI contexts. Boutique valuation firms, Big 4 valuation teams and merchant bankers value RV-registered staff because they can sign reports. For a CMA, the RV route builds on cost audit and valuation knowledge and opens an independent-practice path.

Conditions and nuances:

  • Separate regimes: FEMA pricing valuations can be done by a CA, SEBI-registered merchant banker or practising cost accountant; Income-tax Rule 11UA valuations under the DCF and certain other methods specify a SEBI-registered Category I merchant banker, while the NAV method is computed from the books without a specified valuer. RV registration complements these rather than replacing them.
  • Independence and conduct: RVs must avoid conflicts of interest, not accept contingent fees, and follow the valuation standards specified (ICAI Valuation Standards are commonly applied; the Rules contemplate standards notified by the government — check current status).
  • Entities: partnerships or companies can register as RV entities if partners/directors are RVs for the relevant class.
  • Timeline: from enrolment to registration typically several months, depending on course schedules and exam dates.
  • Complementary credentials: CFA charter or CFA Level progress and FMVA add modelling credibility; RV adds legal signing authority.

Illustration

A CMA aged 30 with four years post-qualification audit experience plans the route:

Month Milestone
1 Confirm eligibility against the Rules; enrol with ICMAI RVO as a valuer member
2–3 Complete the 50-hour SFA educational course; build DCF, comparables and option-pricing practice models
4–5 Prepare for and take the IBBI SFA valuation examination
6 Apply for registration with IBBI; meanwhile, work at a valuation boutique on Companies Act and FEMA valuations
7+ Sign valuations as RV (or under a senior RV's supervision initially), complete continuing education

Career positioning: "CMA, Registered Valuer (SFA) candidate, three-statement and DCF portfolio" is a strong headline for KPO/valuation targets.

Practice Questions

Q1. Map mandates to signing authority [L1]

For each mandate, state whether an IBBI Registered Valuer (SFA) is required, permitted or not the specified professional: (a) valuation for a preferential allotment by an unlisted company under Sec 62(1)(c); (b) fair value of equity shares under CIRP; (c) pricing for issue of shares to a foreign investor under FEMA; (d) Ind AS 113 fair value of an unquoted investment for financial statements.

💡 Hint: Separate requirements under the Companies Act and IBC from those under FEMA, Income-tax and Ind AS.

Q2. Build your RV plan [L2]

Draft a 9-month plan to register as an RV (SFA) while employed, including eligibility check, RVO enrolment, course, exam preparation hours per week, practice models and the role you will target during the process.

Q3. Interview answer [L1]

Prepare a 60-second answer to "Why are you pursuing the Registered Valuer route, and how does it fit with your CMA?" for a valuation boutique interview, including one specific valuation you have modelled.
