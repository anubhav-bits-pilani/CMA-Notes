Subtopic note

Presenting Accretion Heat Maps and Synergies vs Guidance

Scope: Presenting results: accretion/dilution heat maps and "synergies needed vs management guidance" comparisons

Why it matters

A board member reading an M&A paper has minutes, not hours. The analysis in s01–s05 is worthless if the output page does not communicate, at a glance, where the deal is accretive, how much cushion exists between required and guided synergies, and which assumptions matter most. Two exhibits do most of the work in practice: the accretion/dilution heat map (a colour-coded sensitivity grid) and the "synergies needed vs guidance" chart. Both also appear, in simpler form, in investor presentations when Indian listed acquirers announce deals, and in rating agency and lender credit papers.

Core notes

The accretion/dilution heat map

A heat map is a formatted sensitivity table (s03) where colour encodes the sign and size of accretion.

Construction steps in Excel:

  1. Build the data table (concept c04) with the output linked in the top-left cell.
  2. Apply Conditional Formatting → Color Scales → three-colour scale with midpoint fixed at number 0 (not percentile 50), so neutral is always the neutral colour, dilution is one hue and accretion another.
  3. Use a diverging palette that remains readable in greyscale print and for colour-blind readers (e.g. orange–white–blue rather than red–green).
  4. Custom number format +0.0%;(0.0%);0.0% so signs are explicit.
  5. Bold-border the base case cell; add a note listing assumptions held constant.
  6. Optionally overlay the break-even contour: cells within ±0.5% shown in a neutral band.

What the reader should learn: the direction of each axis effect, where the neutral contour lies (e.g. "at a 30% premium we need at least 50% stock"), and how steep the gradient is (sensitivity).

Synergies needed vs management guidance

This chart compares, year by year, the break-even pre-tax synergies with management's phased guidance:

Year Break-even synergies (₹ crore) Guided synergies (₹ crore) Cushion (₹ crore) Required realisation
FY27 100.9 100.0 (0.9) 101%
FY28 48.9 160.0 111.1 31%
FY29 25.0 200.0 175.0 13%

(Bharat Polymers / Deccan Packaging 60/40 deal; FY29 figure illustrative.)

Chart form: clustered columns (break-even vs guidance) per year, or a line for break-even over columns for guidance; annotate the required realisation %. Add a third series for "market-implied synergies" (from announcement-day abnormal returns; see the market-reaction concept in the topic overview) where available.

Supporting metrics:

  • Cushion = guidance − break-even;
  • Required realisation % = break-even ÷ guidance;
  • Break-even as % of target revenue / cost base and of combined cost base (concept c03);
  • Synergy margin of safety = cushion ÷ guidance.

Other standard exhibits

  • EPS bridge (waterfall) from standalone EPS to pro-forma EPS: + target PAT, + synergies, − foregone interest, − new interest, − PPA, − fees, − NCI, − new-share dilution. In Excel use the built-in waterfall chart; in Power BI the waterfall visual with a category per adjustment.
  • Scenario summary table: base, management, downside and severe (s05), showing Year 1 and Year 2 accretion, break-even, leverage and ROIC.
  • Value vs EPS panel: deal NPV (PV net synergies − premium), ROIC vs WACC year, alongside accretion — guarding against the "accretive therefore good" conclusion.
  • Credit panel: Net Debt/EBITDA vs covenant and rating threshold by year.

Power BI implementation

For an interactive deal-committee dashboard:

Premium = GENERATESERIES(0, 0.40, 0.05)
Premium Value = SELECTEDVALUE('Premium'[Premium], 0.20)

Accretion % =
VAR EquityPrice = [Undisturbed Price] * (1 + [Premium Value]) * [Target Shares]
VAR NewShares   = EquityPrice * [Stock Pct] / [Acquirer Price]
VAR PF_PAT      = [Acquirer PAT] + [Target PAT] + [Synergies] * (1 - [Tax])
                  - [PPA AfterTax] - EquityPrice * (1 - [Stock Pct]) * [Debt Rate] * (1 - [Tax])
RETURN DIVIDE(PF_PAT, [Acquirer Shares] + NewShares) / [Standalone EPS] - 1

Place Accretion % in a matrix with premium and stock-% parameter tables on rows and columns, and apply background-colour conditional formatting with a diverging scale centred on zero. The Excel model remains the audited source; reconcile the dashboard's base cell to it.

Narrative discipline

  • Lead with the conclusion: "EPS-neutral in FY27 at guided synergies; 6% accretive in FY28; requires 101% realisation in FY27 and 31% in FY28."
  • State the EPS basis (reported vs adjusted, time basis).
  • Show downside before upside.
  • Never present a heat map without the base-case marker and fixed-assumption note.
  • Keep decimals honest: accretion to 0.1%, synergies to ₹1 crore.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Selective framing. Choosing axes ranges that keep the grid "mostly blue" is a governance issue; the audit committee should see ranges that include plausible downsides.
  • Adjusted EPS as headline. If the headline uses adjusted EPS (ex-PPA), the reported figure must appear with equal prominence and a reconciliation.
  • Guidance credibility. Compare guided synergies with peer deals (as % of target cost base) and with the acquirer's track record on previous integrations.
  • Stale grids. If calculation excludes data tables, a printed heat map can be stale; include a "last recalculated" check cell comparing the base cell with the live model.
  • Confidentiality. Pre-announcement exhibits are UPSI under SEBI PIT Regulations; control distribution of board packs and dashboards.

Key takeaways

  • The heat map is a data table with a diverging colour scale anchored at zero, base case marked and assumptions stated.
  • The synergies-needed chart compares break-even with phased guidance each year, with cushion and required realisation %.
  • Support with an EPS bridge, scenario summary, value-vs-EPS panel and credit panel.
  • Power BI what-if parameters can make the grid interactive, but reconcile to the Excel model.
  • Lead with the conclusion, show downside first, and present reported and adjusted EPS consistently.
