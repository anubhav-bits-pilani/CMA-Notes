Key concept

ICDR Preferential Issue Floor Price for Frequently Traded Shares

Concept: ICDR preferential pricing (frequently traded): not less than higher of 90-trading-day VWAP and 10-trading-day VWAP before relevant date

Statement

For a listed issuer whose shares are frequently traded, SEBI ICDR Regulations 2018 Reg 164(1) (as amended in January 2022) require the preferential issue price to be at least:

\[ P_{\text{floor}} = \max\left(\text{VWAP}_{90}, \ \text{VWAP}_{10}\right) \]

where:

  • \(\text{VWAP}_{90}\) = volume-weighted average price of the related equity shares quoted on the recognised stock exchange during the 90 trading days preceding the relevant date;
  • \(\text{VWAP}_{10}\) = volume-weighted average price during the 10 trading days preceding the relevant date;
  • Relevant date (Reg 161) = the date 30 days before the date on which the meeting of shareholders is held to approve the preferential issue (adjusted for weekends and holidays per the regulation);
  • the exchange used is the one with the highest trading volume during the 90 trading days.

Additional floors:

\[ P_{\text{issue}} \geq \max\left(P_{\text{floor}}, \ P_{\text{articles}}, \ P_{\text{valuer}}\right) \]

where \(P_{\text{articles}}\) is any price determined by a method in the articles of association, and \(P_{\text{valuer}}\) is the price in an independent registered valuer's report, required under Reg 166A if the allotment results in a change of control or more than 5% of post-issue fully diluted capital to an allottee or group acting in concert.

Explanation

The floor protects existing shareholders from dilution through cheap allotments to favoured parties. The 10-day leg prevents an issue at a stale 90-day average when the price has risen recently. The 90-day leg prevents issuing at a short-term dip.

  • Frequently traded: shares whose traded turnover in the 240 trading days preceding the relevant date is at least 10% of the total number of shares. Otherwise, Reg 165 requires a valuation-based price by an independent registered valuer.
  • Consideration other than cash (share swaps): the target shares received must also be valued by an independent registered valuer (Reg 163(3)), and the swap must satisfy both the issue-price floor and the value of the shares received.
  • Price adjustments: if the issuer's shares are not listed for at least 90 trading days, adjusted methods apply. Certain allottees (for example QIBs, or pursuant to a resolution plan) have separate rules.
  • Timing risk: the floor is known only on the relevant date, 30 days before the meeting. A rising market between signing and the relevant date increases the floor.

For modelling, the floor sets the minimum issue price, so it caps the number of new shares for a given consideration, which affects pro-forma EPS and ownership.

Illustration

Relevant date 10 August 2026. 90-trading-day VWAP ₹1,412; 10-trading-day VWAP ₹1,468. The articles prescribe no method. The allotment is 7% of post-issue capital, so Reg 166A applies, and the valuer's price is ₹1,450.

  • Floor = max(1,412, 1,468) = ₹1,468.
  • Issue price ≥ max(1,468, 1,450) = ₹1,468.
  • For ₹2,200 crore of consideration, maximum shares issued = 2,200 / 1,468 = 1.499 crore shares.

If the negotiated price had been ₹1,420, the issue would need to be repriced to ₹1,468, reducing the number of shares by about 3.3%.

Connections

This concept constrains s04 (stock-for-stock via preferential issue) and c03 (the exchange ratio's acquirer price). It also applies to fund-raising for cash deals (preferential issue to a strategic investor as a Source, s02). It links to the FEMA pricing related item, where the fair-value floor for issues to non-residents must also be met, and to t4 s03 (pro-forma share count).

Practice Questions

Q1. Floor price [L1]

90-day VWAP ₹642; 10-day VWAP ₹615; articles prescribe the average of the last 26 weeks' closing prices, giving ₹630. Allotment is 3% of post-issue capital. Compute the minimum issue price.

Q2. Relevant date and market movement [L2]

An EGM is planned for 25 September 2026. Determine the relevant date. Between signing (1 August) and the relevant date, the share price rises 12%, lifting the 10-day VWAP from ₹900 to ₹1,008 while the 90-day VWAP rises from ₹880 to ₹930. Compute the floor at signing and on the relevant date, and the reduction in shares issued for ₹1,500 crore of consideration.

💡 Hint: Count back 30 calendar days from the meeting date, then check the regulation's weekend and holiday rule.

Q3. Infrequently traded issuer [L2]

An SME-exchange-listed acquirer's shares traded 4% of total shares in the last 240 trading days. Explain how the preferential price is set, who sets it and what documents the stock exchange will require for in-principle approval.
