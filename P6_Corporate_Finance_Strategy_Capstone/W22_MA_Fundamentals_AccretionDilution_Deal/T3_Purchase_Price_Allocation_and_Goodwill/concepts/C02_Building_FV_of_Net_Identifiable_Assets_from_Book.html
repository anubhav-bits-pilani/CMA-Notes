Key concept

Building FV of Net Identifiable Assets from Book Net Assets

Concept: FV of net identifiable assets = Book net assets − existing target goodwill + asset write-ups + new intangibles − DTL on (write-ups + intangibles)

Statement

\[ FV_{\text{NIA}} = BNA - GW_{\text{old}} + \sum WU + \sum INT - t \times \left(\sum WU + \sum INT\right) \pm \text{Other adjustments} \]

where:

  • \(BNA\) = target's book net assets (total equity per the target's consolidated balance sheet, excluding its own NCI);
  • \(GW_{\text{old}}\) = goodwill already on the target's balance sheet from its own past acquisitions, which is not an identifiable asset of the acquiree from the acquirer's perspective and must be eliminated;
  • \(\sum WU\) = fair value write-ups (minus write-downs) of existing assets and liabilities: PPE, land, inventory, investments, debt at fair value, plus recognised contingent liabilities (as negative amounts);
  • \(\sum INT\) = newly recognised intangibles (customer relationships, brands, technology, backlog, non-compete, licences), net of any intangibles already on the target's books;
  • \(t\) = tax rate for the temporary differences (25.17% for a Sec 115BAA company), giving the incremental DTL;
  • Other adjustments = policy alignment, removal of the target's existing deferred tax on eliminated goodwill, write-off of capitalised costs that are not assets at fair value (for example unamortised debt fees when debt is remeasured).

Explanation

The book net assets are a starting point that reflects historical cost. The acquisition method needs a fair value balance sheet. Three adjustments do most of the work:

  1. Remove existing goodwill. The target's old goodwill is a residual from its previous deals. It is not separately identifiable, so it is replaced by the new goodwill computed in c01. Leaving it in double counts.
  2. Add step-ups and new intangibles. Each is a temporary difference in a share deal or amalgamation.
  3. Deduct the incremental DTL. Because tax bases do not change, the step-ups create DTL. A DTL that the target already recognised on book vs tax differences stays in book net assets. Only the incremental DTL on the new step-ups is added.

If the target has previously recognised intangibles (for example a brand bought earlier), revalue them to fair value; the increment, not the full new value, is the adjustment.

Illustration

Target book net assets ₹2,400 crore, including existing goodwill of ₹300 crore and a previously recognised brand of ₹100 crore. PPA findings: brand FV ₹450 crore; customer relationships ₹380 crore; PPE step-up ₹220 crore; inventory step-up ₹40 crore; contingent liability ₹60 crore (deductible when paid). Tax 25.17%.

₹ crore Amount
Book net assets 2,400.0
Less existing goodwill (300.0)
Brand increment (450 − 100) 350.0
Customer relationships 380.0
PPE step-up 220.0
Inventory step-up 40.0
Contingent liability (60.0)
Net adjustments subject to tax 930.0
DTL at 25.17% on 930 (234.1)
FV of net identifiable assets 2,795.9

If consideration is ₹4,000 crore for 100%, goodwill = 4,000 − 2,795.9 = ₹1,204.1 crore.

Connections

This concept is the arithmetic core of the PPA, using s02–s04 (what to recognise and how to value it) and s05 (DTL). It feeds c01 (goodwill) and c03 and c04 (DTL creation and unwinding). In the exercise, it forms the PPA tab of the accretion/dilution model and drives t4's amortisation line. The pitfalls on existing goodwill and DTL are errors in this formula.

Practice Questions

Q1. Compute FV of net assets [L1]

Book net assets ₹1,000 crore (including goodwill ₹120 crore). Write-ups: land ₹150 crore, plant ₹80 crore. New intangibles: technology ₹200 crore, non-compete ₹30 crore. Tax 25.17%. Compute FV of net identifiable assets.

Q2. Existing intangibles and deferred tax [L2]

Book net assets ₹1,600 crore include goodwill of ₹200 crore, customer relationships of ₹90 crore from an earlier acquisition, and a DTL of ₹22.7 crore on those customer relationships. The new PPA values customer relationships at ₹260 crore and identifies a brand of ₹310 crore. Tax 25.17%. Compute FV of net identifiable assets.

💡 Hint: Only the increase over the existing carrying amount creates new DTL.
