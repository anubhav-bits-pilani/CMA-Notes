Subtopic note

Pro-forma Share Count and Ind AS 33 Time-Weighting

Scope: Pro-forma share count: acquirer diluted shares + new shares issued; Ind AS 33 time-weighting for mid-year closings

Why it matters

The denominator of pro-forma EPS determines the dilution from issuing stock. In an all-stock or mixed deal, a 5% error in new shares issued produces roughly a 5% error in the accretion figure. And because Indian deals frequently close mid-year — after CCI approval, SEBI observations on a scheme or NCLT sanction — the first reported year will contain only a few months of the target. Ind AS 33 time-weights shares from the acquisition date, and the pro-forma model must mirror that if Year 1 accretion is to match what the company will actually report.

Core notes

Building blocks

\[ \text{Pro-forma shares} = \text{Acquirer diluted shares}_{\text{standalone}} + \text{New shares issued to target holders} + \text{New shares issued to fund cash} + \text{Dilutive replacement awards} \]

Acquirer diluted shares (standalone). Take the weighted average diluted share count from the standalone model — basic weighted shares plus dilutive potential shares (ESOPs under the treasury stock method, convertible instruments under the if-converted method), per Ind AS 33.31 onward. Use the same figure in standalone EPS so that accretion reflects only the deal. Buyback plans already announced should be in both standalone and pro-forma unless the deal cancels them, in which case the forgone buyback must be modelled as a deal effect.

New shares to target holders. In a stock-for-stock deal (merger by scheme under Sec 230–232, or a share swap via preferential allotment under ICDR Chapter V):

\[ \text{New shares} = \text{Target fully diluted shares} \times \text{Exchange ratio} \qquad \text{Exchange ratio} = \frac{\text{Offer price per target share}}{\text{Acquirer share price}} \]

For a mixed deal, only the stock-funded portion: new shares = (equity price × stock %) ÷ acquirer issue price.

Shares issued to raise cash. If the "cash" portion is financed by a QIP or rights issue rather than existing cash, those shares also belong in the pro-forma denominator, at the expected issue price net of discount (QIP floor price under ICDR uses the two-week average). A rights issue has a bonus element that Ind AS 33.26–27 requires to be applied retrospectively to all periods' share counts, including standalone.

Replacement awards. Where the acquirer replaces target employees' ESOPs with its own awards, the portion attributable to pre-combination service is part of consideration transferred (Ind AS 103) and the awards are potential shares for diluted EPS.

Treasury shares and ESOP trusts. Shares held by an employee welfare trust are treated as treasury shares and excluded from the count; reciprocal holdings eliminated on consolidation (if the target holds acquirer shares) also reduce shares outstanding.

Ind AS 33 time-weighting

Basic EPS uses the weighted average number of shares outstanding during the period (Ind AS 33.19–20). Shares issued as part of the consideration transferred in a business combination are included in the weighted average from the acquisition date, because the acquirer includes the acquiree's profits only from that date (Ind AS 33.22). So for a closing on day \(d\) of a 365-day year:

\[ \text{Weighted new shares} = \text{New shares} \times \frac{365 - d + 1}{365} \]

In monthly models the fraction is simply post-closing months ÷ 12.

Consistency principle. Every pro-forma line in the closing year must use the same stub fraction: target PAT, synergies, interest, PPA amortisation, NCI and new shares. The common model input is a single cell, for example:

=MAX(0, MIN(1, (FYEnd - CloseDate + 1) / (FYEnd - FYStart + 1)))

Each pro-forma adjustment line in the closing year multiplies by this cell; subsequent years use 1.

Two presentations. Banks usually show (a) "full-year pro-forma" as if closed on the first day of the fiscal year — clean run-rate comparison, consistent with how investors think about the deal — and (b) "reported basis" with time-weighting, which predicts what the FY27 annual report will show. Label which one each table is.

Diluted pro-forma EPS

If consideration includes CCPS, CCDs or warrants (common in Indian structured deals), build both basic and diluted pro-forma share counts. Mandatorily convertible instruments are included in basic EPS from the date the contract is entered into (Ind AS 33.23). Optionally convertible instruments are included in diluted EPS if dilutive, with their after-tax interest added back to the numerator. Contingently issuable earn-out shares enter basic EPS only when all conditions are met.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Scheme with appointed date earlier than effective date. Under Indian schemes of amalgamation, the "appointed date" in the scheme may precede NCLT sanction. For Ind AS 103 the acquisition date is when control passes (normally the effective date, when the order is filed with the RoC and conditions are met), not the appointed date — except common-control cases under Appendix C, which restate comparatives. EPS weighting follows the acquisition date.
  • Cross-holding cancellation. If the acquirer already holds target shares, those shares are cancelled on merger and new shares are issued only to the other holders.
  • Fractional entitlements. Schemes typically pool fractions and sell them through a trustee; immaterial for EPS but should not be rounded up per holder.
  • Price used for exchange ratio vs Ind AS 103 consideration. The swap ratio is set using negotiated or registered-valuer values; consideration for goodwill is measured at the acquirer's share price on the acquisition date. The share count is fixed by the ratio; the PPA value moves with the share price.
  • Treasury method for acquirer ESOPs uses the average share price; if the deal moves the acquirer's share price materially, standalone and pro-forma ESOP dilution can differ slightly.

Key takeaways

  • Pro-forma shares = standalone diluted shares + consideration shares + shares from any equity raise + dilutive replacement awards.
  • Consideration shares enter the weighted average from the acquisition date (Ind AS 33.22).
  • Time-weight every pro-forma line in the closing year with one stub-fraction cell; never mix full-year profit with time-weighted shares.
  • Show both a full-year pro-forma and a reported (time-weighted) basis, clearly labelled.
  • Mandatorily convertible consideration enters basic EPS; optionally convertible instruments enter diluted EPS if dilutive.

Practice Questions

Q1. Weighted shares for a mid-year scheme [L1]

An acquirer with 84 crore weighted diluted shares issues 9.6 crore shares under a merger scheme that becomes effective (acquisition date) on 15 January 2027. FY ends 31 March 2027 (365-day year). Compute the weighted consideration shares and the pro-forma weighted share count for FY27 and FY28.

Q2. Mixed consideration and QIP [L2]

Equity purchase price ₹3,600 crore: 30% in acquirer shares at ₹720 issue price, 70% in cash, of which ₹1,200 crore is raised through a QIP at ₹680 per share and the rest from new debt. Acquirer standalone diluted shares 52 crore. Closing 1 April 2026. Compute pro-forma shares for FY27 and explain why the QIP shares must be included.

Q3. Correcting an inconsistent model [L2]

A junior analyst's model for a 1 December 2026 closing includes four months of new-share weighting but twelve months of target PAT (₹240 crore), twelve months of new debt interest (₹96 crore pre-tax) and twelve months of synergies (₹60 crore pre-tax). Acquirer PAT ₹1,400 crore, standalone shares 70 crore, new shares 6 crore, PPA after-tax ₹36 crore per year, tax 25%. Recompute reported-basis FY27 accretion consistently and quantify the error in the analyst's version.

💡 Hint: Apply the same 4/12 factor to every deal-related line.

Q4. Convertible consideration [L3]

As part of consideration the acquirer issues ₹500 crore of 0.01% optionally convertible debentures convertible at ₹500 per share at the holder's option after 3 years, in addition to 4 crore ordinary shares. Explain how each instrument enters basic and diluted pro-forma EPS, and what numerator adjustment is needed for diluted EPS.
