Key concept

Pro-forma EPS and the Accretion Percentage

Concept: Pro-forma EPS = Pro-forma PAT ÷ (Acquirer shares + new shares issued); Accretion % = Pro-forma EPS ÷ Acquirer standalone EPS − 1

Statement

\[ \text{EPS}_{PF} = \frac{\text{PAT}_{PF}}{N_{A} + N_{\text{new}}} \qquad \text{Accretion \%} = \frac{\text{EPS}_{PF}}{\text{EPS}_{A}} - 1 \]

where \(\text{PAT}_{PF}\) is pro-forma PAT attributable to owners (c01), \(N_{A}\) is the acquirer's standalone weighted average diluted shares, \(N_{\text{new}}\) is the weighted number of shares issued as consideration or to raise acquisition cash, and \(\text{EPS}_{A} = \text{PAT}_{A} / N_{A}\) is standalone EPS for the same period. A positive result is accretion, a negative result is dilution; zero is EPS-neutral.

Explanation

The comparison is always pro-forma versus the acquirer's own standalone forecast for the same year — not versus last year's actual EPS. That isolates the effect of the deal from organic growth. Both EPS figures must use the same share-count basis (diluted, weighted), the same earnings normalisation and the same time-weighting convention (s03). Under Ind AS 33.22, consideration shares enter the weighted average from the acquisition date, so a reported-basis Year 1 calculation uses a weighted \(N_{\text{new}}\) together with time-weighted target profit.

Accretion is usually presented in three forms:

  • Percentage (headline in announcements);
  • Rupees per share (\(\text{EPS}_{PF} - \text{EPS}_{A}\));
  • Reported vs adjusted — adjusted EPS excludes after-tax PPA amortisation and one-off deal costs, as in Indian broker "cash EPS" conventions.

Accretion % is not linear in PAT when shares are issued: a 10% increase in PAT with 10% more shares gives zero accretion. For cash/debt deals (\(N_{\text{new}} = 0\)), accretion % equals \(\text{PAT}_{PF}/\text{PAT}_{A} - 1\).

A materiality convention used by many bankers: within ±1% is "broadly neutral", ±1–5% modest, beyond ±5% significant. These are presentation norms, not rules.

Illustration

Acquirer: PAT ₹1,200 crore, 60 crore shares, EPS ₹20.00. Target PAT ₹150 crore. Equity price ₹2,700 crore. PPA amortisation after tax ₹60 crore. Synergies ₹100 crore pre-tax (₹75 crore after tax). Tax 25%. Acquirer share price ₹500.

Funding PAT₍PF₎ (₹ crore) New shares (crore) EPS₍PF₎ (₹) Accretion
100% stock (5.4 crore shares) 1,200 + 150 + 75 − 60 = 1,365.0 5.4 20.87 +4.4%
60% debt / 40% cash (ex-fees) 1,365 − 109.35 − 52.65 = 1,203.0 0 20.05 +0.3%
100% debt at 9% 1,365 − 182.25 = 1,182.75 0 19.71 (1.4%)

Adjusted EPS (adding back ₹60 crore PPA amortisation) for the all-stock case is 1,425 ÷ 65.4 = ₹21.79, i.e. +8.9% — showing how much the reported/adjusted distinction can matter.
