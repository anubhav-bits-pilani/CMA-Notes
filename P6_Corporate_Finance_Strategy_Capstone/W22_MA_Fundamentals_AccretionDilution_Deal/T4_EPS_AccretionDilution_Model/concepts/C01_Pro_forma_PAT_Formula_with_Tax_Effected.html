Key concept

Pro-forma PAT Formula with Tax-Effected Adjustments

Concept: Pro-forma PAT = Acquirer PAT + Target PAT + Synergies × (1 − t) − Foregone interest on cash × (1 − t) − New debt interest × (1 − t) − PPA amortisation × (1 − t) − NCI share

Statement

\[ \text{PAT}_{PF} = \text{PAT}_{A} + \text{PAT}_{T} + S(1-t) - I_{c}(1-t) - I_{d}(1-t) - A_{PPA}(1-t) - \text{NCI} \]

where:

  • \(\text{PAT}_{PF}\) = pro-forma profit after tax attributable to owners of the acquirer;
  • \(\text{PAT}_{A}\), \(\text{PAT}_{T}\) = standalone, normalised PAT of acquirer and target for the same fiscal period (target PAT time-weighted for a mid-year closing);
  • \(S\) = pre-tax synergies (phased, net of dis-synergies);
  • \(I_{c}\) = pre-tax interest income forgone on acquirer cash used;
  • \(I_{d}\) = pre-tax interest on new acquisition debt (including financing-fee amortisation if not shown separately);
  • \(A_{PPA}\) = amortisation of acquired intangibles plus incremental depreciation on fair-value step-ups;
  • \(t\) = marginal tax rate (25.168% under Sec 115BAA for most Indian companies);
  • NCI = minority share of the target's post-PPA profit when less than 100% is owned.

Explanation

PAT is an after-tax figure, so every adjustment added to or subtracted from it must also be after tax. Synergies raise taxable profit, so only \(S(1-t)\) reaches PAT. Lost interest income would have been taxed, so the PAT cost is \(I_{c}(1-t)\). New interest is deductible, so the PAT cost is \(I_{d}(1-t)\). PPA amortisation is not tax-deductible in a share deal, yet it still costs only \(A_{PPA}(1-t)\) after tax because the deferred tax liability recognised on the step-ups at acquisition (Ind AS 12, as required by Ind AS 103.24) unwinds as the step-up is consumed, crediting deferred tax expense.

Exceptions and refinements:

  • Non-deductible interest. If acquisition interest cannot be deducted (e.g. borrowing at an investment-holding parent where Sec 57 restricts deduction), the after-tax cost is \(I_{d}\) in full.
  • Different tax rates. Use the rate of the entity where each item arises (a foreign target, an SEZ unit, or an entity on the old regime).
  • Additional lines omitted from the shorthand: reversal of target interest on refinanced debt, reversal of old intangible amortisation, financing-fee amortisation (c06), one-off inventory step-up and expensed transaction costs (Ind AS 103.53) in the closing year.
  • NCI is computed after PPA and target-level synergies but not after parent-level financing costs (s04).
  • Goodwill is never amortised (Ind AS 103 / Ind AS 36), so it is absent from the formula.

Illustration

Acquirer PAT ₹1,200 crore; target PAT ₹150 crore; 66% stake bought for ₹1,782 crore funded 60% debt at 9%, 40% cash at 6.5%; PPA amortisation ₹80 crore pre-tax; synergies ₹100 crore pre-tax arising entirely at target level; tax 25%.

₹ crore Pre-tax After-tax
Acquirer PAT 1,200.00
Target PAT 150.00
Synergies 100.00 75.00
Foregone interest (712.8 × 6.5%) (46.33) (34.75)
New debt interest (1,069.2 × 9%) (96.23) (72.17)
PPA amortisation (80.00) (60.00)
NCI: 34% × (150 + 75 − 60) (56.10)
Pro-forma PAT to owners 1,201.98

Note that NCI absorbs 34% of target-level synergies; if the same synergies arose in the acquirer, NCI would be 34% × 90 = ₹30.60 crore and pro-forma PAT ₹1,227.48 crore.

Practice Questions

Q1. Tax-effecting each line [L1]

Acquirer PAT ₹780 crore; target PAT ₹95 crore; pre-tax synergies ₹30 crore; cash used ₹400 crore at 7%; new debt ₹900 crore at 9.5%; PPA amortisation ₹42 crore; 100% ownership; tax 25.168%. Compute pro-forma PAT, showing each after-tax adjustment.

Q2. Synergy location and NCI [L2]

Using the illustration's data but with ownership of 55% instead of 66% (consideration and funding scaled proportionately), compute pro-forma PAT to owners under two cases: synergies arising wholly in the target, and wholly in the acquirer. Explain why the results differ.

Q3. Non-deductible interest case [L2]

In Q1, tax counsel concludes that only 40% of the new debt interest will be deductible. Recompute pro-forma PAT and state how much accretion is lost relative to the fully deductible case, given acquirer standalone shares of 39 crore and no new shares.
