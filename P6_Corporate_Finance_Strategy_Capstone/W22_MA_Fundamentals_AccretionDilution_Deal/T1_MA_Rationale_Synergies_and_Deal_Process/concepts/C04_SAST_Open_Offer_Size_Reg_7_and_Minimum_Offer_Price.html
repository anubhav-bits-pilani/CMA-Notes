Key concept

SAST Open-Offer Size (Reg 7) and Minimum Offer Price (Reg 8)

Concept: SAST open offer: minimum 26% of total shares (Reg 7); price per Reg 8 = highest of negotiated price, 52-week VWAP paid, 26-week highest price, 60-trading-day VWAP

Statement

Offer size (Reg 7(1)): an open offer triggered under Reg 3 or Reg 4 must be for at least 26% of the target's total shares as of the 10th working day from the closure of the tendering period (Reg 7(2) permits voluntary offers of at least 10%).

\[ Q_{\text{offer}} \geq 26\% \times \text{Total shares (expanded voting capital)} \]

Offer price (Reg 8(2), direct acquisitions of frequently traded shares):

\[ P_{\text{offer}} \geq \max\left(P_{\text{neg}},\ \text{VWAP}^{\text{paid}}_{52w},\ P^{\text{paid}}_{\max, 26w},\ \text{VWAP}^{\text{mkt}}_{60d}\right) \]

where:

  • \(P_{\text{neg}}\) = highest negotiated price per share under the agreement that triggered the offer (for example the SPA price);
  • \(\text{VWAP}^{\text{paid}}_{52w}\) = volume-weighted average price paid or payable by the acquirer or PACs for acquisitions during the 52 weeks immediately before the public announcement;
  • \(P^{\text{paid}}_{\max, 26w}\) = highest price paid or payable by the acquirer or PACs for any acquisition during the 26 weeks immediately before the public announcement;
  • \(\text{VWAP}^{\text{mkt}}_{60d}\) = volume-weighted average market price for the 60 trading days before the public announcement on the exchange with the highest trading volume, if the shares are frequently traded.

If shares are infrequently traded, the fourth parameter is replaced by a price determined by the acquirer and manager to the offer using valuation parameters such as book value, comparable trading multiples and earnings per share.

Explanation

The rules ensure that public shareholders get the same economic terms as the seller and are not hurt by a pre-offer price run-up or by the acquirer's previous purchases.

  • Total shares includes shares that will be issued on conversion of convertibles before the offer closes (fully diluted voting capital).
  • Non-compete fees and other payments to the seller are added to the negotiated price per share if paid in connection with the transaction, so they cannot be used to pay the seller a hidden premium.
  • Indirect acquisitions (Reg 8(3)) add further parameters, such as the price paid during the period between the earlier of announcement and agreement, and interest at 10% per year for delay where specified.
  • Price revisions (Reg 8(8)–(10)): if the acquirer buys shares above the offer price during the offer period, the offer price is revised upward. Buying at a higher price within 26 weeks after the tendering period ends requires paying the difference to all who tendered (except stock-exchange or permitted purchases).
  • Acceptance uncertainty: the acquirer must fund 26% even though actual acceptance may be much lower. That is why Sources & Uses and the EPS model need acceptance scenarios (0%, 50%, 100% of the offer).

Illustration

Target: 12 crore shares. SPA with promoters at ₹520 per share for 30%. In the previous 52 weeks the acquirer bought 0.5 crore shares at a VWAP of ₹480; the highest price paid in the last 26 weeks was ₹535. The 60-trading- day market VWAP is ₹505.

  • Offer price = max(520, 480, 535, 505) = ₹535 per share.
  • Offer size = 26% × 12 crore = 3.12 crore shares; maximum consideration = 3.12 × 535 = ₹1,669.2 crore.
  • Escrow under Reg 17 = 25% × 500 + 10% × 1,169.2 = ₹241.9 crore.
  • If fully accepted, post-offer holding = 30% + 4.17% (earlier market purchase) + 26% = 60.2%.

Practice Questions

Q1. Computing the Reg 8 price [L1]

SPA price ₹310; acquirer's 52-week VWAP of purchases ₹295; highest price paid in the last 26 weeks ₹318; 60-day market VWAP ₹322; the promoter also receives a non-compete fee equivalent to ₹15 per share. Compute the minimum offer price.

Q2. Funding and escrow by acceptance level [L2]

Target shares 25 crore; offer for 26% at ₹880. Compute maximum offer consideration, the Reg 17 escrow, and the cash needed at 30%, 60% and 100% acceptance. Show how much bank guarantee can be used versus cash.

💡 Hint: Reg 17 allows a bank guarantee for most of the escrow but needs a minimum cash component.

Q3. Infrequently traded target [L2]

The target's shares traded on only 40 days in the last year, with total volume of 0.6% of shares. Explain how the fourth price parameter is determined, which valuation methods are acceptable, and how you would document the valuation for the manager to the offer.
