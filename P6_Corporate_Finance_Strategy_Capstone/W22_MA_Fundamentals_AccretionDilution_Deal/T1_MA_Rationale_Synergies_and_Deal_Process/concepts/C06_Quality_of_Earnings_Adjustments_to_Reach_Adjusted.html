Key concept

Quality of Earnings Adjustments to Reach Adjusted EBITDA

Concept: QoE adjustments: Reported EBITDA ± non-recurring items ± related-party normalisation ± Ind AS 116/115 adjustments = Adjusted EBITDA

Statement

\[ \text{Adjusted EBITDA} = \text{Reported EBITDA} \pm \text{NR} \pm \text{RP} \pm \text{IA}_{116} \pm \text{IA}_{115} \pm \text{Other} \]

where:

  • \(\text{NR}\) = non-recurring items: add back one-off costs, deduct one-off gains;
  • \(\text{RP}\) = related-party normalisation: restate related-party transactions to arm's-length terms that will apply after the deal;
  • \(\text{IA}_{116}\) = Ind AS 116 adjustment: deduct lease rentals if a pre-Ind AS 116 (lease-adjusted) EBITDA is needed for comparability with the valuation multiple;
  • \(\text{IA}_{115}\) = Ind AS 115 adjustments: correct revenue recognition (cut-off, bill-and-hold, variable consideration, principal vs agent);
  • \(\text{Other}\) = policy alignment, run-rate and pro-forma effects, standalone-cost adjustments in carve-outs.

Explanation

The purpose is to find the sustainable, recurring EBITDA of the business as the buyer will own it, so that it can be multiplied by a valuation multiple. Each adjustment must be evidenced and must be consistent with the basis of the multiple.

  • Non-recurring: the test is whether the item is unlikely to recur in the normal course. "Recurring non-recurring" items such as annual restructuring charges should not be added back. Exceptional items under Schedule III are a starting point, not a conclusion.
  • Related-party normalisation: common in promoter-run Indian companies, for example below-market rent to a promoter entity, purchases from or sales to group firms at non-arm's-length prices, or management fees to the holding company. If the arrangement will end or be re-priced after closing, normalise it. Sec 188 approvals and transfer-pricing documentation help evidence market terms.
  • Ind AS 116: since 2019, lease rentals are replaced by depreciation and interest, which raises EBITDA. Trading comps and transaction multiples may be on either basis. If EBITDA is post-Ind AS 116, lease liabilities must be included in net debt. If it is pre-Ind AS 116, deduct rentals and exclude lease liabilities. Never mix the two.
  • Ind AS 115: look for revenue pulled forward (year-end channel stuffing reversed by credit notes), bill-and-hold arrangements that fail the criteria, rebates and returns under-provided, and gross vs net presentation for agency sales (affects revenue, not EBITDA, but changes margins).
  • Direction: sellers present add-backs; buyers look for deductions. A good QoE shows both and reconciles reported to adjusted EBITDA line by line.

Illustration

₹ crore FY26
Reported EBITDA (post-Ind AS 116) 240.0
Add: one-off legal settlement 8.0
Less: profit on sale of surplus land included in other income (15.0)
Less: promoter rent below market (market ₹6 crore, paid ₹1 crore) (5.0)
Less: March sales reversed by April credit notes (margin effect) (4.5)
Add: excess bonus to promoter director above market 2.0
Adjusted EBITDA (post-Ind AS 116) 225.5
Less: lease rentals (pre-Ind AS 116 basis) (18.0)
Adjusted EBITDA (pre-Ind AS 116) 207.5

At a 10x EV/EBITDA multiple on a post-Ind AS 116 basis, the adjustments reduce EV by ₹145 crore relative to the reported figure.

Connections

Practice Questions

Q1. Build an adjusted EBITDA bridge [L2]

Reported EBITDA ₹96 crore (post-Ind AS 116). Items: ₹4 crore VRS cost (the company has run a VRS every year for 4 years); ₹3 crore insurance claim received for a fire; purchases from a promoter firm at 8% above market on ₹50 crore of purchases; ₹7 crore of revenue from a bill-and-hold arrangement that fails Ind AS 115 criteria (40% gross margin); lease rentals ₹9 crore. Compute Adjusted EBITDA on both lease bases.

💡 Hint: Ask whether a cost that recurs every year can be non-recurring.

Q2. Consistency with the multiple [L2]

Peer transaction multiples are 9x pre-Ind AS 116 EBITDA. The target's adjusted EBITDA is ₹150 crore post-Ind AS 116, lease rentals are ₹20 crore and lease liabilities are ₹110 crore. Financial net debt is ₹200 crore. Compute equity value correctly and show the error from applying 9x to post-Ind AS 116 EBITDA while deducting only financial debt.

Q3. Interview question on QoE [L3]

"Walk me through the five QoE adjustments you would expect in a promoter-owned Indian manufacturing company and how you would evidence each." Prepare a structured answer.
