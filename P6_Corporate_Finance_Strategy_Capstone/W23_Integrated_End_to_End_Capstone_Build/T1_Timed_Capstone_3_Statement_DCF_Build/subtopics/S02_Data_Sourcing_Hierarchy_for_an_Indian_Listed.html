Data Sourcing Hierarchy for an Indian Listed-Company Model

Scope: Data sourcing: annual reports, LODR Reg 33 quarterly results, investor presentations, earnings call transcripts, XBRL; Screener/Tijori/Trendlyne for cross-checks

Why it matters

Every number in the capstone model must trace back to a source document (Week 23 cross-cutting concept on traceability). In a timed build, the risk is sourcing from whatever is quickest, usually an aggregator website, and then finding on Day 2 that its "other income" includes a fair value gain or its debt figure leaves out lease liabilities. Interviewers and model reviewers often ask "where did this number come from?" The answer should be a document, a page and a date, not "Screener". The discipline is: primary sources for inputs, aggregators only to cross-check.

Core notes

The source hierarchy

Rank Source What you take from it Where to find it
1 Audited annual report (consolidated FS + notes) All historical IS/BS/CFS lines, segment note, lease note, borrowings note, tax note, contingent liabilities, related parties Company website (Investors), NSE corporate filings
2 LODR Reg 33 quarterly/half-yearly results Latest quarters, half-year balance sheet and cash flow, segment results NSE "Financial Results", company website
3 Investor presentations Volumes, capacity, utilisation, realisation, store count, capex plans, guidance NSE "Corporate Announcements" (filed under Reg 30)
4 Earnings call transcripts/recordings Management guidance, explanations of one-offs, pricing and cost commentary Company website (Reg 46), NSE announcements
5 XBRL filings Machine-readable financial data for bulk extraction and cross-checks NSE/BSE XBRL results, MCA AOC-4 XBRL
6 Aggregators (Screener, Tijori, Trendlyne) Cross-checks, quick ratios, peer screens, shareholding Websites (free/paid tiers)
7 Market data CMP, shares outstanding, 10-year G-sec yield, Nifty index levels for beta NSE, RBI/CCIL/FBIL, Investing.com

Annual reports

The annual report is the anchor. Use the consolidated financial statements and take each year's figures from the following year's report where comparatives have been restated (for example FY25 from the FY26 report). Comparatives reflect any reclassifications and Ind AS 8 restatements, so the latest presentation gives you a consistent series. Record the restatement in your source log. Key notes to read before entering any numbers: accounting policies (revenue, leases, inventories), segment information (Ind AS 108), PPE and CWIP schedules (Schedule III requires CWIP ageing), borrowings (security, repayment terms), Ind AS 116 lease note (maturity analysis, ROU asset roll-forward), income tax (effective rate reconciliation, whether the 115BAA regime is used), exceptional items, and contingent liabilities. The auditor's report (Key Audit Matters, Ind AS on Auditing SA 701) tells you where judgement sits.

LODR Reg 33 quarterly results

SEBI LODR Reg 33 requires listed entities to file quarterly and year-to-date financial results within 45 days of each quarter-end, and annual audited results within 60 days of the year-end. At the half-year (Q2) the statement of assets and liabilities and a cash flow statement are also required. Uses in the capstone:

  • Run-rate checks: annualise the latest two quarters (H1) to test whether your Year 1 forecast is plausible.
  • Stub balance sheet: the 30 September balance sheet lets you update net debt for the valuation date if you value mid-year.
  • Segment results for the latest quarters (Reg 33 requires segment reporting in the results format).

Quarterly results carry a limited review report, not an audit. Q4 figures are usually derived as the audited full year minus the reviewed nine months, so treat them with care.

Investor presentations

Investor decks are filed with the exchanges as Reg 30 disclosures. They are where Indian mid-caps disclose the operating KPIs a driver-based model needs: tonnes sold, capacity (MTPA), utilisation, EBITDA/tonne, store count, ARPU, order book. They are unaudited and promotional, so:

  • reconcile deck revenue to reported revenue (decks sometimes show "gross revenue" before discounts, or exclude other operating income);
  • note definition changes between decks (e.g. "EBITDA excluding other income" in one year, including it in the next);
  • keep the deck date and slide number in the source log.

Earnings call transcripts

Since the 2023 LODR amendments, audio/video recordings of earnings calls must be on the website promptly and transcripts within five working days (Reg 46 read with Reg 30). Transcripts give management's explanations: why margins moved, guidance on capex and commissioning dates, pricing actions and the timing of one-offs. In your model, guidance is an input with a source, not a fact. Tag it as "Mgmt guidance, Q3FY26 call, p.7" and decide whether your base case adopts it, haircuts it or rejects it.

XBRL

Listed companies file results in XBRL with the exchanges, and companies file financial statements in XBRL with the MCA (AOC-4 XBRL) under the Companies (Filing of Documents and Forms in XBRL) Rules. XBRL tags line items consistently, so you can pull five years quickly using Power Query on the downloaded instance files or the exchange's XBRL viewer. Limitations: tagging errors happen, custom elements are common, and notes may be less detailed than the PDF annual report. Use XBRL for speed and the PDF for verification.

Aggregators for cross-checks only

Screener, Tijori and Trendlyne standardise statements into their own templates. They are excellent for:

  • catching data-entry errors (if your FY24 revenue differs from Screener's by more than rounding, investigate);
  • quick peer screens and five-year ratio histories (Week 6–7);
  • shareholding pattern and pledge data;
  • industry KPIs that Tijori collects from decks.

They are poor as primary sources because their classifications are opaque: "Other income" may include exceptional gains, "Borrowings" may or may not include lease liabilities, and some report standalone figures by default. Never paste an aggregator export into your Historicals tab as the source of record.

The source log

Keep a "Sources" table on the Inputs or Cover sheet:

Ref Document Date/filed Page/slide Used for
S1 Annual Report FY26 (consolidated) 14-Jul-2026 pp. 212–290 FY25–FY26 IS/BS/CFS
S2 Annual Report FY24 18-Jul-2024 pp. 198–270 FY22–FY23 comparatives
S3 Q2FY27 Reg 33 results 08-Nov-2026 pp. 2–6 H1 run-rate, Sep-26 net debt
S4 Investor presentation Q2FY27 09-Nov-2026 slides 6, 11 Volumes, capacity
S5 Q2FY27 earnings call transcript 13-Nov-2026 pp. 5–9 Capex guidance
S6 FBIL 10-yr G-sec yield 21-Nov-2026 — Risk-free rate

Every hard-coded historical cell then carries a comment or adjacent column with its source reference. An illustration of the tie-out: if your FY26 revenue from operations per S1 is ₹2,416.3 crore and Screener shows ₹2,416 crore, accept it. If Screener shows ₹2,590 crore, check whether it is adding other income (₹173.7 crore).

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Restated comparatives versus original reports. Use the latest restated figures for trend analysis, but if a restatement is material (an Ind AS 8 error correction), note the original figure as well. It is a governance signal (Week 6–7 forensic checks).
  • Standalone versus consolidated in Reg 33. Companies can file both; quarterly segment information may appear only in the consolidated results. Do not mix them.
  • Units. Annual reports may be in ₹ lakh, ₹ million or ₹ crore, and the unit can change between years. Convert at entry and label the unit on every row header.
  • Deck numbers that do not reconcile. If deck EBITDA cannot be tied to reported figures, build your own EBITDA from the IS and footnote the gap rather than adopting the deck figure.
  • Paywalled transcripts. Use the company-hosted versions. LODR requires them on the company website.
  • Market data timing. Fix one valuation date and take CMP, the G-sec yield and the share count on that date. Mismatched dates are a common consistency failure.

Key takeaways

  • Primary sources (annual report, Reg 33 results) feed inputs. Aggregators only cross-check them.
  • Take each year from the latest report that presents it, to pick up restated comparatives.
  • Reg 33: quarterly results within 45 days, annual within 60. Half-year results add a balance sheet and cash flow statement for a stub update.
  • Investor decks and call transcripts provide KPIs and guidance. They are unaudited and must be reconciled and tagged as guidance.
  • XBRL speeds extraction, but verify against the PDF. Keep a dated source log referenced from every hard-code.

Practice Questions

Q1. Build a source log [L1]

List the six documents you would download first for an NSE-listed cement mid-cap valued as at 30 November 2026, and for each give the exact data items you would extract and how you would reference them in the model.

Q2. Reconciling an aggregator [L2]

Your Historicals tab shows FY25 consolidated revenue from operations of ₹1,842.6 crore, finance costs of ₹38.4 crore and borrowings of ₹410.0 crore. Screener shows sales of ₹1,921.0 crore, interest of ₹52.9 crore and borrowings of ₹512.3 crore. The annual report shows other operating income of ₹78.4 crore, interest on lease liabilities of ₹14.5 crore and lease liabilities of ₹102.3 crore. Explain each difference and state which figures your model should use.

💡 Hint: Test each gap against a single disclosed item before you assume an entry error.

Q3. Using H1 results for a run-rate test [L2]

A company's Reg 33 results show H1FY27 revenue of ₹1,140 crore against H1FY26 revenue of ₹1,010 crore. Its FY26 full-year revenue was ₹2,180 crore and your FY27 forecast is ₹2,600 crore. Assess whether the forecast is plausible, stating what seasonality information you would look for and where.

Q4. Guidance as an input [L3]

On the Q2 call, management guides that a 2 MTPA brownfield line will be commissioned "by Q1FY28" at a capex of ₹900 crore. The previous two expansions were commissioned six and nine months late, and capex overran by 12% and 18%. Describe how you would encode this guidance in the Inputs sheet, base case and downside scenario, and how you would document it.

Q5. Power Query XBRL extraction [L3]

Outline a Power Query process to load five years of exchange XBRL results files into a single table of line item, period and value, and design the validation step that compares the output with your manually entered annual report figures.
