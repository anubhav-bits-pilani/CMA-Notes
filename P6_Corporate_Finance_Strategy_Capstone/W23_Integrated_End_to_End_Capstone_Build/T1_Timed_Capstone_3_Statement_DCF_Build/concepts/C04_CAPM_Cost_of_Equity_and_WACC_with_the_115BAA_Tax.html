Key concept

CAPM Cost of Equity and WACC with the 115BAA Tax Rate

Concept: Ke = Rf + β × ERP; WACC = E/V × Ke + D/V × Kd × (1 − t); t ≈ 25.17% under Sec 115BAA

Statement

\[ K_e = R_f + \beta \times \text{ERP} \]

\[ \text{WACC} = \frac{E}{V} \times K_e + \frac{D}{V} \times K_d \times (1 - t), \qquad V = E + D \]

\[ t_{115\text{BAA}} = 22\% \times (1 + 10\%) \times (1 + 4\%) = 25.168\% \]

where \(R_f\) is the 10-year Government of India bond yield, \(\beta\) is the levered equity beta against the Indian market index, ERP is the equity risk premium, \(K_d\) is the marginal pre-tax cost of debt, \(E\) and \(D\) are market (or target) values of equity and debt, and \(t\) is the marginal corporate tax rate (22% base rate, 10% surcharge and 4% Health and Education Cess under Sec 115BAA).

Explanation

CAPM prices equity for systematic risk only. \(R_f\) compensates for time and inflation, and \(\beta \times \text{ERP}\) compensates for co-movement with the market. In India:

  • \(R_f\): the 10-year G-sec yield on the valuation date (s06). Matching rupee cash flows with a rupee risk-free rate keeps inflation consistent. Using a US Treasury yield with rupee cash flows is a classic error.
  • \(\beta\): regressed on Nifty 50 or Nifty 500, Blume-adjusted, or preferably bottom-up (unlever peers, relever at the target D/E).
  • ERP: about 6–8% for India from practitioner sources, cited with a date.

WACC weights should reflect the long-run target capital structure at market values, not book values. If the company is moving from high to low leverage, a single WACC is an approximation, and the APV approach or a year-varying WACC is more precise.

Tax rate. Domestic companies opting for Sec 115BAA pay 22% plus a flat 10% surcharge and 4% cess, which is 25.168%. They forgo most deductions and incentives, and MAT (Sec 115JB) does not apply. Once exercised, the option cannot be withdrawn. Sec 115BAB (new manufacturing companies, 15% base rate, effective 17.16%) applied only to companies commencing manufacturing by 31 March 2024. Companies still on the old regime (30% base rate, effective up to about 34.94% including the 12% surcharge for income above ₹10 crore) often plan to switch once MAT credits and incentives are used up. Model the switch year. The "≈" in the concept reflects this: use 25.17% unless the company's tax note shows otherwise. Note that the Income-tax Act, 2025 replaces the 1961 Act from 1 April 2026 with renumbered sections. The concessional regime continues, but check the new section reference for any filing dated after that.

Kd after tax reflects the interest tax shield, so FCFF must use unlevered tax (c01) to avoid counting the shield twice. On the post-Ind AS 116 basis, include lease liabilities in D, at a Kd near the incremental borrowing rate.

Illustration

Mid-cap auto component maker. \(R_f = 6.6\%\), peer unlevered β 0.85, target D/E 0.30, ERP 7.0%, \(K_d = 9.2\%\), \(t = 25.17\%\).

\[ \beta_L = 0.85 \times [1 + 0.7483 \times 0.30] = 1.041, \qquad K_e = 6.6\% + 1.041 \times 7.0\% = 13.89\% \]

D/E 0.30 means D/V = 0.30 ÷ 1.30 = 23.1% and E/V = 76.9%.

\[ \text{WACC} = 0.769 \times 13.89\% + 0.231 \times 9.2\% \times 0.7483 = 10.68\% + 1.59\% = 12.27\% \]

Sensitivity: each 0.1 change in β moves Ke by 70 bps and WACC by about 54 bps.

Practice Questions

Q1. Compute WACC [L1]

\(R_f\) 6.45%, β 1.15, ERP 7.5%, \(K_d\) 9.8%, market cap ₹7,200 crore, borrowings ₹1,350 crore, lease liabilities ₹150 crore (post-116 basis), tax 25.17%. Compute Ke and WACC.

Q2. Old regime versus 115BAA [L2]

A company currently pays tax under the old regime at an effective 34.94% with ₹220 crore of unutilised MAT credit, and expects to use it up by FY29 and then opt for 115BAA. Explain how the tax rate in FCFF and in WACC should be set for FY27–FY29 and thereafter.

💡 Hint: Separate the cash tax in FCFF from the marginal rate that determines the debt shield.

Q3. Currency mismatch [L2]

An intern uses a 4.3% US 10-year Treasury yield and a 5.0% mature-market ERP to discount rupee FCFF forecasts that assume 5% Indian inflation. Explain the error and its direction, and fix the inputs.
