Key concept

Value Creation Spread: ROIC Minus WACC

Concept: Value creation spread = ROIC − WACC, ranked alongside growth

Statement

\[ \text{Spread} = \text{ROIC} - \text{WACC}, \qquad \text{Economic profit} = (\text{ROIC} - \text{WACC}) \times \text{Invested capital} \]

where:

  • \(\text{ROIC} = \text{NOPAT} / \text{Average invested capital}\), with \(\text{NOPAT} = \text{EBIT} \times (1 - t)\);
  • \(\text{WACC} = \frac{E}{D+E} k_e + \frac{D}{D+E} k_d (1 - t)\), with \(k_e\) from CAPM (\(r_f + \beta \times \text{ERP}\)), \(k_d\) the pre-tax cost of debt, and \(t\) the marginal tax rate (25.17% under Sec 115BAA for most domestic companies).

A positive spread means each rupee of invested capital earns more than its opportunity cost. Growth then creates value. A negative spread means growth destroys value.

Explanation

Why rank it alongside growth. The value-driver relationship

\[ \text{Value} = \frac{\text{NOPAT}_{t+1} \times \left(1 - \frac{g}{\text{ROIC}}\right)}{\text{WACC} - g} \]

shows that growth \(g\) increases value only if ROIC > WACC. A peer matrix that rewards CAGR without the spread can rank a value-destroying grower first. Pairing spread with growth, either as two matrix metrics or as the growth vs ROIC quadrant with a WACC line (s05), fixes this.

Absolute anchor. Z-scores and ranks are relative (c03). The spread anchors the matrix to an absolute hurdle, so a peer set that is entirely below WACC cannot look "good" just because it beats its peers.

Estimating WACC for the peer report.

  • Use a consistent methodology for all peers: the same risk-free rate (10-year G-sec yield, roughly 6.3–7.2% over 2024–25), the same equity risk premium (commonly 5–7% for India), and the same tax rate convention.
  • Betas: peer-specific regression betas from NSE data are noisy. A common approach is to unlever an industry beta and relever at each peer's target capital structure.
  • Net-cash IT peers have WACC ≈ cost of equity. Treatment of surplus cash in ROIC must match (exclude it from IC).
  • Lease liabilities: if IC includes ROU assets (post-Ind AS 116), WACC should include lease debt at its incremental borrowing rate. Consistency matters more than precision.

Cautions. ROIC on book IC is distorted by old asset bases, acquired goodwill and intangibles that are not capitalised (IT talent and brand). Asset-light IT companies show very high ROIC (40–60%+), where the spread mostly tells you "excellent business", not "excellent incremental investment". Incremental ROIC (ΔNOPAT ÷ ΔIC over three years) is a useful supplement.

Illustration

Peer ROIC WACC Spread Avg IC (₹ crore) Economic profit (₹ crore) 3-yr CAGR
J1 24.0% 12.0% +12.0 pp 18,000 2,160 21%
J2 14.0% 12.5% +1.5 pp 14,000 210 30%
J3 11.0% 13.0% −2.0 pp 6,500 −130 26%

J2's fastest growth adds little value (spread 1.5 pp). J3 grows at 26% while destroying ₹130 crore of value a year. A growth-only ranking (J2 > J3 > J1) inverts the value ranking (J1 > J2 > J3).

Practice Questions

Q1. Compute spread and economic profit [L1]

A jeweller reports EBIT ₹1,450 crore, tax rate 25.17%, opening invested capital ₹7,800 crore and closing ₹8,600 crore. Its cost of equity is 13.5%, pre-tax cost of debt 9.0%, and target D/(D+E) 25%. Compute ROIC, WACC, spread and economic profit.

Q2. Growth that destroys value [L2]

Using the value-driver formula, compare two companies with NOPAT next year of ₹500 crore, WACC 12% and growth 8%. Company A has ROIC 20% and Company B has ROIC 10%. Compute each value. Then recompute at growth 10%, and explain the opposite directions of the change.

💡 Hint: The reinvestment rate is g/ROIC. Check what happens to free cash flow as growth rises when ROIC is below WACC.

Q3. Consistent WACC across peers [L3]

Your peer set has two net-cash IT companies and one with modest net debt. Regression betas are 0.62, 0.95 and 1.30 with low R-squared. Propose a consistent WACC method for all three. Cover risk-free rate, ERP, beta approach, capital structure weights, treatment of surplus cash and lease liabilities. Explain how inconsistency could reverse the spread ranking.
