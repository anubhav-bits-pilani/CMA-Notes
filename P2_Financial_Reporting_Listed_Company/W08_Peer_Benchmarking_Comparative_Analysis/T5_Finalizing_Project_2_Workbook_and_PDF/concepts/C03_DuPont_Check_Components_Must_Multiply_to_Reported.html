Key concept

DuPont Check: Components Must Multiply to Reported ROE

Concept: DuPont check: product of components − Reported ROE = 0

Statement

Three-step DuPont:

\[ \text{ROE} = \frac{\text{NI}}{\text{Revenue}} \times \frac{\text{Revenue}}{\text{Avg TA}} \times \frac{\text{Avg TA}}{\text{Avg Equity}} \]

Five-step DuPont:

\[ \text{ROE} = \frac{\text{NI}}{\text{EBT}} \times \frac{\text{EBT}}{\text{EBIT}} \times \frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Revenue}} \times \frac{\text{Revenue}}{\text{Avg TA}} \times \frac{\text{Avg TA}}{\text{Avg Equity}} \]

The check:

\[ \text{DuPontCheck} = \prod_{j} \text{Component}_j - \text{ROE}_{\text{engine}} = 0 \]

where NI is net income (profit for the year), EBT is profit before tax, EBIT is earnings before interest and tax, Avg TA is average total assets, Avg Equity is average shareholders' equity, the components are tax burden (NI/EBT), interest burden (EBT/EBIT), EBIT margin, asset turnover and equity multiplier, and \(\text{ROE}_{\text{engine}}\) is ROE computed directly in the ratio engine (NI ÷ Avg Equity) per the definitions sheet.

Explanation

Algebraically the components telescope to NI/Equity, so a non-zero check means the components and the direct ROE are not using the same definitions. That is exactly why the check is valuable: it enforces definitional consistency. Common causes:

  • NCI mismatch: ROE in the engine uses profit attributable to owners of the parent and owners' equity (excluding NCI), but DuPont uses total profit (including NCI share) or total equity. Choose one pairing — attributable profit with owners' equity is standard — and use it in both.
  • Average vs closing balances: asset turnover on average assets but equity multiplier on closing figures.
  • Exceptional items: DuPont on normalized NI but engine ROE on reported NI (or vice versa).
  • EBIT definition: other income included in EBIT in one place and excluded in another — the five-step product still telescopes only if EBIT is consistent across the interest-burden and margin terms.
  • Rounding: if each component is rounded before multiplying (e.g., to one decimal of %), the product drifts; multiply unrounded values and round only for display.

"Reported ROE" in this concept means the ROE in your ratio engine, not a figure from the company's investor presentation, which may use different definitions; a company-published ROE can be shown as a cross-reference warning, not an error.

DuPont3    = NPM * AssetTurnover * EquityMultiplier
DuPont5    = TaxBurden * InterestBurden * EBITMargin * AssetTurnover * EquityMultiplier
CheckDuP   = ROUND(DuPont5 - ROE_Engine, 6)

Use a tight tolerance (e.g., 0.0001, i.e., 0.01 percentage point) because this is a pure identity.

Illustration

Peer B FY25 (₹ crore): revenue 18,500; EBIT 1,420; EBT 1,310; profit for the year 980 (of which NCI 30); average total assets 12,400; average total equity 6,250 (of which NCI 150).

Engine ROE (owners) = 950 ÷ 6,100 = 15.57%. A DuPont table using profit for the year (980) and total equity (6,250): tax burden 0.7481 × interest burden 0.9225 × EBIT margin 7.676% × asset turnover 1.4919 × equity multiplier 1.9840 = 15.68%. Check = +0.11 pp → fails. Rebuilding with attributable profit (950) and owners' equity (6,100) gives 15.57% and the check is zero.

Practice Questions

Q1. Build and check a DuPont table [L2]

Peer C FY25 (₹ crore): revenue 6,240; EBIT 512; EBT 436; profit for the year 324 (no NCI); total assets FY24 4,880 and FY25 5,460; total equity FY24 2,010 and FY25 2,290. Build the five-step DuPont on average balances, compute engine ROE and the check, and state the tolerance you would use.

Q2. Diagnose a failure [L2]

A DuPont check fails by 0.42 percentage points for one peer only. The peer had a ₹210 crore exceptional impairment in FY25 and has NCI. List the checks you would run, in order, to locate the cause.

💡 Hint: Compare the numerator and denominator of each component with the engine's ROE definition one at a time.

Q3. Interview question [L1]

An interviewer asks why you need a DuPont check if the components "mathematically" equal ROE. Answer in four or five sentences with one concrete example.
