Phase 2: Financial Reporting & Listed Company Analysis

Weeks 5–8 (192 Hours)

Develop institutional equity/credit analyst skills to dissect Indian listed company annual reports, evaluate earnings quality, and benchmark peers across 3+ years.

Phase deliverable: Comparative Financial Analysis Report covering 3 NSE-listed Indian companies across 3 historical years (Ratios, DuPont, Cash Conversion, Earnings Quality & Executive Memo).

Week 5: Income Statement, Balance Sheet & Cash Flow Statement Dissection

Normalize reported GAAP/Ind AS financial statements to uncover core recurring operating earnings and true cash generation.

  • T1 · Horizontal and Vertical (Common-Size) Analysis
  • T2 · Normalizing EBITDA and Core Operating Profit
  • T3 · Balance Sheet Quality and Capital Employed
  • T4 · Cash Flow Forensics, Capex and FCFF/FCFE
  • T5 · Standalone vs Consolidated Statements
  • T6 · Segment Reporting Analysis (Ind AS 108)
Week 6: Ratio Architecture, DuPont Decomposition & Working Capital Efficiency

Master operational efficiency metrics, 3-step and 5-step DuPont ROE decomposition, and cash conversion cycle diagnostics.

  • T1 · Profitability and Return Ratios
  • T2 · 3-Step and 5-Step DuPont Analysis
  • T3 · Working Capital and Cash Conversion Cycle
  • T4 · Liquidity and Solvency Ratios
  • T5 · Asset Turnover and Fixed Asset Productivity
  • T6 · Sector-Specific KPIs
Week 7: Annual Report Forensics, Auditor's Report, CARO & MD&A

Apply your CMA audit background to spot accounting red flags, aggressive revenue recognition, and governance risks in listed company filings.

  • T1 · Auditor's Report, KAMs and CARO 2020
  • T2 · MD&A Extraction and Interpretation
  • T3 · Earnings Quality and Accounting Red Flags
  • T4 · Off-Balance-Sheet and Contingent Liabilities
  • T5 · Related Party and Promoter Governance Checks
  • T6 · Beneish M-Score and Altman Z-Score
Week 8: Peer Benchmarking & Comparative Analysis Report (Project 2 Delivery)

Synthesize multi-company financial data into an institutional-grade comparative financial analysis report and executive presentation.

  • T1 · Peer Selection and Calendarization
  • T2 · Relative Margin and Capital Efficiency Matrix
  • T3 · Executive Narrative (Pyramid Principle)
  • T4 · Charting for Financial Reports
  • T5 · Finalizing Project 2 Workbook and PDF Report
