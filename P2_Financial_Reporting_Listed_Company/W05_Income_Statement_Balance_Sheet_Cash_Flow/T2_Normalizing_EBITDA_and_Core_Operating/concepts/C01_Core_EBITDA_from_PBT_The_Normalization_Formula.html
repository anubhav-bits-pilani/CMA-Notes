Key concept

Core EBITDA from PBT: The Normalization Formula

Concept: Core EBITDA = PBT + finance cost + D&A − other income ± exceptional items ± non-recurring items embedded in operating lines

Statement

\[ \text{Core EBITDA} = \text{PBT} + \text{FC} + \text{DA} - \text{OI} + \text{EX}_{\text{loss}} - \text{EX}_{\text{gain}} + \text{NR}_{\text{exp}} - \text{NR}_{\text{inc}} \]

where PBT = profit before tax (continuing operations, as reported), FC = finance costs, DA = depreciation and amortisation expense, OI = other income (non-operating portion per policy), \(\text{EX}_{\text{loss}}\) / \(\text{EX}_{\text{gain}}\) = exceptional losses added back / gains deducted, and \(\text{NR}_{\text{exp}}\) / \(\text{NR}_{\text{inc}}\) = non-recurring expenses / income embedded within operating lines (COGS, employee cost, other expenses, other operating revenue), added back / deducted. In consolidated statements, PBT should exclude share of profit of associates and JVs (deduct it if the format places it above PBT).

Explanation

The formula starts from PBT because PBT is the last Ind AS-defined subtotal that captures all operating, financing and non-operating items before tax, so working upwards is mechanical and auditable:

  1. Add finance costs: removes the cost of debt and leases (interest on lease liabilities, Ind AS 23.6(e) forex), making EBITDA independent of capital structure.
  2. Add D&A: removes non-cash capital consumption (including ROU depreciation post-Ind AS 116).
  3. Subtract other income: removes treasury and non-operating income so that EBITDA reflects only the operating business; the cash generating it is valued separately in the EV bridge.
  4. Reverse exceptional items: add back exceptional losses, deduct exceptional gains — but only after a recurrence test (items recurring every year stay in).
  5. Reverse non-recurring items in operating lines: one-off inventory write-downs, provision reversals, prior-year incentive catch-ups, rent concessions, lease modification gains, one-time Labour Codes past service cost if not in exceptional items.

The signs (±) matter: gains are deducted, losses added. Symmetry is essential — the most common bias is to add back losses while leaving gains in.

Policy choices that must be stated alongside the formula: lease basis (post- or pre-Ind AS 116; for pre-116 deduct lease payments, see c02), operating forex (included), ESOP cost (kept), operating elements of other income (e.g., sub-letting rent — kept by adding back to EBITDA after the OI deduction).

Illustration

₹ crore FY2025
Profit before tax 1,320
Add: finance costs 210
Add: D&A 480
Less: other income (all non-operating) (290)
Add: exceptional loss — impairment of a brand 110
Less: exceptional gain — sale of land (75)
Add: inventory write-down after flood (in changes in inventories) 45
Less: reversal of excess warranty provision (in other expenses) (30)
Core EBITDA 1,770
Revenue from operations 12,000
Core EBITDA margin 14.75%

Mechanical EBITDA would be 1,320 + 210 + 480 = ₹2,010 crore (16.75%). The 200 bps gap is mostly other income.

Connections

This formula is the backbone of the whole topic: s01 (policy-consistent definition), s02 (exceptional items), s03 (other income), s04 (forex), s05 (other operating revenue), s06 (lease basis) and s07 (associates, discontinued operations, ESOP). It feeds c03 (core PAT, with tax effects) and Topic 4's CFO-to-EBITDA bridge. Core EBITDA is the base for EV/EBITDA multiples and DCF terminal-year margins (Weeks 15–16) and for quality-of-earnings work in M&A (Week 22).

Practice Questions

Q1. Apply the formula [L1]

From the following (₹ crore) compute core EBITDA and margin: revenue 6,800; PBT 740; finance costs 95; D&A 260; other income 145 (interest 80, FVTPL gains 50, sub-letting rent 15 — treat sub-letting as operating); exceptional items: VRS ₹30 crore (first such scheme in ten years); other expenses include ₹18 crore one-off consultancy for a cancelled acquisition.

Q2. Consolidated with associates [L2]

A consolidated P&L shows: profit before share of associates, exceptional items and tax ₹2,050 crore; share of profit of associates ₹170 crore; exceptional items ₹(60) crore (restructuring); PBT ₹2,160 crore; finance costs ₹240 crore; D&A ₹610 crore; other income ₹330 crore. Compute core EBITDA, stating your treatment of the associates line.

💡 Hint: Check whether the associates line is above or below the PBT figure you start from.

Q3. Bias detection [L3]

A company's adjustments over three years were: FY2023 add-backs ₹140 crore, deductions ₹0; FY2024 add-backs ₹165 crore, deductions ₹10 crore; FY2025 add-backs ₹190 crore, deductions ₹0. Meanwhile other income included gains on sale of PPE of ₹40 crore, ₹55 crore and ₹70 crore respectively. Explain the bias and recompute a symmetric adjustment total for each year.
