Key concept

Normalized ETR and the Ind AS 12 Tax-Rate Reconciliation

Concept: Normalized ETR = Normalized tax ÷ Normalized PBT; reconcile using Ind AS 12 tax-rate reconciliation note

Statement

\[ \text{ETR}_{\text{norm}} = \frac{T_{\text{reported}} - \sum_k A_k t_k - \text{NR}_{\text{tax}}}{\text{PBT}_{\text{reported}} - \sum_k A_k} \]

where \(T_{\text{reported}}\) is total tax expense (current + deferred) in the P&L, \(A_k\) are pre-tax non-recurring items removed (gains positive, losses negative), \(t_k\) is the tax rate applicable to each, and \(\text{NR}_{\text{tax}}\) are non-recurring tax items (rate-change remeasurements, prior-year tax adjustments, MAT credit write-offs, recognition/derecognition of DTA on past losses). The numerator is normalized tax; the denominator is normalized PBT. For consolidated statements, PBT should exclude share of profit of associates and JVs (which is post-tax).

Explanation

Ind AS 12.81(c) requires an explanation of the relationship between tax expense and accounting profit, either as a numerical reconciliation between tax expense and accounting profit multiplied by the applicable rate, or between the average effective rate and the applicable rate (or both), with disclosure of the basis on which the applicable rate is computed. In India this note typically starts with PBT × 25.168% (or 34.944%) and lists reconciling items:

Typical reconciling item Recurring?
Non-deductible expenses (CSR, penalties, s.14A disallowance) Mostly recurring
Income taxed at different rates (capital gains, foreign subsidiaries) Depends on item
Exempt income / deductions (s.80M dividends, s.80JJAA) Recurring if business pattern stable
Share of associates (post-tax, in consolidated PBT) Recurring but should be excluded from PBT for ETR
Effect of rate change on deferred tax Non-recurring
Tax relating to earlier years Non-recurring
Unrecognised DTA on losses of subsidiaries Recurring while losses continue
Recognition of previously unrecognised DTA Non-recurring
Goodwill impairment (non-deductible) Non-recurring

The normalized ETR is the rate to use in forecasts. Rules:

  • Use the reconciliation note to identify non-recurring items; remove them from tax and their pre-tax amounts from PBT.
  • Keep structural items (persistent non-deductible CSR at 2% of profits, foreign subsidiaries with different rates, ongoing unrecognised losses in start-up subsidiaries).
  • For consolidated groups, the ETR mixes parent and subsidiary rates; a growing loss-making subsidiary without DTA raises the group ETR — model by entity if material.
  • Compare with cash taxes (Topic 4 c06) to see whether the P&L rate is backed by payments.

Illustration

₹ crore Amount
PBT (consolidated, excluding share of associates) 2,000
Tax expense (reported) 470
Reported ETR 23.5%
Reconciliation (from Ind AS 12 note):
PBT × 25.168% 503.4
Non-deductible CSR and other 9.6
Losses of subsidiaries without DTA 22.0
Tax relating to earlier years (reversal on assessment) (40.0)
Remeasurement of DTL for rate change in an overseas subsidiary (25.0)
Total 470.0

Non-recurring items also include an exceptional restructuring loss of ₹100 crore in PBT (tax effect 25.168% = ₹25.2 crore).

Normalized tax = 470 + 40 + 25 + 25.2 = ₹560.2 crore. Normalized PBT = 2,000 + 100 = ₹2,100 crore. Normalized ETR = 560.2 ÷ 2,100 = 26.7%.

The normalized rate (26.7%) is above the statutory 25.168% because of structural items — CSR and subsidiary losses. Using the reported 23.5% in forecasts would overstate forecast PAT by about 4%.

Practice Questions

Q1. Compute normalized ETR [L1]

PBT ₹1,250 crore including an exceptional gain of ₹80 crore on sale of land (tax ₹11.4 crore); tax expense ₹305 crore including a reversal of ₹18 crore of tax provisions for earlier years. Compute reported and normalized ETR.

Q2. Read a reconciliation note [L2]

A tax reconciliation shows: PBT ₹3,400 crore × 25.168% = ₹855.7 crore; add non-deductible expenses ₹14.2 crore; add effect of losses of subsidiaries without DTA ₹48.0 crore; less income taxed at lower rates ₹(21.5) crore; less recognition of previously unrecognised DTA ₹(62.0) crore; less tax of earlier years ₹(9.4) crore. Compute reported tax and ETR, classify each item as recurring or non-recurring, and compute the normalized ETR.

💡 Hint: Only remove the items that will not repeat; keep structural ones.

Q3. Forecast ETR for a group [L3]

A group's parent (s.115BAA) earns PBT of ₹1,800 crore; a 100%-owned start-up subsidiary loses ₹200 crore a year (no DTA recognised) and is expected to break even in three years; a UAE subsidiary earns ₹300 crore taxed at 9%. Build a three-year forecast of group tax and ETR, and explain why the group ETR will change even if the parent's rate does not.
