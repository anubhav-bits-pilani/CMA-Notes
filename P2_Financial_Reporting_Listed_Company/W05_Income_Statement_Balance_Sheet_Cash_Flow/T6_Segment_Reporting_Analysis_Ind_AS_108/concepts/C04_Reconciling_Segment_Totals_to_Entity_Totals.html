Key concept

Reconciling Segment Totals to Entity Totals

Concept: Ind AS 108 para 28: reconcile segment revenue, profit, assets and liabilities to entity totals

Statement

Ind AS 108.28 requires reconciliations of:

\[ \sum_i \text{Segment revenue}_i - \text{Inter-segment eliminations} \pm \text{Other reconciling items} = \text{Entity revenue} \]

\[ \sum_i \text{Segment result}_i - \text{Finance cost} - \text{Unallocable expenses} + \text{Unallocable income} \pm \text{Other items} = \text{Entity PBT (continuing operations)} \]

\[ \sum_i \text{Segment assets}_i + \text{Unallocable assets} = \text{Entity total assets} \]

\[ \sum_i \text{Segment liabilities}_i + \text{Unallocable liabilities} = \text{Entity total liabilities} \]

plus the total of every other material segment item disclosed (e.g. D&A, capex) to the corresponding entity amount. All material reconciling items are separately identified and described (Ind AS 108.28).

Explanation

Because segment measures follow the CODM's basis (c05), the totals differ from Ind AS entity amounts. The reconciliation makes the gap transparent. Typical reconciling items:

  • inter-segment revenue eliminations;
  • corporate (unallocable) expenses and income, including treasury income;
  • finance cost (where segment result is pre-interest);
  • exceptional items not allocated;
  • differences in accounting policies — e.g. CODM reviews segments on a pre-Ind AS 116 basis, at budgeted exchange rates, or before inventory provisions;
  • unallocable assets (cash, investments, tax assets) and liabilities (borrowings, tax liabilities).

If taxes are allocated to segments, the profit reconciliation can be to profit after tax. The profit reconciliation is to profit before tax and discontinued operations.

Analytical uses: the size and trend of reconciling items is itself a quality signal (t6 s03). The reconciliation is also the mechanical check in any segment model: segment data that does not reconcile to the consolidated P&L and balance sheet should not be used to drive forecasts.

Connections

Applies the mechanics of s03 and depends on c05 (CODM basis). Supports s05 (segment ROCE requires reconciled assets and liabilities) and s06 (quarterly reconciliation in the Reg 33 format). Used in Week 13–14 operating models as a hard check between segment drivers and the consolidated statements.

Practice Questions

Q1. Complete the reconciliation [L1]

Segment revenue: X ₹4,500 crore, Y ₹2,100 crore (incl. ₹300 crore inter-segment). Segment results: X ₹520 crore, Y ₹180 crore. Finance cost ₹90 crore; unallocable expenses ₹130 crore; unallocable income ₹60 crore; exceptional loss (unallocated) ₹40 crore. Prepare both reconciliations.

Q2. Policy difference [L2]

The CODM reviews segment results before ESOP cost (₹45 crore) and at budgeted USD/INR of 83 while actual export revenue was translated at 85 (exports USD 400 million). Show the reconciling items needed to arrive at Ind AS PBT and explain how an analyst should treat them.

💡 Hint: Quantify the translation difference on revenue first, then decide whether each item is operating.

Q3. Asset reconciliation check [L2]

Segment assets total ₹18,400 crore; total assets ₹24,100 crore; unallocable assets disclosed as ₹5,200 crore. Identify the ₹500 crore difference's possible causes and the steps to resolve it before computing segment ROCE.
