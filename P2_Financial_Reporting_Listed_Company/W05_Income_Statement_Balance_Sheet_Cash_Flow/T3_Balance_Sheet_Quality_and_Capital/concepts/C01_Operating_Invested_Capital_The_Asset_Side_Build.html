Key concept

Operating Invested Capital: The Asset-Side Build

Concept: Operating invested capital = Operating NWC + Net PPE + ROU assets + Goodwill + Intangibles + other operating non-current assets

Statement

\[ \text{IC}_{\text{op}} = \text{NWC}_{\text{op}} + \text{PPE}_{\text{net}} + \text{ROU} + \text{GW} + \text{INT} + \text{ONCA}_{\text{op}} \]

where \(\text{NWC}_{\text{op}}\) = operating net working capital (operating current assets less operating current liabilities, s02), \(\text{PPE}_{\text{net}}\) = property, plant and equipment net of accumulated depreciation and impairment (plus CWIP per policy), ROU = right-of-use assets (included only if EBITDA/NOPAT is post-Ind AS 116 and lease liabilities are treated as debt), GW = goodwill, INT = other intangible assets (plus intangibles under development per policy), \(\text{ONCA}_{\text{op}}\) = other operating non-current assets (security deposits for leased premises and utilities, capital advances if genuine, long-term prepayments, non-current operating receivables) net of operating non-current liabilities (long-term operating provisions, deferred revenue).

Explanation

Operating invested capital is the cumulative net investment the business needs to generate its operating profit. It is the denominator of ROIC (Week 6) and the base for economic profit and the value driver formula. It is built from the asset side so that each component can be traced to an operating driver (days, capex, acquisitions).

Inclusion rules:

  • Include only what generates EBITDA/NOPAT. Exclude surplus cash, investments, investment property, ICDs, assets held for sale (s03–s04).
  • ROU assets: include if lease cost sits below EBITDA (post-116 basis) and lease liabilities are in the financing side; exclude both if using rent-adjusted NOPAT.
  • Goodwill and acquired intangibles: include for "ROIC including goodwill" (returns on all capital spent); exclude for organic ROIC. Report both.
  • CWIP: policy choice (s07) — disclose it.
  • Tax balances: usually excluded (NOPAT uses operating cash taxes); if included, be consistent with NOPAT tax definition.
  • Average vs closing: ROIC uses average IC (opening + closing ÷ 2) or opening IC; state which.

With NOPAT of ₹1,100 crore: ROIC (incl. goodwill and CWIP) = 14.7%; excl. goodwill = 16.0%; capital in use excl. goodwill = 17.0%. Each answers a different question.

Connections

This concept assembles s02 (operating NWC), s05 (soft assets — goodwill, intangibles, capital advances), s06 (ROU assets matched with lease liabilities) and s07 (CWIP policy). It is the asset-side identity checked by c02 (financing side). It feeds ROIC in Week 6 (topic 1) and the operating balance-sheet drivers in the 3-statement model (Week 13), and its change each year is the net investment term in FCFF (Topic 4).

Practice Questions

Q1. Compute operating IC [L1]

From the following (₹ crore) compute operating invested capital including and excluding goodwill: trade receivables 640; inventories 980; trade payables 720; customer advances 150; other operating current assets 110; other operating current liabilities 190; net PPE 2,450; CWIP 210; ROU 840; goodwill 380; intangibles 95; security deposits 70; liquid funds 1,100; investment property 60.

Q2. Lease-basis consistency [L2]

A retailer has operating IC (including ROU of ₹2,400 crore) of ₹5,600 crore, post-116 NOPAT of ₹780 crore, and annual lease payments of ₹520 crore (ROU depreciation ₹400 crore, lease interest ₹170 crore). Compute ROIC on the post-116 basis and on a rent-adjusted basis (excluding ROU from IC and deducting lease payments after tax at 25.168% from pre-tax operating profit), and explain why the two differ.

💡 Hint: Rebuild NOPAT on the rent-adjusted basis from EBIT by adding back ROU depreciation and deducting lease payments before tax.

Q3. Average vs closing [L2]

A company's operating IC was ₹4,000 crore at 31 March 2024 and ₹5,800 crore at 31 March 2025 after an acquisition on 1 January 2025 that added ₹1,500 crore of IC. FY2025 NOPAT was ₹650 crore, of which ₹40 crore came from the acquiree. Compute ROIC on opening, closing, simple average and time-weighted average IC, and recommend one.
