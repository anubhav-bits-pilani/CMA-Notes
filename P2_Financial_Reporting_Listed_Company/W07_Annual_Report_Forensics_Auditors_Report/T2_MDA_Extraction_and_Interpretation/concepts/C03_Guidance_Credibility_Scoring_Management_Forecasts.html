Key concept

Guidance Credibility: Scoring Management Forecasts Against Outcomes

Concept: Guidance credibility = Actual outcome vs previously guided figures over 4–8 quarters

Statement

Guidance credibility measures how reliably management's past guidance predicted actual outcomes over a rolling window of four to eight quarters. For each guided metric \( m \) in quarter or year \( t \):

\[ e_{m,t} = \frac{A_{m,t} - G_{m,t}}{\left\lvert G_{m,t} \right\rvert} \]

where \( A_{m,t} \) is the actual outcome and \( G_{m,t} \) the guided figure (midpoint of a range, or the point estimate). Summary statistics over \( n \) observations:

\[ \text{Bias} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{t=1}^{n} e_{m,t}, \qquad \text{MAPE} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{t=1}^{n} \left\lvert e_{m,t} \right\rvert, \qquad \text{Hit rate} = \frac{\text{Number of outcomes within the guided range}}{n} \]

A credible management team has bias near zero (or slightly positive, i.e., conservative guidance), low MAPE and a high hit rate.

Explanation

Indian companies rarely give formal numerical guidance in the annual report; it is usually given on earnings calls and in presentations as revenue growth bands, EBITDA margin bands, volume targets, capex amounts and commissioning dates. The MD&A outlook section is typically qualitative. Credibility scoring therefore requires a claims register built from transcripts and presentations (see s06), with each claim dated and sourced.

Why four to eight quarters: fewer observations are dominated by one-off shocks; more than eight quarters may span a change in management or business model. Use a full cycle for cyclical businesses.

Design choices that matter:

  • Which guidance counts: use the latest guidance before the period begins (initial guidance) and track revisions separately. Repeated downward revisions during a year are a credibility signal even if the final revised figure is met.
  • Non-numeric guidance: convert to bands where possible ("double-digit growth" = at least 10%; "margins to remain stable" = ±50 bp).
  • Timeline guidance: for capex commissioning, measure slippage in months against the originally guided date.
  • Attribution: separate misses caused by disclosed external shocks (commodity spikes, regulation) from those within management's control; but do not excuse every miss.

Using the score: in forecasting, adjust management guidance by the historical bias (for example, haircut guided growth by the average overshoot) and widen scenario ranges for teams with high MAPE. In equity research, credibility is part of the management quality assessment; in credit, it informs how much weight to give to deleveraging plans.

Illustration

A listed consumer company's guided versus actual revenue growth (year-on-year, %):

Quarter Guided (mid) Actual Error \( e \)
Q1 FY24 12 10.5 –12.5%
Q2 FY24 12 9.8 –18.3%
Q3 FY24 11 8.2 –25.5%
Q4 FY24 10 9.1 –9.0%
Q1 FY25 10 7.4 –26.0%
Q2 FY25 10 8.0 –20.0%

Bias = –18.5%; MAPE = 18.5%; hit rate (actual within ±2 pp of the guided midpoint) = 3 of 6 (Q1 FY24, Q4 FY24, Q2 FY25). Every miss is on the downside: management is systematically optimistic. A forecaster using a FY 2025-26 guidance of 11% would justify a base case nearer 9% and present the guided figure as an upside case.

Practice Questions

Q1. Compute credibility statistics [L2]

EBITDA margin guidance and actuals for eight quarters (guided mid %, actual %): (16, 15.2), (16, 16.4), (16.5, 15.8), (16.5, 14.9), (17, 16.1), (17, 15.7), (17, 16.6), (17, 15.9). Compute bias, MAPE and the hit rate using a guided range of ±0.5 pp around the midpoint, and interpret the result.

💡 Hint: Compute the relative error first, then summarise.

Q2. Capex timeline credibility [L2]

A company announced three projects with guided commissioning dates of June 2023, March 2024 and December 2024; actual commissioning was December 2023, September 2024 and (still pending at) March 2025. Compute average slippage in months and explain how you would incorporate it into commissioning assumptions for a newly announced project guided for March 2026.

Q3. Design question [L3]

Design an Excel or Power BI guidance tracker for a coverage universe of ten companies: required fields, data validation rules, the calculations for bias, MAPE and hit rate by metric and company, and a visual that shows credibility ranking. Explain how you would handle guidance revisions within a year.
