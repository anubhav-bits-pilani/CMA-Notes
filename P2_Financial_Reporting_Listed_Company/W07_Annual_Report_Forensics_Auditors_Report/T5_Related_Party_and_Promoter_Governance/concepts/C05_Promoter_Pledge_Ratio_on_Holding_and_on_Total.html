Key concept

Promoter Pledge Ratio on Holding and on Total Capital

Concept: Pledge ratio = Encumbered promoter shares ÷ Promoter holding; also ÷ Total share capital

Statement

\[ \text{PR}_{\text{hold}} = \frac{E}{H}, \qquad \text{PR}_{\text{cap}} = \frac{E}{S} = \text{PR}_{\text{hold}} \times \frac{H}{S} \]

where \( E \) is the number of promoter and promoter group shares pledged or otherwise encumbered (pledge, lien, negative lien, NDU or similar arrangements under SAST Reg 28(3)), \( H \) is the number of shares held by the promoter and promoter group, and \( S \) is total shares outstanding. A derived control measure:

\[ \text{Unencumbered promoter stake} = \frac{H - E}{S} \]

Explanation

The two denominators answer different questions:

  • \( \text{PR}_{\text{hold}} \) measures promoter financial stress — how much of the promoter's own wealth in the company is borrowed against. A ratio above 50% means most of the stake is collateral.
  • \( \text{PR}_{\text{cap}} \) measures market overhang — the share of the company's total equity that could be sold by lenders on invocation. A ratio above 20% is significant relative to free float.

SEBI uses both: the SAST Reg 31(1) proviso requires detailed reasons where combined encumbrance equals or exceeds 50% of the promoter's shareholding or 20% of total share capital. Rating agencies and index providers also monitor pledge levels.

Conditions and nuances:

  • Include NDUs and other encumbrances, not just pledges; the shareholding pattern columns and Reg 31 event filings may show them separately.
  • Use the latest Reg 31 event disclosures to update the quarter-end figure; quarter-end shareholding patterns can be stale or window-dressed.
  • Distinguish purpose: encumbrance to secure the listed company's own borrowings carries less conflict than encumbrance for promoter personal or holding-company debt.
  • Holding-company level pledges (pledging the unlisted holdco's shares) escape the ratio.
  • Read the ratio with promoter holding trend: a falling \( H \) with rising \( \text{PR}_{\text{hold}} \) indicates invocations or distress sales.

Illustration

A listed company has 100 crore shares. Promoters hold 46 crore; 25 crore are pledged to NBFCs and 6 crore are under NDU with a bank.

  • Pledges only: \( \text{PR}_{\text{hold}} = 25 \div 46 = 54.3\% \); \( \text{PR}_{\text{cap}} = 25\% \).
  • Including NDUs: \( E = 31 \); \( \text{PR}_{\text{hold}} = 67.4\% \); \( \text{PR}_{\text{cap}} = 31\% \).
  • Unencumbered promoter stake: \( (46 - 31) \div 100 = 15\% \).

Both SAST thresholds are crossed. If lenders invoke all encumbered shares, the promoter's stake falls to 15%, below the 26% special-resolution blocking level and potentially exposing control.

Practice Questions

Q1. Compute both ratios [L1]

Total shares 80 crore; promoter holding 52 crore; pledged 18 crore; NDU 9 crore; lien 1 crore. Compute \( \text{PR}_{\text{hold}} \), \( \text{PR}_{\text{cap}} \) and the unencumbered stake, with and without NDUs and lien, and state which SAST Reg 31 thresholds are crossed.

Q2. Trend interpretation [L2]

Over four quarters, promoter holding moved 58% → 55% → 51% → 47% and \( \text{PR}_{\text{hold}} \) moved 20% → 35% → 55% → 72%. Compute \( \text{PR}_{\text{cap}} \) each quarter and explain the likely sequence of events.

💡 Hint: Consider what happens to holding and encumbrance when a lender invokes and sells.

Q3. Which denominator? [L1]

Explain to a portfolio manager in under 120 words when the pledge ratio on promoter holding is more informative than the ratio on total capital, and vice versa.
