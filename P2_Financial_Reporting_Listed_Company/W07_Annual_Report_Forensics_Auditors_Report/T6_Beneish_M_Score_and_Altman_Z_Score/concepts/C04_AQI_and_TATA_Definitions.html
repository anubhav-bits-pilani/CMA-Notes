Key concept

AQI and TATA Definitions

Concept: AQI = [1 − (CA_t + PPE_t) ÷ TA_t] ÷ [1 − (CA_t−1 + PPE_t−1) ÷ TA_t−1]; TATA = (Income from continuing ops − CFO) ÷ TA_t

Statement

\[ \text{AQI} = \frac{1 - (\text{CA}_t + \text{PPE}_t) / \text{TA}_t}{1 - (\text{CA}_{t-1} + \text{PPE}_{t-1}) / \text{TA}_{t-1}} \]

\[ \text{TATA} = \frac{\text{ICO}_t - \text{CFO}_t}{\text{TA}_t} \]

where \( \text{CA} \) is total current assets, \( \text{PPE} \) is net property, plant and equipment (scope defined consistently — with or without ROU assets and CWIP), \( \text{TA} \) is total assets, \( \text{ICO}_t \) is income from continuing operations (Indian proxy: profit for the period from continuing operations), and \( \text{CFO}_t \) is net cash from operating activities for year \( t \).

Explanation

AQI measures the share of total assets that are neither current assets nor PPE — the "soft" assets: intangible assets, goodwill, intangibles under development, capital advances, long-term deposits, deferred tax assets, non-current investments and other non-current assets. An AQI above 1 means soft assets have grown as a share of the balance sheet, which can indicate capitalisation of costs that should have been expensed (development costs, borrowing costs, pre-operative expenses) — the Week 7 topic 3 s03 and s04 red flags. Coefficient 0.404.

Indian specifics: if CWIP is excluded from PPE, it becomes a soft asset and AQI rises during capex cycles; if non-current investments (e.g. in subsidiaries for standalone statements) are large, AQI reflects investment activity rather than capitalisation. State the choice and test it (s06).

TATA is the accrual component of earnings scaled by assets. Positive and high TATA means profit is running ahead of cash; Beneish found it the most powerful single predictor, with coefficient 4.679. Note Beneish's original definition of total accruals used balance-sheet changes (change in working capital excluding cash and current debt, less depreciation); the cash-flow version shown here is the common modern implementation and is less affected by acquisitions and reclassifications.

Conditions:

  • Use CFO under Ind AS 7 as reported, but check classification of interest and dividends (Ind AS 7.31–34) for both years.
  • Exceptional items in PAT affect TATA — e.g. a large non-cash impairment lowers PAT and TATA.
  • Factoring, supplier finance and securitisation can boost CFO (Week 7 topic 4 s06), lowering TATA artificially.
  • Use \( \text{TA}_t \) (end of year), not average, consistent with the formula.

Illustration

Listed consumer durables company (₹ crore):

Item FY24 FY25
Total current assets 2,100 2,300
PPE (net, incl. ROU) 1,500 1,550
Intangibles + intangibles under development 150 420
Capital advances and other non-current assets 250 430
Total assets 4,000 4,700
PAT from continuing operations 320 360
CFO 300 120
  • Soft-asset share: FY24 = 1 − 3,600/4,000 = 0.100; FY25 = 1 − 3,850/4,700 = 0.181. AQI = 1.809.
  • TATA = (360 − 120) ÷ 4,700 = 0.051.
  • Contributions vs neutral: AQI \( 0.404 \times 0.809 = 0.327 \); TATA \( 4.679 \times 0.051 = 0.239 \).

Intangibles under development nearly tripled while CFO collapsed — consistent with capitalised development cost and weak cash conversion. Investigate the capitalisation policy (Ind AS 38 criteria) and the capital advances' counterparties.

Practice Questions

Q1. Compute AQI and TATA [L1]

FY24/FY25 (₹ crore): current assets 1,200/1,350; PPE 900/950; total assets 2,400/2,900; PAT from continuing operations 180/210; CFO FY25 150. Compute AQI and TATA and their contributions to M.

Q2. CWIP scope choice [L2]

A cement company's CWIP rose from ₹200 crore to ₹1,100 crore during a capacity expansion; PPE (excluding CWIP) was ₹3,000 crore and ₹3,050 crore; current assets ₹1,500 crore and ₹1,600 crore; total assets ₹5,200 crore and ₹6,300 crore. Compute AQI with CWIP inside and outside PPE, and explain which you would present and why.

💡 Hint: Ask whether CWIP represents real capacity build-out (check capital commitments and ageing) or capitalised expenses.

Q3. CFO manipulation and TATA [L2]

A company factored ₹300 crore of receivables without recourse on 30 March 2025, raising CFO from ₹80 crore to ₹380 crore. PAT is ₹350 crore and total assets ₹5,000 crore. Compute TATA with and without the factoring and discuss how it affects the M-score interpretation.
