Subtopic note

Beneish 8-Variable Model: Mapping Schedule III Data for t and t−1

Scope: Beneish 8-variable model: data mapping from Schedule III statements for years t and t−1

Why it matters

The Beneish M-score is the most widely used quantitative screen for earnings manipulation. It was estimated on US companies subject to SEC enforcement for earnings overstatement, and it converts eight year-on-year ratios — each mirroring a qualitative red flag from Week 7 topic 3 — into one probit score. In Indian practice it appears in forensic screens by research desks, in credit "early warning" frameworks, in audit analytics, and in interview case studies. The model is only as good as the data mapping: Schedule III statements present items differently from US 10-K formats (no "SG&A" line, no "cost of goods sold" line for many companies, lease liabilities separate from borrowings), so you must map deliberately and consistently for both years.

Core notes

The eight variables and what each captures

Variable Formula (t vs t−1) Red flag captured Beneish (1999) mean: manipulators vs non-manipulators
DSRI — Days' Sales in Receivables Index \( (\text{Rec}_t / \text{Sales}_t) \div (\text{Rec}_{t-1} / \text{Sales}_{t-1}) \) Receivables growing faster than revenue; aggressive recognition 1.465 vs 1.031
GMI — Gross Margin Index \( \text{GM}_{t-1} \div \text{GM}_t \) Deteriorating margins create pressure to manipulate 1.193 vs 1.014
AQI — Asset Quality Index \( [1 - (\text{CA}_t + \text{PPE}_t)/\text{TA}_t] \div [1 - (\text{CA}_{t-1} + \text{PPE}_{t-1})/\text{TA}_{t-1}] \) Growth in "soft" assets — capitalised costs, intangibles, deferred charges 1.254 vs 1.039
SGI — Sales Growth Index \( \text{Sales}_t \div \text{Sales}_{t-1} \) Growth firms face pressure to sustain growth 1.607 vs 1.134
DEPI — Depreciation Index \( \text{DR}_{t-1} \div \text{DR}_t \), with \( \text{DR} = \text{Dep} / (\text{Dep} + \text{PPE}) \) Slowing depreciation — useful-life extension 1.077 vs 1.001
SGAI — SG&A Index \( (\text{SGA}_t / \text{Sales}_t) \div (\text{SGA}_{t-1} / \text{Sales}_{t-1}) \) Falling administrative efficiency 1.041 vs 1.054
LVGI — Leverage Index \( [(\text{CL}_t + \text{LTD}_t)/\text{TA}_t] \div [(\text{CL}_{t-1} + \text{LTD}_{t-1})/\text{TA}_{t-1}] \) Rising leverage; covenant pressure 1.111 vs 1.037
TATA — Total Accruals to Total Assets \( (\text{Income from continuing ops}_t - \text{CFO}_t) \div \text{TA}_t \) Earnings not backed by cash 0.031 vs 0.018

All indices equal 1 when nothing changes; TATA equals 0 when profit equals operating cash flow. The score (c01) is:

\[ M = -4.84 + 0.920\,\text{DSRI} + 0.528\,\text{GMI} + 0.404\,\text{AQI} + 0.892\,\text{SGI} + 0.115\,\text{DEPI} - 0.172\,\text{SGAI} + 4.679\,\text{TATA} - 0.327\,\text{LVGI} \]

Mapping from Schedule III (Division II)

Model input Schedule III source Mapping notes
Sales Revenue from operations (Statement of P&L) Exclude other income. Use net of GST (Schedule III shows revenue net of GST for Ind AS companies). Include other operating revenue (scrap sales, export incentives) consistently
Receivables (Rec) Trade receivables — current + non-current (Balance Sheet; ageing note) Gross or net of ECL consistently; consider adding unbilled revenue/contract assets for IT and EPC (Week 7 topic 3 s01); keep same choice for t and t−1
COGS (for GM) Cost of materials consumed + Purchases of stock-in-trade + Changes in inventories of FG, WIP and stock-in-trade Optionally add manufacturing overheads from "other expenses" (power and fuel, stores and spares, job work) if separately disclosed — but then remove them from SGA (s03)
Current assets (CA) Total current assets Includes current investments and cash; Beneish used total current assets
PPE Property, plant and equipment (net) Decide whether to include ROU assets, CWIP and investment property; apply the same choice in AQI and DEPI (s03)
Total assets (TA) Total assets Consolidated or standalone, consistently
Depreciation (Dep) Depreciation and amortisation expense Split PPE depreciation from intangible amortisation and ROU depreciation using the PPE note if DEPI uses PPE only
SGA Employee benefits expense + Other expenses (adjusted) Indian proxy (s03)
Current liabilities (CL) Total current liabilities Includes current maturities of long-term debt and current lease liabilities
Long-term debt (LTD) Non-current borrowings + non-current lease liabilities Include lease liabilities consistently (s03)
Income from continuing operations Profit for the period from continuing operations (before OCI) Use PAT from continuing operations; exceptional items are included as reported (or adjusted consistently)
CFO Net cash from operating activities (Ind AS 7 statement) Check classification choices for interest paid/received (Ind AS 7.31–33) and consistency across years

Two years of data

Every variable except TATA needs year \( t \) and year \( t-1 \) values; to compute M for two consecutive years you need three balance sheets and three income statements (\( t-2 \), \( t-1 \), \( t \)). Ind AS annual reports present two years, so you need two annual reports (or the restated comparative columns) to compute two years of scores. Use the restated comparatives in the latest report where a restatement (Ind AS 8 error, common-control merger, discontinued operation under Ind AS 105) has changed prior-year figures — otherwise the indices pick up the restatement rather than the trend.

Worked illustration

Mid-cap manufacturer (consolidated, ₹ crore):

Item FY24 (t−1) FY25 (t)
Revenue from operations 4,000 5,000
Trade receivables 600 1,050
COGS (materials + purchases + inventory change) 2,600 3,400
Employee benefits + other expenses (adjusted SGA) 700 850
Total current assets 1,800 2,500
PPE (net, incl. ROU) 1,600 1,700
Total assets 4,200 5,300
Depreciation (PPE + ROU) 160 150
Total current liabilities 1,100 1,500
Non-current borrowings + lease liabilities 900 1,200
PAT from continuing operations 300 420
CFO 280 90

Computed indices: DSRI = (1,050/5,000) ÷ (600/4,000) = 0.21 ÷ 0.15 = 1.400; GMI = 0.350 ÷ 0.320 = 1.094; AQI = (1 − 4,200/5,300) ÷ (1 − 3,400/4,200) = 0.2075 ÷ 0.1905 = 1.090; SGI = 1.250; DEPI = [160/1,760] ÷ [150/1,850] = 0.0909 ÷ 0.0811 = 1.121; SGAI = 0.170 ÷ 0.175 = 0.971; LVGI = (2,700/5,300) ÷ (2,000/4,200) = 0.5094 ÷ 0.4762 = 1.070; TATA = (420 − 90) ÷ 5,300 = 0.0623.

\[ M \approx -4.84 + 1.288 + 0.578 + 0.440 + 1.115 + 0.129 - 0.167 + 0.291 - 0.350 = -1.52 \]

M is above −1.78, so the company is flagged. The drivers are DSRI (receivables up 75% on sales up 25%), SGI and TATA (CFO collapsed while PAT rose) — exactly the qualitative flags of Week 7 topic 3.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Mergers and demergers in the year distort SGI, DSRI and AQI; use pro forma or restated comparatives.
  • Negative or near-zero gross margin makes GMI explode or flip sign; cap or exclude and explain.
  • Zero receivables in t−1 (new business) makes DSRI undefined; set to 1 with a note.
  • Ind AS transition years (FY 2016-17 to FY 2018-19 for most) mix Indian GAAP and Ind AS; avoid scoring across the transition.
  • Exceptional items (impairment, gains on sale) affect TATA via PAT; decide whether to use reported or pre-exceptional PAT and apply consistently.
  • Discontinued operations: use continuing operations for both P&L and, if available, CFO.

Key takeaways

  • Eight variables, each a year-on-year index (or TATA level) mirroring a qualitative red flag.
  • Map Schedule III lines deliberately: Sales = revenue from operations; COGS = materials + purchases + inventory change; SGA is a proxy; LTD includes lease liabilities consistently.
  • Two years of scores need three years of data; use restated comparatives.
  • A "no change" company scores about −2.48; indices above 1 and positive TATA push the score up.
  • Document every mapping choice with the note/page reference.

Practice Questions

Q1. Map the inputs [L1]

From a Schedule III statement of profit and loss containing: Revenue from operations, Other income, Cost of materials consumed, Purchases of stock-in-trade, Changes in inventories, Employee benefits expense, Finance costs, Depreciation and amortisation expense, Other expenses (with a note splitting power and fuel, freight, advertisement, rent, repairs, CSR, miscellaneous), Exceptional items and Tax expense — identify the lines you would use for Sales, COGS, SGA, Depreciation and Income from continuing operations, with reasons.

Q2. Compute all eight variables [L2]

Using (₹ crore) FY24/FY25: revenue 2,000/2,300; trade receivables 300/420; COGS 1,300/1,540; SGA 380/420; total current assets 900/1,150; PPE 1,000/1,050; total assets 2,300/2,800; depreciation 90/85; current liabilities 600/750; long-term debt incl. leases 500/700; PAT from continuing operations 150/190; CFO FY25 60 — compute DSRI, GMI, AQI, SGI, DEPI, SGAI, LVGI, TATA and the M-score, and identify the top two drivers.

💡 Hint: Compute each contribution as coefficient × variable and compare it with the coefficient × 1 baseline.

Q3. Restatement effect [L2]

A company merged a sister concern under common control on 1 April 2024 and restated FY24 comparatives. FY24 revenue was ₹3,000 crore as originally reported and ₹3,600 crore restated; FY25 revenue is ₹4,200 crore. Compute SGI both ways and explain which you would use and why.

Q4. Interview: why these eight? [L1]

Explain in under 150 words, for an interviewer, the economic intuition behind DSRI, AQI and TATA, and why SGI is included even though growth itself is not manipulation.
