Sensitivity Analysis: Score Drivers and Alternative Proxies

Scope: Sensitivity: which variables drive the score; testing alternative proxies

Why it matters

A score near a cut-off can flip with one mapping choice — receivables with or without contract assets, SGA with or without freight, PPE with or without ROU assets. Before an M-score or Z-score goes into a memo, the analyst must show which variables drive the result and whether the conclusion survives reasonable alternative proxies. Sensitivity analysis converts a fragile number into defensible evidence, and it tells you which qualitative red flags (Week 7 topic 3) to investigate first.

Core notes

Driver decomposition

For any linear score \( S = a + \sum_k b_k V_k \), the contribution of variable \( k \) relative to a neutral baseline \( V_k^{0} \) is:

\[ \Delta_k = b_k \left( V_k - V_k^{0} \right) \]

For Beneish, neutral values are 1 for each index and 0 for TATA, giving a baseline of about −2.48; the score is then \( M = -2.48 + \sum_k \Delta_k \). Ranking \( \Delta_k \) shows the drivers. Because coefficients differ, the marginal impact per unit change varies widely:

Variable Coefficient Change that moves M by +0.10 Typical range in practice
TATA 4.679 +0.021 (2.1% of total assets) −0.15 to +0.15
DSRI 0.920 +0.109 0.7 to 2.0
SGI 0.892 +0.112 0.8 to 2.0
GMI 0.528 +0.189 0.8 to 1.5
AQI 0.404 +0.248 0.7 to 2.5
LVGI −0.327 −0.306 (fall in leverage index) 0.8 to 1.3
SGAI −0.172 −0.581 0.8 to 1.3
DEPI 0.115 +0.870 0.8 to 1.3

TATA, DSRI and SGI dominate in practice; DEPI and SGAI rarely move the conclusion. That also tells you where proxy choices matter: receivables definition (DSRI), CFO classification and PAT definition (TATA), and revenue definition (SGI, DSRI).

For Altman, the partial derivative of each score with respect to each ratio is the coefficient; but ratios have different scales. In Z, X4 (market equity/TL) typically ranges 0.5–10 and with a 0.6 coefficient can dominate; in Z'', X1 and X3 (coefficients 6.56 and 6.72) dominate.

Testing alternative proxies

Proxy choice Alternatives Variables affected
Receivables Trade receivables only; + contract assets/unbilled; gross vs net of ECL DSRI
Sales Revenue from operations; excluding other operating revenue; excluding export incentives SGI, DSRI, GMI, SGAI
COGS / gross margin Materials-only; + power and freight; + manufacturing employee cost GMI, SGAI
PPE scope Net PPE; + ROU; + CWIP; + investment property AQI, DEPI
Leases Include/exclude lease liabilities in LTD (with ROU consistency) LVGI, AQI, DEPI
Income PAT from continuing operations; pre-exceptional PAT TATA
CFO Reported; adjusted for supplier finance or factoring reclassification (Week 7 topic 4 s06) TATA
Basis Standalone vs consolidated All
Altman X2 Retained earnings only; + general reserve; total other equity (not recommended) X2
Altman X3 EBIT incl. or excl. other income X3
Altman X4 Year-end market cap; 3-month average; book equity X4

Run each alternative as a scenario and report the range of scores, not just the base case.

Excel implementation

  1. Put each proxy switch in the Map sheet as a named cell (Sw_ContractAssets, Sw_InclLeases, Sw_InclCWIP, Sw_PreExceptional).
  2. Use Scenario Manager or a data table to recalculate M and Z'' for combinations of switches.
  3. For one-at-a-time sensitivity, build a tornado table: for each variable, recompute M with that variable at its neutral value (or at the peer median) and record the change.
Contribution_k  =Coef_k*(Var_k-Neutral_k)
M_without_k     =M_base-Contribution_k
Rank            =RANK.EQ(ABS(Contribution_k),ABS(Contribution_range),0)

A two-way data table (e.g. DSRI from 1.0 to 1.6 by 0.1 across, TATA from −0.04 to 0.10 by 0.02 down) shows the combinations that cross −1.78:

=TABLE(DSRI_input_cell, TATA_input_cell)

(entered via Data → What-If Analysis → Data Table, with the M-score formula in the top-left cell of the grid).

  1. Chart the tornado (bar chart sorted by absolute contribution) and include it in the Output sheet and the Week 12 dashboard.

Illustration

Base case for an IT services company (FY25): M = −1.84 (just below −1.78). Alternatives:

Scenario DSRI TATA M Flag
Base: trade receivables only, reported CFO 1.12 0.030 −1.84 Watch
+ unbilled revenue in receivables 1.38 0.030 −1.60 Likely manipulator
Base + CFO adjusted for ₹150 crore receivables factored at year-end (TA ₹6,000 crore) 1.12 0.055 −1.72 Likely manipulator
Both 1.38 0.055 −1.48 Likely manipulator

DSRI moves by 0.26 (contribution +0.24) once unbilled revenue is included, because unbilled grew 60% while billed receivables were flat — exactly the Week 7 topic 3 s01 red flag. The conclusion: the base proxy understated risk; the memo should report the range −1.84 to −1.48 and lead with unbilled revenue and factoring as findings.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Proxy shopping: choosing the proxy that produces the desired answer. Fix a base case ex ante (documented in Map) and report alternatives transparently.
  • Interaction effects: switches are not additive when a proxy affects several variables (leases affect LVGI, AQI and DEPI together); always recompute the whole score.
  • Neutral vs peer baseline: neutral (1, 0) shows absolute drivers; peer median shows company-specific drivers — present both for cyclical sectors.
  • Coefficient uncertainty: coefficients are estimates with standard errors; small differences around the cut-off (±0.1) are not meaningful.
  • Multi-year consistency: a proxy change must be applied to every year in the trend.

Key takeaways

  • Decompose the score into contributions \( b_k (V_k - V_k^{0}) \) and rank them.
  • TATA (4.679), DSRI (0.920) and SGI (0.892) drive most M-scores; DEPI and SGAI rarely matter.
  • Test receivables, CFO, revenue, PPE and lease proxies as scenarios; report a range.
  • Use switches, data tables and tornado charts in Excel; fix the base case before testing.
  • Scores within about ±0.1 of a cut-off are inconclusive; let the drivers direct the diligence.

Practice Questions

Q1. Driver ranking [L1]

For a company with DSRI 1.25, GMI 1.05, AQI 1.30, SGI 1.20, DEPI 1.10, SGAI 0.95, TATA 0.045, LVGI 1.08, compute each variable's contribution relative to the neutral baseline, rank them, and compute M.

Q2. Two-way sensitivity [L2]

Holding other variables at the values in Q1, find the TATA level at which M crosses −1.78 for DSRI values of 1.0, 1.2 and 1.4. Describe how you would build this as an Excel data table.

💡 Hint: Rearrange the score equation to solve for TATA given each DSRI.

Q3. Proxy alternatives for a cement company [L2]

For a cement company, list four proxy alternatives that could plausibly change the M-score (with the variables each affects), and design a scenario table that reports the base case and each alternative.

Q4. Defending the score [L3]

In an investment committee, a portfolio manager says your M-score of −1.70 is "just a proxy artefact". Prepare a structured response using sensitivity results, peer comparison and qualitative evidence, and state what result would make you withdraw the flag.
