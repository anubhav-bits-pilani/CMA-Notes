Key concept

Constant-Currency Growth

Concept: Constant-currency growth = Current revenue at prior-period exchange rates ÷ Prior revenue − 1

Statement

\[ g_{\text{CC}} = \frac{\sum_{c} R_{c,t} \times X_{c,t-1}}{\sum_{c} R_{c,t-1} \times X_{c,t-1}} - 1 \]

where \(R_{c,t}\) = revenue in currency \(c\) (USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, etc.) earned in period \(t\), \(X_{c,t-1}\) = the average exchange rate of currency \(c\) into the reporting currency (USD for dollar-reported metrics, INR for rupee metrics) in the prior period, and the denominator is simply prior-period reported revenue. The difference between reported growth and CC growth is the currency (translation) effect.

Explanation

Indian IT services, pharma exporters and other multinationals earn revenue in many currencies. Reported growth mixes business performance with exchange-rate movements; CC growth removes the latter by re-translating current revenue at prior-period rates. It is the basis on which Indian IT companies issue revenue guidance (e.g., "CC revenue growth of 3–5% for the year").

Conditions and nuances:

  • Base currency: Indian IT firms compute CC growth on USD-reported revenue (removing cross-currency effects of EUR, GBP, AUD vs USD). INR reported growth adds the USD/INR movement on top.
  • Period: quarter-on-quarter CC uses the prior quarter's rates; year-on-year uses the prior-year quarter's rates.
  • Hedging: CC growth excludes hedge gains/losses; companies that include hedge results in revenue (Ind AS 109 cash flow hedges reclassified to revenue) show reported growth that differs further.
  • Organic CC: excludes acquisitions and divestitures — the cleanest measure of underlying growth.
  • Pricing in local currency: CC growth does not adjust for pricing; a contract priced in EUR that renews at a higher EUR price counts as CC growth.
  • CC is a non-GAAP measure; it should be clearly defined and reconciled to reported growth.

Illustration

IT company, FY2024-25 Q2 vs Q1 (US$ million):

Currency Q1 revenue (local) Q1 rate (USD per unit) Q1 USD Q2 revenue (local) Q2 rate Q2 USD Q2 at Q1 rate
USD 1,800 1.00 1,800 1,840 1.00 1,840 1,840
EUR 500 1.08 540 515 1.10 566.5 556.2
GBP 250 1.26 315 252 1.30 327.6 317.5
Total 2,655 2,734.1 2,713.7
  • Reported USD growth = 2,734.1 ÷ 2,655 − 1 = 3.0%
  • CC growth = 2,713.7 ÷ 2,655 − 1 = 2.2%
  • Cross-currency tailwind ≈ 0.8 percentage points (EUR and GBP strengthened against USD).

If the rupee also depreciated 0.6% against the USD, INR reported growth ≈ 1.030 × 1.006 − 1 = 3.6%.

Practice Questions

Q1. Compute CC growth [L1]

Prior-year revenue was US$9,000 million. Current-year revenue by currency: USD 6,300 million; EUR 2,100 million at an average rate of 1.09 (prior year 1.06); GBP 800 million at 1.28 (prior year 1.24). Compute reported USD revenue, CC revenue, reported and CC growth.

Q2. Organic CC growth [L2]

Reported USD growth is 7.5%, cross-currency effect +1.0 percentage point, and an acquisition completed at the start of the year contributed US$180 million on a prior-year base of US$10,000 million. Compute organic CC growth.

💡 Hint: Remove currency effect and acquired revenue from the growth figure step by step.

Q3. Interview — why guide in CC? [L1]

Explain to a non-finance board member why Indian IT companies guide revenue growth in constant currency, and one limitation of CC as a performance measure.
