Subtopic note

Schedule III Liquidity and Solvency Ratios with Variance Explanations

Scope: Schedule III ratios: current ratio, debt-equity, DSCR with 25% variance explanations

Why it matters

The 2021 amendment to Schedule III (effective from FY2021-22) added "Additional Regulatory Information" to Division I, II and III, including mandatory disclosure of eleven ratios with numerator, denominator, current and previous-year values, percentage variance and an explanation for any change of more than 25%. Three of the eleven are liquidity and solvency ratios: the current ratio, the debt-equity ratio and the debt service coverage ratio. For an auditor the disclosure is an audit area (computations must tie to the financial statements and explanations must be factual); for an analyst it is the one place every Indian company's own definitions are published side by side, which exposes inconsistencies; for an FP&A team preparing the annual report it is a year-end deliverable that should be prepared from the same ratio dictionary used in the MIS.

Core notes

Scope of the requirement

  • Applies to the standalone financial statements of companies following Schedule III; the general instructions for consolidated financial statements do not carry over the additional regulatory information (ICAI's Guidance Note on Schedule III clarifies the position), though many listed groups disclose voluntarily.
  • The eleven ratios: current ratio, debt-equity ratio, DSCR, ROE, inventory turnover, trade receivables turnover, trade payables turnover, net capital turnover, net profit ratio, ROCE and return on investment. t1 covers the profitability ones and t5 the turnover ones; this note covers the three liquidity and solvency ratios.
  • Disclosure: numerator, denominator, ratio for current and previous period, % variance, and reasons for variance above 25%. Companies disclose definitions they use; the ICAI Guidance Note on Division II offers suggested definitions.

The three ratios and typical definitions

Ratio Numerator Denominator Points of variation in practice
Current ratio Current assets Current liabilities Few — taken straight from the balance sheet
Debt-equity Total debt (non-current + current borrowings, often incl. lease liabilities) Shareholders' equity (total equity) Lease liabilities in/out; net of cash or gross
DSCR Earnings available for debt service = PAT + non-cash operating expenses (D&A) + interest + other adjustments (e.g., loss on sale of PPE) Debt service = interest and lease payments + principal repayments Whether lease payments, short-term loan repayments, and prepayments are included

The Guidance Note-based DSCR deliberately includes lease payments in debt service, which aligns with the post-Ind AS 116 fixed-charge thinking in s03. Short-term borrowings that revolve (cash credit) are generally excluded from principal repayments; many companies explicitly exclude prepayments refinanced from new debt.

Computing the variance

\[ \text{Variance \%} = \frac{\text{Ratio}_{t} - \text{Ratio}_{t-1}}{\text{Ratio}_{t-1}} \times 100 \]

The variance is the relative change in the ratio, not the percentage-point difference. A current ratio moving from 1.20x to 1.50x is a 25% change (exactly at the threshold, no explanation strictly needed, though prudent practice explains it); a DSCR moving from 2.0x to 1.4x is −30% and requires an explanation.

Worked illustration

Mid-cap pharma company (₹ crore):

Item FY2023-24 FY2024-25
Current assets 1,800 2,050
Current liabilities 1,000 1,450
Total debt (incl. leases) 600 950
Total equity 2,000 2,150
PAT + D&A + interest + loss on sale 520 560
Interest + lease payments + principal 210 400
Ratio FY2023-24 FY2024-25 Variance Explanation required?
Current ratio 1.80x 1.41x −21.5% No
Debt-equity 0.30x 0.44x +47.3% Yes
DSCR 2.48x 1.40x −43.5% Yes

Writing a good variance explanation

Weak explanation (common in annual reports): "Due to increase in borrowings." It restates the arithmetic.

Good explanation: "Debt-equity increased from 0.30x to 0.44x due to ₹530 crore of term loans drawn in Q2 FY2024-25 to fund the Vizag API plant (CWIP ₹370 crore at year-end) and refinance the ECB, partly offset by the ₹180 crore ECB bullet repaid; equity growth limited to retained profit of ₹150 crore after a ₹120 crore dividend." It names the cause, the amount, the timing and whether it is expected to recur. For DSCR: "DSCR declined to 1.40x because FY2024-25 included a ₹180 crore bullet repayment of the 2020 ECB, which was refinanced through the new term loan; excluding the refinanced bullet, DSCR was 2.55x."

An auditor reviewing the disclosure checks that numerators and denominators agree with the financial statements, definitions are consistent with the prior year (or the change is disclosed), and explanations are consistent with the board's report and MD&A.

Related LODR disclosures

Entities with listed non-convertible securities disclose a parallel set under SEBI LODR Reg 52(4) with their half-yearly/annual results — including debt-equity, DSCR, interest service coverage, net worth, current ratio, long-term debt to working capital, current liability ratio, total debts to total assets and bad debts to receivables. Definitions should be reconciled with the Schedule III note; inconsistent definitions across the two disclosures are a frequent review finding.

Building it in Excel or Power BI

Maintain a ratio dictionary table (Ratio, Numerator definition, Denominator definition, Source lines, Threshold = 25%). Compute variance and a flag:

Variance_Pct = (Ratio_CY - Ratio_PY) / ABS(Ratio_PY)
Expl_Needed  = IF(ABS(Variance_Pct) > 0.25, "Explain", "")

In Power BI, a DAX measure using DIVIDE([Ratio CY] - [Ratio PY], ABS([Ratio PY])) with conditional formatting lets the reporting team see which ratios need narrative before the annual report draft goes to audit.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Negative or near-zero prior-year ratio (e.g., negative equity): the percentage variance is mathematically meaningless; disclose the ratios and explain qualitatively.
  • Change in definition (e.g., including lease liabilities for the first time): restate the prior year on the new definition or disclose the change; otherwise the variance mixes definition and performance.
  • Zero debt in one year: debt-equity variance is undefined; disclose "Not applicable".
  • DSCR distorted by refinancing: explain and optionally provide an underlying figure.
  • Mid-year mergers under a scheme of arrangement with retrospective appointed dates restate comparatives — the variance should be computed on restated figures.
  • Consistency with covenant ratios: the Schedule III DSCR is not the bank's covenant DSCR; do not present it as covenant compliance.

Key takeaways

  • Schedule III (2021 amendment) requires current ratio, debt-equity and DSCR with numerator, denominator and variance, in standalone financial statements.
  • Variance is the relative change in the ratio; explanations are needed when it exceeds 25%.
  • The Guidance Note DSCR includes lease payments in debt service; disclose definitions and keep them consistent.
  • Good explanations name cause, amount, timing and recurrence — not just the arithmetic.
  • Reconcile Schedule III, Reg 52(4) and covenant definitions; they are frequently different.

Practice Questions

Q1. Compute and flag [L1]

From the following (₹ crore), compute the three Schedule III liquidity/solvency ratios for both years, the variance and whether an explanation is required. FY2023-24: CA 960, CL 640, debt 300, equity 1,200, earnings for debt service 330, debt service 140. FY2024-25: CA 1,040, CL 820, debt 280, equity 1,330, earnings for debt service 360, debt service 210.

Q2. Draft the explanation [L2]

For Q1, the debt service increase arose because a ₹70 crore term-loan instalment was prepaid from surplus cash in March 2025 and lease payments rose ₹15 crore on new warehouses. Draft a two-sentence variance explanation suitable for the annual report.

Q3. Definition change mid-series [L2]

A company excluded lease liabilities from debt in FY2023-24 (debt ₹250 crore, equity ₹1,000 crore, lease liabilities ₹180 crore) and includes them in FY2024-25 (borrowings ₹240 crore, lease liabilities ₹210 crore, equity ₹1,100 crore). Compute the variance as reported and on a consistent basis, and recommend a disclosure.

💡 Hint: Restate the comparative on the new definition before computing the variance.

Q4. Auditor review of ratio note [L3]

As the statutory auditor, design a review procedure for the Schedule III ratio note covering data tie-out, definition consistency with the prior year and with Reg 52(4) disclosures, and the quality of variance explanations. List the evidence you would obtain for a DSCR explanation that cites refinancing.
