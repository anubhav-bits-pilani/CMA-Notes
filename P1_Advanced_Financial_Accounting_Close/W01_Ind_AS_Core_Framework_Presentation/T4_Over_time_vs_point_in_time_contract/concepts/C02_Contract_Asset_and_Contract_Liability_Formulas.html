Key concept

Contract Asset and Contract Liability Formulas

Concept: Contract asset = Revenue recognised − Billed (where right is conditional); Contract liability = Billings/advances received − Revenue recognised

Statement

For each contract \(j\) (after applying the combination rules of Ind AS 115.17), at reporting date \(t\):

\[ N_{j,t} = \text{CumRev}_{j,t} - \text{CumBill}_{j,t} \]

\[ CA_{j,t} = \max(N_{j,t}, 0) \qquad CL_{j,t} = \max(-N_{j,t}, 0) \]

where \(\text{CumRev}_{j,t}\) is cumulative revenue recognised on contract \(j\); \(\text{CumBill}_{j,t}\) is cumulative consideration that has become unconditional (invoiced or certified, i.e., transferred to receivables) plus cash received in advance of any invoice; \(CA_{j,t}\) is the contract asset (conditional right to consideration); \(CL_{j,t}\) is the contract liability. Balances presented:

\[ CA_t = \sum_j CA_{j,t} \qquad CL_t = \sum_j CL_{j,t} \]

Receivables are measured separately as \(\text{CumBill} - \text{CumCash}\) (for billed amounts).

Explanation

The contract asset captures performance that has run ahead of an unconditional right to bill — the entity still has to do something (reach a milestone, obtain a certificate) before the right becomes a receivable (Ind AS 115.107). The contract liability captures consideration received or unconditionally due before performance (115.106). Because each contract produces only one net position (115.105), a contract cannot show both an asset and a liability at the same time; but different contracts are summed separately, never netted — Ind AS 1.32 prohibits offsetting unrelated assets and liabilities.

Key conditions:

  • "Billed" must mean an unconditional right. A milestone invoice raised in advance on a cancellable contract is not a receivable and does not reduce the contract asset.
  • Where an invoice is raised before performance on a non-cancellable contract, the entity recognises a receivable and a contract liability (both gross).
  • Retention whose release is conditional on performance remains part of the contract asset; an advance recoverable from future bills is part of the contract liability. Many Indian EPC companies disclose retention and mobilisation advances separately; consistency and disclosure of the policy matter.
  • Both balances are classified current/non-current using the operating cycle (Ind AS 1.66, 1.69).
  • Contract assets are subject to Ind AS 109 ECL.

Illustration

Three contracts at 31 March 2025 (₹ lakh):

Contract CumRev CumBill N CA CL
P 640 500 140 140 —
Q 220 300 −80 — 80
R 0 50 (advance) −50 — 50
Total 10 140 130

The balance sheet shows contract assets ₹140 lakh and contract liabilities ₹130 lakh — not a net ₹10 lakh. In Excel, compute per row and sum the columns:

=MAX(CumRev - CumBill, 0)
=MAX(CumBill - CumRev, 0)

Connections

Applies the definitions in s04 and the EPC features in s05 (retention, mobilisation advance). The journal entries are in c03. The movement in these balances is a working-capital line in the Ind AS 7 cash flow (Topic 6) and the 115.116–118 roll-forward (s08). ECL on contract assets links to Week 3 (w3_t2). The SaaS and EPC exercise in Week 1 requires these two columns per contract per month with a check that CA − CL + receivables − cash ties to cumulative revenue less cumulative cash.

Practice Questions

Q1. Compute presented balances [L1]

Four contracts (₹ crore): A CumRev 12, CumBill 9; B CumRev 4, CumBill 7; C CumRev 15, CumBill 15; D CumRev 0, advance 3. Compute contract assets and contract liabilities to present, and explain why the net figure is not presented.

Q2. Conditional invoice [L2]

On 28 March 2025 a contractor raises a milestone invoice of ₹5 crore for "design approval" which the customer approves on 10 April 2025; contract terms say payment becomes due only after approval. Cumulative revenue on the contract is ₹18 crore and previously unconditional billings total ₹14 crore. Compute the contract asset and receivable at 31 March 2025 and explain the treatment of the invoice.

💡 Hint: Decide whether the right to the ₹5 crore was unconditional at the reporting date.

Q3. Monthly model check row [L2]

Design a check row for a monthly waterfall that confirms, for every contract and month, that contract asset − contract liability + receivable = cumulative revenue − cumulative cash. State the Excel formula and explain which errors it would catch.
