Key concept

Schedule III as a Minimum: Ind AS Prevails on Conflict

Concept: Schedule III is a minimum; where Ind AS requires different treatment/disclosure, Ind AS prevails

Statement

Schedule III Division II sets out the minimum requirements for disclosure on the face of the financial statements and in the notes. Its General Instructions provide that where compliance with the requirements of the Act, including Ind AS (except the option of presenting assets and liabilities in order of liquidity as provided by the relevant Ind AS), requires any change in treatment or disclosure — including addition, amendment, substitution or deletion in the head or sub-head, or any change inter se — in the financial statements or statements forming part thereof, the same shall be made and the requirements of Schedule III shall stand modified accordingly.

Further, the disclosure requirements of Schedule III are in addition to and not in substitution of the disclosure requirements of Ind AS.

Explanation

Three consequences follow.

1. Ind AS overrides format where they conflict — with one express exception. If an Ind AS requires a line item not in Schedule III (assets held for sale under Ind AS 105, regulatory deferral account balances under Ind AS 114, contract assets/liabilities under Ind AS 115), the company adds it. If Ind AS requires a different measurement or classification (for example, redeemable preference shares as borrowings under Ind AS 32 rather than share capital), Ind AS governs. The one thing Ind AS permits that Schedule III does not allow Division II companies to do is the liquidity-order balance sheet under Ind AS 1.60; current/non-current presentation is mandatory.

2. Schedule III adds disclosures that Ind AS does not require. Ageing schedules, promoter shareholding, title deeds, struck-off company relationships, the 11 ratios, CSR, crypto, MSME dues, utilisation of borrowed funds and undisclosed income are Companies Act requirements on top of Ind AS. Complying with Ind AS alone is not enough for statutory financial statements.

3. Ind AS disclosures are not displaced by Schedule III. Ind AS 1.117 policy information, Ind AS 115 disaggregation and contract balance disclosures, Ind AS 107 risk disclosures, Ind AS 24 related party disclosures and Ind AS 108 segment information all apply in full.

Sec 129(1) ties this together: financial statements must give a true and fair view, comply with accounting standards notified under Sec 133 and be in the form provided in Schedule III. Where Schedule III and Ind AS seem to conflict, the ICAI Guidance Note on Division II resolves most practical questions (for example, presentation of contract balances, unbilled revenue and current maturities).

Other statutes can impose formats too: under the proviso to Sec 129(1), insurance and banking companies, companies engaged in generation or supply of electricity and other classes governed by special Acts follow the form specified under those Acts (where an Act prescribes none, as is currently the case for electricity companies, Schedule III applies); SEBI LODR prescribes the results format, which is itself based on Schedule III.

Illustration

Shivalik Power Ltd, a regulated distribution utility, prepares FY 2025-26 statements:

Issue Schedule III alone Ind AS requirement Outcome
Regulatory deferral account debit balances ₹420 crore No line item Ind AS 114 requires separate line after total assets, with movements in P&L shown separately Add line items
8% redeemable preference shares ₹100 crore "Equity share capital" note covers preference shares in Division I thinking Ind AS 32: financial liability Present in borrowings
Liquidity-order presentation proposed by CFO for "better readability" Not permitted Ind AS 1.60 option exists Not available — current/non-current
Ageing of trade receivables Required Not required by Ind AS 107 in this form Disclose (in addition to Ind AS 107 credit risk disclosures)
Assets held for sale ₹35 crore No specific line Ind AS 105.38 separate presentation Add line items

Practice Questions

Q1. Resolve the conflict [L2]

For each, state whether Schedule III or Ind AS governs, and the final presentation: (a) a CFO wants to present "Revenue from operations" net of commission paid to dealers who act as the company's agents; (b) compulsorily convertible debentures convertible into a variable number of shares; (c) deferred government grant for a capital asset — the company wants to deduct it from PPE; (d) a manufacturer wants to show expenses by function on the face of the P&L.

💡 Hint: Check whether Ind AS itself offers an option and whether Schedule III removes that option.

Q2. Interview: minimum not maximum [L1]

Explain to a junior colleague, in under 150 words, why a set of financial statements that follows every Schedule III caption can still fail Sec 129, and give two examples.

Q3. Adding line items [L2]

An infrastructure company has ₹1,200 crore of service concession intangible assets under development and ₹300 crore of financial assets under service concession arrangements (Appendix D to Ind AS 115). Explain where these appear on the face of the balance sheet and what additional line items or sub-totals you would add.
