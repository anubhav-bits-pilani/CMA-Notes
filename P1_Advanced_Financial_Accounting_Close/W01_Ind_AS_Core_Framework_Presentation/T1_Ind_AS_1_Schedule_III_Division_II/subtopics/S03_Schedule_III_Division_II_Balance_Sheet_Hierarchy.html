Subtopic note

Schedule III Division II Balance Sheet Hierarchy and Line Items

Scope: Schedule III Division II balance sheet hierarchy: non-current/current assets, equity (share capital, other equity), financial vs non-financial liabilities, mandatory line items

Why it matters

Every Ind AS company's balance sheet in India follows one fixed skeleton, so analysts, lenders, NFRA reviewers and XBRL filings on the MCA portal all expect the same captions in the same order. When you convert a Tally trial balance, the hardest work is not arithmetic but mapping: which ledger goes into "Other financial liabilities" rather than "Other current liabilities", which advance is a financial asset, which "deposit" is cash. Getting the hierarchy right is the foundation for the mapping engine you build in Week 4, for ratio computation, and for three-statement models whose balance sheet must reconcile line-by-line with reported numbers.

Core notes

The face of the balance sheet

Schedule III Division II (applicable to companies whose financial statements comply with Companies (Ind AS) Rules 2015, other than NBFCs) prescribes the following order. Each caption carries a note reference and both current and previous reporting-period columns.

Section Line items (in order)
(1) Non-current assets (a) Property, plant and equipment; (b) Capital work-in-progress; (c) Investment property; (d) Goodwill; (e) Other intangible assets; (f) Intangible assets under development; (g) Biological assets other than bearer plants; (h) Financial assets — (i) Investments, (ii) Trade receivables, (iii) Loans, (iv) Others; (i) Deferred tax assets (net); (j) Other non-current assets
(2) Current assets (a) Inventories; (b) Financial assets — (i) Investments, (ii) Trade receivables, (iii) Cash and cash equivalents, (iv) Bank balances other than (iii), (v) Loans, (vi) Others; (c) Current tax assets (net); (d) Other current assets
Equity (a) Equity share capital; (b) Other equity
(1) Non-current liabilities (a) Financial liabilities — (i) Borrowings, (ia) Lease liabilities, (ii) Trade payables [(A) total outstanding dues of micro and small enterprises; (B) others], (iii) Other financial liabilities; (b) Provisions; (c) Deferred tax liabilities (net); (d) Other non-current liabilities
(2) Current liabilities (a) Financial liabilities — (i) Borrowings, (ia) Lease liabilities, (ii) Trade payables [(A) MSE; (B) others], (iii) Other financial liabilities; (b) Other current liabilities; (c) Provisions; (d) Current tax liabilities (net)

Non-controlling interests appear within equity in the consolidated balance sheet. Assets (and liabilities) classified as held for sale under Ind AS 105 are presented separately, added as line items because Ind AS requires it.

Mandatory line items and the "add, don't delete" principle

Ind AS 1.54 lists the minimum line items (PPE, investment property, intangibles, financial assets, equity-accounted investments, biological assets, inventories, receivables, cash, held-for-sale items, payables, provisions, financial liabilities, tax balances, NCI, issued capital and reserves). Ind AS 1.55 requires additional line items, headings and subtotals when relevant. Schedule III's General Instructions allow additional line items and sub-totals where needed to comply with Ind AS, but captions cannot be dropped merely because the entity prefers a different format; a nil line may be omitted only if both years are nil. The liquidity-order presentation permitted by Ind AS 1.60 is expressly excluded for Division II companies.

Equity: share capital

The equity share capital line carries only equity shares; preference shares that are financial liabilities under Ind AS 32 (redeemable preference shares with mandatory dividends) sit in borrowings. The share capital note discloses, for each class: authorised, issued, subscribed and fully paid, subscribed but not fully paid; par value; reconciliation of shares outstanding at the beginning and end of the period; rights, preferences and restrictions (including on dividends and repayment of capital); shares held by the holding company, ultimate holding company, subsidiaries or associates; shareholders holding more than 5%; shares reserved for options and contracts; for the five years immediately preceding — shares allotted as fully paid without payment in cash, bonus shares, shares bought back; terms of convertible securities; calls unpaid (separately by directors and officers); forfeited shares; and, since 2021, promoter shareholding (covered in s06).

Equity: other equity

"Other equity" is a single face line supported by a note and the SOCIE columns: share application money pending allotment; equity component of compound financial instruments; reserves and surplus (capital reserve, securities premium, other reserves such as general reserve, capital redemption reserve, debenture redemption reserve, share-based payment reserve, and retained earnings); debt instruments through OCI; equity instruments through OCI; effective portion of cash flow hedges; revaluation surplus; exchange differences on translating foreign operations; other items of OCI; money received against share warrants. A debit balance of retained earnings is shown as a negative figure within other equity — never as an asset.

Financial vs non-financial liabilities

Schedule III forces an Ind AS 32 test on every liability ledger: is there a contractual obligation to deliver cash or another financial asset?

Ledger (typical Tally group) Schedule III line Reason
Term loans, NCDs, ECBs, CCDs (liability portion), redeemable preference shares Borrowings Contractual obligation, financing
Current maturities of long-term debt Current borrowings 2021 amendment
Lease liabilities Lease liabilities (ia) Ind AS 116
Sundry creditors for goods and services Trade payables (MSE / others) Contractual, operating
Capex creditors, interest accrued, unpaid dividends, security deposits received, employee dues payable, derivative liabilities Other financial liabilities Contractual, not trade
GST, TDS, PF/ESI payable, customer advances (contract liabilities), deferred government grants Other current / non-current liabilities Statutory or performance obligation, not contractual cash
Gratuity, leave encashment, warranty, decommissioning Provisions Ind AS 37 / Ind AS 19
Income-tax payable (net of advance tax) Current tax liabilities (net) Ind AS 12

Statutory dues are non-financial because the obligation arises from law, not contract. Advances from customers are non-financial because they are settled by delivering goods or services. The same logic applies on the asset side: GST input credit, capital advances, prepaid expenses and advances to suppliers are other (non-financial) assets, while security deposits paid, interest accrued receivable and loans to employees are other financial assets or loans.

Cash, bank balances and investments

"Cash and cash equivalents" follows Ind AS 7 (cash, demand deposits, deposits with original maturity of three months or less). "Bank balances other than cash and cash equivalents" carries deposits with original maturity above three months but realisable within twelve months, earmarked balances (unpaid dividend accounts) and margin money. Deposits with remaining maturity above twelve months move to non-current "Other financial assets". Current investments include liquid mutual fund units unless the entity's policy, consistently applied, treats them as cash equivalents — Indian practice usually presents them as current investments.

Contract balances

Contract assets (conditional right) and contract liabilities (Ind AS 115.105) must be presented or disclosed separately. Common Indian practice presents contract assets under "Other current assets" (or "Other financial assets" for unbilled amounts where only the passage of time is required — those are, in substance, receivables), and contract liabilities under "Other current liabilities". The note must disclose the balances separately from receivables.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Dealer deposits refundable on demand are current even if historically retained for years: no right to defer.
  • Inter-corporate deposits given are "Loans" (financial asset) — not investments; deposits placed with banks are not loans.
  • Debit balances in creditors (advances to suppliers): reclassify to other current assets (non-financial) if for goods, or financial assets if refundable in cash.
  • Investments in subsidiaries in standalone accounts at cost (Ind AS 27) are non-current financial assets — Investments, with the basis (cost, FVOCI, FVTPL) disclosed in the note.
  • Bearer plants are PPE (Ind AS 16), not biological assets.
  • Negative "other equity" is allowed; negative share capital is not.

Key takeaways

  • Division II prescribes a fixed order: non-current before current, equity before liabilities, financial liabilities before provisions.
  • Every liability ledger must pass an Ind AS 32 contractual-obligation test to decide financial vs non-financial placement.
  • Statutory dues and customer advances are other liabilities; capex creditors and accrued interest are other financial liabilities.
  • Line items may be added, not dropped; liquidity-order presentation is not available.
  • Share capital notes carry extensive mandatory disclosures, including five-year history and promoter holdings.
