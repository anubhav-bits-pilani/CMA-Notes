Key concept

Prior-Period Error Disclosures Under Ind AS 8.49

Concept: Error disclosures: nature, amount per line item and basic/diluted EPS impact for each prior period presented (Ind AS 8.49)

Statement

In applying Ind AS 8.42, an entity shall disclose (Ind AS 8.49):

  • (a) the nature of the prior period error;
  • (b) for each prior period presented, to the extent practicable, the amount of the correction:
  • (i) for each financial statement line item affected; and
  • (ii) if Ind AS 33 applies, for basic and diluted earnings per share;
  • (c) the amount of the correction at the beginning of the earliest prior period presented; and
  • (d) if retrospective restatement is impracticable for a particular prior period, the circumstances that led to the existence of that condition and a description of how and from when the error has been corrected.

Financial statements of subsequent periods need not repeat these disclosures.

Explanation

The disclosure exists so users can bridge from what was previously published to what is now presented. Practical requirements:

  • Nature: what went wrong and how it was discovered (e.g., "cut-off error on export shipments under CIF terms"; "inventory overstatement due to a reconciliation failure at the Pune warehouse"; "fraudulent invoicing by a regional sales team").
  • Line items: every affected line — not just profit. A cut-off error affects revenue, cost of materials consumed or changes in inventories, tax expense, trade receivables, inventories, current/deferred tax balances, retained earnings and possibly the cash flow statement's working-capital lines.
  • EPS: basic and diluted, for each prior period presented.
  • Opening effect: the cumulative correction at 1 April of the comparative year (for FY 2025-26 statements, 1 April 2024), typically to retained earnings, plus any other equity component.
  • Format: a reconciliation table — "As previously reported", "Adjustment", "Restated" — for the balance sheet(s), P&L and EPS is the clearest way to satisfy 8.49; Schedule III SOCIE rows complement it.

Related disclosures: Ind AS 1.40A third balance sheet (with 1.40C notes exemption); Ind AS 1.41 if classification also changed; Ind AS 34.16A in interim reports; SEBI results notes (s08); and the auditor's report may include an emphasis of matter referring to the restatement (SA 706, SA 710).
