Key concept

Non-Adjusting Events and Post-Year-End Dividends

Concept: Non-adjusting: fall in investment values, major acquisition, restructuring announcement, dividends declared after reporting date — not a liability (Ind AS 10.12–13)

Statement

A non-adjusting event is an event after the reporting period that is indicative of conditions that arose after the reporting period (Ind AS 10.3(b)). Amounts are not adjusted (10.10); material non-adjusting events are disclosed — the nature of the event and an estimate of its financial effect, or a statement that such an estimate cannot be made (10.21).

Dividends: If an entity declares dividends to holders of equity instruments after the reporting period, it shall not recognise those dividends as a liability at the end of the reporting period (10.12), because no obligation exists at that time (10.13). Such dividends are disclosed in the notes in accordance with Ind AS 1 (Ind AS 1.137: amount of dividends proposed or declared before approval but not recognised, and the related amount per share).

Explanation

Fall in investment values (10.11): a decline in the fair value of investments between the reporting date and approval does not normally relate to their condition at year-end but reflects circumstances arising subsequently. Investments are measured at 31 March fair value; disclosure is made if the decline is material.

Major acquisition: a business combination after year-end is a new transaction. Disclose under Ind AS 10.22(a) and Ind AS 103.59/B66 (information about combinations after the reporting period but before approval, unless the initial accounting is incomplete).

Restructuring announcement: a restructuring provision requires a constructive obligation at the reporting date — a detailed formal plan and a valid expectation raised in those affected (Ind AS 37.72). A Board decision before year-end without announcement is not enough; an announcement in April is non-adjusting. Disclose.

Dividends: in India, the Board recommends a final dividend at the meeting approving the accounts; shareholders declare it at the AGM (Sec 123). The recommendation is non-adjusting — no liability at 31 March. Interim dividends declared by the Board before year-end are liabilities from declaration. This is a clean break from pre-2016 Indian GAAP practice (AS 4 was amended in 2016 to align). Dividends on preference shares classified as financial liabilities are interest expense and accrue regardless.

Other non-adjusting examples (10.22): plan to discontinue an operation, asset purchases or disposals, fire or flood destroying a plant, major share transactions, abnormal changes in asset prices or FX rates, tax law changes enacted after year-end, major commitments or guarantees, litigation arising solely from post-year-end events.

Exception: if a non-adjusting event undermines the going concern assumption, Ind AS 10.14 overrides (s06).

Illustration

Sindhu Retail Ltd, year-end 31 March 2026, Board approval 14 May 2026; 20 crore shares.

Event Treatment
14 May: Board recommends final dividend ₹3.50 per share No liability; disclose ₹70 crore (₹3.50 per share), subject to AGM approval
20 April: Nifty falls 12%; FVTPL equity portfolio (₹150 crore at 31 March) falls to ₹128 crore No adjustment; disclose ₹22 crore decline
2 May: acquires 100% of a regional chain for ₹410 crore Disclose; Ind AS 103 disclosures as available
28 April: announces closure of 40 loss-making stores; estimated cost ₹35 crore No provision at 31 March; disclose; provision in FY 2026-27
5 May: fire destroys a warehouse (stock ₹18 crore, insured ₹12 crore) Disclose nature and estimated net loss ₹6 crore

Q1. Draft the note [L1]

Q2. Restructuring timing [L2]

A Board approves a detailed plan to close a plant on 20 March 2026, and the plan is communicated to unions and employees on (a) 28 March 2026, (b) 8 April 2026. Approval of FS: 15 May 2026. Estimated costs: VRS ₹14 crore, lease termination ₹3 crore, relocation of retained staff ₹2 crore. Determine the provision at 31 March 2026 in each case.

💡 Hint: Ind AS 37.72 and 37.80 decide both timing and which costs qualify.

Q3. Interim vs final dividend [L2]

A company's Board declared an interim dividend of ₹2 per share on 15 March 2026 (paid 5 April 2026) and recommended a final dividend of ₹4 per share on 18 May 2026. 15 crore shares. Show the balance sheet, SOCIE and cash flow treatment for FY 2025-26 and FY 2026-27.
