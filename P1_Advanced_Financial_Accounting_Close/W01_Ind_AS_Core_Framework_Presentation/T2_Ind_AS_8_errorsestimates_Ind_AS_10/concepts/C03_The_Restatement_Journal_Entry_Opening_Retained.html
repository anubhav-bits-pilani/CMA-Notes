Key concept

The Restatement Journal Entry: Opening Retained Earnings, Tax and EPS

Concept: Restatement entry: Dr/Cr Opening retained earnings (net of tax) ↔ affected asset/liability, with DT effect and comparative EPS restated

Statement

A prior-period error correction booked in the current year's ledger takes the form:

\[ \Delta \text{Opening RE} = \Delta \text{Pre-tax cumulative effect} \times (1 - t) \quad \text{(where tax effects apply)} \]

Account Dr Cr
Opening retained earnings (net-of-tax cumulative effect) X × (1 − t)
Deferred tax asset / current tax (tax effect) X × t
Affected asset (reduction) or liability (increase) X

(reverse the signs for an error that understated profit), where \(X\) is the cumulative pre-tax misstatement at the start of the current period and \(t\) is the tax rate applicable to the temporary or current tax difference. Comparative EPS is recomputed:

\[ \text{Restated EPS}_{\text{prior}} = \frac{\text{Reported earnings}_{\text{prior}} \pm \text{Net-of-tax correction}_{\text{prior}}}{\text{Weighted average shares}_{\text{prior}} \text{ (adjusted for bonus/splits)}} \]

Explanation

The entry sits in the current-year ledger, but the presentation is retrospective. In Tally or SAP, you cannot reopen a closed year; you pass the entry on day one of the current year against opening retained earnings. The financial statements then present the comparatives as restated (with the comparative-year effect moved into the comparative P&L), and the SOCIE shows the cumulative effect at the start of the earliest period in the "prior period errors" row.

Tax effect — three possibilities:

  1. Deferred tax — where the tax base was unaffected (tax computation was right, books were wrong), the correction creates or changes a temporary difference: record DTA/DTL. Example: omitted provision deductible on payment.
  2. Current tax — where the tax computation also followed the wrong book figure and a revised return is possible (Sec 139(5)) or the tax will be corrected in assessment: record a current tax liability/asset and possibly interest under Sec 234B/234C (interest is not part of the restatement of tax expense; it is a current-period expense when it becomes probable).
  3. No tax effect — permanent differences (e.g., penalties, impairment of goodwill with no tax base) or where the related DTA is not recognisable under Ind AS 12.24/12.34.

OCI items are restated to the relevant OCI reserve, not to retained earnings (e.g., an actuarial loss omitted).

Equity components other than retained earnings can be affected: securities premium (share issue costs wrongly expensed), share-based payment reserve (ESOP charge omitted), NCI in CFS.

EPS: restate basic and diluted EPS for each prior period presented (Ind AS 33.64; Ind AS 8.49(b)(ii)), using the share count adjusted for any bonus issues or splits, including those after the reporting date but before approval.

Illustration

In FY 2025-26, Krishna Agro Ltd discovers that ₹20 crore of trade discounts given in FY 2024-25 were recorded as "selling expenses" and, separately, that ₹8 crore of FY 2023-24 repairs were capitalised as PPE (depreciation charged on it: FY 2023-24 ₹0.4 crore; FY 2024-25 ₹0.8 crore). Tax 25.168%, tax computation correctly treated repairs as revenue expenditure (so a temporary difference exists). Weighted shares 10 crore; FY 2024-25 reported profit ₹75 crore.

Discounts: reclassification within FY 2024-25 P&L (revenue −₹20 crore, selling expenses −₹20 crore) — no profit effect, no tax, no EPS change; disclose.

Repairs capitalised: at 1 April 2024, PPE overstated by 8.0 − 0.4 = ₹7.6 crore; at 31 March 2025, by 7.6 − 0.8 = ₹6.8 crore.

Entry on 1 April 2025 Dr ₹ crore Cr ₹ crore
Opening retained earnings 5.09
Deferred tax liability (reduction; temporary difference reverses) 1.71
PPE (net carrying amount) 6.80

Comparative-year effect: FY 2024-25 depreciation falls by ₹0.8 crore, deferred tax expense rises by ₹0.20 crore → restated FY 2024-25 profit ₹75.60 crore; restated EPS = 75.60 ÷ 10 = ₹7.56 (reported ₹7.50). Opening RE at 1 April 2024 falls by 7.6 × (1 − 0.25168) = ₹5.69 crore; at 31 March 2025 by ₹5.09 crore.

Practice Questions

Q1. Pass the entry [L1]

An onerous lease provision of ₹5 crore should have been recognised at 31 March 2024 and remained unchanged at 31 March 2025 (the books recorded nothing; the tax base is nil because the expense is deductible only on payment). Tax rate 25.168%. Pass the entry on 1 April 2025 and state the restated retained earnings at 1 April 2024 and 31 March 2025.

Q2. Current vs deferred tax effect [L2]

FY 2024-25 revenue of ₹12 crore was recognised in error (goods delivered in FY 2025-26); the tax return for FY 2024-25 also included the revenue and can still be revised. Explain whether the tax effect of the restatement is current or deferred, pass the restatement entry, and state how FY 2025-26 revenue and tax are affected.

💡 Hint: Follow the tax base of the revenue in both years.

Q3. EPS with bonus after year-end [L2]

Restated FY 2024-25 profit is ₹48 crore (reported ₹52 crore); weighted shares FY 2024-25 were 6 crore. A 1:2 bonus issue was made in December 2025, and a 2-for-1 split on 10 May 2026 (FS approval 20 May 2026). Compute reported and restated FY 2024-25 basic EPS as presented in FY 2025-26 financial statements.
