Key concept

Prospective Recognition of Estimate Changes and the 8.35 Tie-Breaker

Concept: Estimate changes recognised prospectively in current/future P&L (Ind AS 8.36); if policy vs estimate unclear, treat as estimate (Ind AS 8.35)

Statement

Ind AS 8.36: The effect of a change in an accounting estimate, other than a change to which 8.37 applies, shall be recognised prospectively by including it in profit or loss in:

  • (a) the period of the change, if the change affects that period only; or
  • (b) the period of the change and future periods, if the change affects both.

Ind AS 8.37: To the extent that a change in an accounting estimate gives rise to changes in assets and liabilities, or relates to an item of equity, it is recognised by adjusting the carrying amount of the related asset, liability or equity item in the period of the change.

Ind AS 8.35: When it is difficult to distinguish a change in an accounting policy from a change in an accounting estimate, the change is treated as a change in an accounting estimate.

Explanation

Estimates are, by nature, revised as new information arrives. Revising them is not a correction of a mistake, so comparatives are not restated. "Prospective" has two flavours:

  • Spread forward — depreciation and amortisation: the revised life or residual value applies to the remaining carrying amount from the date of change, affecting current and future periods (8.36(b)).
  • Catch-up now — balance-sheet estimates remeasured at each reporting date (ECL, warranty, litigation provisions, cost-to-complete in revenue): the full difference is recognised in the current period (8.36(a) or 8.37), even though it partly relates to earlier sales.

Ind AS 8.38 confirms that a change in estimate may affect only the current period (bad debts) or both current and future periods (useful life).

Location may not be P&L. Ind AS 8.36 says "profit or loss", but 8.37 and specific standards override: actuarial assumption changes for post-employment plans go to OCI (Ind AS 19.120), decommissioning estimate changes adjust the asset cost (Appendix A to Ind AS 16).

The 8.35 tie-breaker. Some changes have characteristics of both — for example, a change from straight-line to units-of-production amortisation of an intangible may reflect both a new principle and a revised expectation of consumption. Ind AS 8.35 resolves ambiguity in favour of an estimate (prospective). It is a tie-breaker for genuinely ambiguous cases, not a way to avoid restating errors or genuine policy changes. Since the 2023 amendment (8.34A), changes in measurement techniques and inputs are expressly estimates, which narrows the ambiguous zone.

Disclosure (8.39–40): nature and amount of the change affecting the current period or expected to affect future periods; if future effect is impracticable to estimate, say so.

Illustration

On 1 April 2025, Tunga Cement Ltd revises the remaining useful life of a kiln (carrying amount ₹90 crore, nil residual) from 15 to 10 years, and changes its ECL provision matrix, increasing the allowance by ₹4 crore at 31 March 2026.

\[ \text{FY 2025-26 depreciation} = \frac{90}{10} = 9.0 \text{ crore (previously } \tfrac{90}{15} = 6.0\text{)} \]

Effect: current year +₹3.0 crore; each future year to the end of the revised life +₹3.0 crore versus the old plan, and nil instead of ₹6.0 crore in the five years beyond it (spread forward). ECL: +₹4.0 crore in FY 2025-26 only (catch-up). FY 2024-25 comparatives unchanged. Disclosure: "The change in estimated useful life of the kiln has increased depreciation by ₹3.0 crore in FY 2025-26 and will increase depreciation by ₹3.0 crore in each of the next nine years, with no charge in the five years thereafter that the previous estimate would have covered."

Practice Questions

Q1. Spread or catch-up? [L1]

For each change, say whether the effect is recognised only in the current period, in current and future periods, or outside P&L: (a) residual value of a fleet increased; (b) litigation provision increased after a lower court ruling; (c) salary escalation assumption for gratuity increased; (d) revised estimate of total cost on a 3-year EPC contract; (e) revised useful life of a brand acquired in a business combination.

Q2. Applying 8.35 [L2]

A company changes the basis of allocating fixed production overhead to inventory from a machine-hours rate to a labour-hours rate, arguing that labour now better reflects normal capacity usage. The CFO wants to restate comparatives; the auditor suggests prospective treatment. Analyse under Ind AS 2, 8.32–35 and 8.34A.

💡 Hint: Ask whether the measurement basis (cost) has changed or only the technique for estimating it.

Q3. Disclosure computation [L2]

A software intangible with carrying amount ₹24 crore at 1 April 2025 and 6 years remaining is reassessed to have 3 years remaining; residual nil. Compute the effect on FY 2025-26 and on each of the next five years, and draft the 8.39 disclosure.
