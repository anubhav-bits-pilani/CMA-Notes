Subtopic note

SEBI LODR: Restated Comparatives, Modified Opinions and SIAQ

Scope: SEBI LODR: restated comparatives in quarterly results, modified audit opinions and Statement on Impact of Audit Qualifications

Why it matters

For listed companies, restatements and audit qualifications do not wait for the annual report: they surface in the quarterly results filed with NSE and BSE under SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations 2015. Markets react to a "restated" column or a Statement on Impact of Audit Qualifications (SIAQ) within minutes of filing. Reporting teams must prepare restated comparatives in the SEBI format, explain them in notes, and coordinate with auditors whose limited review or audit report may be modified. Analysts reading results need to know where to find the quantified impact of a qualification and how to adjust their models.

Core notes

Reg 33 framework (recap)

  • Quarterly standalone and consolidated financial results within 45 days of quarter-end (first three quarters), subject to limited review (or audit) by the statutory auditor.
  • Annual audited results within 60 days of year-end, with the audit report.
  • Results in the SEBI-prescribed format derived from Schedule III Division II, with columns for current quarter, preceding quarter, corresponding quarter of previous year, year-to-date and previous year.
  • Half-yearly statement of assets and liabilities and cash flow statement.
  • Approval by the Board (after audit committee review), signed by the chairperson, MD or whole-time director.

Restated comparatives in quarterly results

When an Ind AS 8 restatement (or a retrospective policy change, or a common-control business combination under Appendix C to Ind AS 103) affects prior periods, every comparative column in the results must be presented on the restated basis:

  • the corresponding quarter of the previous year, preceding quarter (if affected), previous year-to-date and previous full year are restated;
  • columns are labelled "Restated" and a note explains the nature of the error, the line items affected, and the amounts (consistent with Ind AS 8.49 and Ind AS 34.16A);
  • the restated figures should have been subjected to limited review or audit — auditors typically include the restated comparatives in their review scope or report on them;
  • restated EPS (basic and diluted) for comparative periods;
  • the fourth-quarter figure, being the balancing figure between audited annual and published nine-month figures, is computed on the restated basis.

Where the error is discovered in Q2 of FY 2025-26 and relates to FY 2024-25, the Q2 results will show restated "corresponding quarter of previous year" and "previous year" columns, and the annual FY 2025-26 financial statements will later show the full Ind AS 8 restatement (with a third balance sheet if required).

Illustration of the note:

"The figures for the quarter ended 30 September 2024 and year ended 31 March 2025 have been restated to correct an error in the recognition of revenue on bill-and-hold arrangements that did not meet the criteria in Ind AS 115 B79–B82. Revenue from operations for the year ended 31 March 2025 is reduced by ₹42.50 crore, profit after tax by ₹9.10 crore, and basic and diluted EPS by ₹1.82. Opening retained earnings at 1 April 2024 are reduced by ₹6.40 crore."

Modified audit opinions

Under SA 705 (Revised), the auditor modifies the opinion when the financial statements are materially misstated or sufficient appropriate audit evidence cannot be obtained:

Modification Condition
Qualified opinion Misstatements (or possible effects of undetected misstatements) material but not pervasive
Adverse opinion Misstatements material and pervasive
Disclaimer of opinion Unable to obtain sufficient appropriate evidence; possible effects material and pervasive

An "emphasis of matter" (SA 706) or "material uncertainty related to going concern" section (SA 570) is not a modification. For limited reviews of quarterly results (SRE 2410), the reviewer issues a qualified, adverse or disclaimer of conclusion in equivalent circumstances.

Statement on Impact of Audit Qualifications (SIAQ)

Under Reg 33(3)(d) read with SEBI's circular of May 2016 (now consolidated in the LODR Master Circular), when submitting annual audited results, a listed entity whose audit report contains a modified opinion must submit a Statement on Impact of Audit Qualifications, for both standalone and consolidated results. If the audit report is unmodified, the entity submits a declaration to that effect.

The SIAQ has two parts:

Part I — Financial impact

Particulars Audited figures (as reported before adjusting for qualifications) Adjusted figures (audited figures after adjusting for qualifications)
Turnover / Total income
Total expenditure
Net profit / (loss)
Earnings per share
Total assets
Total liabilities
Net worth
Any other financial item(s) (as felt appropriate by management)

Part II — Details of each audit qualification

  • details of the qualification;
  • type: qualified opinion / disclaimer of opinion / adverse opinion;
  • frequency: whether appeared first time, repetitive, or since how long continuing;
  • for qualifications where the impact is quantified by the auditor, management's views;
  • for qualifications where the impact is not quantified: management's estimation of the impact; if management is unable to estimate, the reasons; and the auditor's comments.

It is signed by the CEO/MD, CFO, Audit Committee chairperson and the statutory auditor. The stock exchanges review SIAQs and can seek clarifications or refer matters to SEBI or ICAI's Quality Review Board; repetitive qualifications draw attention.

Worked illustration

Auditor's qualified opinion on Saptagiri Textiles Ltd for FY 2025-26: (1) non-provision of ECL on a receivable of ₹18 crore from a customer in CIRP — auditor quantifies impact at ₹14 crore; (2) inability to verify inventory of ₹6 crore at a third-party warehouse — impact not quantified. Reported PAT ₹40 crore; net worth ₹350 crore; tax 25.168%.

Part I adjusted figures for qualification (1): total expenditure + ₹14 crore; net profit − ₹14 crore × (1 − 0.25168) = − ₹10.48 crore → adjusted PAT ₹29.52 crore; total assets and net worth reduced correspondingly; EPS adjusted. Qualification (2) is described in Part II with management's estimate or the reasons for being unable to estimate.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Qualification on comparatives only (SA 710): still reported in the SIAQ if the current-year report is modified on account of comparatives.
  • Consolidated modified, standalone clean (or vice versa): separate SIAQ for the modified set and a declaration for the clean set.
  • Qualifications in limited review of quarterly results: disclosed with the results; the next audited results must address them.
  • Restatement in the middle of the year may trigger Reg 30 disclosure (material event) and a statement from the Board explaining the error and remediation.
  • Restatement versus qualification: management that corrects the misstatement through restatement removes the basis for qualification; refusal leads to qualification and the SIAQ.
  • Format changes under SEBI circulars (e.g., moving to the Master Circular of 2023 and later updates) — always check the current format before filing.

Key takeaways

  • Restated comparatives in SEBI results are labelled "Restated", explained in a note and reflected in every comparative column and EPS.
  • SA 705 modifications (qualified, adverse, disclaimer) differ from emphasis of matter and going concern paragraphs.
  • Annual audited results with a modified opinion require a SIAQ; clean reports require a declaration.
  • The SIAQ shows reported vs adjusted figures and, for each qualification, type, frequency, management's views and auditor comments.
  • Stock exchanges review SIAQs; repetitive qualifications invite regulatory follow-up.

Practice Questions

Q1. Prepare a SIAQ Part I [L2]

A listed company reports FY 2025-26 total income ₹1,240 crore, total expenditure ₹1,110 crore, PAT ₹97 crore (tax 25.168%), 12 crore shares, total assets ₹1,800 crore, total liabilities ₹950 crore. The auditor qualifies for (a) unrecognised impairment of a subsidiary investment of ₹30 crore (no tax effect) and (b) an unprovided onerous-contract loss of ₹12 crore (deferred tax applies). Prepare Part I of the SIAQ (standalone).

💡 Hint: Apply the tax effect only where a deductible temporary difference would arise.

Q2. Restated quarterly column [L2]

In Q2 FY 2025-26 a company discovers that FY 2024-25 employee costs were understated by ₹16 crore (₹4 crore per quarter). Tax rate 25.168%; shares 8 crore. Describe exactly which columns in the Q2 FY 2025-26 results change, compute the restated PAT and EPS adjustments for the "corresponding quarter" and "previous year" columns, and draft the explanatory note.

Q3. Qualification or emphasis? [L1]

Classify each auditor communication as qualified, adverse, disclaimer, emphasis of matter or material uncertainty paragraph, and state whether a SIAQ is required: (a) auditor unable to attend inventory count and could not verify ₹4 crore (material, not pervasive); (b) disclosure of a pending tax demand of ₹300 crore that the auditor highlights; (c) material uncertainty about going concern adequately disclosed; (d) non-consolidation of a material subsidiary.

Q4. Board briefing [L3]

The audit committee of a listed company faces its third consecutive qualified opinion for the same issue (non-recognition of ECL on dues from a state discom). Prepare a briefing covering the SIAQ content, market and regulatory consequences, options to resolve the qualification (including restatement of comparatives), and the effect on the FY 2025-26 results presentation.
