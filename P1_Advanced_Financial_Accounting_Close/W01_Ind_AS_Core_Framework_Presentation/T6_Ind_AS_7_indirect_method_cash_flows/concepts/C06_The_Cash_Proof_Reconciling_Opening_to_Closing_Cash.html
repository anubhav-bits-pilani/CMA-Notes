Key concept

The Cash Proof: Reconciling Opening to Closing Cash

Concept: Proof: Opening C&CE + CFO + CFI + CFF + FX effect = Closing C&CE (reconciled to balance sheet incl. overdrafts)

Statement

\[ \text{C\&CE}_1 = \text{C\&CE}_0 + \text{CFO} + \text{CFI} + \text{CFF} + FX_{7.28} \]

where \(\text{C\&CE}_0\) and \(\text{C\&CE}_1\) are opening and closing cash and cash equivalents as defined for the cash flow statement (including the deduction of bank overdrafts repayable on demand that form part of cash management, Ind AS 7.8); CFO, CFI and CFF are net cash flows from operating, investing and financing activities; and \(FX_{7.28}\) is the effect of exchange-rate changes on C&CE held in foreign currency. Closing C&CE must be reconciled to the balance sheet line "Cash and cash equivalents", with the overdraft shown within current borrowings (Ind AS 7.45).

\[ \text{C\&CE}_{\text{CF}} = \text{C\&CE}_{\text{BS}} - \text{Overdrafts in C\&CE policy} \]

Explanation

The proof is the integrity check on the whole statement. Every balance-sheet movement other than cash must be used exactly once, with non-cash movements stripped out; if any line is missed, double-counted or misclassified across the cash boundary, the proof fails. Passing the proof does not guarantee correct classification (an item moved from CFO to CFI still balances) — it guarantees completeness and arithmetic integrity. Classification needs separate review checks (interest in CFF, ROU additions excluded, capex creditors excluded from ΔWC).

Typical causes of a failed proof:

  • an overdraft included in C&CE at one date but not the other;
  • "other bank balances" movements omitted from investing;
  • FX on foreign-currency cash deducted in CFO without a 7.28 line (difference equals the FX amount);
  • acquired balances left in ΔWC without the net acquisition line being adjusted;
  • dividends paid taken from the SOCIE while unpaid dividend balances moved;
  • OCI items (remeasurements, FVOCI changes) that changed balance-sheet lines without a matching non-cash elimination;
  • sign errors on liabilities.

In a model, the proof is a check cell that must be zero for every period, with a master check flag:

=ROUND(Open_CCE + CFO + CFI + CFF + FX_Effect - (BS_Cash - BS_Overdraft), 2)
=IF(ABS(Check)>0.5, "ERROR", "OK")

A second "completeness" check sums all balance-sheet movements mapped to cash flow lines and confirms they equal the change in cash — useful when diagnosing where a failed proof originates.

Illustration

Ganga Pharma Ltd, FY 2024-25 (₹ crore):

Line Amount
Opening C&CE (balance sheet ₹46, less CC overdraft ₹14) 32.0
Net cash from operating activities 182.5
Net cash used in investing activities (140.0)
Net cash used in financing activities (49.0)
Effect of exchange rate changes on C&CE 1.5
Closing C&CE per cash flow 27.0
Reconciliation: balance sheet C&CE 39.0
Less: CC overdraft repayable on demand (within current borrowings) (12.0)
Closing C&CE per balance sheet reconciliation 27.0
Check 0.0

If the FX effect had been omitted (but removed from CFO as unrealised gain), the check would show −1.5, pointing directly to the missing line.

Connections

Closes the loop on c01 (CFO), c04 (capex in CFI), s07 (financing reconciliation), s05 and c05 (the cash boundary) and s08 (FX line). The same proof appears in every 3-statement model as the balance-sheet balancing check (cash from the cash flow equals cash on the balance sheet). Week 4's TB-tagging engine automates it by ensuring every ledger has a cash flow tag. The Week 1 checklist item on deriving an indirect cash flow that ties to closing cash is tested by this proof.

Practice Questions

Q1. Locate the difference [L2]

A draft cash flow shows opening C&CE ₹20 crore, CFO ₹95 crore, CFI ₹(60) crore, CFF ₹(40) crore, giving closing ₹15 crore; the balance sheet shows C&CE ₹22 crore and a CC overdraft of ₹9 crore (opening overdraft ₹5 crore, which was deducted in opening C&CE). FX on USD bank balances was a gain of ₹2 crore, deducted in CFO. Identify why the proof fails and correct it.

💡 Hint: Restate closing C&CE on the same overdraft policy and look for a missing reconciling line.

Q2. Build the check block [L2]

Design the check block for a monthly 3-statement model: proof check, completeness check (all BS lines mapped), sign checks on capex and dividends, and a summary flag. Specify formulas and conditional formatting.

Q3. Interview — "the cash flow balances, so it's right" [L1]

A junior analyst says the cash flow is correct because opening plus flows equals closing cash. Explain what the proof does and does not verify, and list four classification errors that would still pass it.
