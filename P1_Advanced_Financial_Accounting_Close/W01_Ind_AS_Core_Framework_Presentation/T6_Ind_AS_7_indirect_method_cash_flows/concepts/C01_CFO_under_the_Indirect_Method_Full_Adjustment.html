Key concept

CFO under the Indirect Method: Full Adjustment Formula

Concept: CFO = PBT + D&A + Impairment + ECL + Unrealised FX loss + Share-based payment + Finance cost − Interest/dividend income ± Disposal gains/losses ± ΔWC − Taxes paid

Statement

\[ \text{CFO} = \text{PBT} + DA + I + ECL + FX_u + SBP + FC - II - DI - G + L - \Delta WC_{\text{op}} - T_{\text{paid}} \]

where PBT is profit before tax; \(DA\) depreciation and amortisation (including ROU depreciation); \(I\) impairment losses on non-financial assets and investments; \(ECL\) the expected credit loss charge (added back when receivable movements are computed gross); \(FX_u\) unrealised foreign exchange loss (deduct if a gain) on monetary items other than C&CE; \(SBP\) equity-settled share-based payment expense; \(FC\) finance cost (classified as financing under Ind AS 7.33); \(II\) interest income and \(DI\) dividend income (investing); \(G\) and \(L\) gains and losses on disposal of PPE, investments and subsidiaries (investing); \(\Delta WC_{\text{op}}\) the net increase in operating working capital (increase in operating assets less increase in operating liabilities, excluding acquired balances, capex creditors and intercompany loans); and \(T_{\text{paid}}\) income taxes paid (net of refunds).

Explanation

The formula reverses three things in PBT: (1) items that do not involve cash (depreciation, impairment, ECL, unrealised FX, SBP, fair value changes, share of associates); (2) items whose cash flows are investing or financing (finance cost, interest and dividend income, disposal results); and (3) the timing difference between accrual revenue/expense and cash (working capital). Taxes paid are deducted separately because Ind AS 7.35 requires income taxes to be shown separately and classified as operating unless specifically identified with investing/financing.

Extensions for completeness: deduct share of profit of associates/JVs; deduct fair value gains on FVTPL investments; deduct liabilities written back; deduct government grant amortisation; add unwinding of discount if not in finance cost; adjust for provisions consistently (either add back the charge and deduct utilisation, or include the provision movement in ΔWC).

Conditions and consistency rules:

  • ECL add-back only if receivables movement is gross; otherwise use net receivables and no add-back.
  • FX on foreign-currency cash goes to the separate 7.28 line, not \(FX_u\) in CFO (after first removing it from PBT).
  • Inventory write-downs are captured through the net inventory movement — no separate add-back unless the movement is computed on gross inventory.
  • Financial institutions (banks/NBFCs) do not add back finance cost or deduct interest income — interest is operating for them.

Connections

The formula combines s01 (non-cash and reclassification adjustments), s02 and c02 (working-capital rule), c03 (taxes paid) and s03 (interest classification). It feeds c06 (cash proof). In FP&A and valuation, CFO is the starting point for FCFF/FCFE; lenders use "cash generated from operations" for DSCR. The topic pitfalls on ECL double counting and total tax expense both arise inside this formula.

Q1. Compute CFO [L1]

PBT ₹88 crore; depreciation ₹21 crore; ECL ₹2 crore (net receivables used, increase ₹15 crore); finance cost ₹14 crore; interest income ₹3 crore; loss on sale of investments ₹1 crore; SBP ₹2.5 crore; increase in inventories ₹9 crore; decrease in payables ₹4 crore; taxes paid ₹20 crore. Q2. NBFC vs manufacturer [L2]

Using identical P&L data — PBT ₹60 crore, finance cost ₹40 crore, interest income ₹110 crore, depreciation ₹5 crore — explain how the CFO computation differs between an NBFC (interest income from lending) and a manufacturer (interest income on surplus deposits), and compute the pre-working-capital subtotal for each.

Q3. Build the formula in Excel [L2]

Design an Excel CFO block where every adjustment links to a supporting schedule, including a sign convention, a check that finance cost added back equals the P&L line, and a check that the ECL basis matches the receivables basis. List the named ranges you would create.
