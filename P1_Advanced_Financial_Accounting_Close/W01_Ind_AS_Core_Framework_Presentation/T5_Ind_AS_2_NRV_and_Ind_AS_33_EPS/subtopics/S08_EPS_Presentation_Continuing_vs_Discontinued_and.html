Subtopic note

EPS Presentation: Continuing vs Discontinued and SEBI Quarterly EPS

Scope: Presentation: continuing vs discontinued EPS on face of P&L; SEBI quarterly EPS not annualised

Why it matters

EPS is one of the few figures that must appear on the face of the Statement of Profit and Loss and in every quarterly results announcement. Presentation errors — missing discontinued-operations EPS, annualising quarterly EPS, failing to show both basic and diluted with equal prominence — attract NFRA and stock-exchange queries and mislead users of results. For FP&A and investor-relations teams, EPS presentation determines how consensus estimates and P/E multiples are compared quarter by quarter.

Core notes

Face of the Statement of Profit and Loss (Ind AS 33.66–69)

  • Present basic and diluted EPS for profit or loss from continuing operations attributable to ordinary equity holders of the parent, and for profit or loss attributable to them, for each class of ordinary shares with a different right to share in profit, with equal prominence for all periods presented (33.66).
  • If an entity reports a discontinued operation (Ind AS 105), present basic and diluted EPS for the discontinued operation either on the face of the P&L or in the notes (33.68).
  • Present EPS even if the amounts are negative (loss per share) (33.69).
  • If basic and diluted EPS are equal, dual presentation may be accomplished in one line (33.67).
  • In consolidated statements, EPS is based on consolidated profit attributable to the parent; in separate statements, on standalone profit (33.4). An entity presenting both may present EPS in each set only based on that set's information.

Schedule III Division II format

Part II of Schedule III (Statement of Profit and Loss) presents:

Line Content
XIII Profit/(loss) for the period from continuing operations
XIV–XVI Profit/(loss) from discontinued operations, tax expense of discontinued operations, profit/(loss) from discontinued operations after tax
XVII Profit/(loss) for the period
XVIII Other comprehensive income
XIX Total comprehensive income
XX Earnings per equity share (for continuing operation): (1) Basic (2) Diluted
XXI Earnings per equity share (for discontinued operation): (1) Basic (2) Diluted
XXII Earnings per equity share (for discontinued and continuing operations): (1) Basic (2) Diluted

EPS is not presented for total comprehensive income. Face value per share is commonly shown alongside.

Discontinued operations EPS computation

\[ \text{EPS}_{\text{disc}} = \frac{\text{Profit/(loss) from discontinued operations attributable to parent}}{\text{WANS (basic or diluted)}} \]

The diluted denominator uses the same potential shares determined as dilutive by reference to the continuing-operations control number (33.42) — even if they are antidilutive for the discontinued component. Hence continuing + discontinued diluted EPS = total diluted EPS (subject to rounding).

Disclosures (Ind AS 33.70–73A)

  • Amounts used as numerators for basic and diluted EPS and a reconciliation of those amounts to profit attributable to the parent, including the individual effect of each class of instrument.
  • Weighted average number of shares used as denominators, and a reconciliation of the denominators to each other, including the individual effect of each class.
  • Instruments that could potentially dilute basic EPS in the future but were excluded because antidilutive for the period.
  • Description of ordinary share transactions or potential ordinary share transactions occurring after the reporting period that would have significantly changed the number of shares (other than those adjusted retrospectively under 33.64).
  • If additional per-share amounts are disclosed using a reported component of profit other than required (e.g., EPS before exceptional items), use the same WANS, present basic and diluted with equal prominence, disclose in notes (not on the face of the P&L), and reconcile to a line item (33.73).

SEBI LODR Regulation 33 — quarterly results

Listed entities file quarterly standalone and consolidated financial results within 45 days of quarter-end (60 days for Q4/annual). The SEBI-prescribed formats (Master Circular for listed entities) include:

  • "Earnings per equity share (of ₹ __ each) (for continuing and discontinued operations) — Basic, Diluted", with a specific footnote that EPS for the quarters is not annualised.
  • Columns: current quarter, preceding quarter, corresponding quarter of the previous year, year-to-date figures for current and previous year (for Q2/Q3 period ended), previous year.
  • Paid-up equity share capital and face value, other equity (annual only).

Not annualised: a quarterly EPS of ₹5.20 is reported as ₹5.20 — not ₹20.80. Users annualise if they choose; the regulatory format explicitly states "(not annualised)" for quarterly and year-to-date periods other than the full year.

Quarterly WANS: computed for the quarter itself. Because share counts change, the sum of four quarterly EPS figures may not equal annual EPS. Year-to-date EPS uses year-to-date WANS — not the average of quarterly EPS.

Bonus/split adjustments: when a bonus or split occurs, all comparative columns (previous quarters, corresponding quarter last year) are restated — consistent with Ind AS 33.64 and Ind AS 34.

Interim reporting (Ind AS 34.11–11A)

Interim financial statements present basic and diluted EPS on the face of the interim P&L when the entity is within the scope of Ind AS 33. Diluted EPS for the year-to-date is computed independently (not by summing quarters); the antidilution test uses year-to-date numbers.

Illustration: quarterly vs annual EPS

Vyom Ltd: Q1–Q4 profits ₹50, ₹60, ₹40, ₹70 crore; shares 10 crore until a QIP of 2 crore shares on the first day of Q3.

Period Profit WANS EPS (not annualised)
Q1 50 10 5.00
Q2 60 10 6.00
Q3 40 12 3.33
Q4 70 12 5.83
Sum of quarters 20.17
Full year 220 11 20.00

The full-year EPS (₹20.00) differs from the sum of quarters (₹20.17), and the results note explains the difference only if asked; preparers must not plug Q4 EPS as "annual − 9M".

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Exceptional items: "EPS before exceptional items" is an alternative performance measure — notes only, reconciled (33.73); not on the face.
  • Discontinued operation classification after year-end: not retrospective; EPS split applies from when Ind AS 105 criteria are met.
  • Restatement of comparative quarters for errors (Topic 2, SEBI Reg 33 requires disclosure of restated figures).
  • Two classes of shares (DVR): present EPS for each class with different profit rights.
  • Q4 figures in SEBI results are derived as the balancing figure between audited annual and published 9-month figures — profit, but EPS for Q4 must be computed on Q4 WANS.
  • Negative EPS formatting: show in brackets with "loss per share".

Key takeaways

  • Present basic and diluted EPS for continuing operations and total profit on the face, with equal prominence; discontinued EPS on the face or in notes.
  • Schedule III lines XX–XXII prescribe the three EPS lines; no EPS for TCI.
  • Diluted discontinued EPS uses the potential shares selected on the continuing-operations control number.
  • Reconcile numerators and denominators, disclose antidilutive instruments and post-period share events.
  • SEBI quarterly and YTD EPS are not annualised; quarterly EPS need not add up to annual EPS.
  • Alternative EPS measures are permitted only in notes, reconciled and with equal prominence.

Practice Questions

Q1. Schedule III EPS lines [L1]

Continuing profit attributable to parent ₹84 crore; loss from discontinued operations attributable to parent ₹14 crore; basic WANS 28 crore; diluted WANS (dilutive by continuing control number) 30 crore. Present lines XX–XXII.

Q2. Quarterly result EPS [L2]

For a company with 25 crore shares, a 1:1 bonus on 15 November 2024 (Q3), and quarterly profits Q1 ₹60 crore, Q2 ₹72 crore, Q3 ₹80 crore, Q4 ₹95 crore, prepare the EPS row for the Q3 results announcement (current quarter, preceding quarter, corresponding quarter last year with profit ₹55 crore, 9M current, 9M previous ₹160 crore, previous year ₹230 crore), with the required footnote.

💡 Hint: The bonus affects every comparative column, including those of the previous year.

Q3. Discontinued-operation EPS with dilution [L2]

Continuing profit ₹45 crore; discontinued profit ₹15 crore; basic WANS 15 crore. Convertible bonds: post-tax interest ₹6 crore, 2 crore shares. Determine whether the bonds are dilutive and present basic and diluted EPS for continuing, discontinued and total operations.

Q4. Review of an investor presentation [L3]

An investor deck shows "Annualised EPS ₹42" (Q1 EPS ₹10.5 × 4), "Adjusted EPS before exceptional items ₹12.1" on the same slide as reported EPS, and "Diluted EPS ₹10.9" while basic EPS is ₹10.5. Identify the regulatory and Ind AS 33 issues and recommend corrections.
