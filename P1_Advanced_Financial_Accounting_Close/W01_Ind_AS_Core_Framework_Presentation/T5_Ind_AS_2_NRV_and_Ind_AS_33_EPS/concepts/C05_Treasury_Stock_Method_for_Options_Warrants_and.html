Key concept

Treasury Stock Method for Options, Warrants and ESOPs

Concept: Treasury stock incremental shares = Options × (1 − Exercise price ÷ Average market price)

Statement

For options, warrants and their equivalents (Ind AS 33.45–47A):

\[ \Delta N = N \times \left(1 - \frac{X}{\bar{P}}\right) = N - \frac{N \times X}{\bar{P}} \]

where \(\Delta N\) is the number of incremental shares treated as issued for no consideration, \(N\) the number of shares issuable on exercise, \(X\) the exercise price per share and \(\bar{P}\) the average market price of ordinary shares during the period. For share-based payment arrangements, \(X\) is replaced by the assumed proceeds per option — exercise price plus the fair value of goods or services still to be received, i.e., unrecognised compensation cost per option (Ind AS 33.47A):

\[ \Delta N = N \times \left(1 - \frac{X + UC}{\bar{P}}\right) \]

Options are dilutive only when \(\bar{P} > X\) (or \(X + UC\)); otherwise \(\Delta N\) is taken as zero (antidilutive, excluded).

Explanation

The method assumes the entity receives the exercise proceeds and uses them to buy back its own shares at the average market price. Only the shares not covered by the buy-back are dilutive — they represent shares issued for nothing. Because there is no numerator adjustment, options have an earnings per incremental share of zero and are always the most dilutive class, added first in the sequence (33.44).

Key points:

  • Use the average market price for the period (not the closing price) — for each interim period computed independently; daily or weekly closing averages are typical (A4–A5).
  • Options outstanding for part of the period are weighted for the period outstanding; options exercised during the period are included up to exercise date.
  • Unvested ESOPs with time-based vesting are included from grant date under the treasury method; those with non-market performance conditions follow contingently issuable share rules (33.48).
  • Unrecognised compensation cost is the grant-date fair value not yet expensed under Ind AS 102 (e.g., options with ₹60 fair value, 40% vested → ₹36 UC). Including it reduces dilution and can make in-the-money options antidilutive.
  • Shares held by an ESOP trust are treasury shares for basic EPS; the trust's options to employees still enter diluted EPS.
  • In valuation, the treasury method for fully diluted share count uses the current share price rather than the period average.

Illustration

Indra Software Ltd, FY 2024-25: average market price ₹500.

Tranche Options Exercise price UC per option Assumed proceeds Incremental shares
A (vested) 20 lakh ₹150 0 ₹150 \(20 \times (1 - 150/500) = 14.0\) lakh
B (unvested) 30 lakh ₹300 ₹120 ₹420 \(30 \times (1 - 420/500) = 4.8\) lakh
C (unvested) 10 lakh ₹450 ₹90 ₹540 0 (antidilutive)
Promoter warrants 15 lakh ₹400 — ₹400 \(15 \times (1 - 400/500) = 3.0\) lakh
Total 21.8 lakh

Tranche C is in the money on exercise price alone (₹450 < ₹500) but antidilutive once unrecognised cost is included.

Excel per tranche:

=IF(AvgPrice > ExPrice + UC, Options * (1 - (ExPrice + UC) / AvgPrice), 0)

Connections

Applied within s07 (diluted EPS) and the sequencing in c06–c07. Unrecognised cost comes from the Ind AS 102 expense schedule (topic related concept on ESOPs, Week 3 share-based payments). Bonus/split events adjust \(N\) and \(X\) (s06). For valuation (DCF equity bridge), the same formula with current price gives the fully diluted count — a frequent FP&A and interview topic.

Practice Questions

Q1. Basic treasury computation [L1]

Warrants for 50 lakh shares at ₹120; average market price ₹160; closing price ₹190. Compute incremental shares for diluted EPS and for a valuation share count.

Q2. ESOP tranches with unrecognised cost [L2]

Grant on 1 April 2023 of 40 lakh options, exercise price ₹80, grant-date fair value ₹50, graded vesting 25% per year over 4 years. At 31 March 2025 two tranches have vested. Average market price FY 2024-25 ₹180. Compute incremental shares, showing UC per tranche.

💡 Hint: Under graded vesting, each tranche has its own recognised and unrecognised cost.

Q3. Mid-year grant and exercise [L2]

On 1 October 2024, 12 lakh options (exercise price ₹200, no UC) are granted; 4 lakh earlier options (exercise price ₹100) are exercised on 1 January 2025. Average market price for the year ₹300 (for 1 April–31 December ₹290). Compute the incremental shares for diluted EPS and the effect on basic WANS.
