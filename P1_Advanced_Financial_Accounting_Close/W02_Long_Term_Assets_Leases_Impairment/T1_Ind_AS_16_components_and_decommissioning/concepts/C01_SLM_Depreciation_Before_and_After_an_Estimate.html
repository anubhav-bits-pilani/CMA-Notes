Key concept

SLM Depreciation Before and After an Estimate Revision

Concept: SLM depreciation = (Cost − Residual value) ÷ Useful life; after revision = (Carrying amount − Revised RV) ÷ Remaining useful life

Statement

Under the straight-line method, the depreciable amount is allocated evenly over useful life:

\[ D_{\text{annual}} = \frac{C - RV}{N} \]

where \( C \) is the cost of the asset (or component), \( RV \) is the residual value (current-price estimate of net disposal proceeds at the end of life) and \( N \) is the useful life in years.

After a revision of residual value or useful life at time \( k \) (years elapsed), depreciation is recomputed prospectively:

\[ D_{\text{revised}} = \frac{CA_k - RV'}{N' - k} \]

where \( CA_k = C - k \times D_{\text{annual}} \) (less any impairment) is the carrying amount at the date of revision, \( RV' \) is the revised residual value and \( N' - k \) is the revised remaining useful life.

Explanation

Depreciation begins when the asset is available for use — in the location and condition necessary to operate as intended — and ceases at the earlier of classification as held for sale and derecognition (Ind AS 16.55). SLM is appropriate when benefits are consumed evenly over time; Ind AS 16.62 allows SLM, diminishing balance and units-of-production, and Ind AS 16.62A prohibits revenue-based methods.

The revision formula embodies prospective treatment under Ind AS 8.36: the carrying amount already recognised is not touched; only the remaining depreciable amount is re-spread. Two consequences follow. First, a life extension reduces future depreciation rather than creating a credit to P&L. Second, if the revised residual value exceeds the carrying amount, depreciation is zero until residual value falls below carrying amount (Ind AS 16.54).

Mid-year acquisitions are depreciated pro rata (by days or months). For components, the formula applies to each component separately. After an impairment, the same formula applies with the post-impairment carrying amount (Ind AS 36.63). Under Schedule II, depreciation of assets added or disposed of during the year is on a pro-rata basis from/up to the date of addition/disposal.

Illustration

A CNC machine costs ₹12 crore on 1 April 2022, life 10 years, residual value ₹0.6 crore.

\[ D = \frac{12 - 0.6}{10} = ₹1.14 \text{ crore per year} \]

Carrying amount at 31 March 2025 (3 years) = 12 − 3.42 = ₹8.58 crore.

On 1 April 2025 the total life is revised to 8 years and RV to ₹0.3 crore; remaining life = 8 − 3 = 5 years.

\[ D_{\text{revised}} = \frac{8.58 - 0.3}{5} = ₹1.656 \text{ crore per year} \]

Year Opening CA Depreciation Closing CA
FY26 8.580 1.656 6.924
FY27 6.924 1.656 5.268
FY28 5.268 1.656 3.612
FY29 3.612 1.656 1.956
FY30 1.956 1.656 0.300

Closing carrying amount equals the revised RV — the model check. Excel:

=IF(Year<RevYear,(Cost-RV)/Life,(CA_Rev-RV_Rev)/(Life_Rev-YearsElapsed))
=SLN(CA_Rev, RV_Rev, Life_Rev-YearsElapsed)

Connections

Links to s06 (annual review as estimate change), s02 (component-level application), s03 and s04 (new components after inspections and replacements), and c04 (Schedule II benchmarks for lives and residuals). The same prospective re-spreading applies to ROU assets after remeasurement (t2) and to PPE after impairment (t3). In FP&A models, depreciation forecasts on existing net block use the revision formula by vintage, while new capex uses the original formula.

Practice Questions

Q1. Pro-rata first-year depreciation [L1]

A delivery van costs ₹24 lakh and is put to use on 15 October 2025; life 8 years; residual value 5% of cost. Compute depreciation for FY26 (days basis) and the carrying amount at 31 March 2026.

Q2. Revision after impairment [L2]

Equipment costing ₹50 crore on 1 April 2021 (life 10 years, nil RV) is impaired by ₹6 crore at 31 March 2024. On 1 April 2025, its total life is extended to 12 years and residual value set at ₹2 crore. Compute depreciation for FY25 and FY26.

💡 Hint: Use the post-impairment carrying amount as the base for subsequent depreciation, then apply the revision.

Q3. Model check design [L2]

Write the Excel logic for an asset-level depreciation schedule that (a) applies the original SLM formula until a revision year, (b) applies the revised formula afterwards, and (c) never depreciates below the revised residual value. List two integrity checks you would add.
