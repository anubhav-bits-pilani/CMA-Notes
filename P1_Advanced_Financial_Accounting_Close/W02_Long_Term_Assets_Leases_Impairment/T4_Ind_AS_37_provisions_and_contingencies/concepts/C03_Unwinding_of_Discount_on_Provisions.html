Key concept

Unwinding of Discount on Provisions

Concept: Unwinding = Opening provision × discount rate; Dr Finance cost Cr Provision

Statement

For a provision measured at present value, the carrying amount increases each period to reflect the passage of time (Ind AS 37.60):

\[ U_t = P_{t-1} \times r \]

\[ P_t = P_{t-1} + U_t + A_t - S_t - R_t \pm \Delta_t \]

where \(U_t\) is unwinding in period \(t\), \(P_{t-1}\) the opening provision, \(r\) the pre-tax discount rate used at the start of the period (per period), \(A_t\) new provisions, \(S_t\) amounts utilised, \(R_t\) unused amounts reversed, and \(\Delta_t\) remeasurement for changes in cash-flow estimates or discount rates at the reporting date.

Entry:

Account Dr Cr
Finance costs — unwinding of discount \(U_t\)
Provision \(U_t\)

Explanation

Unwinding is the accounting expression of the time value of money: a ₹100 crore outflow due in 10 years is measured today at its present value, and as settlement approaches the provision accretes to the nominal amount. Ind AS 37.60 calls the increase a borrowing cost; in the statement of profit and loss it is presented within finance costs, not in the expense line where the provision was first charged. This keeps operating expense (and EBITDA) reflecting the original obligation while the financing-like accretion sits below EBIT.

Key conditions:

  • Rate. Use the rate applied at the start of the period (the rate at which the opening balance was measured). The effect of a change in rate at the reporting date is a separate remeasurement, disclosed separately in the roll-forward (37.84(e)).
  • Periodicity. For quarterly reporting under SEBI LODR, compute quarterly unwinding as \(P_{q-1} \times \left[(1+r)^{1/4} - 1\right]\) to avoid overstating with \(r/4\).
  • Mid-period cash flows. If utilisation occurs evenly during the year, the more precise unwinding is on the average balance; most entities use opening balance plus a mid-year adjustment for new provisions — set a policy.
  • Not capitalised. Unwinding is expensed as it occurs; Ind AS 23 capitalisation does not apply (Ind AS 16 Appendix A confirms for decommissioning).
  • Tax. Unwinding increases the carrying amount of a provision deductible only on payment, so the deductible temporary difference and DTA grow.
  • Cash flow statement. Non-cash; added back in the indirect method reconciliation.

Illustration

A telecom tower company's site-restoration provision (not capitalised to assets because the related towers have been fully impaired) is ₹62.0 crore at 1 April 2025, measured at a pre-tax rate of 7.4%. During FY26, sites are restored for ₹5.0 crore (utilisation, assume at year-end). At 31 March 2026 the rate rises to 7.8%, reducing the provision by ₹1.9 crore.

Roll-forward FY26 ₹ crore
Opening 62.00
Unwinding \(62.0 \times 7.4\%\) 4.59
Utilised (5.00)
Effect of change in discount rate (1.90)
Closing 59.69

Entries: Dr Finance costs ₹4.59 crore, Cr Provision; Dr Provision ₹5.00 crore, Cr Bank; Dr Provision ₹1.90 crore, Cr P&L (finance costs or other expenses, per policy).

Connections

Core mechanics for s03 (discounting) and for the provision roll-forward in the topic's related Excel concept. The identical pattern applies to decommissioning provisions in Week 2 Topic 1, lease liabilities in Topic 2 (interest on the liability) and net interest on the defined benefit obligation in Topic 5 — the cross-cutting "unwinding" theme of this week. In 3-statement models, unwinding is a non-cash finance cost: model it from the opening balance to avoid circularity.

Practice Questions

Q1. Three-year unwinding schedule [L1]

A provision of ₹15 crore is payable on 31 March 2029. On 1 April 2025 the pre-tax rate is 7.0%. Required: compute the opening PV, the unwinding for each of FY26–FY29 and the closing balance each year, with entries for FY26.

Q2. Quarterly unwinding for results [L2]

Using a provision of ₹80 crore at 1 April 2025 and an annual pre-tax rate of 8.0%, compute Q1 FY26 unwinding using (a) the simple rate \(r/4\) and (b) the equivalent quarterly rate. Explain which method you would recommend for SEBI quarterly results and why.

💡 Hint: Check that four quarters of your chosen method compound to the annual unwinding.

Q3. Roll-forward with all movements [L2]

Opening provision ₹40 crore (rate 7.2%). New provision recognised on 31 March 2026 ₹6 crore; utilisation ₹3 crore; release of an unused element ₹2.5 crore; effect of rate decrease ₹1.4 crore increase. Required: prepare the Ind AS 37.84 roll-forward and identify the P&L line for each movement.
