Key concept

KMP Compensation: The Five Ind AS 24.17 Categories

Concept: KMP compensation split: short-term, post-employment, other long-term, termination, share-based payment (24.17)

Statement

An entity discloses key management personnel compensation in total and for each of the following categories (Ind AS 24.17):

\[ \text{KMP compensation} = \text{STB} + \text{PEB} + \text{OLTB} + \text{TB} + \text{SBP} \]

where STB is short-term employee benefits, PEB post-employment benefits, OLTB other long-term benefits, TB termination benefits and SBP share-based payment — each measured as defined in Ind AS 19 and Ind AS 102 and including all forms of consideration paid, payable or provided by the entity, or on its behalf, in exchange for services rendered to the entity (24.9 "compensation").

Explanation

"Compensation" covers all employee benefits within Ind AS 19 and share-based payments within Ind AS 102, including consideration paid on behalf of a parent in respect of the entity (24.9). Mapping Indian pay components:

Category Indian components
Short-term employee benefits Salary, allowances, perquisites (car, housing, club), annual performance bonus payable within 12 months, commission to executive directors, sitting fees and commission to non-executive and independent directors, medical benefits, leave availed within 12 months
Post-employment benefits Employer PF, superannuation, NPS contributions; gratuity (service cost or actuarially allocated amount); retiree medical; pension
Other long-term benefits Leave encashment not expected to be settled within 12 months; long-term cash incentive plans; deferred bonuses
Termination benefits Severance, VRS ex-gratia, compensation for loss of office (Sec 202 limits for directors)
Share-based payment Ind AS 102 expense for ESOPs, RSUs and SARs granted to KMP during the vesting period

Practical points:

  • Gratuity and leave on an actuarial basis. Actuaries value the plan as a whole, so the amounts attributable to individual KMP are often "not ascertainable". Many Indian companies state that post-employment and other long-term benefits for KMP are excluded because they are determined for the company as a whole. Better practice is to ask the actuary for a KMP-level allocation (service cost) or disclose contributions paid; if excluded, say so explicitly.
  • Independent directors. Their sitting fees and commission are KMP compensation (they are KMP under Ind AS 24) — the topic pitfall.
  • Accrual basis. Disclose amounts recognised as expense (for example, commission for FY26 payable after AGM approval), not cash paid.
  • Management entity. Fees to a management entity for KMP services are disclosed separately (24.17A), not split into categories.
  • Companies Act interface. Sec 197 limits managerial remuneration (11% of net profits overall; 5% for one MD/WTD, 10% for all; 1% or 3% for non-executive directors); Sec 197(12) requires disclosure of the ratio of each director's remuneration to median employee remuneration in the Board's report; SEBI LODR Schedule V requires director remuneration in the corporate-governance report. Ind AS 24 amounts should reconcile to these.

Practice Questions

Q1. Classify and total [L1]

Classify the following FY26 amounts for the MD into the 24.17 categories and compute the total: basic and allowances ₹3.2 crore; car and housing perquisites ₹0.4 crore; FY26 bonus payable in June 2026 ₹1.1 crore; employer PF ₹0.2 crore; NPS ₹0.1 crore; gratuity service cost allocated by actuary ₹0.12 crore; 3-year cash LTIP accrued ₹0.5 crore; ESOP charge ₹0.9 crore; leave encashed on availing (short-term) ₹0.05 crore.

Q2. Independent directors' pay [L2]

A company's draft KMP note excludes five independent directors who received sitting fees of ₹48 lakh and commission (approved by shareholders under Sec 197) of ₹1.2 crore. Required: explain the error, the category in which these amounts are disclosed and any Sec 197 limits to check.

💡 Hint: Compare the Ind AS 24 KMP definition with Sec 2(51).

Q3. Actuarial allocation [L2]

The actuary declines to allocate gratuity and leave costs to individual KMP. Required: propose two acceptable disclosure approaches and draft the wording for each.
