Subtopic note

Group Mapping for Related Parties: Holding to Promoter Group

Scope: Group mapping: holding, subsidiaries, fellow subsidiaries, associates, JVs and their subsidiaries, promoter-group entities

Why it matters

Indian business groups are layered: a listed flagship with listed and unlisted subsidiaries, step-down subsidiaries abroad, associates and JVs with foreign partners, and a promoter group of holding companies, trusts, LLPs and family entities sitting above. The related-party population for each entity in the group is different, and it is built from one master map. A group map that is current, version-controlled and linked to the ERP's counterparty master is the single most useful control for RPT compliance, consolidation scoping and Ind AS 24 disclosure.

Core notes

Building the group map

  1. Start with the ultimate controlling party. Identify the person(s) or entity that ultimately controls the group (for example, a promoter family through a holding company and trusts). Ind AS 24.13 requires disclosure of the name of the parent and, if different, the ultimate controlling party.
  2. Map the control tree downward. For each entity, record direct and effective ownership, voting rights, and the basis of control (majority voting, Board control, contractual rights). Effective ownership is not the test for control — a 51%-owned subsidiary's 51%-owned subsidiary (26% effective) is still a subsidiary.
  3. Add associates and JVs at every level. Including those of subsidiaries — they are related to every member of the group (24.9(b)(2)) — and their own subsidiaries (24.12).
  4. Add investors with joint control or significant influence over group entities. A 26% foreign partner in a JV is related to the JV; it is not related to the JV's co-venturer merely because of shared joint control (24.11(b)).
  5. Overlay promoter-group and KMP-controlled entities. Entities controlled or jointly controlled by the promoter, KMP or their close family (24.9(b)(6)), and entities over which those persons have significant influence or are KMP (24.9(b)(7)).
  6. Add post-employment plans and management entities.
  7. Record effective dates. When entities join or leave the group, or a relationship changes.

Perspective matters

The map is common, but each entity's related-party list is drawn from its own perspective. In the standalone financial statements of a subsidiary S1:

  • the parent, the ultimate parent, and fellow subsidiaries are related;
  • the parent's associates and JVs are related (associate/JV of a member of the group);
  • S1's own associates and JVs are related;
  • an associate of the parent and a JV of a fellow subsidiary are related to S1, but they are not related to each other unless another test is met.

Promoter group (SEBI ICDR Reg 2(1)(pp))

The promoter group includes the promoter; immediate relatives of the promoter (spouse, parents, brothers, sisters, children and spouse's parents, brothers and sisters); where the promoter is a body corporate, its subsidiary, holding and entities where the promoter holds 20% or more; and where the promoter is an individual, bodies corporate in which 20% or more is held by the promoter, relatives or firms/HUFs in which they are members, and so on. Since April 2022, SEBI LODR Reg 2(1)(zb) makes every person or entity in the promoter or promoter group a related party of the listed entity irrespective of shareholding (s05). For Ind AS 24, promoter-group entities are related only if they meet an Ind AS 24.9 test, but in practice most do, through control by the promoter or close family.

Illustration

Brahmaputra Group: B Holdings Pvt Ltd (owned by the Baruah family trust) holds 58% of B Industries Ltd (listed). B Industries has subsidiaries B Power Ltd (74%) and B Global Pte Ltd (100%, Singapore), which owns B Trading LLC, Dubai (100%). B Industries holds 35% of Assam Gas Ltd (associate); B Power has a 50% JV, BP Solar Ltd, with a European utility. B Holdings also owns 90% of B Realty Ltd. The MD's wife controls Heritage Tea LLP.

In B Power's standalone financial statements: parent — B Industries; ultimate parent — B Holdings (ultimate controlling party — Baruah family trust); fellow subsidiaries — B Global, B Trading, B Realty; associate of a member of the group — Assam Gas; own JV — BP Solar; entity controlled by close family of KMP of the parent (if the MD of B Industries is KMP of the parent) — Heritage Tea LLP; the European utility — related to BP Solar but not to B Power (two venturers, 24.11(b)), unless it has significant influence over B Power.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Structured entities and trusts. Employee welfare trusts and ESOP trusts controlled by the company are subsidiaries (or controlled entities) and related parties.
  • Entities under common control of the promoter, not in the listed group. Related under 24.9(b)(6) as controlled by a person who controls the reporting entity.
  • Investments of 20% without significant influence. If the presumption is rebutted, not an associate; document the rebuttal.
  • Disposals during the year. Transactions up to the date of disposal are RPTs.
  • Insolvency. A subsidiary under CIRP may cease to be controlled (resolution professional takes over); assess control and the related-party status from that date.

Key takeaways

  • The group map starts with the ultimate controlling party and runs through holding, subsidiaries, fellow subsidiaries, associates, JVs and their subsidiaries.
  • Associates and JVs of any group member are related to all group members; two associates of the same investor are not.
  • Promoter-group entities are related under SEBI regardless of holding; under Ind AS 24 only if a 24.9 test is met (usually through control by the promoter or family).
  • Each entity draws its own list from the common map; perspective changes the answer.
  • Build the master with three relationship flags, effective dates and ERP codes.

Practice Questions

Q1. Draw the related-party list from a map [L2]

Ganga Holdings owns 60% of Ganga Cement Ltd (listed), which owns 100% of Ganga RMC Ltd and 45% of Himalaya Logistics Ltd (associate). Ganga Holdings owns 30% of Yamuna Paper Ltd (associate) and 100% of Ganga Hotels Ltd. Himalaya Logistics owns 100% of HL Warehousing Ltd. Required: list the related parties, by Ind AS 24.19 category, in the standalone financial statements of (a) Ganga RMC; (b) Himalaya Logistics; (c) Yamuna Paper.

💡 Hint: Remember 24.12 for subsidiaries of associates, and that two associates of the same investor are not related to each other.

Q2. Promoter group versus Ind AS 24 [L2]

The promoter of a listed company holds 4% of an unlisted textile company; his brother holds 22% and is a director of that company; the promoter's wife's brother owns 100% of a trading firm. Required: determine whether each is a related party of the listed entity under Ind AS 24 and under SEBI Reg 2(1)(zb), using the ICDR promoter-group definition.

Q3. Related-party master design [L3]

Design the related-party master and the monthly process for a listed group with 45 entities in 6 countries, covering data fields, owners, update triggers (MBP-1, KMP joins, acquisitions), ERP linkage and the reports produced for the audit committee and for Ind AS 24.

Q4. Ultimate controlling party disclosure [L1]

A listed company's parent is a holding company 51% owned by a family trust whose trustees are the promoter family. Required: draft the Ind AS 24.13 disclosure of parent and ultimate controlling party and explain what is disclosed if neither produces consolidated financial statements available for public use.
