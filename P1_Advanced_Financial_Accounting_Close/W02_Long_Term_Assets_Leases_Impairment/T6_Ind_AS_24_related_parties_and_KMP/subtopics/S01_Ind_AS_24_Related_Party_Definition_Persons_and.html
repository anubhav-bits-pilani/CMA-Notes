Subtopic note

Ind AS 24 Related Party Definition: Persons and Entities

Scope: Related party definition (Ind AS 24.9): control, joint control, significant influence, KMP and close family, post-employment plans, management-service entities

Why it matters

Every Ind AS 24 disclosure, and much of the audit work under SA 550, depends on getting the population right. The definition is mechanical but wide: it reaches through family members, through entities controlled by family members, through post-employment plans and through management-service companies. A related party missed at the identification stage means transactions are not captured, approvals are not sought and disclosures are incomplete. For a CFO's office, the related-party master is a controlled document updated from director declarations (MBP-1), KMP declarations, group structure changes and shareholder data.

Core notes

The definition (Ind AS 24.9)

A related party is a person or entity that is related to the entity preparing its financial statements (the reporting entity).

(a) A person or a close member of that person's family is related to a reporting entity if that person:

  1. has control or joint control over the reporting entity;
  2. has significant influence over the reporting entity; or
  3. is a member of the key management personnel of the reporting entity or of a parent of the reporting entity.

(b) An entity is related to a reporting entity if any of the following conditions applies:

  1. The entity and the reporting entity are members of the same group (each parent, subsidiary and fellow subsidiary is related to the others).
  2. One entity is an associate or joint venture of the other (or an associate or JV of a member of a group of which the other entity is a member).
  3. Both entities are joint ventures of the same third party.
  4. One entity is a joint venture of a third entity and the other is an associate of the third entity.
  5. The entity is a post-employment benefit plan for the benefit of employees of either the reporting entity or an entity related to the reporting entity. If the reporting entity is itself such a plan, the sponsoring employers are also related to it.
  6. The entity is controlled or jointly controlled by a person identified in (a).
  7. A person identified in (a)(1) has significant influence over the entity or is a member of the KMP of the entity (or of a parent of the entity).
  8. The entity, or any member of a group of which it is a part, provides key management personnel services to the reporting entity or to the parent of the reporting entity (a "management entity").

For the definition, an associate includes subsidiaries of the associate and a joint venture includes subsidiaries of the joint venture (24.12).

Close members of the family (24.9)

Close members of the family of a person are those family members who may be expected to influence, or be influenced by, that person in their dealings with the entity, including:

  • that person's children and spouse or domestic partner (Ind AS 24 also explicitly lists brother, sister, father and mother — an Indian expansion of the IAS 24 list);
  • children of that person's spouse or domestic partner; and
  • dependants of that person or that person's spouse or domestic partner.

The list is inclusive: other relatives (for example, a son-in-law who runs the family business with the promoter) may be close family if they may be expected to influence or be influenced.

Key terms

  • Control, joint control, significant influence: as defined in Ind AS 110, Ind AS 111 and Ind AS 28 (24.9).
  • Key management personnel: persons having authority and responsibility for planning, directing and controlling the activities of the entity, directly or indirectly, including any director (whether executive or otherwise) of that entity (s02).
  • Related party transaction: a transfer of resources, services or obligations between a reporting entity and a related party, regardless of whether a price is charged.

Reading the entity tests — symmetric relationships

The definition is designed to be symmetric: if A is related to B in B's financial statements, B is related to A in A's financial statements (with some exceptions for significant influence by persons). Practical applications:

Situation Related? Test
Subsidiary S1 and fellow subsidiary S2 Yes (b)(1)
Parent P and its associate A Yes (b)(2)
Subsidiary S1 and the parent's associate A Yes (b)(2) — associate of a member of the group
Two associates of the same investor No Not listed; two associates are not related to each other (24.11 implication)
JV J1 and associate A1 of the same investor Yes (b)(4)
Two JVs of the same investor Yes (b)(3)
Company and its gratuity trust Yes (b)(5)
Company and a firm owned by the MD's wife Yes (b)(6) — controlled by close family of KMP
Company X and entity Y where X's salaried MD (no shareholding) is also a director of Y No, merely a common KMP (24.11(a)) —
Company X and entity Y where X's controlling promoter is a director (KMP) of Y Yes (b)(7) — a person in (a)(1) is KMP of Y
Company and an outsourced CFO services firm Yes, management entity (b)(8)

What is not a related party (24.11)

  • Two entities simply because they have a director or other KMP in common, or because a KMP of one has significant influence over the other.
  • Two venturers simply because they share joint control of a JV.
  • Providers of finance, trade unions, public utilities, departments and agencies of a government that does not control, jointly control or significantly influence the entity — simply by virtue of normal dealings (even if they affect freedom of action or participate in decision-making).
  • A customer, supplier, franchisor, distributor or general agent with whom an entity transacts a significant volume of business, simply by virtue of the resulting economic dependence.

Worked example

Godavari Foods Ltd is 55% owned by Godavari Holdings Pvt Ltd, which is wholly owned by Mr R (promoter). Mr R's daughter S is a non-executive director of Godavari Foods. S's husband owns 100% of Spice Route LLP. Godavari Foods holds 26% of Agri Coldchain Ltd (associate). Godavari Holdings also owns 60% of Godavari Realty Ltd. The CFO's brother owns 15% of Nidhi Packaging Pvt Ltd (no board seat). Godavari Foods sponsors the Godavari Foods Employees' Gratuity Trust.

Party Related to Godavari Foods? Basis
Godavari Holdings Yes — parent (b)(1)
Mr R Yes — controls the parent, hence controls the entity (a)(1)
S Yes — KMP (non-executive director); also close family of Mr R (a)(3)
Spice Route LLP Yes — controlled by close family (spouse) of KMP S (b)(6)
Agri Coldchain Yes — associate (b)(2)
Godavari Realty Yes — fellow subsidiary (b)(1)
Nidhi Packaging No — the CFO's brother has neither control nor joint control (15%, no board seat); test (b)(7) needs a person in (a)(1) —
Gratuity trust Yes — post-employment plan (b)(5)

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Promoters without control. A promoter holding 18% with board nomination rights may have significant influence; their entities are related under (b)(7) only where the person in (a)(1) has significant influence over, or is KMP of, the other entity.
  • Close family judgement. Brothers-in-law, uncles and cousins are not automatically close family; apply the "influence or be influenced" test and document it.
  • Changes during the year. A party that became or ceased to be related during the year: disclose transactions for the period of the relationship.
  • Government-related entities. Relationships with a controlling government and other entities it controls are related parties, but a partial exemption applies (s07).
  • Trusts and HUFs. Control of a trust or HUF by the promoter makes it an entity controlled by a person in (a).

Key takeaways

  • Ind AS 24.9 has a persons limb (control, joint control, significant influence, KMP — plus close family) and an entity limb (group, associates/JVs, common JVs, plans, entities controlled by persons, management entities).
  • Close family includes spouse/partner, children, spouse's children, dependants and, in Ind AS, parents and siblings.
  • Associates and JVs include their subsidiaries; two associates of the same investor are not related to each other.
  • Post-employment benefit plans and management-service entities are related parties.
  • Common directors or economic dependence alone do not create a relationship (24.11).

Practice Questions

Q1. Apply the entity tests [L1]

Holding H has subsidiaries S1 (80%) and S2 (100%); S1 has a 40% associate A1; S2 has a 50% JV J1 with an outsider; H has a 25% associate A2. Required: for each pair — (S1, S2), (S2, A1), (A1, A2), (J1, A2), (H, J1), (A1, J1) — state whether they are related in each other's financial statements and cite the Ind AS 24.9 test.

Q2. Family relationships [L2]

The MD of a listed company has: a wife who owns 40% of a catering company (with joint control under a shareholders' agreement); a married daughter living separately who owns 100% of a travel agency; a brother-in-law who owns 100% of a transport company and is financially independent; and a dependent mother-in-law. Required: identify which entities are related parties under Ind AS 24 and explain the judgement for each.

💡 Hint: Separate the "close family" question from the "control of the entity" question.

Q3. Not related parties [L1]

Explain, with reasons from Ind AS 24.11, whether the following are related parties of a cement company: (a) its main lender, a public sector bank, which has a nominee director under a loan agreement; (b) a dealer that buys 40% of output; (c) another company where the cement company's independent director is also an independent director; (d) the trade union with which it has a wage settlement.

Q4. Management entity [L2]

A listed NBFC outsources its CFO and Company Secretary functions to a professional services firm under a contract for ₹2.4 crore a year. Required: explain the related-party status of the firm, what Ind AS 24.17A requires to be disclosed, and whether the individuals who perform the roles are also KMP.
