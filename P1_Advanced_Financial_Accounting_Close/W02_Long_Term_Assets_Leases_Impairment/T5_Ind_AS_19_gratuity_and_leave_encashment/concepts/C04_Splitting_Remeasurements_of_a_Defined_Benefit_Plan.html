Key concept

Splitting Remeasurements of a Defined Benefit Plan

Concept: Remeasurement split: demographic assumptions, financial assumptions, experience adjustments, return on plan assets excluding interest

Statement

\[ \text{Remeasurement} = \underbrace{\text{AGL}_{\text{demo}} + \text{AGL}_{\text{fin}} + \text{AGL}_{\text{exp}}}_{\text{actuarial (gains)/losses on DBO}} - \underbrace{\left(R_{\text{actual}} - \text{II}\right)}_{\text{return on plan assets excl. interest}} + \Delta \text{Ceiling}_{\text{excl. interest}} \]

where \(\text{AGL}_{\text{demo}}\) is the effect on the DBO of changes in demographic assumptions (attrition, mortality, retirement age, disability); \(\text{AGL}_{\text{fin}}\) the effect of changes in financial assumptions (discount rate, salary escalation, benefit inflation); \(\text{AGL}_{\text{exp}}\) experience adjustments (actual outcomes differing from previous assumptions); \(R_{\text{actual}}\) actual return on plan assets net of plan administration costs; \(\text{II}\) interest income at the discount rate; and \(\Delta \text{Ceiling}\) the change in the effect of the asset ceiling other than interest (Ind AS 19.127–130). A positive total is a loss.

Explanation

Ind AS 19.141(c) requires the DBO reconciliation to show separately actuarial gains and losses from changes in demographic assumptions, changes in financial assumptions and experience adjustments, plus the return on plan assets excluding interest and changes in the asset-ceiling effect. The split explains why the OCI number arose and whether it is likely to recur.

How actuaries derive the split (sequential method):

  1. Roll forward the opening DBO with expected flows using opening assumptions → expected closing DBO.
  2. Measure closing DBO with actual closing data but opening assumptions → the difference from step 1 is the experience adjustment (salary increases higher than assumed, more or fewer leavers, data corrections).
  3. Remeasure with new demographic assumptions (keeping opening financial assumptions) → difference is the demographic effect.
  4. Remeasure with new financial assumptions → difference is the financial effect, giving the final closing DBO.

The order affects the attribution slightly; actuaries apply a consistent order.

What drives each component in Indian plans:

Component Typical cause Direction
Financial — discount rate G-sec yields fall (e.g., 7.2% → 6.7%) Loss
Financial — salary escalation Assumption raised (e.g., 7% → 8%) Loss
Demographic — attrition Higher attrition assumed for young staff Gain (usually)
Demographic — mortality Table changed (e.g., to IALM 2012–14) Small
Experience Actual increments above assumption; lower actual attrition Loss
Return on assets excl. interest Fund credited less than discount rate Loss

Excluded from remeasurements: past service cost and settlements (service cost), interest components, and changes in DBO from benefits paid.

Leave encashment (other long-term) has the same components, but all go to P&L (19.156). For post-employment plans the total goes to OCI and is never recycled (c06).

Illustration

Opening DBO ₹4,000 lakh at 7.1%; CSC 380; interest 276; benefits paid 300; expected closing = 4,356.

Step DBO (₹ lakh) Component
Expected closing (opening assumptions) 4,356
Actual data, opening assumptions 4,431 Experience loss 75
New attrition table 4,396 Demographic gain (35)
New discount rate 6.7% and salary 8% 4,560 Financial loss 164
Closing DBO 4,560 Total actuarial loss 204

Plan assets: interest income at 7.1% ₹213 lakh; actual return ₹190 lakh → return excluding interest −₹23 lakh (loss).

Total OCI remeasurement loss = 204 + 23 = ₹227 lakh; deferred tax credit in OCI at 25.168% = ₹57.1 lakh; net OCI loss ₹169.9 lakh.

Practice Questions

Q1. Attribute the actuarial loss [L1]

An actuary reports: expected closing DBO ₹2,100 lakh; closing DBO at actual data and opening assumptions ₹2,140 lakh; after new attrition ₹2,120 lakh; after new discount rate and salary escalation ₹2,230 lakh. Plan assets: interest income ₹95 lakh, actual return ₹110 lakh. Required: split the remeasurement into its four components and compute total OCI.

Q2. Plausibility check [L2]

A plan with DBO ₹60 crore and duration 8 years shows a financial-assumption loss of ₹0.4 crore. The discount rate moved from 7.15% to 6.70% and salary escalation was unchanged. Required: estimate the expected financial loss and comment on the reported figure.

💡 Hint: Use modified duration as a first approximation.

Q3. Experience losses every year [L2]

A company reports experience losses of 3–4% of DBO for five consecutive years, while demographic and financial components alternate. Required: explain what the pattern suggests, which assumption is likely biased and how Ind AS 19.75–77 applies.
