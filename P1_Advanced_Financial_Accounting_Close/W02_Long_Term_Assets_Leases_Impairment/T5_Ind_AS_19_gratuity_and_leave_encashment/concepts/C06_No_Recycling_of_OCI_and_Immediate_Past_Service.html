Key concept

No Recycling of OCI and Immediate Past Service Cost

Concept: OCI remeasurements never recycled (19.122); past service cost from plan amendment recognised immediately in P&L (19.103)

Statement

  1. No recycling (Ind AS 19.122). Remeasurements of the net defined benefit liability (asset) recognised in OCI shall not be reclassified to profit or loss in a subsequent period. The entity may, however, transfer those amounts recognised in OCI within equity (for example, to retained earnings).
  2. Immediate past service cost (Ind AS 19.103). Past service cost is recognised as an expense at the earlier of: (a) when the plan amendment or curtailment occurs; and (b) when the entity recognises related restructuring costs (Ind AS 37) or termination benefits (19.165). There is no deferral over vesting or remaining service.

\[ \text{PSC} = \text{DBO}_{\text{after amendment}} - \text{DBO}_{\text{before amendment}} \]

both measured at the amendment date using current fair value of plan assets and current actuarial assumptions (19.99).

Explanation

Why no recycling. The 2011 revision of IAS 19 (carried into Ind AS 19) removed the corridor and deferred-recognition options and decided that remeasurements, being driven by changes in market conditions and assumptions, are not part of the entity's performance in any period. They therefore never pass through P&L. In Indian financial statements they appear in "Items that will not be reclassified to profit or loss", and many companies transfer the cumulative balance to retained earnings each year (Schedule III "Other equity" allows either retaining in an OCI reserve or transferring).

Consequences:

  • A plan that is settled or curtailed does not release accumulated OCI to P&L.
  • On disposal of a subsidiary, accumulated DB remeasurements are transferred within equity, not recycled (contrast with foreign currency translation reserve).
  • Profit-based metrics (EPS, ROE on profit) never include DB remeasurements; analysts using comprehensive income see them.

Why immediate PSC. A plan amendment changes the benefit promised for past service. Since the service has already been rendered, the cost belongs to the period of the amendment. Under old IAS 19 (and US GAAP ASC 715), unvested PSC was spread over the vesting period or remaining service; Ind AS 19 removed this.

Plan amendment vs actuarial change:

Change Treatment
Statutory formula changes (labour code wage definition, fixed-term gratuity) Plan amendment → PSC in P&L
Increase in statutory ceiling (e.g., ₹10 lakh → ₹20 lakh in 2018) Plan amendment → PSC in P&L
Company improves its own scheme (uncapped gratuity) Plan amendment → PSC in P&L
Change in discount rate, salary escalation, attrition Actuarial assumption → OCI
Curtailment (closure reducing employees significantly) PSC (negative, usually) in P&L
Settlement (bulk buy-out with insurer) Settlement gain/loss in P&L

Before determining PSC or a settlement gain/loss, the entity remeasures the net defined benefit liability using current assumptions (19.99), and service cost and net interest for the remainder of the period use the updated assumptions (19.122A, 19.123A).

Illustration

The Code on Social Security 2020 takes effect on 21 November 2025. A company's actuary measures at that date (₹ crore): DBO under old formula, at 21 November assumptions, 62.0; DBO under new formula, same assumptions, 71.5. PSC = ₹9.5 crore, recognised in P&L in Q3 FY26 and presented as an exceptional item. At 31 March 2026 a fall in the discount rate adds ₹1.8 crore — an OCI remeasurement.

In FY28, the company transfers the cumulative OCI remeasurement reserve of ₹6.4 crore to retained earnings: Dr Other equity — remeasurement of DB plans ₹6.4 crore, Cr Retained earnings ₹6.4 crore. No P&L effect ever arises.

Connections

Underpins the P&L/OCI split in s05 and the labour-code analysis in s02. Contrasts with US GAAP ASC 715 (corridor amortisation and deferred prior service cost, listed in topic related concepts) and with Ind AS 21 translation reserves, which are recycled. For other long-term benefits (s07, leave encashment), the no-recycling question does not arise because nothing goes to OCI.

Practice Questions

Q1. Classify the change [L1]

For each, state whether the effect is past service cost (P&L) or a remeasurement (OCI): (a) the company raises its gratuity cap from ₹20 lakh to uncapped for managers; (b) G-sec yields fall 40 bp; (c) the Code extends gratuity to fixed-term employees; (d) attrition assumption for under-30s raised from 12% to 18%; (e) 30% of the workforce is retrenched on closure of a plant.

Q2. Measuring PSC at the amendment date [L2]

At 1 April 2025, the DBO was ₹40 crore at a discount rate of 7.0%. On 21 November 2025, the rate was 6.8%. The actuary reports: DBO at 21 November under old terms and 6.8% = ₹43.1 crore; under new terms and 6.8% = ₹48.0 crore. Required: compute PSC, explain why the old-terms DBO is remeasured at 6.8% first, and identify where the ₹(43.1 − expected) difference goes.

💡 Hint: Separate the remeasurement before the amendment from the amendment itself.

Q3. Disposal of a subsidiary [L2]

A group disposes of a subsidiary whose accumulated DB remeasurement losses in group OCI total ₹3.2 crore and whose accumulated foreign currency translation reserve is a gain of ₹1.4 crore. Required: explain the treatment of each reserve on disposal and the effect on the group's gain on disposal.
