Key concept

Defined Benefit Obligation Roll-Forward

Concept: Closing DBO = Opening DBO + Current service cost + Interest cost + Past service cost − Benefits paid ± Actuarial (gain)/loss

Statement

\[ \text{DBO}_{\text{close}} = \text{DBO}_{\text{open}} + \text{CSC} + \text{IC} + \text{PSC} - \text{BP} + \text{AL} \]

where \(\text{DBO}_{\text{open}}\) is the present value of the obligation at the start of the year (measured at the prior reporting date's assumptions); \(\text{CSC}\) is current service cost; \(\text{IC}\) is interest cost on the DBO; \(\text{PSC}\) is past service cost from plan amendments or curtailments (including settlements' effect where relevant); \(\text{BP}\) is benefits paid; and \(\text{AL}\) is the net actuarial loss (positive) or gain (negative), being the balancing item between the expected closing DBO and the actuarially measured closing DBO. Other lines that may appear: business combinations, transfers in/out, settlements and exchange differences.

Explanation

The roll-forward is required by Ind AS 19.140–141 and is the backbone of the actuarial report and of the note. Each element has a defined source and destination:

Line Source Destination
CSC PUCM using start-of-year assumptions (19.122A) P&L (service cost)
Interest cost Start-of-year discount rate × DBO, adjusted for in-year benefit payments P&L (net interest)
PSC Plan amendment / curtailment (19.102–106) P&L immediately (19.103)
Benefits paid Payroll / insurer records Reduces DBO (and plan assets if paid by the fund)
Actuarial (gain)/loss Balancing figure; split into demographic, financial and experience OCI for post-employment; P&L for other long-term

The actuarial gain or loss is determined last: the actuary computes the "expected" closing DBO from the opening position and the year's flows, then measures the actual closing DBO with updated data and assumptions; the difference is the remeasurement, which the actuary attributes to sources (c04).

Why rebuild it yourself: the roll-forward lets you verify that the opening equals last year's closing, that interest cost approximates opening DBO × opening discount rate, that benefits paid agree to records, and that the actuarial loss is plausible given rate movements (duration × change in rate) and salary experience. It also provides the numbers for the journal entries and the Ind AS 12 deferred tax split.

In a model, the DBO roll-forward sits alongside the plan-asset roll-forward:

\[ \text{FVPA}_{\text{close}} = \text{FVPA}_{\text{open}} + \text{II} + \text{C} - \text{BP}_{\text{fund}} + \text{RPA} \]

with \(\text{II}\) interest income at the discount rate, \(\text{C}\) contributions, \(\text{BP}_{\text{fund}}\) benefits paid from the fund and \(\text{RPA}\) return on plan assets excluding interest.

Illustration

FY26 data (₹ lakh): opening DBO 3,600; discount rate at 1 April 2025 7.0%; CSC 340; benefits paid 300 evenly through the year; labour code PSC on 21 November 2025 of 280; closing DBO measured by the actuary at 31 March 2026 4,210.

Interest cost (approximate, mid-year convention for benefits, ignoring the remeasurement of interest after the amendment for simplicity) = \(3{,}600 \times 7\% - 300 \times 7\% \times 0.5 = 252.0 - 10.5 = 241.5\).

Expected closing before remeasurement = 3,600 + 340 + 241.5 + 280 − 300 = 4,161.5. Actuarial loss = 4,210 − 4,161.5 = 48.5 (to OCI).

Practice Questions

Q1. Derive the actuarial gain or loss [L1]

Opening DBO ₹2,750 lakh; CSC ₹260 lakh; discount rate at start 7.1%; benefits paid ₹190 lakh on 30 September; no PSC; closing DBO per actuary ₹2,960 lakh. Required: compute interest cost (time-weight the benefits paid) and the actuarial gain or loss, and state where it is recognised.

Q2. Rebuild and challenge [L2]

An actuarial report shows interest cost of ₹318 lakh on an opening DBO of ₹3,900 lakh with a start-of-year discount rate of 7.2% and benefits paid of ₹260 lakh spread evenly. Required: recompute interest cost, quantify the difference and list three explanations you would ask the actuary to confirm.

💡 Hint: Consider mid-year events such as acquisitions or plan amendments that trigger remeasurement.

Q3. Model build [L3]

Design an Excel tab that rebuilds a five-year history of the gratuity DBO and plan-asset roll-forwards from actuarial reports, with checks against the balance sheet, P&L and OCI each year, and a variance analysis of actuarial gains/losses against discount-rate movements and duration.
