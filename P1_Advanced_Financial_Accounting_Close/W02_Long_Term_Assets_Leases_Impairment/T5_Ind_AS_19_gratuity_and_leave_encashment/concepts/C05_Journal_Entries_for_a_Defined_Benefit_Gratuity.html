Key concept

Journal Entries for a Defined Benefit Gratuity Plan

Concept: Entry: Dr Employee benefit expense (CSC + net interest) Dr/Cr OCI Cr Net DB liability; contribution Dr Net DB liability Cr Bank

Statement

For a post-employment defined benefit plan, the year's entries are:

# Entry Dr Cr
1 P&L cost Employee benefits expense (CSC + PSC + net interest) Net defined benefit liability
2 Remeasurement loss (or reverse for gain) OCI — remeasurement of defined benefit plans Net defined benefit liability
3 Employer contribution to fund Net defined benefit liability Bank
4 Benefits paid directly by employer (unfunded portion) Net defined benefit liability Bank
5 Deferred tax on P&L element Deferred tax asset Deferred tax credit (P&L)
6 Deferred tax on OCI element Deferred tax asset OCI — income tax on items not reclassified

Benefits paid by the fund (LIC) reduce both DBO and plan assets and require no entry in the company's books.

\[ \text{NDBL}_{\text{close}} = \text{NDBL}_{\text{open}} + \text{P\&L cost} + \text{OCI remeasurement} - \text{Contributions} - \text{Direct benefit payments} \]

where NDBL is the net defined benefit liability (DBO less fair value of plan assets, adjusted for the asset ceiling).

Explanation

The company carries one net balance — the net defined benefit liability (or asset) — rather than separate DBO and plan-asset accounts, because Ind AS 19.63 requires recognition of the net deficit or surplus. Many ERP set-ups nonetheless keep two sub-ledgers ("Gratuity obligation" and "Gratuity fund with LIC") to aid reconciliation; they are netted in the balance sheet. Under Schedule III the net liability is a provision for employee benefits (current and non-current); a net asset (after ceiling) is presented in other assets.

Practicalities:

  • Monthly/quarterly accruals. Many companies accrue one-twelfth of the budgeted annual P&L cost each month and true up at year-end to the actuarial report.
  • Contributions are typically paid on LIC's demand; under Sec 43B they are deductible only when paid, often before the return filing date.
  • Capitalisation. The service-cost portion relating to employees on capital projects may be capitalised (Ind AS 16.17); remeasurements never.
  • Past service cost (e.g., labour code) is charged in entry 1 but often presented separately as an exceptional item.
  • Deferred tax. Allocate the DTA movement between P&L and OCI in proportion to the source of the movement (Ind AS 12.61A, 12.63 allows reasonable pro rata allocation).

Illustration

From an actuarial report (₹ lakh): opening net liability 1,300 (DBO 5,200; assets 3,900). FY26: CSC 520; net interest 92; remeasurement loss 213; contributions 600; all benefits paid by LIC. Closing net liability 1,525. Tax rate 25.168%.

Entry Dr (₹ lakh) Cr (₹ lakh)
Employee benefits expense — gratuity 612.0
Net DB liability — gratuity 612.0
OCI — remeasurement of defined benefit plans 213.0
Net DB liability — gratuity 213.0
Net DB liability — gratuity 600.0
Bank 600.0
Deferred tax asset 56.6
Deferred tax (P&L) 3.0
OCI — income tax on remeasurements 53.6

Deferred tax: closing DTA = 1,525 × 25.168% = 383.8; opening DTA = 1,300 × 25.168% = 327.2; movement 56.6. OCI portion = 213 × 25.168% = 53.6; P&L portion = 56.6 − 53.6 = 3.0 (equivalently, (612 − 600) × 25.168%).

Check: 1,300 + 612 + 213 − 600 = 1,525 ✓.

Q1. Post the year-end entries [L1]

FY26 report: CSC ₹150 lakh; net interest ₹55 lakh; remeasurement gain ₹(38) lakh; contributions ₹200 lakh; benefits paid directly by the company (not from fund) ₹25 lakh; benefits paid by LIC ₹90 lakh. Tax rate 25.168%. Required: pass all entries including deferred tax and verify the closing net liability.

Q2. Correcting an LIC-premium-only approach [L2]

A company has booked "Gratuity expense — LIC premium ₹3.2 crore, Dr Expense Cr Bank" and nothing else for FY26. The actuarial report shows opening net liability ₹2.1 crore, CSC ₹2.0 crore, net interest ₹0.15 crore, remeasurement loss ₹0.6 crore, closing net liability ₹1.65 crore. Required: pass the correcting entries and state the effect on profit before tax and OCI.

💡 Hint: Start by reconstructing what the net liability account should look like, then reverse the expense.

Q3. Net asset presentation [L2]
