Key concept

Initial Measurement of the ROU Asset (Ind AS 116.24)

Concept: ROU at commencement = Lease liability + Prepaid payments − Lease incentives + Initial direct costs + Restoration estimate (116.24)

Statement

\[ ROU_0 = LL_0 + PP - LI + IDC + RE \]

where \( ROU_0 \) is the cost of the right-of-use asset at commencement, \( LL_0 \) is the initial lease liability (c01), \( PP \) is lease payments made at or before commencement (including the first advance instalment and the prepaid-rent element of an interest-free deposit), \( LI \) is lease incentives received, \( IDC \) is initial direct costs incurred by the lessee, and \( RE \) is the estimate of costs to dismantle and remove the underlying asset, restore the site or restore the asset to the condition required by the lease (measured under Ind AS 37), unless incurred to produce inventories (Ind AS 116.24(a)–(d)).

Explanation

The ROU is measured at cost, built from the liability plus everything else the lessee has paid or committed for the right of use:

  • Prepaid payments: advance rent paid on commencement, upfront premiums on leasehold land, and the difference between face value and fair value of interest-free deposits (s06).
  • Lease incentives are payments by the lessor to the lessee associated with the lease, or reimbursement/assumption of the lessee's costs (e.g. fit-out contribution, relocation reimbursement). Incentives received reduce ROU; incentives receivable reduce lease payments (and therefore the liability) instead. Rent-free periods are captured in the payment vector, not as a separate incentive.
  • Initial direct costs are incremental costs of obtaining a lease that would not have been incurred had the lease not been obtained — brokerage, stamp duty and registration charges on a lease deed. Legal review fees and internal staff costs that would be incurred anyway are not IDC.
  • Restoration estimate relates to obligations under the lease (Ind AS 37 provision recognised simultaneously); restoration of the lessee's own fit-outs belongs to the cost of those fit-outs under Ind AS 16 (t1 s05).

Subsequently, ROU is measured at cost less accumulated depreciation and impairment, adjusted for remeasurements (Ind AS 116.30), unless the revaluation model is applied to a class of PPE or the ROU meets the investment property definition (Ind AS 116.34–35).

Connections

Builds on c01 (liability), s06 (deposits), s04 (incentives and rent-free periods), t1 s05 (restoration under Ind AS 37). Depreciation of the ROU (c04) uses this cost over the shorter of lease term and useful life. Remeasurements (c05, c06) adjust it. ROU enters CGU carrying amounts for impairment (t3). Deferred tax under the 2023 Ind AS 12 amendment arises on the full ROU (Week 3).

Practice Questions

Q1. Compute ROU [L1]

Lease liability ₹1,250 lakh; advance rent paid on commencement ₹18 lakh; lessor reimbursed relocation costs ₹25 lakh; legal fees for internal review ₹3 lakh; stamp duty ₹6 lakh; restoration PV ₹12 lakh. Compute the ROU and pass the commencement entry.

Q2. Incentive received vs receivable [L2]

A lessor agrees to contribute ₹40 lakh towards fit-outs, payable to the lessee 6 months after commencement. Explain whether this reduces the ROU or the lease liability, and how the treatment differs if the amount is received on commencement.

💡 Hint: Ind AS 116.24(c) refers to incentives received; amounts receivable are reflected in lease payments under 116.27(a).

Q3. Leasehold land premium [L2]

A company pays a ₹20 crore upfront premium for 99-year leasehold industrial land plus ₹2 lakh annual rent (IBR 9%), with stamp duty of ₹1.2 crore. Compute the ROU and liability at commencement and the annual depreciation, and state the presentation under Schedule III.
