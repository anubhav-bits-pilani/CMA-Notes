Key concept

Core Lessee Journal Entries: Recognition, Depreciation, Interest

Concept: Entries: Dr ROU Cr Lease liability; Dr Depreciation Cr ROU (SLM over shorter of term and useful life); Dr Finance cost Cr Lease liability

Statement

Depreciation period: if ownership transfers by the end of the lease term, or the ROU cost reflects a reasonably certain purchase option, depreciate from commencement to the end of the useful life of the underlying asset; otherwise to the earlier of the end of useful life of the ROU and the end of the lease term (Ind AS 116.31–32).

Explanation

Ind AS 116 uses a single lessee model: every non-exempt lease produces an asset (depreciated under Ind AS 16 principles, Ind AS 116.31) and a financial liability (interest at a constant rate, Ind AS 116.36–37). Depreciation is usually straight-line, reflecting even consumption of the right of use; other methods are allowed if they reflect the pattern of consumption. Impairment is tested under Ind AS 36 (Ind AS 116.33).

Presentation (Ind AS 116.47–50, Schedule III):

  • ROU assets presented separately or within PPE with disclosure of the line items; Schedule III (2021 amendment) PPE note includes ROU as a class or a separate note.
  • Lease liabilities presented separately within financial liabilities (current and non-current) — Schedule III has explicit "Lease liabilities" lines.
  • Interest on lease liabilities is part of finance costs; depreciation is part of depreciation and amortisation — both below EBITDA.
  • Cash flows: principal in financing; interest per Ind AS 7 policy; exempt and variable payments in operating.

The combined P&L cost (depreciation + interest) is higher in early years and lower in later years than straight-line rent — the "front-loading" effect — though total cost over the lease equals total payments plus IDC/restoration minus incentives.

Practice Questions

Q1. Entries for an advance-payment lease [L1]

A 3-year lease has annual payments of ₹12 lakh in advance; IBR 10%. Compute the initial liability and ROU, and pass all entries for year 1.

Q2. Depreciation period with purchase option [L2]

A company leases a machine (useful life 12 years) for 6 years with a purchase option at the end at ₹1 lakh when expected fair value is ₹40 lakh. Determine the depreciation period of the ROU and explain why.

💡 Hint: A reasonably certain purchase option changes the depreciation period to the asset's useful life.

Q3. P&L and EBITDA comparison [L2]

For a portfolio with total ROU ₹600 crore (average remaining term 6 years), total lease liability ₹640 crore (average IBR 9%), and annual fixed rent ₹140 crore, estimate year-1 depreciation, interest, EBITDA uplift and PBT impact versus straight-line rent, and explain the direction of change in later years.
