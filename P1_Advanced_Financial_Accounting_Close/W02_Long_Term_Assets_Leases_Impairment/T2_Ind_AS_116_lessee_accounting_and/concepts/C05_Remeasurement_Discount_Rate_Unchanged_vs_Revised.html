Key concept

Remeasurement Discount Rate: Unchanged vs Revised

Concept: Remeasure at unchanged rate for index/rate changes and RVGs; at revised rate for lease term or purchase option changes (116.40–43)

Statement

On reassessment, the remeasured liability is:

\[ LL' = \sum_{k} \frac{P'_k}{(1+r^{*})^{t_k}}, \qquad \Delta ROU = LL' - LL_{\text{before}} \]

where \( P'_k \) are the revised remaining lease payments and \( r^{*} \) is:

  • the original discount rate if the change arises from (a) amounts expected to be payable under a residual value guarantee, or (b) a change in an index or rate used to determine payments, when the cash-flow change takes effect (Ind AS 116.42–43) — except changes in floating interest rates, which use a revised rate reflecting the change;
  • a revised discount rate (implicit rate for the remainder of the term if readily determinable, otherwise IBR at the reassessment date) if there is (a) a change in the lease term or (b) a change in the assessment of a purchase option (Ind AS 116.40–41).

The adjustment \( \Delta ROU \) is recognised against the ROU asset; if ROU is reduced to zero, any remaining reduction goes to P&L (Ind AS 116.39).

Explanation

The logic follows the nature of the change. Index-linked and RVG changes are updates of cash flows within the original bargain; the economics of the financing are unchanged, so the original rate continues — just as a floating-rate loan's margin stays the same. A floating-rate change is itself a change in the rate, so a revised rate reflects it. A change in lease term or purchase option is effectively a new financing decision about a different period or a different asset ownership outcome, so current market rates apply.

Timing of index changes: remeasure only when the change in cash flows takes effect (e.g. when the CPI-based rent reset is applied), not when the index moves. Future index changes are never forecast.

Modifications (Ind AS 116.45) also use a revised rate; this concept covers reassessments under original terms — see c06 and c07 for modifications.

Illustration

A warehouse lease: 5 years remaining at 31 March 2026, rent ₹30 lakh per year in arrears; original IBR 9%; current IBR 10%. Liability before remeasurement = 30 × 3.8897 = ₹116.69 lakh.

Case 1 — CPI reset: from 1 April 2026, rent becomes ₹31.5 lakh. Remeasure at 9%: 31.5 × 3.8897 = ₹122.53 lakh. Increase ₹5.84 lakh: Dr ROU, Cr Lease liability.

Case 2 — Term reassessment: the lessee constructs significant unanticipated improvements and becomes reasonably certain to exercise a 3-year renewal at ₹30 lakh. Remeasure 8 remaining payments at 10%: 30 × 5.3349 = ₹160.05 lakh. Increase ₹43.36 lakh: Dr ROU, Cr Lease liability. Interest from now on accrues at 10%.

Case 3 — RVG: a vehicle lease's expected RVG shortfall rises from ₹0 to ₹2 lakh payable in 2 years; remeasure at the original rate: 2 ÷ 1.09² = ₹1.68 lakh added to liability and ROU.

Connections

Applies within s07 (reassessment vs modification) and s03 (term reassessment triggers); s04 (index-linked payments); s05 (maintaining an IBR history for revised rates). In Excel, the model needs a "remeasurement date" switch, a rate selector =IF(ChangeType="Term", IBR_at_date, IBR_original), and a recomputation of the payment vector from that date.

Practice Questions

Q1. Choose the rate [L1]

For each event state whether the original or revised rate applies: (a) WPI-based rent increase; (b) lessee becomes reasonably certain to exercise a purchase option; (c) rent linked to the repo rate resets; (d) expected RVG amount falls; (e) lessee exercises a termination option previously assessed as not reasonably certain.

Q2. CPI remeasurement with ROU adjustment [L2]

An office lease (remaining 6 years, rent ₹48 lakh per year in arrears, original IBR 8.75%, current IBR 9.6%) has rent reset by CPI to ₹50.4 lakh from today. Compute the remeasurement and the new annual ROU depreciation if the ROU carrying amount before remeasurement is ₹205 lakh.

💡 Hint: Index-linked changes use the original discount rate and adjust the ROU, which is then depreciated over the remaining term.

Q3. Term reduction by reassessment [L2]

A lessee that had included a 5-year extension in a store lease decides (Board-approved, within its control) to close the store at the end of the non-cancellable period, 2 years from now. Rent ₹24 lakh per year in arrears; liability before reassessment ₹130 lakh; ROU ₹110 lakh; revised IBR 10%. Compute the remeasurement and describe the effect on ROU, P&L and impairment assessment.
