Key concept

Initial Measurement of the Lease Liability (Ind AS 116.26)

Concept: Lease liability at commencement = PV of unpaid lease payments at implicit rate, else IBR (116.26)

Statement

\[ LL_0 = \sum_{k=1}^{N} \frac{P_k}{(1+r_p)^{t_k}} \]

where \( LL_0 \) is the lease liability at the commencement date, \( P_k \) is the \( k \)-th lease payment not paid at commencement (fixed and in-substance fixed less incentives receivable, index-linked at commencement value, expected RVG amounts, reasonably certain purchase option price, termination penalties if the term reflects termination), \( t_k \) is the number of periods from commencement to payment \( k \), \( r_p \) is the periodic discount rate derived from the rate implicit in the lease or, if that cannot be readily determined, the lessee's incremental borrowing rate, and \( N \) is the number of payments within the lease term.

For level payments with \( n \) periods:

\[ \text{Arrears: } LL_0 = P \times \frac{1-(1+r_p)^{-n}}{r_p}, \qquad \text{Advance: } LL_0 = P \times \frac{1-(1+r_p)^{-(n-1)}}{r_p} \]

(the advance formula excludes the first payment, which is paid at commencement and goes into ROU as a prepaid payment).

Explanation

Ind AS 116.26 requires measurement at the PV of lease payments "that are not paid at that date". This is why, for payments in advance, the first instalment paid on commencement is not in the liability — it is a lease payment made at or before commencement, included in the ROU (Ind AS 116.24(b)). Excel's PV(rate, n, -P, 0, 1) includes the first payment; subtract it (or use PV(rate, n-1, -P)) to get the liability, and add the first payment to ROU separately. Both approaches give the same ROU.

The discount rate is fixed at commencement. The implicit rate (rate that makes PV of lease payments plus unguaranteed residual value equal to fair value of the asset plus lessor's initial direct costs) is rarely available to lessees, so the IBR is the norm (s05). The rate must be converted to the payment frequency (c03).

Exclusions: variable payments linked to performance or usage, non-lease components (unless expedient), refundable deposits (Ind AS 109 asset, s06), recoverable GST.

Illustration

A store lease commences 1 April 2025: 5 years, ₹6 lakh per month in advance, 5% escalation annually, IBR 9% effective annual → \( r_m = 1.09^{1/12}-1 = 0.7207\% \).

Payments: months 1–12 ₹6.00 lakh; 13–24 ₹6.30; 25–36 ₹6.615; 37–48 ₹6.946; 49–60 ₹7.293.

Liability = PV of payments 2 to 60 (payment 1 is paid at commencement):

=SUMPRODUCT(Payments_2_to_60, 1/(1+rm)^(Periods_1_to_59))

Result ≈ ₹315.7 lakh; the first payment of ₹6 lakh goes directly to ROU, so ROU (before other adjustments) ≈ ₹321.7 lakh. Equivalent XNPV check: =XNPV(9%, Payments, Dates) - FirstPayment, with dates generated by EDATE(Commencement, k-1) — the result will differ slightly because XNPV uses actual days.

Connections

Feeds c02 (ROU at commencement), c03 (interest recursion), c04 (initial entry Dr ROU Cr Lease liability). Inputs come from s03 (term), s04 (payments) and s05 (IBR). Remeasurement rules in c05 and s07 reuse the same PV with new payments and rates. The same PV mechanics appear in decommissioning (t1 c02) and DBO measurement (t5), consistent with the week's discount-rate discipline theme.

Practice Questions

Q1. Arrears vs advance [L1]

A vehicle lease has 36 monthly payments of ₹50,000; IBR 10% effective annual. Compute the lease liability at commencement if payments are (a) in arrears and (b) in advance, and state the ROU in each case (no other adjustments).

Q2. Escalation and rent-free period [L2]

An office lease from 1 July 2025 for 6 years has rent of ₹20 lakh per quarter in advance, the first two quarters rent-free, and 15% escalation after 12 quarters. IBR 9.5% effective annual. Build the payment vector and write the formula to compute the liability; state which amounts go to ROU directly.

💡 Hint: Convert the annual rate to a quarterly rate and remember that a zero payment at commencement means nothing is prepaid.

Q3. Implicit rate determinable [L2]

A lessor discloses that a machine with fair value ₹50 lakh is leased for 4 years at ₹13.5 lakh per year in arrears with an unguaranteed residual of ₹6 lakh and no lessor initial direct costs. Determine the implicit rate (describe the Excel approach) and the lessee's liability if the lessee's IBR is 11%.
