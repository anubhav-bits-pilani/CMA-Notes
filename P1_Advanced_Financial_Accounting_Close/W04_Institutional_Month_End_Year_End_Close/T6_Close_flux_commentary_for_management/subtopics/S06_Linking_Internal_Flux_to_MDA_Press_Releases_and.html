Subtopic note

Linking Internal Flux to MD&A, Press Releases and the Ratio Note

Scope: External narrative linkage: SEBI LODR Schedule V MD&A, results press releases, Schedule III ratio >25% explanations

Why it matters

The explanations written for internal flux commentary eventually reach the outside world in three forms: the Management Discussion and Analysis (MD&A) in the annual report, quarterly results press releases and investor presentations, and the Schedule III ratio note in the audited FS. If these narratives are written independently, they diverge — the press release attributes margin decline to raw-material prices while the ratio note blames finance costs and the MD&A mentions neither. Regulators, analysts and auditors compare them. Building the external narrative from the same driver-based internal commentary, tied to the same final numbers, keeps the story consistent and defensible.

Core notes

1. SEBI LODR Schedule V — MD&A

Reg 34(3) read with Schedule V Part B requires the annual report of a listed entity to include an MD&A covering, within the limits set by the company's competitive position:

  • Industry structure and developments.
  • Opportunities and threats.
  • Segment-wise or product-wise performance.
  • Outlook.
  • Risks and concerns.
  • Internal control systems and their adequacy.
  • Discussion on financial performance with respect to operational performance.
  • Material developments in human resources / industrial relations, including number of people employed.
  • Details of significant changes (i.e., change of 25% or more as compared to the immediately previous financial year) in key financial ratios, along with detailed explanations — including debtors turnover, inventory turnover, interest coverage ratio, current ratio, debt-equity ratio, operating profit margin (%) and net profit margin (%), or sector-specific equivalent ratios.
  • Details of any change in return on net worth as compared to the immediately previous financial year along with a detailed explanation.

Where there is a deviation from an Ind AS treatment, the MD&A (Schedule V Part B(2)) also requires a statement explaining the alternative treatment and management's reason — rare in practice but required.

Linkage: the internal flux pack's driver analysis (PVM, rate × volume, DSO/DIO/DPO, net debt bridge) is the raw material for "financial performance with respect to operational performance" and for the 25% ratio explanations.

2. Schedule III ratio note (> 25% explanations)

The Division II ratio note (w4_t5 c05) requires explanations for changes of more than 25% in eleven prescribed ratios. Overlap and differences with Schedule V:

Aspect Schedule III ratio note LODR Schedule V MD&A
Applies to All companies (Division II for Ind AS) Listed entities (annual report)
Ratios Current, debt-equity, DSCR, ROE, inventory, receivables and payables turnover, net capital turnover, net profit, ROCE, ROI Debtors turnover, inventory turnover, interest coverage, current, debt-equity, operating profit margin, net profit margin; return on net worth
Threshold More than 25% 25% or more
Audited? Yes (part of FS) No (but auditors read for consistency — SA 720)

Use one ratio engine with shared definitions where ratios overlap (current, debt-equity, inventory and receivables turnover, net profit margin), and make sure explanations are consistent. SA 720 requires the auditor to read the other information (including MD&A) for material inconsistency with the FS.

3. Results press releases and investor presentations

  • Quarterly results under Reg 33 are filed with the exchanges; most listed companies also issue a press release and investor presentation (filed under Reg 30 as disclosures of information) explaining performance.
  • Typical content: revenue, EBITDA, EBITDA margin, PAT, EPS with YoY and QoQ changes; segment commentary; key drivers (volume, realisation per unit, raw material cost per unit); exceptional items; net debt; outlook.
  • Non-GAAP measures (EBITDA, adjusted PAT, constant-currency growth, normalised EBITDA — c05) must be clearly defined, reconciled to the reported Ind AS figures and applied consistently. India does not have a SEBI rule equivalent to the US SEC's Regulation G for non-GAAP measures. Two reference points apply instead: Division II (as explained in the ICAI Guidance Note) allows EBITDA to be presented as an additional line item on the face of the P&L, provided it is measured on a consistent, disclosed basis; and IFRS 18's management-defined performance measure (MPM) rules show where practice is heading — define the measure, reconcile it to the nearest Ind AS subtotal and explain why it is useful (w1_t1).
  • Selective disclosure risk: any material information in the press release must have been disclosed to the exchanges first or simultaneously (Reg 30); the press release cannot contain numbers that differ from the filed results.

4. Building the narrative from the flux engine

  1. Final post-adjustment TB → statements and ratios (single engine).
  2. Internal flux commentary (driver-based, quantified) → reviewed by controller.
  3. Narrative extraction: headline drivers for press release; ratio explanations for Schedule III and MD&A; segment drivers for MD&A.
  4. Consistency check: a matrix listing each external statement, its source number and the internal commentary reference.
  5. Review by CFO, audit committee (results), and auditors (limited review/audit, SA 720 reading).

Illustration — one driver, three outputs

Internal flux: "EBITDA margin 14.2% vs 17.8% PY: raw-material cost per tonne +11% (PVC resin), price increases recovered only 4%; volume +9%."

Output Wording
Press release "EBITDA of ₹186 crore (−11% YoY); margin 14.2%, down 360 bps, as resin cost inflation of 11% was only partly passed through."
MD&A (operating profit margin change ≥ 25%?) Operating margin fell from 17.8% to 14.2% (−20% relative) — below 25%, so not mandatory, but discussed under financial performance
Schedule III ratio note Net profit ratio 6.1% vs 8.4% (−27%): "Lower net profit ratio due to resin cost inflation not fully recovered in prices and higher finance costs on the new term loan"

The numbers and causes are identical across outputs; only the format differs.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Different definitions of the same ratio across MD&A, ratio note and investor presentation are a common audit and analyst query; reconcile or align.
  • Relative vs absolute change for percentage ratios (360 bps vs −20%) — the 25% test is typically applied to the relative change; state the method.
  • Forward-looking statements in MD&A and press releases need cautionary language; avoid guidance that contradicts the internal forecast.
  • Segment narrative must be consistent with Ind AS 108 segment information (same segments, same measures as reviewed by the CODM).
  • Restated comparatives — external narrative must compare with restated figures and say so.
  • Insider information — internal flux and forecast impacts are UPSI until results are published; the PIT trading window applies (w4_t1 c04).

Key takeaways

  • Schedule V Part B MD&A requires explanations for changes of 25% or more in key ratios and any change in return on net worth.
  • The Schedule III ratio note requires explanations for changes above 25% in eleven ratios; align definitions and explanations with the MD&A.
  • Press releases and investor presentations must use numbers identical to filed results and define and reconcile non-GAAP measures.
  • Build all external narratives from the same driver-based internal commentary and final TB.
  • Auditors read MD&A for consistency with FS (SA 720), so divergence is a real risk.

Practice Questions

Q1. Which ratios need explanation? [L1]

For a listed company, determine which ratios require explanation in (a) the Schedule III ratio note and (b) the Schedule V MD&A: current ratio 1.20 → 1.50; debt-equity 0.80 → 0.60; interest coverage 6.0 → 4.4; debtors turnover 8.0 → 6.0; net profit margin 10.0% → 7.5%; return on net worth 18% → 15%.

💡 Hint: Note the difference between "more than 25%" and "25% or more", and the separate requirement for return on net worth.

Q2. Consistency matrix [L2]

Design a consistency matrix to be reviewed before the board approves annual results, linking each number and causal statement in the press release, investor presentation, MD&A and Schedule III ratio note to the final TB and internal commentary. Specify columns, owners and sign-off.

Q3. Drafting MD&A from flux [L3]

Using this internal commentary — "Revenue ₹2,340 crore (+14%): volume +9%, realisation +5%; EBITDA margin 16.8% (PY 18.9%) on coal cost +22%; net debt ₹610 crore (PY ₹420 crore) after ₹380 crore capex on the Odisha plant; debtors turnover 7.1× (PY 9.6×) due to extended credit to two state utilities" — draft the "financial performance" and "significant changes in key financial ratios" sections of the MD&A (maximum 250 words).

Q4. Non-GAAP in the press release [L2]

The draft press release headlines "Adjusted PAT up 18%" while reported PAT is down 6%. Describe the reconciliation and disclosures you would require, how the adjustments must relate to exceptional items in the FS, and the governance review before release.
