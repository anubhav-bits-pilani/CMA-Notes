Flux Analysis in Governance: SA 520, Audit Committees, Limited Review

Scope: Governance use: SA 520 analytical procedures, audit committee packs, limited review queries

Why it matters

Management's flux analysis is not only an internal management tool; it is the same analysis that auditors perform under SA 520, that audit committees use to discharge their oversight of financial reporting under Sec 177 of the Companies Act and Reg 18 of SEBI LODR, and that drives the queries the auditor raises during a quarterly limited review. A finance team that prepares driver-based, threshold-based flux commentary before the auditor arrives shortens the audit, reduces the number of queries, and gives the audit committee a credible basis for recommending the results to the board. A team that does not ends up answering the same questions under time pressure at T+30.

Core notes

1. SA 520 — analytical procedures

SA 520 governs the auditor's use of analytical procedures:

  • Substantive analytical procedures (SA 520.5): the auditor determines the suitability of the procedure for the assertion, evaluates the reliability of the data, develops an expectation of recorded amounts or ratios precise enough to identify a misstatement, and determines the amount of any difference from expectation that is acceptable without further investigation.
  • Analytical procedures near the end of the audit (SA 520.6): to form an overall conclusion on whether the FS are consistent with the auditor's understanding of the entity.
  • Investigating results (SA 520.7): where analytical procedures identify fluctuations or relationships that are inconsistent with other relevant information or that differ from expected values by a significant amount, the auditor investigates by inquiring of management and obtaining appropriate audit evidence relevant to management's responses, and performing other procedures as necessary.
  • Risk assessment analytics are required separately under SA 315.

Implications for management's flux analysis:

SA 520 element What management should provide
Expectation Driver-based model (volume × price, headcount × cost, DSO × revenue) rather than PY comparison alone
Reliability of data Non-financial drivers reconciled to source systems (production MIS, HRMS headcount, despatch data)
Acceptable difference Thresholds consistent with performance materiality (s01)
Inquiry + corroboration Commentary with evidence references (contracts, price lists, approvals) — the auditor must corroborate, not just accept, management's explanation

A management explanation without evidence will not satisfy SA 520.7; commentary should cite the evidence the auditor will ask for.

2. Audit committee packs

The audit committee (Sec 177; Reg 18 and Part C of Schedule II, LODR) reviews quarterly results before submission to the board, the annual FS and auditor's report, related-party transactions, internal financial controls and the functioning of the vigil mechanism. A finance-prepared audit committee pack typically includes:

  • Results summary with flux vs PY quarter, preceding quarter and budget, with driver bridges for revenue, EBITDA and PAT.
  • Significant judgements and estimates (ECL, impairment, provisions, deferred tax recognition, revenue recognition on complex contracts) and changes in them.
  • One-off register and exceptional items classification (s03).
  • Balance sheet risk indicators: DSO/DIO/DPO, CWIP ageing, net debt and covenant headroom, aged suspense and reconciliation items (w4_t4 s06).
  • Close quality KPIs: days to close, post-close and top-side entries, % reconciliations on time (w4_t1 c02).
  • Auditor matters: limited review/audit observations, summary of unadjusted differences (SA 450), management letter points and status.
  • RPT summary against approved omnibus limits (Reg 23).

The committee's questions are typically "why" and "is it sustainable" — exactly what the five-element commentary structure (s04) answers.

3. Limited review queries

Listed entities' quarterly results are subject to limited review by the statutory auditor under SRE 2410 (Review of Interim Financial Information Performed by the Independent Auditor of the Entity), with the review report filed along with the results under Reg 33. A review consists mainly of inquiries and analytical procedures, so the auditor's query list is largely a flux list:

  • Movements in revenue, gross margin, employee costs, other expenses, finance costs and tax rate vs PY quarter and preceding quarter.
  • Changes in significant estimates; new contracts; litigation updates.
  • Balance sheet movements at half-year (the statement of assets and liabilities is part of half-yearly results).
  • Consolidation: results of significant components, IC eliminations.

A pre-emptive query pack — the flux commentary formatted to the auditor's typical query list, with evidence references — reduces review time and protects the Reg 33 deadline (45 days for quarterly results; w4_t1 s07).

Illustration — from flux to auditor query response

Flux flag: Other expenses ₹62.4 crore vs ₹51.0 crore PY quarter (+22%).

Element Content
Expectation Run-rate ₹51.0 crore + 3% inflation + ₹4.8 crore new warehouse rent (Ind AS 116 short-term lease exemption, 11-month lease) = ₹57.3 crore
Difference vs expectation ₹5.1 crore
Explanation ₹3.6 crore legal fees on arbitration (one-off), ₹1.5 crore advertising for product launch (expensed on receipt of services, Ind AS 38.69)
Evidence Arbitration engagement letter and invoices; media agency release orders and telecast certificates; lease agreement
Recurring? Legal fees non-recurring; advertising campaign through Q4

The auditor can corroborate each element directly; the query closes in one exchange.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Auditor independence: management prepares the analysis; the auditor cannot rely on it without testing data reliability and corroborating explanations. Do not ask auditors to "help write" commentary.
  • Expectation precision: PY comparison alone is rarely precise enough for substantive analytics on material lines; driver models are.
  • Disaggregation: analytics at consolidated level can mask offsetting movements in components; prepare component-level flux for significant subsidiaries.
  • Fraud risk: unusual relationships identified by analytics (revenue up, receivables up more, cash flat) are SA 240 indicators; commentary should address them squarely.
  • Audit committee minutes should evidence discussion of significant fluctuations — useful for ICFR (entity-level controls) and Sec 143(3)(i) reporting.
  • Timing: limited review queries arrive while the board date is fixed; build a query log with owners and response deadlines.

Key takeaways

  • SA 520 requires auditors to build precise expectations, set acceptable differences, and corroborate management's explanations — design management flux to meet that standard.
  • Driver-based expectations and evidence references turn management commentary into audit-ready analytics.
  • Audit committee packs use flux, one-offs, BS risk indicators, close KPIs and auditor matters to support oversight under Sec 177 and Reg 18.
  • Limited review under SRE 2410 is mainly inquiry and analytics; a pre-emptive query pack protects Reg 33 deadlines.
  • Explanations must be evidence-backed and consistent across management, audit committee and auditor audiences.

Practice Questions

Q1. SA 520 elements [L1]

List the four requirements an auditor must meet when designing substantive analytical procedures under SA 520 and, for each, state one thing management can provide to make the auditor's work efficient.

Q2. Building an expectation [L2]

A hospital chain's pharmacy revenue for Q3 was ₹84 crore. Inpatient days rose from 1.62 lakh to 1.78 lakh YoY; pharmacy revenue per inpatient day was ₹4,700 in Q3 last year; a 4% price revision was effective 1 July. Build an expectation for Q3 pharmacy revenue, compute the difference from expectation, and draft the explanation and evidence list you would prepare for the auditor.

💡 Hint: Apply the price revision to the per-day revenue before multiplying by actual inpatient days.

Q3. Audit committee pack design [L3]

Design the contents (sections, exhibits, one-page summaries) of a quarterly audit committee pack for an NSE-listed mid-cap manufacturer, explaining how each section supports the committee's responsibilities under Sec 177 and LODR Reg 18 and Schedule II Part C.

Q4. Limited review query pack [L2]

Draft a pre-emptive query pack outline (lines, comparatives, evidence) for a Q2 limited review of a consumer durables company whose revenue grew 21% YoY, gross margin fell 280 bps and finance costs doubled after an acquisition financed by debt.
