Key concept

Close KPIs: Speed, Quality and Reconciliation Health

Concept: Close KPIs: days to close, number/value of post-close and top-side entries, % recs on time, ageing of unreconciled items

Statement

Four KPI families measure a close:

\[ \text{Days to close} = \text{Working days from } T0 \text{ to reporting-pack sign-off} \]

\[ \text{Post-close entry ratio} = \frac{\text{Value of entries posted after GL lock}}{\text{Materiality}} \quad \text{and} \quad \text{count of such entries} \]

\[ \text{Recs on time (\%)} = \frac{\text{Number of reconciliations prepared and reviewed by due date}}{\text{Total reconciliations due}} \times 100 \]

\[ \text{Aged unreconciled value} = \sum_{k} \text{Value of reconciling items in ageing bucket } k \]

with buckets typically 0–30, 31–60, 61–90 and over 90 days. Top-side entries (posted at consolidation, outside entity ledgers) are tracked by count and value like post-close entries.

Explanation

Speed alone is a dangerous target — a T+3 close with ₹15 crore of post-close adjustments is worse than a T+6 close with none. A balanced scorecard therefore pairs speed (days to close, per-phase durations) with quality (post-close and top-side entries, audit adjustments, restatements) and reconciliation health (% on time, % reviewed, ageing). Benchmarks: global fast-closers report about five working days; Indian listed mid-caps commonly run T+7 to T+10 for monthly MIS.

Interpretation rules:

  • Rising top-side entries suggest entity books are not being corrected at source — a root-cause issue (topic pitfall 3).
  • Recs on time without "reviewed on time" can be gamed by preparers.
  • Ageing must be computed on gross reconciling items; netting debits against credits hides old items (Topic 2 pitfall).
  • Report KPIs by entity and by owner so slippage is attributable.

Practice Questions

Q1. Compute KPIs [L1]

For June: T0 = 30 June; pack signed off on 9 July (weekends 4–5 July); 72 reconciliations due, 61 prepared and reviewed on time, 7 prepared on time but reviewed late, 4 late; post-close entries 5 totalling ₹1.9 crore; materiality ₹6 crore. Compute days to close, recs on time (%) and the post-close entry ratio.

Q2. Designing a close scorecard [L2]

Design a one-page close scorecard for a CFO with KPIs, definitions, targets, RAG thresholds and data sources from the ERP and close tracker. Explain how you would prevent gaming of each KPI.

💡 Hint: Pair each speed metric with a quality metric that would deteriorate if the speed metric were gamed.

Q3. Diagnosing a KPI trend [L3]

Over four quarters, days to close fell from 10 to 6 while top-side entries rose from 4 to 19 and audit adjustments rose from ₹0.8 crore to ₹3.5 crore. Diagnose likely causes and propose corrective actions.
