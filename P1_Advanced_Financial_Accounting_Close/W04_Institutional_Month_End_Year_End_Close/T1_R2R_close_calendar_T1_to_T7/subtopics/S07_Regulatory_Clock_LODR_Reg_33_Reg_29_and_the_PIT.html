Regulatory Clock: LODR Reg 33, Reg 29 and the PIT Trading Window

Scope: Regulatory clock: SEBI LODR Reg 33 (45 days quarterly, 60 days annual), Reg 29 board meeting intimation, PIT trading window

Why it matters

For a listed company the close calendar is anchored on external deadlines, not internal preference. Results must reach the stock exchanges within prescribed days; the board meeting that approves them must be pre-intimated; and the close team, who handle unpublished price-sensitive information (UPSI), are subject to trading restrictions from quarter-end until results are public. Missing a Reg 33 deadline attracts fines under SEBI's standard operating procedure for non-compliance, and leaks of results data are insider-trading matters. A controller who sequences year-end work without these dates (pitfall 4 of this topic) risks a board meeting without audited numbers.

Core notes

Reg 33 — financial results

Under Regulation 33 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015:

  • Quarterly results (standalone and consolidated, for the first three quarters) must be submitted to the stock exchange within 45 days of the end of the quarter. They may be audited or subjected to limited review by the statutory auditor; the limited review report or audit report accompanies the results.
  • Annual audited results for the financial year must be submitted within 60 days of the end of the financial year, with the audit report and (where the report is modified) a statement on impact of audit qualifications. The fourth-quarter figures are the balancing figures between the annual audited figures and the published year-to-date figures up to the third quarter.
  • Results must be approved by the board, reviewed by the audit committee, and certified by the CEO and CFO (Reg 33(2)) that they do not contain false or misleading statements.
  • Results follow the formats prescribed by SEBI circulars, include a statement of assets and liabilities for half-years and a cash flow statement for half-years, and are filed in XBRL (Week 4 Topic 5).
  • Newspaper publication of results (Reg 47) and website upload follow.

Practical dates for an FY ending 31 March:

Period Period-end Reg 33 deadline
Q1 30 June 14 August
Q2 / H1 30 September 14 November
Q3 31 December 14 February
Q4 / FY (audited) 31 March 30 May

Reg 29 — prior intimation of board meetings

Regulation 29 requires a listed entity to give the stock exchange prior intimation of a board meeting at which specified matters will be considered, including financial results. For financial results, intimation is required at least two working days in advance, excluding the date of intimation and the date of the meeting (this was five days before the 2021-22 amendments; check the current text when setting the calendar). Intimation for other matters (fund raising, buy-back, dividends) follows the same or shorter rules specified in Reg 29(2)–(3). The outcome of the board meeting, including the results, is disclosed under Reg 30 and Reg 33 within 30 minutes of the conclusion of the meeting at which results are considered (Reg 30 read with Schedule III Part A).

PIT trading window

The SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015, Schedule B (minimum standards for code of conduct of listed companies), require that designated persons and their immediate relatives do not trade when the trading window is closed. The trading window for financial results is closed from the end of every quarter until 48 hours after the declaration of financial results (concept c04). The compliance officer may close the window earlier or for other UPSI events. Close-team members — accountants, controllers, consolidation and FP&A staff — are usually "designated persons" or must be brought into the structured digital database (SDD) under Reg 3(5) when they receive UPSI.

Building the regulatory clock into the calendar

Work backwards from the board date:

Step Quarter (limited review) Year-end (audit)
Board meeting / results to exchange Day B (≤ 45 days) Day B (≤ 60 days)
Audit committee meeting B or B−1 B or B−1
Reg 29 intimation ≥ 2 working days before B, excluding both dates Same
Draft results and notes to auditors B−10 B−20
Auditor limited review/audit fieldwork B−15 to B−3 T+5 to B−5
Internal close complete (T+7 reporting pack) B−20 or earlier B−30 or earlier
Trading window closure From quarter-end to 48 hours after results Same

Illustration: for Q2 ending 30 September 2026, the 45-day limit is 14 November 2026. If the board meets on Friday 6 November 2026, intimation under Reg 29 must reach the exchange no later than Tuesday 3 November (excluding 3 November and 6 November leaves 4–5 November as the two clear working days). T+7 falls on 12 October (2 October is a national holiday and weekends are skipped), leaving about three and a half weeks for limited review, the audit committee pack and board papers. The trading window closed on 1 October 2026 and reopens 48 hours after the results announcement.

Related statutory clocks for year-end

  • Companies Act 2013: AGM within six months of FY-end (Sec 96) — by 30 September; financial statements filed with ROC in AOC-4 within 30 days of AGM (Sec 137); annual return MGT-7 within 60 days of AGM (Sec 92).
  • Income-tax: tax audit report and return due dates (for companies, typically 30 September and 31 October; 30 November for transfer pricing cases) — check the current year's notifications.
  • Advance tax: 15 June, 15 September, 15 December, 15 March instalments — the quarterly close informs the estimate.
  • GST: GSTR-1 by the 11th, GSTR-3B by the 20th (for monthly filers), GSTR-9/9C by 31 December following the FY.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Extension and relaxations: SEBI has extended Reg 33 timelines in exceptional circumstances (e.g., 2020); never assume an extension — build to the regulation.
  • Listed debt only: entities with only listed non-convertible securities follow Reg 52 rather than Reg 33; its timelines have been broadly aligned with Reg 33 by amendments since 2021 (45 days for interim periods, 60 days for the year), but check the current text and which chapter applies.
  • Results on a holiday or weekend: deadlines are calendar days; boards meeting near the deadline leave no buffer for auditor issues.
  • Subsidiaries: consolidated results need subsidiaries' closes earlier — plan overseas audit or review timelines into the group calendar.
  • Leak and SDD: e-mailing draft results to personal accounts or unregistered recipients breaches PIT controls; the close tracker should list who has access to UPSI.

Key takeaways

  • Reg 33: quarterly results within 45 days; annual audited results within 60 days; CEO/CFO certification and audit committee review required.
  • Reg 29: prior intimation of a results board meeting at least two working days before, excluding the intimation and meeting dates (verify current text).
  • PIT trading window closes from quarter-end until 48 hours after results; close staff with UPSI are captured in the SDD.
  • Build the close by working backwards from the board date with auditor and audit committee buffers.
  • Year-end calendars must also respect Companies Act, Income-tax and GST deadlines.

Practice Questions

Q1. Reg 33 deadlines [L1]

For an FY ending 31 March 2027, list the Reg 33 deadlines for each quarter and for the annual audited results, and state which results require a limited review and which require an audit.

Q2. Reg 29 timing [L2]

A board meeting to approve Q3 results is planned for Monday 8 February 2027. Saturday and Sunday are non-working days and there are no holidays. Determine the last date by which Reg 29 intimation must be made and explain your counting.

💡 Hint: Exclude both the intimation date and the meeting date, and count only working days.

Q3. Backward calendar for year-end [L3]

The audit committee and board will meet on Friday 21 May 2027 to approve FY 2026-27 audited results. The group has two overseas subsidiaries audited by component auditors. Build a backward calendar listing internal close milestones, component audit deadlines, draft FS to auditors, audit committee pack, Reg 29 intimation and trading window dates.

Q4. Trading window breach [L2]

An FP&A analyst who prepared the Q2 flash on T+3 buys shares of the company on 20 October; results are announced on 6 November. Explain the PIT implications, the role of the SDD and the controls the close team should have had in place.

Q5. Interview question — board date risk [L3]

"Our board date for annual results is day 58 after year-end. The auditors have flagged an impairment issue on day 50. What would you do?" Discuss options, regulatory constraints and communication.
