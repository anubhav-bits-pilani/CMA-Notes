Subtopic note

T+1 to T+7 Day Plan: From Sub-Ledgers to Reporting Pack

Scope: Day plan: T+1 sub-ledgers; T+2 accruals/FA; T+3 IC and FX revaluation; T+4 reconciliations; T+5 tax/provisions; T+6 consolidation/review; T+7 reporting pack

Why it matters

A day plan is the operating contract of the close: each day has a defined output, an owner and a gate that must be passed before the next day's work can start. Without it, teams work on whatever is loudest and the critical path (concept c01) is invisible. A seven-working-day plan is realistic for a mid-sized Indian listed group with several entities; best-in-class global groups run about five days (see topic overview). The plan also has to meet external clocks — SEBI LODR Reg 33 results within 45 days of quarter-end and 60 days of year-end, and audit committee and board dates (s07) — so the internal T+7 is the base from which audit, review and board time is scheduled.

Core notes

The seven-day plan

Day Theme Key tasks Gate (exit criterion)
T+1 Sub-ledgers Final billing and revenue cut-off review; GRN posting; payroll register; bank statements downloaded; inventory movements complete AR, AP, payroll sub-ledgers closed; bank data loaded
T+2 Accruals / FA Post accruals and prepaid amortisation (Topic 3); inventory costing and variance settlement; CWIP capitalisation; depreciation run; lease schedules (Week 2) Sub-ledger-to-GL recs clean; sub-ledger account types locked
T+3 IC and FX revaluation IC invoices and accruals agreed; IC matching report; FX revaluation of monetary items at closing rate (Ind AS 21.23); EIR interest (Week 3) IC differences within tolerance or tagged; FX run posted
T+4 Reconciliations Bank, GST (2B/3B), TDS (26AS), payroll clearing, GR/IR, suspense and clearing accounts; balance sheet rec certification All key recs prepared and reviewed; reconciling items aged
T+5 Tax / provisions Current tax provision; deferred tax (Week 3); ECL (Week 3); Ind AS 37 provisions, warranty, litigation; employee benefit accruals Tax and provision schedules approved; final adjustments posted
T+6 Consolidation / review Entity TB lock; consolidation, eliminations, NCI, FCTR; flux review (Topic 6); top-side entries Consolidated TB approved by financial controller
T+7 Reporting pack Schedule III statements, notes, KPIs, MIS, commentary, board/audit committee pack draft CFO sign-off; master check flag = 0

Why the order is what it is

  • T+1 before T+2: accruals are defined as expected cost less invoiced amounts (Topic 3 c01), so they can be computed only after AP is complete; FA capitalisation needs capital invoices from AP.
  • T+3 after T+2: IC balances must include IC accruals and recharges; FX revaluation must run on final monetary balances, otherwise late postings remain at transaction rates.
  • T+4 after T+3: reconciliations of GL balances are meaningful only after revaluation, because the GL balance in INR for foreign-currency bank accounts changes on revaluation.
  • T+5 after T+4: tax provisions depend on final book profit and on reconciled tax ledgers (TDS receivable, GST); deferred tax depends on final carrying amounts.
  • T+6 after T+5: consolidation should use locked entity TBs; posting tax after consolidation forces re-runs.
  • T+7 last: the reporting pack is generated from the final TB through the mapping engine (Topic 5), so it should be mechanical.

Daily rhythm

Each day starts with a 15-minute stand-up: status by task (not started, in progress, blocked, complete), blocked items and owner of resolution, and critical path status. Each day ends with a gate check signed off by the close manager. A blocked gate triggers a decision — extend the day, apply a documented estimate, or escalate. Estimates applied at gates must be logged for true-up next month.

Excel close tracker structure

A practical tracker has columns: Task ID, Description, Day (T+n), Owner, Reviewer, Predecessor IDs, Planned date, Actual completion, Status, Evidence link. Derived columns calculate planned date from a calendar of working days:

=WORKDAY(PeriodEnd, [@Day], Holidays)
=IF([@Status]="Complete", "", IF(TODAY()>[@PlannedDate], "Overdue", IF(TODAY()=[@PlannedDate], "Due today", "")))
=IF([@Predecessors]="", "Ready", IF(SUM(COUNTIFS(Tracker[TaskID], TRIM(TEXTSPLIT([@Predecessors], ",")), Tracker[Status], "<>Complete"))>0, "Blocked", "Ready"))

The first formula skips weekends and the holiday list (include national and state holidays where entities operate). The third flags a task as blocked if any predecessor is incomplete — the core of a dependency-driven calendar.

Illustration — mapping days to dates

For the quarter ended 30 June 2026 (a Tuesday) with no holidays on the next seven working days, T+1 = Wednesday 1 July and T+7 = Thursday 9 July (weekend of 4–5 July skipped). If the board meeting for results is scheduled for 7 August, there are about 20 working days after T+7 for limited review, audit committee pack and Reg 29 intimation — a comfortable buffer. If an entity's T+3 IC matching slips by two days, every downstream gate moves unless float exists; the tracker shows this immediately.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Year-end: add physical verification results, actuarial valuations (Ind AS 19), impairment testing (Ind AS 36), going-concern assessment and subsequent events review (Ind AS 10), which typically extend the plan to T+10 or more; plan them as parallel workstreams starting before T0.
  • Holidays and multi-state entities: use the holiday calendar of each entity in WORKDAY.INTL; T+3 in Mumbai may not be T+3 in Chennai.
  • Overseas subsidiaries with different fiscal years: Ind AS 110.B92–B93 allows a difference of up to three months with adjustments for significant transactions; the calendar must specify which period-end is used.
  • Monthly vs quarterly: months that are not quarter-ends can collapse T+4 and T+5 into one day by using estimates (soft close, c05), but tax provisions should still be run-rated.
  • Shortening the close: compress by moving work earlier (pre-close accruals, continuous IC matching) and by parallelising non-dependent tasks, not by skipping reconciliations.

Key takeaways

  • Each day of the T+1 to T+7 plan has a theme, tasks, an owner and an exit gate.
  • The order follows data dependencies: AP before accruals, final balances before FX revaluation, recs before tax, tax before consolidation.
  • WORKDAY with a holiday list converts T+n into dates; predecessor checks flag blocked tasks.
  • Year-end adds actuarial, impairment, going-concern and subsequent-events workstreams that should start pre-close.
  • Faster closes come from moving and parallelising work, not removing controls.

Practice Questions

Q1. Converting T+n to dates [L1]

The period ends on Friday 31 March 2028. Holidays on 3 April (Monday) and 14 April (Friday) apply. Determine the calendar dates for T+1 to T+7 and write the Excel formula you would use.

Q2. Sequencing error diagnosis [L2]

In a group's close, FX revaluation runs on T+2 and IC accruals are posted on T+3. The USD IC receivable of the Indian entity was USD 8,50,000 at T+2 and USD 9,40,000 after T+3 accruals. Carrying rate of the accrual is ₹84.10/USD and the closing rate is ₹85.30/USD. Explain the error in sequencing, quantify the unrevalued amount and describe the fix.

💡 Hint: Only the incremental accrual misses revaluation; compute the difference between closing and carrying rate on it.

Q3. Build a dependency-aware tracker [L3]

Design an Excel close tracker for 40 tasks across three entities. Specify the table columns, the formulas for planned date, overdue flag and blocked status, the conditional formatting rules and a summary dashboard showing % tasks complete by day and critical path status.

Q4. Compressing T+7 to T+5 [L3]

A listed company with five entities closes on T+7. The CFO wants T+5 within two quarters. Using the day plan, propose which tasks move to pre-close, which can run in parallel and which controls must not be removed. Quantify the target number of working days per phase.

Q5. Year-end extension [L2]

List the additional year-end workstreams that must be added to the monthly T+7 plan and, for each, state whether it can start before T0, the evidence required and its dependency on other close tasks.
