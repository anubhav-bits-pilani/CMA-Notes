Key concept

Abnormal Balance Test for Account Nature vs Sign

Concept: Abnormal balance test: debit-nature accounts with credit balances (and vice versa) flagged for reclassification

Statement

Each natural account \(a\) is assigned an expected sign \(s_{a} \in \{+1, -1\}\) in the mapping table (+1 for debit-nature: assets, expenses; −1 for credit-nature: liabilities, equity, income). With the TB storing debits as positive and credits as negative:

\[ \text{Abnormal}_{a} = \left[ s_{a} \times B_{a} < 0 \right] \wedge \left[ \lvert B_{a} \rvert > T \right] \]

where \(B_{a}\) is the closing balance and \(T\) a small de-minimis threshold. For control accounts (trade receivables, trade payables, employee advances, IC), the test is run at sub-ledger party level \(p\):

\[ \text{Abnormal}_{a,p} = \left[ s_{a} \times B_{a,p} < 0 \right] \]

Flagged balances are investigated and, if genuine, reclassified to the line whose nature they represent.

Explanation

Contra balances usually mean one of three things: (1) a genuine item of a different nature (customer advance inside receivables, advance to supplier inside payables, overdraft inside bank), (2) a posting error (wrong account, reversal posted twice, credit note without invoice), or (3) a timing issue (payment recorded before invoice).

Presentation rules that make reclassification mandatory:

  • No offsetting of assets and liabilities unless required or permitted by an Ind AS (Ind AS 1.32); for financial instruments only where there is a legally enforceable right and intention to settle net (Ind AS 32.42). A customer with a credit balance cannot be netted against other customers' debit balances.
  • Schedule III requires advances to suppliers to be shown under other current assets (capital advances under other non-current assets), customer advances as contract liabilities / other current liabilities, and bank overdrafts under borrowings.
  • Accumulated depreciation, provisions and revenue with abnormal signs usually indicate errors rather than reclassification candidates.

Common reclassifications:

Abnormal balance Reclassify to Notes
Debit-balance trade payables Other current assets — advances to suppliers (or other financial assets if a refundable overpayment) Split capital advances to non-current
Credit-balance trade receivables Contract liabilities (Ind AS 115) or other financial liabilities (refunds due) Affects ECL base and DSO
Credit-balance bank account Current borrowings (overdraft) Ind AS 7.8 may still include it in cash and cash equivalents for CFS
Debit-balance GST payable GST input / balances with government authorities Check electronic ledgers
Debit-balance accrued expenses Prepayment or reversal error Investigate missed invoice (w4_t3)

Illustration

Vendor ledger extract at 31 March, ₹ lakh (credits shown positive for readability):

Vendor Balance Nature
V101 820.0 Cr Normal
V204 46.0 Dr Advance for raw material (PO open)
V318 12.5 Dr Duplicate payment, refund requested
V422 95.0 Dr Advance for machinery
Control account 666.5 Cr

Reported trade payables should be ₹820.0 lakh (assuming V101 represents all credit balances); other current assets include advances to suppliers ₹46.0 lakh; other current financial assets include ₹12.5 lakh recoverable; other non-current assets include capital advance ₹95.0 lakh. Presenting ₹666.5 lakh understates both assets and liabilities by ₹153.5 lakh and distorts DPO and the current ratio.
