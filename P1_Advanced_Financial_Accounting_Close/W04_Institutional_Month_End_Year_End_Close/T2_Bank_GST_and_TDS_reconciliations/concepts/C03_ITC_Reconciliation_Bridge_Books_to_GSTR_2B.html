Key concept

ITC Reconciliation Bridge: Books to GSTR-2B

Concept: ITC rec: ITC per books − Not in 2B (timing/vendor default) + In 2B not in books (missed/ineligible) = ITC per 2B

Statement

\[ ITC_{2B} = ITC_{\text{books}} - N_{\text{timing}} - N_{\text{default}} + M_{\text{missed}} + M_{\text{inel}} \pm V \]

where \(ITC_{\text{books}}\) is input tax recorded in the purchase register for the tax period; \(N_{\text{timing}}\) is ITC on invoices in books that the supplier will report in a later period; \(N_{\text{default}}\) is ITC on invoices the supplier has not reported (non-filer, wrong GSTIN); \(M_{\text{missed}}\) is ITC on invoices in GSTR-2B not booked (invoice not received or not processed); \(M_{\text{inel}}\) is ITC in 2B that is not ours or not eligible to us (wrong recipient, Sec 17(5) items not booked as ITC); and \(V\) is the net value difference on matched invoices. The ITC claimable in GSTR-3B is the eligible portion of matched invoices (and earlier-period items that now appear in 2B), not \(ITC_{\text{books}}\).

Explanation

The bridge converts two lists into an explained difference. Each component has a different action:

  • Timing (not in 2B): carry forward; claim in the period the supplier reports, subject to Sec 16(4) time limits.
  • Vendor default (not in 2B): follow up, hold payment, and if never reported, expense the ITC (it becomes a cost) — do not leave it in ITC indefinitely.
  • Missed (in 2B, not in books): book the invoice if goods/services were received; otherwise keep pending in IMS.
  • Ineligible/not ours (in 2B): reject in IMS where the record is wrong; for blocked credits (Sec 17(5)), do not claim even though they appear.
  • Value differences: claim the lower of books and 2B pending resolution; ask the supplier to amend or the recipient to correct the booking.

The bridge must be prepared per GSTIN and per tax head (IGST, CGST, SGST, cess), because utilisation rules differ by head.

Illustration

Item ₹ lakh
ITC per books — March, GSTIN 27 312.4
Less: not in 2B — supplier will report in April (timing) (14.8)
Less: not in 2B — 6 suppliers not filed (default) (9.3)
Add: in 2B not in books — invoices received 2 April 5.6
Add: in 2B not in books — invoice addressed to group company (not ours) 1.2
Add/(less): value differences on matched invoices (net) (0.4)
ITC per 2B — March 294.7

ITC claimable in March GSTR-3B = ₹312.4 − 14.8 − 9.3 − 0.4 = ₹287.9 lakh (matched, eligible, at lower of books and 2B), plus any earlier-period items now appearing in 2B. The ₹1.2 lakh is rejected in IMS; ₹5.6 lakh is claimed in April after booking.

Practice Questions

Q1. Build the bridge [L1]

ITC per books ₹86.2 lakh; timing items ₹4.1 lakh; vendor defaults ₹2.7 lakh; 2B-only items: ₹1.9 lakh genuine but not booked, ₹0.6 lakh blocked under Sec 17(5); value differences: books higher by ₹0.3 lakh. Compute ITC per 2B and the ITC claimable in GSTR-3B.

Q2. Ageing vendor defaults [L2]

Vendor-default ITC has accumulated: ₹3.1 lakh (current FY, < 3 months), ₹2.4 lakh (current FY, 6–9 months), ₹1.8 lakh (previous FY). Today is 15 December. Explain the treatment of each bucket considering Sec 16(4) and the accounting entries for amounts that can no longer be claimed.

💡 Hint: Check whether the 30 November deadline for the previous FY has passed.

Q3. Interview question [L2]

"Why can't we just claim whatever is in GSTR-2B?" Explain the risks of claiming 2B as-is, with two examples, and how the bridge controls them.
