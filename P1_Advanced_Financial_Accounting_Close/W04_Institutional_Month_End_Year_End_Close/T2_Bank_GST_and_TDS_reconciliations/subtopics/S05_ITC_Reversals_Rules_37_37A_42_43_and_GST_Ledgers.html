Subtopic note

ITC Reversals (Rules 37, 37A, 42, 43) and GST Ledgers vs GL

Scope: ITC reversals: Rule 37 (180-day non-payment), Rule 37A (supplier non-filing), Rules 42/43; electronic credit/cash ledgers vs GL

Why it matters

ITC that was validly claimed can still become reversible: if the supplier is not paid within 180 days, if the supplier fails to file returns, or if inputs and capital goods are used for exempt supplies or non-business purposes. Each reversal has its own trigger, timing, computation and re-availment rule, and each must be reflected both in GSTR-3B and in the GL. Separately, the GST portal maintains electronic ledgers (credit, cash, liability) that are the "bank statements" of GST — and the GL balances of input tax and GST payable must reconcile to them every month. Unreconciled differences surface as demands, blocked refunds or audit adjustments, and are a frequent CARO clause 3(vii) discussion point.

Core notes

Rule 37 — non-payment within 180 days

The second proviso to Sec 16(2) of the CGST Act and Rule 37 require a recipient who has not paid the supplier the value of supply plus tax within 180 days of the invoice date to reverse the ITC availed on that invoice. As amended from 1 October 2022:

  • The ITC (proportionate to the unpaid amount) is reversed in the GSTR-3B for the tax period immediately following the 180th day, with interest under Sec 50.
  • On subsequent payment, the ITC is re-availed in the return for the period of payment (re-availment is generally understood not to be subject to the Sec 16(4) time limit — see concept c06).
  • Exclusions: supplies on reverse charge, deemed supplies without consideration (Schedule I), and amounts added to value under Sec 15(2)(b) paid by the recipient.

Model implementation: from AP ageing, flag every invoice whose unpaid amount is outstanding beyond 180 days from invoice date (not due date), and compute ITC on the unpaid share:

\[ \text{ITC reversal} = \text{ITC on invoice} \times \frac{\text{Unpaid amount (incl. tax)}}{\text{Invoice value (incl. tax)}} \]

The same AP ageing drives Sec 43B(h) MSME tracking (topic related item).

Rule 37A — supplier has not filed GSTR-3B

Rule 37A (inserted in 2023) requires a recipient to reverse ITC where the supplier has furnished the invoice in GSTR-1/IFF (so it appeared in GSTR-2B) but has not filed the GSTR-3B for that period by 30 September following the end of the FY. The recipient reverses the ITC by 30 November of that following year (through GSTR-3B), with interest where applicable; re-availment is allowed when the supplier subsequently files GSTR-3B. GSTR-2B provides supplier GSTR-3B filing status to identify such cases. Example: a supplier's invoice of June 2025 appeared in 2B, but the supplier had not filed June 2025 GSTR-3B by 30 September 2026; the recipient reverses by 30 November 2026.

Rules 42 and 43 — common credit

Where inputs, input services or capital goods are used partly for taxable (including zero-rated) supplies and partly for exempt supplies or non-business purposes:

  • Rule 42 (inputs and input services): exclude ITC exclusively for exempt/non-business use (T1, T2, T3), and of common credit \(C_2\) attribute to exempt supplies

\[ D_1 = \frac{E}{F} \times C_2 \]

where \(E\) is the value of exempt supplies and \(F\) the total turnover of the registered person in the state during the tax period, plus \(D_2 = 5\%\) of \(C_2\) for non-business use. Computation is monthly on provisional figures with a final annual true-up by the due date of the September return following the FY (Rule 42(2)), with interest on any excess credit.

  • Rule 43 (capital goods): common capital goods' ITC is credited to the electronic credit ledger and their useful life is taken as five years (60 months); monthly common credit \(T_m = T_c / 60\) and the exempt share \(T_e = (E/F) \times T_m\) is reversed each month.

Illustration (Rule 42): common credit \(C_2\) for June = ₹18,00,000; exempt supplies ₹2.4 crore; total turnover ₹30 crore. \(D_1 = (2.4/30) \times 18{,}00{,}000\) = ₹1,44,000; \(D_2 = 5\% \times 18{,}00{,}000\) = ₹90,000 (only if there is non-business use). Reversal ₹1,44,000 (+ ₹90,000 if applicable).

Electronic ledgers vs GL

Portal ledger Contents GL counterpart
Electronic credit ledger (ECL) ITC available by head (IGST, CGST, SGST/UTGST, cess) after GSTR-3B; utilisations Input tax accounts by head (net of reversals), excluding "ITC pending reflection"
Electronic cash ledger Deposits via PMT-06 challans by minor head (tax, interest, penalty, fee, others) GST cash balance (advance tax deposit)
Electronic liability register Liability per returns and demands; discharge Output GST payable
Electronic credit reversal and re-claimed statement Cumulative ITC reversed (Rule 37, 37A etc.) and re-claimed (from August 2023 period) Memo schedule of reversible/re-claimable ITC

Monthly reconciliation:

Item IGST (₹ lakh)
Balance per ECL, 31 March 182.4
Add: ITC booked in March, to be claimed in April 3B (reflected in April 2B) 21.6
Less: ITC reversed in books, not yet in 3B (2.1)
Add: Rule 37 reversals in 3B pending re-claim (tracked in reversal statement, not in books as asset) 0.0
Balance per GL — Input IGST 201.9

Differences that cannot be explained are investigated: wrong head (CGST paid as IGST), manual GL entries to input accounts, ITC claimed on portal but expensed in books, or transitional credit disputes. The credit reversal and re-claimed statement must also be reconciled: the opening balance of reversed-but-reclaimable ITC was declared once by taxpayers, and re-claims beyond the statement balance are flagged by the system.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Rule 37 and disputed invoices: non-payment due to quality disputes still triggers reversal after 180 days; ITC returns on payment of the agreed amount, with the reduced value adjusted via credit note.
  • Partial payments under Rule 37: payments are generally applied to the value plus tax proportionately; document the allocation policy.
  • Rule 42 for banks/financial entities: financial-sector entities may opt for Sec 17(4) (50% ITC) instead of Rules 42/43.
  • Exempt supplies definition: for Rule 42/43, exempt supplies include certain items such as land sale and some securities transactions (the Explanations to Rule 43, which apply for both Rules 42 and 43); interest on deposits is excluded in certain cases — read the Explanation carefully.
  • ITC as asset vs expense: reversed ITC that is permanently lost (Rules 42/43) is a cost; reversals expected to be re-claimed (Rules 37, 37A) are better kept as a receivable-like balance with ageing, assessed for recoverability.

Key takeaways

  • Rule 37: unpaid beyond 180 days → reverse in the following period with interest; re-avail on payment.
  • Rule 37A: supplier not filing GSTR-3B by 30 September after FY-end → reverse by 30 November; re-avail on supplier filing.
  • Rules 42/43: proportionate reversal of common credit for exempt/non-business use, annual true-up for inputs, 60-month spread for capital goods.
  • Reconcile the electronic credit ledger, cash ledger, liability register and reversal statement to GL monthly.
  • Classify reversed ITC as recoverable (track and age) or permanently lost (expense).

Practice Questions

Q1. Rule 37 computation [L2]

An invoice dated 10 September 2025 for ₹11,80,000 (₹10,00,000 + IGST ₹1,80,000) was paid ₹5,90,000 on 15 November 2025. The balance remained unpaid on 9 March 2026 (day 180). Compute the ITC reversal, identify the GSTR-3B period of reversal, and state the re-availment if the balance is paid on 20 May 2026.

💡 Hint: The reversal is proportionate to the unpaid amount including tax.

Q2. Rule 42 true-up [L2]

Monthly Rule 42 reversals for FY 2025-26 totalled ₹16.2 lakh based on monthly turnover ratios. For the full year: common credit ₹2.40 crore; exempt supplies ₹14 crore; total turnover ₹180 crore; no non-business use. Compute the annual reversal, the true-up amount and the due date of the true-up.

Q3. ECL vs GL reconciliation [L2]

At 31 March: ECL CGST ₹42.6 lakh, GL Input CGST ₹51.9 lakh. Known items: ITC on March invoices reflected in April 2B ₹6.3 lakh; Rule 37 reversal of ₹1.8 lakh made in 3B but not booked; a ₹1.2 lakh SGST invoice wrongly booked to CGST. Prepare the reconciliation and identify any unexplained difference and the corrective entries.

Q4. Rule 37A monitoring design [L3]

Design a monthly monitoring process using GSTR-2B supplier filing status and AP data to anticipate Rule 37A reversals before 30 November, including vendor communication, payment holds and the GL treatment of reversed and re-claimed ITC.
