Subtopic note

Schedule III Presentation: Rounding, Comparatives, 2021 Disclosures

Scope: Presentation: Schedule III rounding, regrouped comparatives, 2021 additional disclosures and ratio note

Why it matters

The mapping engine produces correct numbers; presentation makes them compliant. Schedule III Division II prescribes how figures are rounded, how comparatives are presented and which additional disclosures (largely introduced by the MCA notification of 24 March 2021, effective from FY 2021-22) must appear. These disclosures — ageing schedules, promoter shareholding, title deeds, ratios with explanations, and a long list of "additional regulatory information" — are where auditors, lenders and the NFRA most often find deficiencies. Because most of them are data-driven, they should be generated by the engine from sub-ledger data and mapping attributes rather than typed in.

Core notes

1. Rounding

Division II General Instructions (rounding off): depending on total income, figures may be rounded off as follows (c04):

Total income (turnover) of the company Permitted units
Less than ₹100 crore Nearest hundreds, thousands, lakhs or millions, or decimals thereof
₹100 crore or more Nearest lakhs, millions or crores, or decimals thereof
  • Once a unit is chosen it must be used uniformly throughout the FS (faces, notes, comparatives).
  • Implementation: compute in rupees, round only at the output layer: =ROUND(LineValue/Unit, Decimals). Rounding differences in totals are absorbed by computing totals from unrounded values and then rounding, accepting small casting differences — or by a documented rounding-adjustment approach applied consistently. Never round account by account before aggregating.
  • A rounding check: rounded total − sum of rounded components ≤ 1 unit per line; differences beyond that indicate a formula problem.

2. Regrouped comparatives

  • Ind AS 1.38 requires comparative information for all amounts reported in the current period; Ind AS 1.41 requires that when presentation or classification is changed, comparatives are reclassified unless impracticable, with disclosure of the nature, amount and reason for the reclassification.
  • Schedule III's general instruction also requires corresponding amounts for the immediately preceding reporting period for all items in the FS, including notes.
  • Third balance sheet (opening of the preceding period) is required when an accounting policy is applied retrospectively, a retrospective restatement is made, or items are reclassified, and the change has a material effect on the opening balance sheet (Ind AS 1.40A–40D).
  • Engine approach: keep the prior-year final TB and apply the current mapping to it; differences between the regenerated prior-year FS and the last audited FS are the regroupings, listed in a reconciliation and disclosed as "previous year figures regrouped/reclassified" with specifics where material.

3. 2021 additional disclosures (Division II) — key items

Balance sheet notes:

  • Shareholding of promoters — shares held by promoters at year-end, % of total shares, and % change during the year.
  • Trade receivables ageing — undisputed and disputed, considered good / significant increase in credit risk / credit impaired, by buckets (less than 6 months, 6 months–1 year, 1–2, 2–3, more than 3 years from due date of payment; or from transaction date where no due date).
  • Trade payables ageing — MSME, others, disputed MSME, disputed others; buckets less than 1 year, 1–2, 2–3, more than 3 years.
  • CWIP ageing (projects in progress and temporarily suspended; less than 1 year to more than 3 years) and completion schedule for projects overdue or exceeding original cost.
  • Intangible assets under development — same ageing and completion schedule.
  • Title deeds of immovable property not held in the name of the company (with details and reason).
  • Revaluation of PPE/intangibles — whether based on a registered valuer's valuation (Companies (Registered Valuers and Valuation) Rules 2017).
  • Loans or advances to promoters, directors, KMPs and related parties repayable on demand or without specifying terms/period of repayment.
  • Current maturities of long-term borrowings shown within current borrowings; security deposits presented under other financial assets (not loans).
  • Borrowings secured by current assets — whether quarterly returns/statements filed with banks agree with books; summary of reconciliation and reasons for material discrepancies.
  • Wilful defaulter status; registration of charges pending with ROC beyond the statutory period.

Additional regulatory information: details of benami property proceedings; relationship with struck-off companies (Sec 248); compliance with the number of layers (Sec 2(87)); compliance with approved schemes of arrangement; utilisation of borrowed funds and share premium through intermediaries (with declarations); undisclosed income surrendered in tax assessments; corporate social responsibility (Sec 135) details; details of crypto/virtual currency.

Ratio note (c05): current ratio, debt-equity, debt service coverage, ROE, inventory turnover, trade receivables turnover, trade payables turnover, net capital turnover, net profit ratio, ROCE and return on investment — with numerator/denominator and explanation for any change of more than 25% compared with the preceding year.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • "Total income" for the rounding test — Division II refers to total income; apply consistently and revisit when the company crosses ₹100 crore (the unit may need to change, with comparatives in the same unit).
  • Ageing basis — from due date of payment; where no due date is specified, from the date of transaction. Unbilled revenue is not a receivable and is excluded from the receivables ageing (but some disclose it separately).
  • Nil disclosures — where an item does not apply (e.g., no benami proceedings), state so; omission can be read as non-compliance.
  • Regrouping vs restatement — a reclassification is presentation (Ind AS 1.41); correcting an error is an Ind AS 8 restatement requiring its own disclosures. Do not label an error correction as "regrouped".
  • Ind AS vs Schedule III precedence — where an Ind AS requires a different presentation or additional disclosure, the Ind AS prevails (Schedule III Division II general instructions).
  • Ratio definitions are not prescribed in Schedule III; follow the ICAI Guidance Note formulas and disclose the definitions used.

Key takeaways

  • Rounding: < ₹100 crore turnover — hundreds/thousands/lakhs/millions or decimals; ≥ ₹100 crore — lakhs/millions/crores or decimals; one unit throughout.
  • Round only at the output layer and test that rounded totals cast.
  • Regenerate comparatives by applying current mapping to the prior-year TB; disclose nature, amount and reason of reclassifications (Ind AS 1.41) and consider the third balance sheet (Ind AS 1.40A).
  • The 2021 amendments added ageing schedules, promoter shareholding, title deeds, borrowing-return reconciliations, regulatory information and the ratio note.
  • Generate these disclosures from sub-ledger data and mapping attributes, tied back to the faces.

Practice Questions

Q1. Choosing the rounding unit [L1]

A company's total income was ₹92 crore in FY 2024-25 and ₹108 crore in FY 2025-26. It previously reported in ₹ thousands. State which units are permitted for FY 2025-26, how comparatives must be presented, and how you would implement the change in the engine.

Q2. Receivables ageing schedule [L2]

Using the following receivables (₹ lakh) at 31 March 2026 — not due 820; overdue less than 6 months 310; 6 months–1 year 95; 1–2 years 40 (of which 15 disputed); more than 3 years 22 (all disputed, credit impaired) — and an ECL allowance of ₹48 lakh, prepare the Schedule III ageing table structure and the reconciliation to the face line.

💡 Hint: Check where "not due" amounts sit in the prescribed buckets and how the allowance is shown.

Q3. Regrouping disclosure [L2]

In FY 2025-26 the company moves security deposits of ₹3.6 crore from "Loans" to "Other financial assets" and reclassifies ₹2.1 crore of capital creditors from trade payables to other financial liabilities, for both years. Draft the comparative reclassification disclosure and state whether a third balance sheet is required.

Q4. Disclosure completeness review [L3]

Prepare a review checklist (at least 15 items) for the 2021 Schedule III additional disclosures for an unlisted public company with bank working-capital limits of ₹40 crore, CWIP of ₹22 crore across three projects (one delayed), and a land parcel registered in the name of an amalgamated company. For each item, state the data source in the engine.
