Subtopic note

Adjustment Layer: From Unadjusted TB to Final TB

Scope: Adjustment layer: TB + audit/top-side adjustments + reclassifications → final TB, each entry referenced

Why it matters

Between the ERP trial balance at GL lock and the signed financial statements, there are always adjustments: late accruals, Weeks 1–3 schedule postings (revenue waterfall, lease, EIR, ECL, deferred tax), auditor-proposed adjustments, consolidation-only top-sides and presentation reclassifications. If these are typed directly into FS cells or hidden inside formulas, the FS no longer reconcile to any TB, the audit trail is lost, and flux commentary is written on numbers nobody can rebuild (w4_t6 pitfall). An explicit adjustment layer keeps one rule: Final TB = Unadjusted TB + referenced adjustments, and every output is generated from the final TB.

Core notes

1. Structure

The layer is a table, not a worksheet of ad-hoc cells. Each row is one line of a balanced entry:

Field Purpose
Adj ID Unique reference (e.g., ADJ-26-031)
Type Booked-in-ERP late JE / Top-side (FS only) / Audit adjustment / Reclassification / Schedule posting
Source / reference Schedule or workpaper reference (e.g., "W2 Lease schedule tab, row 18"; "Auditor AJE 7")
Entity, Account Where it posts
Amount (Dr +, Cr −) Same sign convention as the TB
Description What and why
Recurring? One-off or recurring each close
Book in ERP? Yes/No and ERP document number once posted
Prepared / Approved Maker-checker evidence and approval threshold (w4_t1 s06)
Affects tax? For current/deferred tax recomputation

Checks on the layer itself: each Adj ID nets to zero; every row has a reference and approver; accounts used exist in the mapping.

2. Types of adjustment

  • Late ERP journals — posted in the ERP after the TB extract; included in the layer until the TB is re-extracted, then dropped to avoid double counting (flag "Booked in ERP").
  • Schedule postings — monthly or quarterly entries from the Weeks 1–3 schedules: Ind AS 115 revenue deferral/unbilled revenue, Ind AS 116 ROU depreciation and lease interest, EIR amortisation of processing fees (Ind AS 109), ECL allowance, deferred tax. The schedule computes the closing balance; the adjustment posts the difference between schedule and TB (a "true-to-schedule" entry), so the check "schedule ties to GL" holds.
  • Audit adjustments — corrected misstatements proposed by the auditor (recorded with AJE number); uncorrected ones are tracked separately in the summary of unadjusted differences (SA 450), not in the layer.
  • Top-sides — entries made only at reporting level (common in consolidation or where the ERP period is hard-closed); must be booked in the ERP in the next period or in a reporting ledger to keep books and FS aligned.
  • Reclassifications — presentation-only moves between lines (s05): debit-balance creditors to advances, current maturities of long-term debt, overdrafts to borrowings. They do not change profit and usually reverse on day 1 of the next period.

3. Final TB construction

Power Query: append the TB extract and the adjustment table (both with Entity, Account, Amount), group by Entity + Account summing Amount, merge mapping. Worksheet alternative: a columnar final TB:

Account Unadjusted Late JEs Schedules Audit Reclass Final

with each adjustment column computed as =SUMIFS(Adj[Amount], Adj[Account], [@Account], Adj[Type], "Audit") and Final as the sum across. New accounts appearing only in the adjustment table must be added to the final TB account list (a UNIQUE(VSTACK(TB[Account], Adj[Account])) spill handles this).

5. Governance

  • Approval thresholds by amount and type (top-sides above ₹X lakh need CFO approval).
  • Close KPI: number and value of top-side and post-close entries (w4_t1 c02); recurring top-sides signal process gaps to fix in the ERP.
  • Roll-forward: at the start of the next close, the layer for the new period starts empty; reclassifications are regenerated by rule; top-sides not booked in ERP are listed for booking.
  • Version control: the layer is date-stamped per FS version (draft 1, draft 2, audited), so differences between versions are explained by Adj IDs.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Double counting when a late JE is later booked in the ERP and the TB re-extracted — use the "Booked in ERP" flag and ERP document number to exclude it.
  • Tax effects: adjustments affecting PBT change current tax and deferred tax; include a tax recomputation step after all P&L adjustments (and before the final integrity check).
  • Consolidation vs standalone: an adjustment in the consolidated layer (e.g., unrealised profit elimination) must not leak into the standalone FS; tag the layer by reporting basis.
  • Prior-period errors discovered during the close are corrected retrospectively under Ind AS 8.42 — the layer for the comparative year must hold the restatement, with the third balance sheet (Ind AS 1.40A) if material.
  • Hard-coding inside FS formulas (e.g., =SUMIFS(...)+25) bypasses all of this and is the classic topic pitfall; detect with formula auditing or a search for numeric constants in output formulas.

Key takeaways

  • Final TB = Unadjusted TB + referenced, balanced, approved adjustments; outputs are generated only from the final TB.
  • Distinguish late ERP JEs, schedule postings, audit adjustments, top-sides and reclassifications — each has different booking and reversal behaviour.
  • Schedule postings make "schedule ties to GL" checks pass by construction.
  • Track top-side counts and values as close KPIs and push recurring ones back into the ERP.
  • Version the layer so every difference between FS drafts is explained by an Adj ID.

Practice Questions

Q1. Build the final TB [L2]

Unadjusted TB extracts at 31 March (₹ lakh): Revenue (12,400), Unbilled revenue 0, Contract liabilities (180), Lease liability (950), ROU asset 880, Depreciation 310, Interest expense 210. Adjustments: (i) revenue schedule shows unbilled revenue of ₹140 lakh and contract liabilities of ₹220 lakh; (ii) lease schedule shows lease liability ₹910 lakh, ROU ₹846 lakh and shows that ₹34 lakh of March depreciation and ₹9 lakh of March interest have not been posted, with the March lease payment of ₹49 lakh posted to rent expense; (iii) auditor AJE to accrue legal fees ₹12 lakh. Prepare the adjustment table with Adj IDs and references, and the final TB for these accounts.

💡 Hint: Work out the entry each schedule needs to make the GL equal the schedule closing balances, and check every Adj ID balances.

Q2. Classify the adjustment [L1]

For each item state the adjustment type and whether it reverses next period: (a) current maturities of a term loan; (b) auditor-proposed inventory write-down; (c) group-level elimination of unrealised profit; (d) a JE posted in SAP after the TB extract; (e) monthly ECL true-up from the provision matrix.

Q3. Governance design [L3]

Draft the control design for an adjustment layer in a listed company: approval thresholds, evidence, maker-checker, reporting of top-sides to the audit committee, and the KPI targets you would set for reducing top-sides over four quarters.

Q4. Detecting hard-codes [L2]

Describe three techniques (Excel features or formulas) to detect hard-coded adjustments inside FS formulas, and how you would migrate each found item into the adjustment layer.
