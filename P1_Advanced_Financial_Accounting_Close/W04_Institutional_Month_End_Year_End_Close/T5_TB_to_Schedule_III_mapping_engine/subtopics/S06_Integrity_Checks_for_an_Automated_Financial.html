Subtopic note

Integrity Checks for an Automated Financial Statement Build

Scope: Integrity checks: TB nets to zero, BS balances, P&L ties to retained earnings, CF closing cash = BS cash, notes tie to faces

Why it matters

An automated FS build is only trustworthy if it proves itself every time it recalculates. Integrity checks are the built-in proofs: if all of them return zero, the statements are arithmetically complete and internally consistent; if any fails, the FS cannot be released. They protect against the most common workbook failures — a TB extract that dropped rows, an unmapped account, a sign error, a note typed over, a cash flow that "balances" through a plug. For Project 1 these checks roll into a master check flag, and the Saturday self-audit requires BS check = 0.00 and Cash Flow check = 0.00.

Core notes

1. TB nets to zero

\[ \sum_{a} B_{a}^{\text{final}} = 0 \]

over all accounts in the final TB (debits positive, credits negative), excluding statistical accounts. Run it at three points: unadjusted TB (extract completeness), adjustment layer (each Adj ID nets to zero), final TB. A related completeness check compares the TB extract total debits/credits and row count with the ERP TB report or control totals.

2. Balance sheet balances

\[ \text{Total assets} - (\text{Total equity} + \text{Total liabilities}) = 0 \]

computed from the output statement lines, not from the TB — this proves that every mapped balance landed on some BS line. If the TB nets to zero but the BS does not balance, the usual causes are: an account mapped to no line or to a non-existent Line ID, a P&L account mapped to a BS line (or vice versa), profit for the year not flowing into other equity, or inconsistent display signs (c01).

3. P&L ties to retained earnings

\[ \text{Profit for the year} - \left(\text{RE}_{\text{close}} - \text{RE}_{\text{open}} + \text{Dividends} \pm \text{Transfers}\right) = 0 \]

where transfers include transfers to/from general reserve, debenture redemption reserve, remeasurement of defined benefit plans transferred from OCI to retained earnings (if policy), and Ind AS 8 restatement adjustments to opening RE. In a TB-based build where P&L accounts are still open, the closing RE in the BS is "RE opening per TB + current-year P&L"; the check then confirms the SOCIE, the P&L and the BS all agree. OCI items are checked separately against their reserve movements.

4. Cash flow closing cash equals BS cash

\[ \text{Opening cash} + \text{Net CF (operating + investing + financing)} + \text{FX effect on cash} = \text{Closing cash per CF} \]

\[ \text{Closing cash per CF} - \left(\text{BS cash and cash equivalents} - \text{Overdrafts included in cash (Ind AS 7.8)}\right) = 0 \]

The CF must be built from movements, not forced: a CF with a balancing "other adjustments" line passes this check trivially. Supplementary check: each BS line's movement is fully explained in the CF either as a cash flow, a non-cash item (depreciation, ROU additions, FX unrealised, ECL), or a reclassification — a "movement explained" matrix with zero residual per line.

5. Notes tie to faces

For every face line with a note: \(\text{Face line} - \text{Note total} = 0\), for both current and comparative columns. Also: sub-note totals = note total; ageing schedules (receivables, payables, CWIP) = face balances; PPE note closing net block = face; tax note (current tax + deferred tax) = P&L tax expense; DT note closing = BS DTA/DTL; EPS denominator consistent with share capital note.

6. Other checks that complete the set

Check Formula idea
Unmapped accounts Count = 0 (c02)
Suspense/clearing on error line Sum on "ERROR" Line ID = 0
Schedules tie to GL Lease, revenue, ECL, EIR, DT schedules closing balance − GL = 0
IC nets to zero Per counterparty pair (w4_t4 c02)
Comparatives Prior-year output = last audited FS (after documented regrouping)
Rounding Rounded statement casts; rounding differences ≤ 1 unit per line
Hard-codes Output cells without formulas = 0

7. Master check flag

Each check returns a difference; a check passes if \(\lvert \text{difference} \rvert < 0.005\) (or zero in rounded units). Excel:

=LET(diffs, Checks[Difference], fails, SUM(--(ABS(diffs)>=Tolerance)), IF(fails=0, "ALL CHECKS PASS", fails & " CHECK(S) FAILING"))

Place the master flag on every output tab header and on the dashboard; conditional formatting turns it red when failing. Each check row shows: description, value A, value B, difference, status, owner, and a hyperlink to the source.

The receivables ageing fails because the ageing schedule shows gross receivables while the face is net of ECL; the Schedule III ageing must reconcile to the face via the allowance line. Fix the schedule, not the check.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Tolerance: use a tiny tolerance for unrounded checks (floating-point noise) and zero tolerance in reporting units only after rounding logic is applied.
  • FX on cash for foreign-currency balances is a separate CF line (Ind AS 7.28) — omitting it causes a CF check failure equal to the unrealised FX on cash.
  • Restatements: opening RE in the check must be the restated opening balance (Ind AS 8); otherwise the P&L-to-RE check fails by the restatement amount.
  • Checks that cannot fail (e.g., a CF whose closing cash is linked to BS cash) are worthless; build each side independently.
  • Consolidated builds add checks for NCI share of profit and equity, goodwill roll-forward and FCTR movement.
  • Switching off checks to release a draft is a governance breach; use a documented override log instead.

Key takeaways

  • TB nets to zero proves completeness of postings; BS balances proves completeness of mapping.
  • P&L-to-RE ties the P&L, SOCIE and BS together, including dividends, transfers and restatements.
  • CF closing cash must be built independently and agree to BS cash less overdrafts included as cash equivalents.
  • Every note, sub-note and ageing schedule must tie to its face line in both years.
  • Roll all checks into one master flag displayed on every output; never plug a check.

Practice Questions

Q1. Diagnose a failing BS check [L2]

The final TB nets to zero but the BS check shows total assets exceed equity + liabilities by ₹14,26,000. The current-year profit is ₹7,13,000. List the most likely causes and the diagnostic steps you would take, in order.

💡 Hint: Look at the relationship between the difference and the profit figure.

Q2. CF check with overdraft and FX [L2]

Opening cash and cash equivalents ₹12.40 crore; net cash from operating ₹31.20 crore, investing (₹22.75 crore), financing (₹9.10 crore); unrealised FX gain on USD bank balances ₹0.35 crore. BS cash and bank ₹14.90 crore; overdraft repayable on demand used for cash management ₹2.80 crore. Determine whether the CF check passes and write the check formula with each component.

Q3. P&L to retained earnings [L2]

Profit for the year ₹46.0 crore; opening RE ₹180.0 crore (before a prior-period error correction reducing opening RE by ₹3.5 crore); final dividend paid ₹9.0 crore; transfer to general reserve ₹5.0 crore; remeasurement loss on defined benefit plans ₹1.2 crore transferred from OCI to RE. Closing RE per BS ₹207.3 crore. Perform the check and explain any difference.

Q4. Designing the checks tab [L3]

Design the checks tab for the Project 1 workbook covering at least 12 checks (including all five in the scope), with columns, formulas (structured references and named cells only), tolerance logic, master flag and the visual design for reviewers. Explain which checks must be independent of each other and why.
