Key concept

Schedule III Ratio Note and the 25% Explanation Rule

Concept: Ratio note: current, debt-equity, DSCR, ROE, inventory, receivables and payables turnover, net capital turnover, net profit, ROCE, ROI — explain changes >25%

Statement

Schedule III Division II (2021 amendment, additional regulatory information) requires disclosure of the following ratios for the current and previous year, with the numerator and denominator used and an explanation for any item where the change is more than 25% compared with the preceding year. Formulas below follow the ICAI Guidance Note on Division II (the Schedule does not prescribe formulas; disclose the definitions used):

Ratio Numerator Denominator
Current ratio Current assets Current liabilities
Debt-equity ratio Total debt (borrowings incl. lease liabilities, per policy) Shareholders' equity
Debt service coverage ratio Earnings available for debt service (PAT + non-cash operating expenses such as depreciation + finance costs + other adjustments) Debt service (interest + lease payments + principal repayments)
Return on equity PAT − preference dividend Average shareholders' equity
Inventory turnover Cost of goods sold (or sales) Average inventory
Trade receivables turnover Net credit sales Average trade receivables
Trade payables turnover Net credit purchases Average trade payables
Net capital turnover Net sales Working capital (current assets − current liabilities)
Net profit ratio Net profit (PAT) Net sales
Return on capital employed EBIT Capital employed (tangible net worth + total debt + deferred tax liability)
Return on investment Income generated from investments (time-weighted) Average/time-weighted investments

Change test:

\[ \Delta_{r} = \frac{r_{t} - r_{t-1}}{\lvert r_{t-1} \rvert}, \quad \text{explain if } \lvert \Delta_{r} \rvert > 25\% \]

where \(r_{t}\) and \(r_{t-1}\) are the current and previous-year values of the ratio.

Explanation

The ratio note is a mandatory, audited mini-MD&A. Practical points:

  • Consistency: definitions should be applied consistently year to year and disclosed; changing a definition requires recomputing the comparative.
  • Averages use opening and closing balances unless the business is highly seasonal (then disclose the basis).
  • Explanations must give the driver (new borrowing, capacity addition, price fall, receivable build-up), not restate the movement — the same discipline as flux commentary (w4_t6 c04).
  • Negative or near-zero denominators (negative working capital, losses) make % change meaningless; disclose the values and explain qualitatively.
  • Relative vs percentage-point change: for ratios expressed as percentages (net profit ratio, ROE), most preparers compute the relative change of the ratio; state the method.
  • Engine implementation: the mapping "ratio role" attribute (s02) tags each line as current asset, debt, EBIT component, etc., so the ratio table is generated from the final TB and updates with every adjustment.

The ratio note overlaps with but is not identical to SEBI LODR Schedule V MD&A ratios (which require explanations for changes of 25% or more in key ratios) — reconcile definitions across both documents (w4_t6 s06).

Illustration

FY 2025-26 vs FY 2024-25 (₹ crore):

Item FY 26 FY 25
Current assets 412 365
Current liabilities 298 240
Total debt 260 150
Shareholders' equity (closing) 520 470 (opening FY 25: 430)
PAT 58 66
Revenue 1,240 1,180

Current ratio: 1.38 vs 1.52 → −9.1%, no explanation needed. Debt-equity: 0.50 vs 0.32 (unrounded 0.319) → +56.7%, explain (new ₹110 crore term loan for the Hosur plant). ROE: 58 ÷ 495 = 11.7% vs 66 ÷ 450 = 14.7% → −20.1%, below 25%. Net profit ratio: 4.68% vs 5.59% → −16.4%. Compute changes on unrounded ratios to avoid borderline misclassification around 25%.

Practice Questions

Q1. Compute and flag [L2]

Using the following (₹ crore), compute the inventory turnover, trade receivables turnover, trade payables turnover and net capital turnover for both years and identify changes requiring explanation: COGS 820 / 760; average inventory 140 / 98; credit sales 1,150 / 1,090; average receivables 210 / 182; credit purchases 690 / 640; average payables 115 / 120; current assets 455 / 410; current liabilities 330 / 280; net sales 1,180 / 1,120.

💡 Hint: Compute each ratio first, then the relative change.

Q2. DSCR definition choices [L2]

A company has PAT ₹42 crore, depreciation ₹26 crore, finance costs ₹18 crore (including ₹4 crore lease interest), principal repayments ₹30 crore and lease principal ₹9 crore. Compute DSCR under the ICAI-style definition, discuss how the result changes if lease payments are excluded, and state what must be disclosed.

Q3. Drafting explanations [L3]

The debt-equity ratio rose 48% and ROCE fell 31%. Draft the Schedule III explanations (maximum 40 words each) and explain how they should align with the MD&A in the annual report and the lender covenant compliance certificate.
