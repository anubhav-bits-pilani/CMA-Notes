Key concept

Balance Sheet and Profit-to-Retained-Earnings Checks

Concept: Checks: Total assets − (Equity + Liabilities) = 0; Profit − (ΔRetained earnings + Dividends ± transfers) = 0

Statement

Balance sheet check:

\[ \text{BS}_{\text{diff}} = \text{TA} - \left(\text{E} + \text{L}\right) = 0 \]

where \(\text{TA}\) is total assets, \(\text{E}\) total equity (including NCI in consolidated FS) and \(\text{L}\) total liabilities, each computed from the output statement lines.

Profit-to-retained-earnings check:

\[ \text{RE}_{\text{diff}} = \pi - \left(\text{RE}_{c} - \text{RE}_{o}^{\text{restated}} + D \pm T\right) = 0 \]

where \(\pi\) is profit for the year attributable to owners (from the P&L output), \(\text{RE}_{c}\) closing retained earnings per the BS/SOCIE, \(\text{RE}_{o}^{\text{restated}}\) opening retained earnings after any Ind AS 8 restatement, \(D\) dividends declared/paid out of RE during the year, and \(T\) transfers between RE and other reserves (to general reserve or DRR as a deduction; remeasurement of defined benefit plans transferred from OCI, or realised FVOCI equity gains transferred on derecognition, as additions or deductions depending on direction).

Explanation

The two checks prove different things:

  • The BS check proves that every balance has landed on exactly one BS line with the correct sign. Because profit for the year is an equity component in a TB-based build, it also implicitly proves that the P&L total has been carried into equity.
  • The P&L-to-RE check proves that the P&L, the SOCIE and the BS equity line agree — that profit has not been double-counted, that dividends and transfers are captured, and that the opening balance is the audited (or restated) one. A BS can balance while RE is wrong if an offsetting error sits in another equity reserve; the RE check catches that.

Common failure patterns:

Symptom Likely cause
BS diff = 2 × profit Profit carried to equity with the wrong sign
BS diff = a single account balance Account unmapped or mapped to a non-existent line (c02)
BS diff = round amount Hard-coded override in an output cell
RE diff = dividend Dividend debited to a separate account not mapped to RE in SOCIE
RE diff = prior-period adjustment Opening RE not restated, or restated in BS but not in SOCIE
RE diff = OCI remeasurement Policy transfers OCI to RE but SOCIE does not show it

Both are built from independently computed figures — a check where one side is a link to the other cannot fail and proves nothing.

Illustration

FY 2025-26, ₹ crore:

Item Amount
Profit for the year (P&L output) 64.20
Opening RE as audited 310.50
Ind AS 8 correction of prior-year revenue cut-off error (net of tax) (4.30)
Restated opening RE 306.20
Final dividend for FY 2024-25 paid (12.00)
Transfer to debenture redemption reserve (2.50)
Remeasurement loss on DB plan transferred from OCI (0.90)
Closing RE per BS 355.00

\[ \text{RE}_{\text{diff}} = 64.20 - \left(355.00 - 306.20 + 12.00 + 2.50 + 0.90\right) = 64.20 - 64.20 = 0 \]

Had the preparer used unrestated opening RE of 310.50, the check would show a difference of (4.30) — exactly the restatement — pointing straight to the cause.

Connections

Part of s06 integrity checks with the TB, CF and notes checks; relies on c01 sign convention and c02 unmapped check; the SOCIE generation in s03. Week 4 cross-cutting item: Project 1 check flags (BS balances). In 3-statement modelling, the same two checks are the standard model integrity tests (balance check and RE roll-forward).

Practice Questions

Q1. Perform both checks [L1]

Total assets ₹842.6 crore; equity ₹402.1 crore; liabilities ₹440.5 crore. Profit ₹51.3 crore; opening RE ₹188.0 crore; interim dividend ₹8.0 crore; transfer to general reserve ₹5.0 crore; closing RE ₹229.9 crore. Perform both checks and state whether the FS can be released.

💡 Hint: One check can pass while the other fails.

Q2. Diagnose from the difference [L2]

The BS check difference is ₹0.00 but the RE check shows ₹3.60 crore. The company declared a ₹3.60 crore dividend on 25 April (after year-end) for FY 2025-26, which the preparer accrued as a liability at 31 March. Explain the error with reference to Ind AS 10 and correct both the BS and the check.

Q3. Consolidated version [L3]

Extend both check formulas for consolidated FS with non-controlling interests, FCTR and an acquisition of additional interest in a subsidiary during the year (accounted in equity). Define every term.
