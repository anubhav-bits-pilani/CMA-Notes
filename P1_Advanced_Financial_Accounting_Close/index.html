Phase 1: Advanced Financial Accounting & Close Mastery

Weeks 1–4 (192 Hours)

Bridge CMA audit/compliance background into complex Ind AS technical accounting, multi-entity consolidation, and institutional month-end/year-end close governance.

Phase deliverable: Complete Month-End Closing Workbook (GL Trial Balance, Ind AS Adjustment Journals, Reconciliations, Fixed Asset & Lease Schedules, Schedule III Financial Statements).

Week 1: Ind AS Core Framework, Presentation & Revenue Recognition (Ind AS 1, 8, 10, 115)

Transition from voucher-level compliance to Schedule III Division II financial statement architecture and 5-step revenue recognition accounting.

  • T1 · Ind AS 1 & Schedule III Division II
  • T2 · Ind AS 8 errors/estimates, Ind AS 10 events
  • T3 · Ind AS 115 five-step model
  • T4 · Over-time vs point-in-time, contract balances
  • T5 · Ind AS 2 NRV and Ind AS 33 EPS
  • T6 · Ind AS 7 indirect-method cash flows
Week 2: Long-Term Assets, Leases, Impairment & Employee Benefits (Ind AS 16, 19, 36, 37, 116)

Master complex balance sheet schedules for PPE componentisation, Right-of-Use lease accounting, CGU impairment testing, and actuarial provisions.

  • T1 · Ind AS 16 components and decommissioning
  • T2 · Ind AS 116 lessee accounting and modifications
  • T3 · Ind AS 36 impairment and CGU testing
  • T4 · Ind AS 37 provisions and contingencies
  • T5 · Ind AS 19 gratuity and leave encashment
  • T6 · Ind AS 24 related parties and KMP
Week 3: Financial Instruments, Deferred Taxes & Business Combinations (Ind AS 12, 103, 107, 109, 113)

Execute technical accounting for financial assets/liabilities, Expected Credit Loss (ECL), deferred tax balance-sheet proofs, and acquisition accounting.

  • T1 · Ind AS 109 classification and EIR
  • T2 · Ind AS 109 simplified ECL matrix
  • T3 · Ind AS 12 deferred tax and 115BAA
  • T4 · Ind AS 103 acquisitions and PPA
  • T5 · Ind AS 110 consolidation and NCI
  • T6 · Ind AS 113 hierarchy, Ind AS 107 risks
Week 4: Institutional Month-End & Year-End Close Governance (Project 1 Delivery)

Standardize an R2R (Record-to-Report) month-end close calendar, automated reconciliation workpapers, and audit-ready financial statements.

  • T1 · R2R close calendar, T+1 to T+7
  • T2 · Bank, GST and TDS reconciliations
  • T3 · Accrual and prepayment control schedules
  • T4 · Inter-company and suspense clearing protocols
  • T5 · TB-to-Schedule III mapping engine
  • T6 · Close flux commentary for management
