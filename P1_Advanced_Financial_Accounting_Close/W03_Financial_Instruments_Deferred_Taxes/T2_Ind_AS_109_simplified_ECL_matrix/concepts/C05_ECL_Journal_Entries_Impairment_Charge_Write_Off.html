Key concept

ECL Journal Entries: Impairment Charge, Write-Off and Recovery

Concept: Entries: Dr Impairment loss on financial assets Cr ECL allowance; write-off Dr ECL allowance Cr Trade receivables

Statement

Event Debit Credit
Increase in allowance (period-end true-up) Impairment loss on financial assets (P&L) Allowance for ECL
Decrease in allowance Allowance for ECL Impairment loss / reversal (P&L)
Write-off of amount covered by allowance Allowance for ECL Trade receivables
Write-off exceeding allowance Allowance for ECL; Impairment loss (excess) Trade receivables
Recovery after write-off Bank Impairment loss (reversal) or Bad debts recovered

The period charge reconciles as:

\[ \text{Impairment charge} = \text{Closing allowance} - \text{Opening allowance} + \text{Write-offs against allowance} - \text{Recoveries credited to allowance} \]

Explanation

The allowance is a contra-asset; the gross receivable stays at the invoiced amount until written off, so ageing and Schedule III gross disclosures remain intact. Ind AS 109.5.5.8 requires the amount of ECL (or reversal) needed to adjust the loss allowance to the required amount at the reporting date to be recognised in P&L as an impairment gain or loss. Schedule III Division II presents "Impairment loss on financial assets" typically within other expenses (separately disclosed if material), and Ind AS 1.82(ba) requires impairment losses (including reversals) determined under Ind AS 109 to be presented as a separate line item in the statement of profit and loss.

The write-off itself has no P&L effect if fully provided — the P&L cost was recognised when the allowance was built. This is why tax deductions (on write-off, Sec 36(1)(vii)) and accounting expense fall in different periods, creating a DTA.

For FVOCI debt assets, the credit side of the ECL entry is OCI (the fair value reserve), not an allowance against the asset (5.5.2).

Practical points: book the true-up at each quarter-end (SEBI LODR Reg 33 quarterly results); keep the allowance at customer or segment level to support the Ind AS 107.35H reconciliation; and do not net recoveries into the charge without disclosing them.

Illustration

Manish Electricals Ltd, FY 2025-26 (₹ Cr): opening allowance 6.0; write-offs against allowance during the year 1.8 (two customers liquidated); recovery of a debt written off in FY 2023-24 0.3; closing required allowance per matrix and specific assessment 7.2.

Charge = 7.2 − 6.0 + 1.8 = ₹3.0 Cr.

Q1. Entries and reconciliation [L1]

Q2. FVOCI bond ECL entry [L2]

A company holds an FVOCI bond with fair value ₹9.6 Cr and amortised cost ₹10 Cr; 12-month ECL rises from ₹0.04 Cr to ₹0.10 Cr. Pass the ECL entry and explain why no allowance account appears on the balance sheet.

Q3. Quarterly true-ups and tax [L2]

A listed company books ECL true-ups quarterly. Explain how the quarterly P&L charges, write-offs and the year-end DTA computation interact, and what a reviewer should check in the Q4 results.
