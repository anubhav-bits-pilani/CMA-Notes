Key concept

Upstream vs Downstream URP Journal Entries

Concept: Upstream: Dr Group retained earnings (parent %) Dr NCI (NCI %) Cr Inventory; downstream: charged wholly to parent

Statement

For an unrealised profit \(U\) (net of tax effect \(U(1 - t)\) if DT is recognised) in inventory held by a group entity:

Upstream (subsidiary with NCI share \(n\) sells to parent):

Account Dr Cr
Group retained earnings / consolidated profit — owners' share \((1 - n) \times U\)
Non-controlling interest \(n \times U\)
Inventory \(U\)

Downstream (parent sells to subsidiary):

Account Dr Cr
Group retained earnings / consolidated profit — owners \(U\)
Inventory \(U\)

The DTA (\(U \times t_{\text{buyer}}\)) is credited to the same parties in the same proportions.

Explanation

The amount eliminated is always 100% (Ind AS 110.B86(c)), because the whole inventory is in the consolidated balance sheet. The attribution follows the entity that booked the profit:

  • In an upstream sale, the subsidiary recorded the profit; its profit is shared with NCI. When the profit is reversed, NCI's share of the subsidiary's profit is reduced proportionately.
  • In a downstream sale, the parent recorded the profit; NCI never had a share, so the parent's owners bear the entire elimination — even if the buying subsidiary is partly owned.

In a workbook, the P&L side is posted to cost of sales (current-year element) and opening retained earnings/NCI (prior-year element reversing), while the attribution is achieved by adjusting the subsidiary's profit used for the NCI computation. The formulation "Dr Group retained earnings" in the concept is the balance-sheet view at period-end, combining both.

Two errors to avoid:

  1. Eliminating upstream URP wholly against the parent — overstates NCI and understates owners' equity.
  2. Eliminating only the parent's share — that is the equity method rule for associates (Ind AS 28.28), not the rule for subsidiaries.

Illustration

P owns 70% of S. Closing inventory includes ₹30 Cr purchased intra-group at a 25% margin on sales; tax 25.168%. URP = ₹7.5 Cr; DTA = ₹1.89 Cr; net = ₹5.61 Cr.

(a) S sold to P (upstream):

Account Dr (₹ Cr) Cr (₹ Cr)
Retained earnings — owners (70% × 5.61) 3.93
NCI (30% × 5.61) 1.68
Deferred tax asset 1.89
Inventory 7.50

(b) P sold to S (downstream):

Account Dr (₹ Cr) Cr (₹ Cr)
Retained earnings — owners 5.61
Deferred tax asset 1.89
Inventory 7.50

Consolidated inventory and DTA are identical in both cases; only the split of equity between owners and NCI differs.

Practice Questions

Q1. Pass both sets of entries [L1]

P owns 85% of S. Intra-group goods of ₹40 Cr (mark-up 33⅓% on cost) remain in the buyer's inventory; tax 25.168%. Pass the elimination entries if (a) S sold to P and (b) P sold to S.

Q2. Sideways sale [L2]

P owns 90% of S1 and 60% of S2. S2 sold goods to S1; S1's closing inventory includes ₹18 Cr of these at a 20% margin; S1's tax rate is 25.168%. Pass the elimination entry and explain whose NCI bears the reduction.

💡 Hint: Attribution follows the selling entity.

Q3. Correct the NCI [L2]

A consolidation shows NCI of ₹124 Cr in a 75% subsidiary, after eliminating upstream URP of ₹16 Cr (net of tax ₹11.97 Cr) entirely against the parent's retained earnings. Recompute the correct NCI and owners' equity and draft the correcting entry.
