Key concept

Consolidation Control Checks: TB Tie-out and IC Netting

Concept: Check: Σ entity TBs + eliminations = consolidated TB; intra-group balances net to zero

Statement

For each line item \(k\) in the consolidated trial balance:

\[ \text{CTB}_{k} = \sum_{e=1}^{E} \text{TB}_{e,k} + \sum_{j=1}^{J} \text{EJ}_{j,k} \]

where \(\text{TB}_{e,k}\) is entity \(e\)'s (policy-aligned, translated) balance for line \(k\), and \(\text{EJ}_{j,k}\) is consolidation journal \(j\)'s effect on line \(k\). Each journal balances (\(\sum_{k} \text{EJ}_{j,k} = 0\)), each entity TB balances, so the consolidated TB balances. Additionally, for every pair of group entities \((a, b)\) and every intra-group account pair:

\[ \text{IC}_{a \to b} + \text{IC}_{b \to a} = 0 \]

after in-transit and dispute adjustments, and the consolidated value of every intra-group account is zero.

Explanation

Consolidation workbooks are spreadsheets maintained under deadline pressure; control checks turn them into auditable models. The two checks in the concept are the minimum:

  1. Tie-out check. The consolidated TB must be fully explained by entity TBs and documented journals — no hard-coded plugs in the consolidated column. Each consolidation journal is numbered, described and reviewed.
  2. IC netting check. After elimination, every intra-group account (IC receivable/payable, IC revenue/expense, IC loans, IC interest, IC dividends, investment in subsidiary vs share capital) must be zero. Residual differences mean either a missed elimination or an unreconciled difference that must be investigated, not plugged.

Further standard check rows:

  • Balance sheet balances; total equity = owners' equity + NCI.
  • NCI roll-forward closing = NCI% × subsidiary net assets in CFS (+ NCI goodwill) (c03).
  • Goodwill = acquisition working balance less cumulative impairment (translated at closing rate for foreign operations).
  • FCTR movement = translation computation.
  • Retained earnings roll-forward: opening + profit attributable to owners − dividends ± transfers = closing.
  • Cash and cash equivalents in CFS = Σ entity cash (no eliminations should hit cash, except cash in transit).
  • Cash flow statement reconciles to balance sheet movement.
  • DT on eliminations recognised and attributed.

Connections

Links to s03 (workbook procedure), s04 (eliminations), c03 (NCI check), and Week 4 (w4_t4) intercompany reconciliation as the pre-consolidation control that makes the IC netting check pass. In FP&A, the same check discipline applies to consolidated budgets and forecasts: intra-group revenue and charges must net to zero in the group plan, otherwise the forecast overstates revenue. Power BI consolidation dashboards typically include an "IC mismatch" measure with drill-down by entity pair.

Practice Questions

Q1. Diagnose a failed check [L2]

After eliminations, the IC receivable line shows ₹6 Cr and IC payable ₹0; the IC revenue line is ₹0. List three possible causes and the evidence you would examine for each.

💡 Hint: Consider in-transit items, FX remeasurement and entity mapping errors.

Q2. Design check rows [L3]

For a group with a parent, two Indian subsidiaries (one 75%) and a US subsidiary, design ten check rows for the consolidation workbook, specifying the formula logic and tolerance for each.

Q3. Power BI measure [L2]

Write a DAX measure that returns the total intra-group mismatch by entity pair from a table ICBalances[Entity, Counterparty, Amount], and describe how you would visualise it for the close review meeting.
