Key concept

The 10% Test for Substantial Modification of Liabilities

Concept: 10% test: PV of new cash flows (incl. fees) at original EIR differs ≥10% from carrying amount → extinguishment

Statement

Under Ind AS 109 B3.3.6, the terms of an exchanged or modified financial liability are substantially different if:

\[ \frac{\left\lvert PV_{\text{new}} - PV_{\text{old}} \right\rvert}{PV_{\text{old}}} \geq 10\% \]

where \( PV_{\text{new}} \) is the present value of the cash flows under the new terms, including any fees paid net of any fees received (only fees between borrower and lender, including fees paid or received by either on the other's behalf), discounted at the original effective interest rate; and \( PV_{\text{old}} \) is the present value of the remaining cash flows of the original liability at the same rate — which equals the current amortised cost (carrying amount). If the threshold is met, the original liability is extinguished and a new liability recognised (3.3.2); otherwise the change is a modification.

Explanation

The test uses the original EIR so that market-rate changes do not by themselves trigger extinguishment; only changes in contractual terms are measured. Fees are included because a borrower can trade a lower coupon for an upfront payment.

Practical points:

  • Use the current EIR for floating-rate liabilities (the rate after the last reset).
  • If the new terms include an option (prepayment, extension), consider the expected exercise or test the scenarios.
  • The quantitative test is a bright line, but qualitative factors — currency change, equity conversion feature, change of obligor, subordination — can cause extinguishment even below 10%.
  • Third-party costs (legal counsel, rating) are not part of the test; they are treated as part of the extinguishment gain/loss or added to the carrying amount on a modification.
  • On extinguishment, all fees incurred go to P&L with the gain/loss; on modification, fees adjust the carrying amount and are amortised over the remaining term.

Illustration

Ira Logistics Ltd's bullet loan: carrying amount ₹19.79 Cr at 1 April 2026, principal ₹20 Cr, 10% coupon, original EIR 10.6%, 2 years left. Proposal: coupon 8%, tenor extended to 4 years, borrower pays a ₹30 lakh fee to the lender.

\[ PV_{\text{new}} = 1.6 \times \frac{1-(1.106)^{-4}}{0.106} + \frac{20}{(1.106)^4} + 0.30 = 5.01 + 13.37 + 0.30 = 18.68 \text{ Cr} \]

Difference = (19.79 − 18.68) ÷ 19.79 = 5.6% → modification. Recalculate amortised cost at 10.6% excluding fee = ₹18.38 Cr; modification gain ₹1.41 Cr in P&L; the ₹0.30 Cr fee reduces the carrying amount to ₹18.08 Cr, and a revised EIR is solved for the remaining term.

If instead the coupon fell to 6% with the same extension, \( PV_{\text{new}} \) ≈ ₹17.42 Cr including the fee (12% difference) → extinguishment: derecognise ₹19.79 Cr, recognise the new loan at fair value, and recognise the fee within the gain/loss.

Connections

  • s07 covers the full modification/extinguishment framework, refinancing and restructuring.
  • c01 supplies the original EIR used for discounting.
  • Pitfall: treating every interest reset as a modification — contractual resets are not tested (c06).
  • Tax and deferred tax: a modification gain not taxable now creates a temporary difference (w3_t3).
  • FP&A: modification gains are non-recurring items to strip out of adjusted EBITDA/PAT bridges.

Practice Questions

Q1. Apply the test with fees received [L2]

A company's ₹30 Cr loan (carrying amount ₹29.59 Cr, original EIR 9.8%, 3 years remaining, 9.25% coupon, bullet) is amended: coupon 9.75%, remaining tenor 3 years unchanged, and the lender pays the borrower ₹1.5 Cr as a restructuring incentive. Perform the test and pass the entries.

💡 Hint: Fees received reduce the PV of new cash flows paid by the borrower.

Q2. Qualitative override [L2]

A ₹100 Cr INR loan is converted into a USD loan of equal value at the spot rate, with the same tenor and a USD rate that makes the PV difference only 3%. Discuss whether extinguishment accounting applies, and the consequences for the income statement.

Q3. Floating-rate loan modification [L1]

Explain which discount rate you would use for the 10% test on a repo-linked loan whose spread is renegotiated from 3.0% to 2.25%, and why.
