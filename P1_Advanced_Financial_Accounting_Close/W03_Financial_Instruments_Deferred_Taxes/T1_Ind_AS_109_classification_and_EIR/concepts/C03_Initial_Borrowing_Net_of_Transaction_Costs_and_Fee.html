Key concept

Initial Borrowing Net of Transaction Costs and Fee Unwind

Concept: Initial borrowing = Proceeds − Directly attributable transaction costs; Dr Bank Cr Borrowings (net), fees unwind through finance cost

Statement

A financial liability not at FVTPL is initially measured at fair value minus transaction costs directly attributable to its issue (Ind AS 109.5.1.1):

\[ L_0 = P_0 - C_0 \]

where \( L_0 \) is the initial carrying amount of the borrowing, \( P_0 \) the fair value of proceeds (normally the gross amount sanctioned and disbursed, for an at-market loan) and \( C_0 \) the incremental costs directly attributable to the loan (processing fee, stamp duty, legal and arranger fees, non-creditable GST). The entry is Dr Bank (or Dr Bank and Dr cash paid for costs) / Cr Borrowings at \( L_0 \). The costs are not a separate asset; they are recovered through the EIR, so finance cost over the life equals coupon plus \( C_0 \).

Explanation

Netting reflects the economics: the borrower receives only \( L_0 \) of usable cash but repays \( P_0 \). The difference is a cost of borrowing like interest and is spread over the life. Whether the bank deducts the fee from disbursement or the borrower pays it separately does not matter — both routes produce the same net liability.

Conditions and exceptions:

  • Costs must be incremental — they would not have been incurred without the loan (B5.4.8). Internal costs and general overheads are excluded.
  • If the borrowing is designated at FVTPL, costs are expensed immediately.
  • For an undrawn facility likely to be drawn, the fee is deferred as an asset until drawdown, then deducted from the liability (B5.4.2(b)).
  • For a loan raised to fund a qualifying asset, the EIR finance cost (including fee unwind) is the borrowing cost eligible for capitalisation under Ind AS 23.

Presentation: Borrowings in Schedule III are shown at amortised cost; the note may disclose principal and unamortised costs separately. Cash flow statement: proceeds and costs are financing cash flows.

Illustration

Sanya Ceramics Ltd draws a ₹10 Cr term loan on 1 April 2025. The bank deducts a processing fee of ₹10 lakh plus GST ₹1.8 lakh (input credit available) and disburses the balance. The company separately pays stamp duty ₹4 lakh and legal fees ₹2 lakh.

Entry Dr (₹ lakh) Cr (₹ lakh)
Bank 988.2
GST input credit 1.8
Term loan (gross receipt before fee) 990.0
Term loan (stamp duty and legal) 6.0
Bank 6.0

Net initial carrying amount = ₹1,000 − 10 − 4 − 2 = ₹984 lakh. Total ₹16 lakh unwinds through finance cost by EIR. In year 1 if EIR interest is ₹96.1 lakh and coupon ₹92.5 lakh, the entry is Dr Finance cost 96.1; Cr Bank/Interest accrued 92.5; Cr Term loan 3.6.

Practice Questions

Q1. Net-of-fees entries for an NCD issue [L1]

A company issues ₹200 Cr of listed NCDs at par, paying arranger fees ₹1.2 Cr, stamp duty ₹0.10 Cr, listing and trustee acceptance fees ₹0.05 Cr, and annual trustee fees of ₹5 lakh from year 1. Pass the initial recognition entries and identify the amounts excluded from the net carrying amount.

Q2. Fees on a facility drawn in tranches [L2]

A ₹30 Cr capex facility carries an upfront fee of ₹45 lakh; ₹10 Cr is drawn on 1 April 2025 and the remaining ₹20 Cr on 1 October 2025. All drawdowns were probable at sanction. Explain how you allocate the fee to the tranches and set up the EIR schedules.

💡 Hint: Defer the fee relating to the undrawn portion as an asset until that tranche is drawn.

Q3. Review finding [L2]

During audit you find that a ₹25 lakh processing fee on a ₹15 Cr loan drawn on 1 October 2025 was debited to "Bank charges". The loan has a 5-year tenor and 9% coupon. Quantify the misstatement at 31 March 2026 and draft the correcting entry and the deferred tax effect.
