Key concept

Level 3 Roll-forward Reconciliation

Concept: Level 3 roll-forward: Opening + Purchases − Sales ± Gains/losses (P&L/OCI) ± Transfers in/out = Closing

Statement

For each class of recurring Level 3 fair value measurements (Ind AS 113.93(e)):

\[ C = O + \text{Pur} + \text{Iss} - \text{Sal} - \text{Set} + G_{\text{P\&L}} + G_{\text{OCI}} + T_{\text{in}} - T_{\text{out}} \]

where \(O\) and \(C\) are opening and closing Level 3 fair values; \(\text{Pur}\), \(\text{Iss}\), \(\text{Sal}\), \(\text{Set}\) are purchases, issues, sales and settlements (disclosed separately); \(G_{\text{P\&L}}\) and \(G_{\text{OCI}}\) are total gains or losses (negative for losses) recognised in P&L and OCI, with the line items in which they are recognised; and \(T_{\text{in}}\), \(T_{\text{out}}\) are transfers into and out of Level 3, with the reasons and the policy for timing (113.95). Additionally, disclose the portion of \(G_{\text{P\&L}}\) that is unrealised on items held at the period end (113.93(f)).

Explanation

The roll-forward separates what the entity did (bought, sold, issued, settled) from what valuations did (gains/losses) and what observability did (transfers). Users can then see how much of the change in Level 3 balances came from management's model-based remeasurement.

Points:

  • Gross presentation — purchases and sales are not netted; issues and settlements (liabilities such as earn-outs) are separate.
  • Gains on items sold during the period are included in \(G_{\text{P\&L}}\) up to the sale date; the sale line shows proceeds/derecognised carrying amount consistent with the policy. Unrealised gains (113.93(f)) exclude them.
  • Liabilities — e.g., contingent consideration: issues (new acquisitions), settlements (payments), FV changes in P&L (sign conventions must be explained).
  • Transfers — a consistent policy (beginning of period, end of period, or date of event) for transfers in and out. With a beginning-of-period policy, the year's gains on an item transferred out appear outside the Level 3 roll-forward.
  • FX — for foreign-currency Level 3 items, FX translation effects are a separate line or included in gains with explanation.

Illustration

Contingent consideration liabilities (Level 3), ₹ Cr:

Line FY26
Opening balance 48
Issues — new acquisition (Q2) 22
Settlements — earn-out paid (15)
Loss recognised in P&L — "Other expenses: FV change on contingent consideration" 9
Unwind recognised in finance cost 4
Transfers in/out –
Closing balance 68
Unrealised losses in P&L on liabilities held at year-end 11

Check: 48 + 22 − 15 + 9 + 4 = 68. The unrealised figure (11) excludes the ₹2 Cr remeasurement on the earn-out settled during the year (9 + 4 − 2 = 11).

Practice Questions

Q1. Prepare a roll-forward [L2]

Level 3 unlisted equity (FVTPL): opening ₹420 Cr. Purchases ₹75 Cr. One investment (opening FV ₹60 Cr) sold on 30 September for ₹82 Cr (FV at 30 September ₹80 Cr). Year-end FV gains on remaining investments ₹38 Cr. An investment (opening FV ₹45 Cr) listed on NSE on 1 January; policy: transfers at the date of event; its FV on 1 January was ₹58 Cr. Prepare the roll-forward and unrealised gains line.

💡 Hint: Track gains on the sold and transferred items up to their exit dates.

Q2. Transfer timing policy [L2]

Using Q1's listed investment, show how the roll-forward differs under a beginning-of-period transfer policy and an end-of-period policy, given its 31 March FV was ₹71 Cr.

Q3. Reconcile to the hierarchy table [L1]

The Level 3 column of the hierarchy table shows ₹530 Cr, but the roll-forward closes at ₹512 Cr. List possible causes of the difference and the checks to run.
