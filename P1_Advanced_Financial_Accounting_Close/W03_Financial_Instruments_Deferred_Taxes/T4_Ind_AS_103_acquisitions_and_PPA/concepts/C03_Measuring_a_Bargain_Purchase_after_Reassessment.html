Key concept

Measuring a Bargain Purchase after Reassessment

Concept: Bargain purchase = FV of net assets − (Consideration + NCI + Previously held interest), only after reassessing all measurements

Statement

\[ B = A - (C + N + P), \quad B > 0 \]

where \(A\) is the acquisition-date amount of identifiable net assets measured under Ind AS 103 (including Ind AS 12 deferred tax and the other exceptions), \(C\) consideration transferred at fair value, \(N\) the NCI under the elected basis, and \(P\) the acquisition-date fair value of any previously held equity interest. \(B\) may be recognised only after the reassessment required by 103.36. Under the Ind AS carve-out, \(B\) is credited to capital reserve — via OCI if there is clear evidence of the underlying reasons for the bargain, otherwise directly in equity (103.34–36A).

Explanation

A bargain purchase is the mirror image of goodwill: the formula is the same, the sign is reversed. Because a genuine bargain is economically unusual (sellers rarely give away value), Ind AS 103 treats it with suspicion:

  • Reassessment first. Re-examine identification of all assets and liabilities (missed contingent liabilities, environmental obligations, onerous contracts, employee claims) and review the measurement procedures for \(A\), \(N\), \(P\) and \(C\). Economic obsolescence on plant, the DTL on step-ups, and fair-value NCI are the common corrections.
  • Then classify the reason. Distress sales (IBC resolution plans, lender-forced sales, regulatory divestments, promoter liquidity crises) provide clear evidence → OCI → capital reserve. If no reason can be articulated → directly to capital reserve. Either way, nothing in P&L.
  • Interaction with NCI choice. Fair value NCI increases \(N\) and can eliminate a bargain that exists under the proportionate method.
  • Measurement period. Provisional bargain amounts are adjusted retrospectively within 12 months for acquisition-date facts (103.45–48), changing capital reserve rather than goodwill.
  • Distributability. Capital reserve is not a realised profit and is not available for dividends under Sec 123.

Illustration

Nav Udaya Ltd acquires 70% of a stressed textile company through an IBC resolution plan for ₹210 Cr. Initial FV of identifiable net assets: ₹420 Cr (including DTL). NCI proportionate: 30% × 420 = ₹126 Cr.

Initial excess = 420 − (210 + 126) = ₹84 Cr.

Reassessment findings: (i) looms valued at replacement cost need a ₹50 Cr economic obsolescence adjustment, reducing DTL by ₹12.6 Cr at 25.168%; (ii) an unrecorded ₹10 Cr provident fund damages claim (deductible on payment; DTA ₹2.5 Cr).

Revised \(A\) = 420 − 50 + 12.6 − 10 + 2.5 = ₹375.1 Cr. Revised \(N\) = 30% × 375.1 = ₹112.5 Cr. Bargain = 375.1 − (210 + 112.5) = ₹52.6 Cr.

Clear evidence (IBC process) → Cr OCI ₹52.6 Cr, then accumulated in capital reserve.

Connections

Links to s06 (carve-out mechanics and disclosures), c01 (same equation as goodwill), s05 (NCI choice can create or remove the bargain), s07 (previously held interest and measurement period), and w3_t3 (DT on step-ups and economic obsolescence). In w3_t5 consolidation, the capital reserve appears in consolidated other equity; it is attributable to owners only, since NCI is measured on net assets. For credit analysis, bargain-purchase reserves are excluded from tangible net worth computations by many lenders.

Practice Questions

Q1. Bargain with fair value NCI [L2]

Consideration ₹400 Cr for 60%; FV of identifiable net assets ₹720 Cr. Compute the bargain purchase (or goodwill) if NCI is measured (a) at proportionate share, (b) at fair value of ₹300 Cr. State the accounting route in each case if the seller was a lender-driven distressed promoter.

Q2. Reassessment workpaper [L3]

Prepare a reassessment checklist for a ₹180 Cr bargain purchase on a cement plant acquired under an IBC plan, covering identification, PPE valuation basis, DT, contingent liabilities, NCI and consideration. For each item, specify the evidence an auditor would request.

💡 Hint: Tie each checklist item to an element of the formula.

Q3. Measurement-period revision [L2]

A provisional bargain purchase of ₹40 Cr was credited to capital reserve via OCI at acquisition on 1 December 2025. In June 2026 the final valuation (using acquisition-date data) raises inventory FV by ₹8 Cr and identifies a ₹20 Cr environmental liability. Tax rate 25.168%. Compute the revised bargain and pass the adjusting entry, explaining how FY26 comparatives are presented.
