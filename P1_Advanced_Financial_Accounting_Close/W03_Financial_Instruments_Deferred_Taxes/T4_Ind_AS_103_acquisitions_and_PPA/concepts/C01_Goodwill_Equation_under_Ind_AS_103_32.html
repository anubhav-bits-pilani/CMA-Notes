Key concept

Goodwill Equation under Ind AS 103.32

Concept: Goodwill = Consideration transferred + NCI + FV of previously held interest − FV of identifiable net assets (103.32)

Statement

\[ G = C + N + P - A \]

where:

  • \(G\) = goodwill recognised at the acquisition date (if negative, a bargain purchase — see c03);
  • \(C\) = consideration transferred, measured at acquisition-date fair value (cash, shares, deferred and contingent consideration) under 103.37–40;
  • \(N\) = non-controlling interest in the acquiree, at fair value or proportionate share of identifiable net assets (103.19);
  • \(P\) = acquisition-date fair value of the acquirer's previously held equity interest in the acquiree (step acquisitions, 103.42);
  • \(A\) = net of the acquisition-date amounts of identifiable assets acquired and liabilities assumed, measured under Ind AS 103 (fair value plus the exceptions for deferred tax, employee benefits, indemnification assets, leases and others).

Explanation

Goodwill is a residual — the future economic benefits from assets that are not individually identified and separately recognised: synergies, assembled workforce, going-concern element, and any overpayment. Ind AS 103 measures it indirectly as the excess of the "value of the whole" (what was paid plus what others hold) over the "value of the identifiable parts".

Points of discipline:

  • All three components of the "whole" are at fair value, except NCI if the proportionate method is elected.
  • \(A\) is not pure fair value. It includes DT measured under Ind AS 12 (the DTL on step-ups increases goodwill, see c02) and Ind AS 19 employee benefits.
  • What is excluded from \(C\): acquisition costs (103.53), remuneration to selling shareholder-employees (B55), settlement of pre-existing relationships (B52) — all accounted separately.
  • No DTL on goodwill itself — Ind AS 12.15(a) exempts initial recognition of goodwill, otherwise the computation would be circular. In India, goodwill is also not tax-depreciable from AY 2021-22, so its tax base is nil.
  • Subsequent measurement: not amortised; tested for impairment annually and whenever indicators exist (Ind AS 36.90), at the CGU level.
  • Measurement period: provisional goodwill is adjusted retrospectively for acquisition-date information within 12 months (103.45).

Illustration

Unnati Ltd held 15% of Prabhat Ltd (FVTPL; FV on acquisition date ₹45 Cr). On 1 November 2025 it buys a further 55% for ₹200 Cr cash plus an earn-out with FV ₹18 Cr. Acquisition costs ₹3 Cr. Prabhat's book net assets ₹180 Cr; FV step-ups on PPE ₹40 Cr and customer relationships ₹60 Cr; tax rate 25.168%. NCI (30%) at proportionate share.

₹ Cr
Book net assets 180.0
Step-ups (40 + 60) 100.0
DTL on step-ups (100 × 25.168%) (25.2)
\(A\) 254.8
\(C\) = 200 + 18 (costs excluded) 218.0
\(N\) = 30% × 254.8 76.4
\(P\) 45.0
Goodwill = 218.0 + 76.4 + 45.0 − 254.8 84.6

Acquisition costs of ₹3 Cr are expensed. The FVTPL stake was already at fair value, so only any movement up to the acquisition date passes through P&L.

Connections

Links to s02 (consideration measurement), s03 (identifiable net assets and exceptions), s05 (NCI choice), s07 (previously held interest, measurement period, costs), c02 (PPA DTL), c03 (bargain purchase). Goodwill impairment testing links to Week 2 Ind AS 36 (w2_t3). In consolidation (w3_t5), goodwill is the balancing figure of the investment-elimination entry. In FP&A, goodwill drives ROCE denominators and impairment headroom analysis; modelled goodwill does not amortise, so EPS forecasts include only PPA intangible amortisation.

Practice Questions

Q1. Compute goodwill with all components [L2]

Acquirer held 20% of Target (associate, carrying ₹70 Cr, FV ₹110 Cr) and buys 45% more for ₹260 Cr cash and 4 lakh shares (quoted ₹900 on completion). Target's FV of identifiable assets is ₹820 Cr and liabilities ₹240 Cr, before DTL on ₹150 Cr of step-ups at 25.168%. NCI at fair value ₹190 Cr. Compute goodwill and the P&L gain on the previously held interest.

Q2. Spot the errors [L2]

A junior's goodwill working: consideration ₹500 Cr (including ₹6 Cr legal fees and ₹20 Cr extra paid to founders conditional on 3-year employment), NCI ₹0 (wholly owned), book net assets ₹300 Cr with no DTL on ₹120 Cr intangible step-ups, plus a DTL of 25.168% on the goodwill. Identify every error and recompute.

💡 Hint: Four separate errors affect the number.

Q3. Interview: what is goodwill? [L1]

Explain to a non-finance board member what the ₹1,200 Cr of goodwill on a recent acquisition represents, why it is not amortised under Ind AS, and what could cause it to be written down.
