Key concept

DTL Roll-Forward: Opening to Closing Proof

Concept: DTL roll-forward: Opening net DTL + P&L charge + OCI charge ± Acquisitions ± Rate-change effect = Closing net DTL

Statement

\[ DTL_{\text{close}} = DTL_{\text{open}} + DT_{\text{P\&L}} + DT_{\text{OCI}} + DT_{\text{equity}} \pm DT_{\text{acq/disp}} \pm DT_{\text{rate}} \pm DT_{\text{FX}} \]

where \( DTL \) is the net deferred tax liability (DTA shown as negative), \( DT_{\text{P\&L}} \) the deferred tax expense in profit or loss from originating and reversing differences, \( DT_{\text{OCI}} \) and \( DT_{\text{equity}} \) the amounts recognised outside P&L (12.61A), \( DT_{\text{acq/disp}} \) DT acquired in business combinations or derecognised on disposal of subsidiaries, \( DT_{\text{rate}} \) the remeasurement of opening balances for rate changes (allocated to P&L/OCI by origin), and \( DT_{\text{FX}} \) translation differences for foreign operations (consolidated only).

Explanation

The roll-forward proves that the closing DT balance computed bottom-up from the temporary-difference table equals the opening balance plus every recorded movement. It is required in substance by Ind AS 12.81(g), which asks for the amount of DT recognised in P&L for each type of temporary difference, unused loss and credit, and by the reconciliation of movements most companies present by category (PPE, leases, employee benefits, ECL, losses, others).

Build it as a matrix: rows = categories of temporary difference; columns = opening, P&L, OCI, equity, acquisitions, rate change, closing. Each row's closing DT must equal the DT table's closing amount for that category. The P&L column total must equal deferred tax expense in the statement of profit and loss; the OCI column must equal the tax shown against each OCI item.

Common causes of breaks: OCI items posted to P&L (pitfall), rate changes not separated, DT on business combinations not recorded through goodwill, DTA derecognition booked against current tax, and offsets between entities in consolidation that do not meet 12.74.

Practice Questions

Q1. Build and balance [L2]

Opening net DTL ₹8.4 Cr. During the year: PPE DTL increases by ₹1.1 Cr; DTA on gratuity increases by ₹0.3 Cr, of which ₹0.1 Cr relates to OCI; a subsidiary acquired brings DTL of ₹2.0 Cr on fair value step-ups; DTA on ECL increases by ₹0.2 Cr; the company decides to opt for 115BAA, reducing net DTL by ₹1.6 Cr (₹0.1 Cr of the reduction relates to OCI items). Prepare the roll-forward and the entries.

💡 Hint: Keep the acquisition and rate-change columns separate from the P&L column.

Q2. Diagnosing a break [L1]

The DT table closing is ₹15.6 Cr but the roll-forward closing is ₹15.1 Cr. List five likely causes and the check for each.

Q3. Cash flow link [L2]

Explain how the deferred tax expense, the OCI tax and the acquired DTL in Q1 each appear (or not) in the indirect-method cash flow statement.
