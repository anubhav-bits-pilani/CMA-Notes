Key concept

ETR Reconciliation Formula: From PBT × Rate to Tax Expense

Concept: ETR reconciliation: PBT × statutory rate ± Permanent differences ± Rate differentials ± Unrecognised DTA ± Prior-year adjustments = Tax expense

Statement

\[ T = PBT \times r_s + \sum_{i} P_i \times r_s + \sum_{j} I_j \times (r_j - r_s) + \Delta U + \Delta R + A_{\text{PY}} \]

where \( T \) is total tax expense in P&L (current + deferred), \( PBT \) profit before tax, \( r_s \) the statutory (applicable) rate, \( P_i \) permanent differences (positive for non-deductible expenses, negative for exempt income or extra deductions), \( I_j \) income taxed at a different rate \( r_j \) (capital gains, foreign subsidiaries' profits), \( \Delta U \) the effect of DTAs not recognised in the year or previously unrecognised DTAs now recognised (and derecognitions), \( \Delta R \) the effect of rate changes on opening DT, and \( A_{\text{PY}} \) prior-year current and deferred tax adjustments. The effective tax rate is \( ETR = T \div PBT \).

Explanation

The reconciliation explains why the reported tax charge differs from "profit × headline rate" (Ind AS 12.81(c)). Temporary differences do not appear as reconciling items — they change the split between current and deferred tax but not the total, because deferred tax is recognised on them. Only items that permanently change total tax, or that are taxed at a different rate or not tax-effected, appear.

Conditions and conventions:

  • Applicable rate: disclose the basis — for 115BAA companies 25.168%; for groups, either the parent's rate with a foreign-rate differential line, or a weighted average of entity rates.
  • Sign convention: items that increase tax are added. Present both tax amounts and, if helpful, percentage points of ETR.
  • Unrecognised DTA: losses of the year on which no DTA is recognised increase ETR; recognition of previously unrecognised DTAs reduces it.
  • Discrete items: MAT credit write-off, 115BAA remeasurement, settlement of tax disputes — show separately so readers can normalise.
  • Completeness check: the reconciliation total must equal current tax + deferred tax in P&L; investigate any "others" line.

Practice Questions

Q1. Prepare from data [L2]

A 115BAA company has PBT ₹90 Cr; tax expense ₹24.1 Cr. Items: penalties ₹0.5 Cr; CSR ₹1.6 Cr; Sec 80JJAA deduction ₹1.2 Cr; LTCG ₹6 Cr taxed at 14.3%; MAT credit not applicable. Prepare the reconciliation and identify the unexplained amount.

💡 Hint: Start with PBT × 25.168% and add each item's tax effect; the residual is the unexplained difference.

Q2. Temporary differences and the ETR [L1]

Explain why an increase in the PPE DTL of ₹3 Cr does not appear as a line in the ETR reconciliation, and give two situations in which a temporary difference does affect the ETR.

Q3. Group reconciliation design [L3]

A group has an Indian parent (115BAA), an Indian subsidiary in the old regime (34.944%) with an SEZ unit partly exempt, a US subsidiary (21% federal plus state) and a loss-making UK subsidiary. Design the reconciliation format, list the lines you expect, and explain how you would source each from the group reporting pack.
