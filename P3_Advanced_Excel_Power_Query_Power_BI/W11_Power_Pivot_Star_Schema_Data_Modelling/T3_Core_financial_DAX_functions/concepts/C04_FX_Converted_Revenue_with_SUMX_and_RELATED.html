Key concept

FX-Converted Revenue with SUMX and RELATED

Concept: Revenue INR = SUMX(Sales, Sales[AmountFC] * RELATED(FX[Rate]))

Statement

Revenue INR = SUMX ( Sales, Sales[AmountFC] * RELATED ( FX[Rate] ) )

\[ \text{Revenue}_{INR} = \sum_{i \in \text{Sales}} \text{AmountFC}_i \times \text{Rate}_{c(i),\,t(i)} \]

where \(i\) runs over Sales rows visible in the filter context, \(c(i)\) is the row's currency and \(t(i)\) its rate period (day or month), and \(\text{Rate}\) is the ₹ per unit of foreign currency from the FX table on the one side of a many-to-one relationship Sales → FX.

Explanation

  • SUMX creates a row context on Sales; the multiplication happens per invoice line.
  • RELATED(FX[Rate]) follows the active many-to-one relationship from the current Sales row to its FX row. The relationship needs a key that identifies currency and period, e.g. a CurrencyMonthKey built in Power Query on both tables as [Currency] & "-" & Date.ToText(Date.EndOfMonth([InvoiceDate]), "yyyyMM") (and from MonthEnd on the FX side), or CurrencyDateKey for daily rates.
  • Accounting basis. Ind AS 21.21 requires initial recognition of foreign-currency transactions at the spot rate on the transaction date; Ind AS 21.22 permits an average rate for a week or month if rates do not fluctuate significantly. Many Indian companies use the RBI reference rate / FBIL rate or the bank's TT rate per their policy; customs valuation for exports uses CBIC-notified rates. The FX table must reflect the policy.
  • Why not sum then multiply. \(\sum \text{AmountFC} \times \overline{\text{Rate}}\) ignores the mix of currencies and timing; only row-level conversion is right.
  • INR rows. Include INR with rate 1 in the FX table so domestic invoices convert trivially.
  • Missing rates make RELATED return blank and the line drops out. Add a monitor:
Lines Missing FX = COUNTROWS ( FILTER ( Sales, ISBLANK ( RELATED ( FX[Rate] ) ) ) )
  • Translation vs transaction. This converts transactions. Translating a foreign subsidiary's whole P&L at average rates and BS at closing rates (Ind AS 21.39) is a consolidation step with its own measures.

Practice Questions

Q1. Build the key [L1]

Describe the Power Query steps to create a Currency-Month key on both Sales and FX so the relationship is many-to-one, and state how you guarantee uniqueness on the FX side.

Q2. Daily vs monthly policy [L2]

The company's policy changes from monthly average rates to daily RBI reference rates from 1-Apr-2026. Design the FX table and relationship so both periods convert correctly in one measure.

💡 Hint: Consider a key at day grain with monthly rates repeated for each day of the old period.

Q3. Reconcile to books [L3]

[Revenue INR] for FY2025-26 differs from the GL export-revenue account by ₹2.3 crore. List the possible reasons (rates, timing, missing lines, credit notes, forex gain/loss postings) and the measures or checks you would build to locate the difference.
