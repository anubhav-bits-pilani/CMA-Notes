Key concept

Rolling 12-Month Measure with DATESINPERIOD

Concept: Rolling 12M = CALCULATE([Actual], DATESINPERIOD('Date'[Date], MAX('Date'[Date]), -12, MONTH))

Statement

Rolling 12M =
CALCULATE (
    [Actual],
    DATESINPERIOD ( 'Date'[Date], MAX ( 'Date'[Date] ), -12, MONTH )
)

\[ \text{R12M}(d^{*}) = \sum_{d \,=\, \text{EDATE}(d^{*}, -12) + 1}^{d^{*}} A(d) \]

where \(d^{*}\) = MAX('Date'[Date]) in the outer filter context (the anchor), \(\text{EDATE}(d^{*}, -12)\) is the same date twelve months earlier, and \(A(d)\) is the base measure on day \(d\). At a month-end anchor this is the twelve calendar months ending with the visible month.

Explanation

DATESINPERIOD(dates, start_date, number_of_intervals, interval) returns a contiguous set of dates starting at start_date and extending number_of_intervals intervals — backwards when negative. The anchor MAX('Date'[Date]) is computed in the outer context because CALCULATE evaluates its filter arguments before applying them; for a November 2025 row the anchor is 30 November 2025 and the window is 1 December 2024 – 30 November 2025.

What makes R12M useful:

  • No year-end reset, so trend lines are smooth across April.
  • Annualised run rate at any month-end — the basis for LTM multiples (EV/LTM EBITDA), LTM covenant tests and "annualised revenue" in investor decks.
  • Seasonality neutral: every window contains each calendar month exactly once.

Conditions and refinements:

  • Cap the anchor. At an FY total for an incomplete year the anchor is 31 March of a future date; the window then contains blank future months and R12M collapses to YTD. Use MIN(MAX('Date'[Date]), [Last Actual Date]) as anchor.
  • Require a full window. Blank the result until 12 months of data exist (count month-ends with postings inside the window).
  • Ratios as ratio of sums. R12M EBITDA % = R12M EBITDA ÷ R12M Revenue.
  • Flows only. For balance sheet averages over 12 months use AVERAGEX over month-ends of a closing-balance measure (Topic 5).
  • Date table coverage. The window for the first months of data needs dates going back 11 months; if the Date table starts later, the window is silently truncated.

Illustration

Monthly revenue (₹ crore) for a pharma distributor:

Month Revenue R12M
Oct 2025 38.0 431.0
Nov 2025 36.5 434.0
Nov 2024 (for reference) 33.5 —

Check: R12M Nov 2025 = R12M Oct 2025 + Nov 2025 − Nov 2024 = 431.0 + 36.5 − 33.5 = 434.0. Running this identity on every month in a reconciliation table (or in DAX query view) catches window errors quickly. A trailing 3-month variant uses -3 and is annualised by multiplying by 4.

Practice Questions

Q1. Window dates [L1]

State the exact start and end dates of the window for the R12M measure on rows for (a) February 2025, (b) Q3 FY26, (c) the FY26 total when actuals are loaded to 31 January 2026 and the anchor is not capped, and (d) the same total with the anchor capped.

Q2. Guarded R12M with margin [L2]

Write R12M Revenue, R12M EBITDA and R12M EBITDA % measures that cap the anchor at the last actual date and return blank unless the window contains twelve months with GL postings.

💡 Hint: Count month-ends with postings inside the window using SUMMARIZE over the fact evaluated within the window.

Q3. LTM for a covenant [L2]

Net debt at 30 September 2025 is ₹ 640 crore. EBITDA was ₹ 230 crore for FY25, ₹ 104 crore for H1 FY25 and ₹ 121 crore for H1 FY26. Compute LTM EBITDA and the leverage ratio, and state how you would prove the model's R12M EBITDA at September 2025 agrees.
