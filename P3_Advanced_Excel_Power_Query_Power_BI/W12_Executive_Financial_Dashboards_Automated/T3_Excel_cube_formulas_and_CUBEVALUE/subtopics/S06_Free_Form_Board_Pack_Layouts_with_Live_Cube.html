Subtopic note

Free-Form Board-Pack Layouts with Live Cube Numbers

Scope: Free-form board-pack layouts: Schedule III format, variance columns, commentary next to live numbers

Why it matters

Board packs in India follow conventions that PivotTables cannot reproduce: Schedule III line order and wording, note references, a "Total income" line before expenses, profit before exceptional items, OCI below PAT, blank spacer rows, bold subtotals, and a commentary column beside each variance. Cube formulas let you design the sheet like a Word document while every number is live from the model. This is how FP&A teams in listed companies produce monthly MIS that looks like the published results but refreshes in minutes.

Core notes

Layout skeleton

A board-pack page has four zones:

Zone Columns Content
Keys (hidden) A Member keys or unique names per line (e.g. FS line key)
Labels B Schedule III wording, note numbers, indentation
Numbers C–H Actual, Budget, Var ₹, Var %, PY, YoY % for month and YTD
Commentary I Variance explanations tied to the period

Row 1–3 hold the title ("Statement of Profit and Loss – " & selected FY & " – ₹ crore"), the connection and measure header cells (Conn, MsrActual, MsrBudget, MsrPY), and the slicer reference.

Schedule III order (Division II, P&L)

Rows in the order of the published statement: Revenue from operations; Other income; Total income; Cost of materials consumed; Purchases of stock-in-trade; Changes in inventories of FG, WIP and stock-in-trade; Employee benefits expense; Finance costs; Depreciation and amortisation expense; Other expenses; Total expenses; Profit before exceptional items and tax; Exceptional items; Profit before tax; Current tax; Deferred tax; Profit for the period; Other comprehensive income (items that will not / will be reclassified); Total comprehensive income. A management pack adds EBITDA and EBITDA % as memo lines below or as a separate KPI block, clearly labelled as non-GAAP.

Each line's number:

C8:  =CUBEVALUE(Conn, MsrActual, Slicer_FY, Slicer_BU, CUBEMEMBER(Conn, $A8))

where A8 holds [CoA].[FSLine].&[Revenue from operations]. Subtotals use the model's subtotal measures or a parent member (e.g. [CoA].[FSGroup].&[Total income]), not Excel SUM, so that a new FS line within the group is automatically included. If you do use Excel SUM for presentation, add a check against the parent-member value.

Variance columns

  • Var ₹ = a model measure [Variance Fav/Adv] (sign-adjusted) fetched by CUBEVALUE, or Excel =C8-D8 with a sign column for expense lines. Using the measure keeps one definition across Power BI and Excel.
  • Var % = [Variance Fav/Adv %] measure; suppress when the base is immaterial (IF(ABS(D8)<0.5,"n.m.",…)).
  • Conditional formatting in Excel: red/green font based on the sign of Var ₹, with a ▲/▼ custom format [Color10]▲ 0.0;[Red]▼ 0.0;–.
  • Month and YTD blocks side by side: YTD via a [YTD Actual] measure or a set of months; never by summing month cells.

Commentary next to live numbers

Commentary is the analyst's value-add and the part most likely to go stale. Techniques:

  1. Period-keyed commentary table. An Excel table tblComments with columns FY, Month, FSLine key, Comment, Author, Date. Column I uses =XLOOKUP(SelFY & "|" & SelMonth & "|" & A8, tblComments[Key], tblComments[Comment], "") so changing the slicer shows that period's comments (or blank). This prevents October's comment showing in November.
  2. Commentary in the model. Load the comment table into the model with relationships to Date and CoA, and fetch with CUBEVALUE on a text measure ([Comment] = SELECTEDVALUE(Comments[Text])). Works with Power BI too.
  3. Materiality triggers. A helper column flags lines where ABS(Var ₹) > 1 Cr or ABS(Var %) > 5% and no comment exists — a review control ("comment required").

Presentation details

  • Units: ₹ crore with one decimal; title band states "₹ crore unless stated"; numbers from [... ₹ Cr] measures.
  • Indentation via cell indent, not leading spaces (spaces would leak into keys if labels double as keys).
  • Print setup: fit to one page wide, repeat title rows, footer with refresh timestamp (a cell fetching [Last Refresh IST] text measure) and "Totals may not add due to rounding".
  • Lock and protect formula cells; leave commentary cells unlocked.
  • A checks block at the bottom: P&L total vs model, BS check, TB tie-out status from the model's control measures.

Illustration

October 2025 YTD, ₹ crore (extract):

Line Actual Budget Var ₹ Var % Commentary
Revenue from operations 712.4 735.2 (22.8) (3.1%) Agro volumes delayed by late monsoon; Pharma +6%
Other income 5.9 4.5 1.4 31.1% Treasury income on surplus funds
Cost of materials consumed 389.6 405.7 16.1 4.0% Lower volumes; yields improved at Dahej
Power and fuel (within Other expenses) 47.1 42.9 (4.2) (9.8%) Grid tariff revision; DG usage in Sept outage

Every number is a CUBEVALUE; the commentary column is keyed to FY2025-26|Oct|line.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Lines without data (e.g. Purchases of stock-in-trade = 0) still appear in Schedule III format; CUBEVALUE returns blank — format to show "–".
  • Sign presentation. Schedule III shows expenses as positive numbers; the model's signed measures may store them negative. Use presentation measures with the statement's sign convention.
  • Restated PY. If PY figures are restated, the [PY] measure must read restated data; footnote the restatement.
  • Non-GAAP lines. SEBI and ICAI guidance expects EBITDA definitions to be stated; include the definition in a footnote.
  • Commentary ownership. Comments typed directly into cells next to formulas get overwritten when rows move; the keyed table approach survives layout changes.
  • Hard-coded plugs. Never type a number to "make it agree"; fix the mapping or add a documented adjustment ledger in the source.

Key takeaways

  • Design the sheet in Schedule III order with hidden key columns, labels, live numbers and commentary.
  • Fetch subtotals from model measures or parent members, not Excel SUM, or check SUM against them.
  • Use model variance measures so Excel and Power BI share sign logic.
  • Key commentary by period and line so it changes with the slicer and never goes stale.
  • Include a checks block, refresh stamp and rounding note on every printed page.

Practice Questions

Q1. Lay out the P&L [L1]

List the rows (with bold subtotals) of a Schedule III Division II statement of profit and loss for a board pack, and add the EBITDA memo lines with their definition footnote.

Q2. Commentary that follows the slicer [L2]

Design tblComments and the formula in the commentary column so that selecting November FY2025-26 shows November's comments and blank where none exist; include how you would flag lines with variance above ₹1 crore and no comment.

💡 Hint: Build one concatenated key from FY, month and the line's member key.

Q3. Subtotal choice [L2]

"Total expenses" in the pack is =SUM(C12:C18). In December, a new FS line "Impairment of receivables" is mapped to the expenses group. Explain what the pack shows and redesign the subtotal to be robust, with a check.

Q4. Build the balance sheet page [L3]

Design a Schedule III balance sheet page (as at the selected month-end) using cube formulas: rows, the measures it needs (closing balances, not sums), comparison columns (budget, previous year-end), NWC memo lines and the checks block.
