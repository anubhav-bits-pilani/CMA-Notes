Key concept

Net Debt Measure: Closing Borrowings Less Cash and Equivalents

Concept: Net Debt = [Closing Borrowings] - [Closing Cash & Equivalents]

Statement

Net Debt = [Closing Borrowings] - [Closing Cash & Equivalents]

\[ \text{Net Debt}_t = B_t - C_t \]

where \(B_t\) is closing borrowings at period end \(t\) (non-current borrowings, current borrowings and current maturities of long-term borrowings; optionally lease liabilities) and \(C_t\) is closing cash and cash equivalents (Ind AS 7 definition) at the same date. Both inputs are semi-additive snapshot measures, for example:

Closing Borrowings =
CALCULATE (
    -SUM ( TB[Balance] ),                       -- liabilities are credit balances (negative)
    CoA[NetDebtClass] = "Borrowings",
    LASTDATE ( 'Date'[Date] )
)

Closing Cash & Equivalents =
CALCULATE (
    SUM ( TB[Balance] ),
    CoA[NetDebtClass] = "Cash",
    LASTDATE ( 'Date'[Date] )
)

Explanation

Net Debt is a balance concept, not a flow. It must be computed at a point in time (the last date of the selected period) and never summed across months — the Week 11 semi-additive patterns (LASTDATE, LASTNONBLANK, CLOSINGBALANCEMONTH) apply. If TB snapshots are month-end only, LASTNONBLANK avoids blanks when the period ends mid-month.

Definitional choices must be stated in the pack's measure dictionary because Ind AS does not define Net Debt:

  • Borrowings scope. Include current maturities (shown within current borrowings under Schedule III Division II), commercial paper, and interest accrued? Most Indian companies exclude interest accrued but include all borrowings.
  • Lease liabilities. Post Ind AS 116, many companies report Net Debt both excluding and including lease liabilities; covenants usually define which.
  • Cash side. Cash and cash equivalents only, or also other bank balances (fixed deposits over three months) and liquid mutual fund investments? A wider definition is common in investor decks ("net cash including liquid investments") and must be labelled.
  • Ind AS 7 nuance. Bank overdrafts repayable on demand may be included in cash and cash equivalents for the cash flow statement; for Net Debt, treat them consistently on one side only.
  • Mapping. A CoA[NetDebtClass] attribute (Borrowings, Lease, Cash, OtherBank, LiquidInv, None) makes the definition data-driven and auditable.

Net Debt feeds leverage ratios: Net Debt / LTM EBITDA (covenant and rating metric) and Net Debt / Equity. A falling cash balance does not always mean rising Net Debt if borrowings were repaid — which is why the Net Debt trend is on Page 2 rather than cash alone.

Illustration

Sahyadri Chemicals at 31 October 2025 (₹ Cr):

Item Amount
Non-current borrowings 214.5
Current borrowings (incl. current maturities 38.0) 97.0
Cash and cash equivalents 84.6
Other bank balances (FDs > 3 months) 22.0

Net Debt (core definition) = \(214.5 + 97.0 - 84.6 = 226.9\) Cr. Including other bank balances as cash: \(204.9\) Cr. If the user selects Q3 (Oct–Dec), the card must show the 31 December position, not the sum of three month-ends.

Practice Questions

Q1. Compute Net Debt variants [L1]

At 31 March 2026: non-current borrowings ₹198.0 Cr, current borrowings ₹104.5 Cr, lease liabilities ₹36.2 Cr, cash and cash equivalents ₹91.3 Cr, other bank balances ₹15.0 Cr, liquid mutual funds ₹40.0 Cr. Compute Net Debt (i) core, (ii) including leases, (iii) including leases and all liquid investments.

Q2. Why the card is wrong [L2]

A Net Debt card shows ₹681.0 Cr for Q3 while month-end Net Debt for October, November and December was ₹226.9 Cr, ₹229.4 Cr and ₹224.7 Cr. Diagnose the measure error and write the corrected measure.

💡 Hint: Compare the result with the sum of the three month-end values.

Q3. Covenant metric [L3]

A loan covenant requires Net Debt (including lease liabilities) / LTM EBITDA ≤ 2.25x, tested quarterly. Design the measures needed for a covenant headroom card, including how LTM EBITDA is computed and how you would show headroom in ₹ crore.
