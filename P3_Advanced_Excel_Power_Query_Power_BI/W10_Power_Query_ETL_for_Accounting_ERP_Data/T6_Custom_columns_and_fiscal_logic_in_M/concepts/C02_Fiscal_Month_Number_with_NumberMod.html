Key concept

Fiscal Month Number with Number.Mod

Concept: Fiscal month: Number.Mod(Date.Month([Date]) + 8, 12) + 1 (Apr = 1 ... Mar = 12)

Statement

For calendar month \(m \in \{1, \dots, 12\}\), the fiscal month for an April–March year is

\[ \text{FM} = \big((m + 8) \bmod 12\big) + 1 \]

Table.AddColumn(t, "FiscalMonth", each Number.Mod(Date.Month([Date]) + 8, 12) + 1, Int64.Type)
  • Date.Month([Date]) — calendar month (January = 1).
  • Number.Mod(x, 12) — remainder after dividing by 12, in the range 0–11 for positive \(x\).
  • Adding 8 and then 1 shifts April (4) to 1 and March (3) to 12.

Explanation

The fiscal month is the position of the month within the FY. It is needed for sorting months in fiscal order, for YTD logic ("fiscal month ≤ 6" means April–September), for joining to monthly budgets keyed by period number, and for building the PeriodKey (FY end year × 100 + fiscal month).

Why the constants: the FY starts in month 4, so the shift is \(m - 4\) modulo 12, plus 1 to start at 1 rather than 0. Since \(-4 \equiv 8 \pmod{12}\), adding 8 avoids negative numbers: \(((m - 4) \bmod 12) + 1 = ((m + 8) \bmod 12) + 1\). For a fiscal year starting in month \(s\), the general form is Number.Mod(m + 12 - s, 12) + 1 — e.g. a January start (\(s = 1\)) gives the calendar month back, and a July start (\(s = 7\), used by some foreign parents) gives Number.Mod(m + 5, 12) + 1.

An equivalent, more readable version is if m >= 4 then m - 3 else m + 9. Both are correct; the Number.Mod form generalises to a parameterised start month (FYStartMonth).

Type the result as Int64.Type. As text or any, it sorts 1, 10, 11, 12, 2, … in the model.

Illustration

Calendar month m + 8 mod 12 FM
April (4) 12 0 1
July (7) 15 3 4
December (12) 20 8 9
January (1) 9 9 10
March (3) 11 11 12

YTD to September for FY2025-26 = rows where FYEndYear = 2026 and FM ≤ 6.

Practice Questions

Q1. Compute fiscal months [L1]

Using the formula, compute the fiscal month for February, May, September and November, showing the intermediate \(m + 8\) and mod values.

Q2. Parameterised start month [L2]

Write M that computes the fiscal month from a parameter FYStartMonth (4 for Indian entities, 1 for a calendar-year parent, 7 for a July-year subsidiary), and verify it for March under each setting.

💡 Hint: Use Number.Mod(m + 12 - FYStartMonth, 12) + 1.

Q3. YTD filter [L2]

Using FYEndYear and FiscalMonth, write a filter step that returns YTD rows for the period ending AsOfDate, and a second step returning the same YTD period of the prior FY for comparison.
