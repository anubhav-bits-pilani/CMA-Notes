Key concept

Fiscal Quarter with Number.IntegerDivide

Concept: Fiscal quarter: "Q" & Text.From(Number.IntegerDivide(Number.Mod(Date.Month([Date]) + 8, 12), 3) + 1)

Statement

With \(k = (m + 8) \bmod 12\) (the zero-based fiscal month, April = 0 … March = 11), the fiscal quarter is

\[ \text{FQ} = \left\lfloor \frac{k}{3} \right\rfloor + 1 \]

Table.AddColumn(t, "FiscalQuarter", each
    "Q" & Text.From(Number.IntegerDivide(Number.Mod(Date.Month([Date]) + 8, 12), 3) + 1), type text)
  • Number.Mod(Date.Month([Date]) + 8, 12) — zero-based fiscal month 0–11.
  • Number.IntegerDivide(k, 3) — integer quotient, 0–3.
  • + 1 gives 1–4; "Q" & Text.From(...) gives the label "Q1"…"Q4".

Explanation

Indian fiscal quarters are Q1 April–June, Q2 July–September, Q3 October–December, Q4 January–March, matching the quarterly results periods under SEBI LODR Reg 33 and the advance-tax and TDS-return rhythm. The formula groups the zero-based fiscal months in threes.

Practical notes:

  • If a FiscalMonth (1–12) column already exists, the simpler form is Number.IntegerDivide([FiscalMonth] - 1, 3) + 1.
  • Keep a numeric quarter (Int64.Type) alongside the text label for sorting and arithmetic; combine with FY for a unique label ("Q3 FY2025-26") because "Q3" alone repeats each year.
  • Do not use Date.QuarterOfYear, which returns calendar quarters (April = 2, January = 1).
  • Date.EndOfQuarter and Date.StartOfQuarter are safe for dates, because calendar quarter boundaries coincide with Indian fiscal quarter boundaries.
  • Number.IntegerDivide truncates toward zero; with the non-negative \(k\) here, that equals floor division.

Illustration

Month k = (m + 8) mod 12 IntegerDivide(k, 3) Quarter Date.QuarterOfYear (wrong)
April 0 0 Q1 2
June 2 0 Q1 2
July 3 1 Q2 3
October 6 2 Q3 4
December 8 2 Q3 4
January 9 3 Q4 1
March 11 3 Q4 1

For 14-Nov-2025, the result is "Q3"; the full label is "Q3 FY2025-26", and the quarter-end date Date.EndOfQuarter(#date(2025, 11, 14)) is 31-12-2025.

Connections

Builds on concept c02 (fiscal month) and subtopic s02 (FY attributes). Used in quarterly variance reporting (Weeks 17–20), quarterly results under SEBI LODR, and the Calendar dimension in Week 11, where FY + quarter forms the quarter level of the fiscal hierarchy.

Q1. Evaluate the quarter [L1]

Compute the fiscal quarter for 1-Jul-2025, 30-Sep-2025, 1-Jan-2026 and 31-Mar-2026 using the formula, showing \(k\) and the integer quotient.

Q2. Quarter label and sort key [L2]

Write typed M columns for QuarterNum (1–4), QuarterLabel ("Q3 FY2025-26") and QuarterKey (FY end year × 10 + quarter number), starting from a Date column.

💡 Hint: Compute the FY end year first (concept c01) and reuse it in both label and key.

Q3. Calendar-quarter bug [L2]

A board-pack query used Date.QuarterOfYear for quarters. Explain which months were mislabelled, how the Q1 revenue figure in the pack would have been affected for a company with April–June revenue of ₹84 crore and January–March revenue of ₹102 crore, and how you would detect the error in review.
