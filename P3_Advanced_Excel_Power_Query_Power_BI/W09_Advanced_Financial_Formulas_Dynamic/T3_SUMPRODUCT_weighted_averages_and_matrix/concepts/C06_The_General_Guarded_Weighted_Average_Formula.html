Key concept

The General Guarded Weighted-Average Formula

Concept: General weighted average: =IF(SUM(w)=0, 0, SUMPRODUCT(w, x)/SUM(w))

Statement

\[ \bar{x}_w = \begin{cases} \dfrac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i x_i}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i} & \text{if } \sum_{i} w_i \neq 0 \\ 0 & \text{if } \sum_{i} w_i = 0 \end{cases} \]

where \(x_i\) is the value being averaged (rate, price, days, margin), \(w_i\) its weight (balance, quantity, amount, revenue) and \(n\) the number of items. w and x are equal-sized ranges or Table columns. The IF guard returns 0 instead of #DIV/0! when all weights are zero.

Explanation

One template, many uses. The same structure gives every weighted metric in finance:

Metric x w
Pre-tax cost of debt tranche rate tranche (average) balance
Weighted average inventory rate receipt rate receipt quantity
Weighted days outstanding days outstanding open amount
Blended gross margin % product margin % product revenue
Average realisation per tonne realised price tonnes sold
Weighted average remaining lease term remaining term lease liability
Portfolio yield instrument yield carrying amount

The guard: what should "no weight" return? 0 is convenient for projection years with no debt or for products with no sales, because downstream arithmetic keeps working. But 0 can be misleading — a 0% "cost of debt" in a debt-free year is harmless only if nothing multiplies it by a future balance. Alternatives: "" for display, NA() to make charts skip the point, or the prior period's value for rates carried forward. Choose by consequence and document it.

Sign issues. Weights should be non-negative for a meaningful average. With signed data (credits negative), mixed signs can make \(\sum w_i\) near zero and the average explode. Use absolute values or net the data before averaging; never average across mixed-sign weights without understanding them.

Conditional version. For a subgroup, include a condition in both numerator and denominator:

=IF(SUMIFS(w, grp, G2)=0, 0, SUMPRODUCT((grp=G2)*w*x)/SUMIFS(w, grp, G2))

The condition must be identical in both parts, or the average is distorted.

Choosing the right weight is the real skill — see the topic pitfall on weighting on the wrong base. Interest cost for a year should weight rates by average balances, not closing balances; inventory rates by quantity, not by number of receipts; margins by revenue, not by number of products.

Illustration

Blended gross margin for three product lines (FY26):

Product line Revenue w (₹ crore) Gross margin x
Premium 120 46%
Core 380 31%
Value 100 18%
  • Weighted: (120 × 46% + 380 × 31% + 100 × 18%) ÷ 600 = (55.2 + 117.8 + 18.0) ÷ 600 = 191.0 ÷ 600 = 31.8%.
  • Simple average: (46 + 31 + 18) ÷ 3 = 31.7% — close here by coincidence; shift ₹100 crore of revenue from Core to Premium and the weighted margin rises to 34.3% while the simple average does not move at all.
  • In a projection year where the Value line is discontinued, its revenue is 0 and it drops out of the weighted average automatically; if all lines had zero revenue (e.g. a pre-launch year), the guard returns 0 instead of #DIV/0!.

Connections

Generalises the cost-of-debt formula (c01) and the inventory and DSO weighted averages of this topic's second subtopic; it is the formula behind the size-weighted cohort retention curve. The guard reflects the week's error-discipline theme (test the condition rather than wrap everything in IFERROR). In DAX (Week 11), the equivalent is DIVIDE(SUMX(T, T[w]*T[x]), SUM(T[w])), where DIVIDE handles the zero denominator.

Practice Questions

Q1. Average realisation [L1]

A steel trader sold 1,200 t at ₹54,500/t, 850 t at ₹56,200/t and 2,400 t at ₹53,100/t in Q3 FY26. Write the guarded weighted-average formula on a Table with columns Tonnes and Price, and compute the average realisation.

Q2. What should the guard return? [L2]

For each case, decide whether the zero-weight result should be 0, blank, NA() or the prior-period value, and justify: (a) cost of debt in a projection year with no borrowings, used to compute interest on next year's opening debt; (b) average ticket size on a dashboard for a store that was closed all month; (c) blended margin feeding a chart.

💡 Hint: Ask what the downstream formula or reader does with the value.

Q3. Mixed-sign weights [L2]

An analyst computes the weighted average days of a vendor's open items, where open invoices are +₹12 lakh (60 days), +₹8 lakh (35 days) and an unapplied debit note is −₹19 lakh (10 days). Show why the result is meaningless and propose a correct treatment.
