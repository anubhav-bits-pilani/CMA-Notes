Key concept

Monthly Cost per FTE with Start, Exit and Increment Flags

Concept: Monthly cost per FTE = (Annual CTC × (1 + Increment % × [Month ≥ Increment month])) ÷ 12 × [Month ≥ Start month] × [Month ≤ Exit month]

Statement

For position \(p\) and month index \(m\) (April = 1, …, March = 12):

\[ C_{p,m} = \frac{S_p \times \left(1 + g_p \times [m \ge m^{*}]\right)}{12} \times [m \ge s_p] \times [m \le e_p] \]

where \(S_p\) is the pre-increment annual CTC (or fixed gross, depending on the model's convention), \(g_p\) the increment %, \(m^{*}\) the increment effective month, \(s_p\) the start month, \(e_p\) the exit month, and \([\cdot]\) an indicator (Iverson bracket) equal to 1 when the condition holds and 0 otherwise. Annual cost is \(\sum_{m=1}^{12} C_{p,m}\) and department cost is \(\sum_p \sum_m C_{p,m}\).

Explanation

The formula is the engine of a position-level payroll model. Its three flags handle the three timing questions:

  • Start flag \([m \ge s_p]\): new hires and backfills cost nothing before they join (s03). Existing employees have \(s_p = 1\) (or 0).
  • Exit flag \([m \le e_p]\): leavers cost nothing after their exit month. Continuing employees have \(e_p = 12\).
  • Increment flag \([m \ge m^{*}]\): pay rises only from the effective month (s04). Add an eligibility flag (\(\times\) Eligible\(_p\)) for employees who joined after the cut-off or are not eligible.

Practical extensions:

  • Apply the formula to components separately (basic, HRA, allowances) and compute employer statutory costs on the relevant component with caps and thresholds (c02–c04); applying a flat statutory % to CTC is a common error.
  • Use a promotion flag with its own month and uplift.
  • For partial months (mid-month joining), replace the start flag with a fraction (days employed ÷ days in month) in the start month.
  • If CTC includes employer PF and gratuity, do not add them again; it is cleaner to hold fixed gross and compute employer costs explicitly.

In Excel, flags multiply cleanly because TRUE/FALSE coerce to 1/0:

=[@Annual_Fixed]*(1+[@Incr_Pct]*(F$3>=Incr_Month)*[@Incr_Eligible])/12*(F$3>=[@Start_Month])*(F$3<=[@Exit_Month])

Practice Questions

Q1. Applying the formula [L1]

Compute annual cost for: (a) an employee with annual fixed ₹15,00,000, 9% increment from July, exiting 30 November; (b) a new hire at ₹21,00,000 joining 1 August, eligible for no increment; (c) a backfill at ₹8,40,000 joining 1 February.

💡 Hint: Count months in each pay state between the start and exit flags.

Q2. Component-level extension [L2]

Extend the formula to compute monthly employer PF for an employee whose basic is 40% of fixed gross, where PF is on actual basic for some employees and capped at ₹15,000 for others (flag in the roster). Write the Excel formula and test it on fixed gross of ₹4,20,000 and ₹18,00,000.

Q3. Mid-month joining [L2]

A new hire with annual fixed ₹24,00,000 joins on 16 October 2027. Modify the formula to cost the partial month by days, and compute the FY 2027-28 cost.
