Key concept

ESI Contributions and Statutory Bonus on Capped Wages

Concept: ESI (wages ≤ ₹21,000/month): employer 3.25%, employee 0.75%; Statutory bonus 8.33%–20% on capped wages

Statement

ESI (Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948; now Code on Social Security, 2020): for an employee with monthly wages \(W \le \text{₹}21{,}000\) (₹25,000 for persons with disability) at the start of a contribution period,

\[ \text{Employer ESI} = 3.25\% \times W, \qquad \text{Employee ESI} = 0.75\% \times W \]

Contribution periods run 1 April–30 September and 1 October–31 March; an employee covered at the start of a period remains covered (and contributions continue on actual wages) until the period ends, even if wages exceed the ceiling during it. Employees with average daily wages up to ₹176 are exempt from the employee share (employer still pays).

Statutory bonus (Payment of Bonus Act, 1965; now Code on Wages, 2019): for employees with salary or wage (basic + DA) up to ₹21,000 per month who worked at least 30 days in the year,

\[ \text{Bonus} = b \times \max(\text{₹}7{,}000, \; \text{Minimum wage}) \times \text{Months worked}, \qquad 8.33\% \le b \le 20\% \]

where the minimum 8.33% is payable regardless of profit (in covered establishments) and higher rates up to 20% depend on the allocable surplus. Where actual salary is below the calculation ceiling, the actual salary is used.

Explanation

Both costs are threshold-based, so they behave very differently from flat percentages. Points for budgeting:

  • ESI wage base is broad: it includes HRA and most allowances, excluding employer PF, gratuity, annual bonus and certain reimbursements. Apply 3.25% on gross wages, not on basic.
  • Threshold crossings after increments: an employee at ₹20,200 who receives a 6% increment from July (₹21,412) stays covered until 30 September and drops out from 1 October. Model by contribution period, not by month.
  • ESI implemented areas: coverage applies in notified areas to establishments with 10 or more employees; most urban and industrial areas are covered.
  • Benefits offset: ESI-covered employees receive medical benefits, so many employers exclude them from group medical insurance — a real saving to reflect in the insurance budget.
  • Bonus eligibility is on basic + DA (salary/wage as defined), not gross; the calculation ceiling is the higher of ₹7,000 or the scheduled minimum wage for the employment (state-specific, often ₹10,000–₹15,000 in 2025–26). The ₹21,000 eligibility threshold and calculation ceiling continue under the Code on Wages until revised by notification; note also that the Code's bonus provisions refer to "wages" as defined in the Code, which is broader than basic + DA, so the eligibility test and base may widen once rules are settled — verify the current position.
  • Accrual and payment: accrue monthly; pay within eight months of the year-end (commonly before Diwali). Bonus is deductible for tax only on payment (Sec 43B) — accruals unpaid at the return due date create a deferred tax asset.
  • Above-20% payments are ex gratia or performance bonus, not statutory bonus.

Illustration

A plant worker: monthly gross ₹19,500 (basic + DA ₹11,000); state minimum wage ₹12,000; bonus at 8.33%; increment of 9% from July (gross ₹21,255, basic + DA ₹11,990).

Period Gross wages Employer ESI Employee ESI Bonus base (monthly) Bonus accrual (monthly)
April–June 19,500 634 146 11,000 (actual < ceiling) 916
July–September 21,255 691 (still covered in the period) 159 11,990 999
October–March 21,255 0 (not covered from new period) 0 11,990 999

Annual employer ESI = 3 × 634 + 3 × 691 = ₹3,975; annual bonus = 3 × 916 + 9 × 999 = ₹11,739. A flat "3.25% of gross" assumption would give ₹8,118 for ESI — double the correct figure — and would miss that ESI stops in October. If the company had 400 such workers, the error is about ₹16.6 lakh.

Practice Questions

Q1. ESI across contribution periods [L2]

An employee's gross is ₹20,600 from April 2027; increment of 4% from 1 September 2027; a further promotion to ₹23,500 from 1 February 2028. Compute monthly employer and employee ESI for FY 2027-28.

💡 Hint: Coverage is determined at the start of each contribution period.

Q2. Bonus calculation [L1]

Compute FY 2027-28 statutory bonus at 8.33% and at 15% for: (a) basic + DA ₹9,000 all year, minimum wage ₹11,500; (b) basic + DA ₹14,000 all year; (c) basic + DA ₹19,800 for April–August then ₹22,000 (from September), minimum wage ₹13,000; (d) a joiner from 1 January with basic + DA ₹10,500 and minimum wage ₹11,000.

Q3. Budget error quantification [L2]

A plant budget applies employer ESI at 3.25% and bonus at 8.33% of total gross payroll of ₹18 crore for 900 workers. In reality, 520 workers are ESI-covered all year with average gross ₹18,800, 80 drop out of ESI from October after increments, and 600 are bonus-eligible with an average calculation base of ₹12,000. Quantify the overstatement.
