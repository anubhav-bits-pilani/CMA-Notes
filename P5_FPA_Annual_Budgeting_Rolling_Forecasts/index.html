Phase 5: FP&A, Annual Budgeting, Rolling Forecasts & Variance Storytelling

Weeks 17–20 (192 Hours)

Leverage your CMA costing/variance roots and elevate them into enterprise FP&A: driver-based budgeting, 12-month rolling forecasts, and executive board packs.

Phase deliverable: Complete Annual Operating Plan (AOP) Budget, 12-Month Rolling Forecast Model, and Monthly CFO Management Reporting Pack with Price-Volume-Mix Variance Commentary.

Week 17: Enterprise Budgeting Architecture (Operating, Capex, Cash & ZBB)

Design an integrated bottom-up and top-down corporate budgeting framework connecting departmental budgets to the Master Budget.

  • T1 · AOP workflow: top-down vs bottom-up
  • T2 · Revenue, quota, pricing waterfall, seasonality
  • T3 · Opex, headcount payroll model, ZBB
  • T4 · Capex tranches and liquidity cash budget
  • T5 · Master budget consolidation into 3 statements
  • T6 · Flexible budgeting: volume vs efficiency
Week 18: Driver-Based & 12-Month Rolling Forecasts

Replace static annual budgets with agile, driver-based rolling forecasts that continuously update forward visibility.

  • T1 · 12/18-month rolling forecast timeline mechanics
  • T2 · YTD actual + YTG forecast Latest Estimate
  • T3 · Driver-based operational forecasting
  • T4 · Version and scenario governance
  • T5 · Forecast accuracy: MAPE, WAPE, bias
Week 19: Advanced Variance Analysis (Price-Volume-Mix & Cost Variances)

Bridge CMA standard costing variance formulas into commercial FP&A Price-Volume-Mix (PVM) bridges for C-suite decision-making.

  • T1 · Revenue and margin PVM decomposition
  • T2 · Material and labour variances to GM bps
  • T3 · Opex spending vs timing variances
  • T4 · EBITDA bridge waterfall construction
  • T5 · Favourable/unfavourable sign conventions
Week 20: Executive Management Reporting Pack & CFO Storytelling (Project 6 Delivery)

Package budgets, rolling forecasts, KPIs, and variance narratives into a monthly board-level Management Information System (MIS) pack.

  • T1 · MBR pack structure and content
  • T2 · KPI scorecard and RAG thresholds
  • T3 · Action-oriented financial commentary
  • T4 · Business partnering with non-finance heads
  • T5 · Project 6 final assembly
