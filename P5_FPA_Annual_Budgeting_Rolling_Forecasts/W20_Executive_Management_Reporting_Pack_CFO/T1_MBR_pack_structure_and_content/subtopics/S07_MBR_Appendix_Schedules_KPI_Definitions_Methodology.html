MBR Appendix: Schedules, KPI Definitions, Methodology

Scope: Appendix: detailed schedules, KPI definitions, methodology notes

Why it matters

The appendix is what lets the front of the pack stay short. Every detailed table that a stakeholder occasionally needs, every definition that resolves a dispute, and every methodology choice that an auditor or new CFO might question lives here. It also protects FP&A: when a BU head challenges a DSO figure or an allocation, the answer is already written and agreed. A pack without an appendix either bloats the front pages with tables or leaves definitions in people's heads, which is how inconsistencies between months creep in.

Core notes

What belongs in the appendix

Three categories, in this order:

  1. Detailed schedules — data that supports the front pages but is too granular for them.
  2. KPI definitions — the formula, source, owner and frequency of every metric that appears in the pack.
  3. Methodology notes — how numbers are built: allocations, forecasting approach, variance methods, adjustments and changes since last month.

A useful test: if a page is consulted by fewer than half the MBR attendees in a typical month, it belongs in the appendix.

Detailed schedules

Typical schedules for a manufacturing or consumer company:

Schedule Content Feeds front page
A1 Full P&L by month 12 months actual/forecast, AOP, PY by MIS line P&L page, LE
A2 Segment P&L detail Revenue by channel, region, product family; contribution build Segment pages
A3 Opex by function / cost centre Employee, A&P, freight, IT, admin by cost centre vs budget Commentary on cost variances
A4 Headcount Opening, joiners, leavers, closing by function; vacancies; contract staff HR KPIs, employee cost
A5 PVM detail Price, volume, mix by SKU group and segment EBITDA bridge
A6 Balance sheet and NWC detail Receivables by customer, inventory by category and age, payables incl. MSME Cash tracker
A7 Debt schedule Facilities, limits, drawn amounts, rates, maturities, covenants Net debt and covenant table
A8 Capex project list All projects through the five stages, CWIP ageing Capex tracker
A9 Reconciliations MIS EBITDA to reported EBITDA; segment total to entity; MIS to Schedule III P&L Credibility of all pages

Schedules should be generated from the same model as the front pages, carry the same units and sign convention, and be numbered so commentary can cross-reference ("see A3, cost centre 4120").

KPI definitions

Every metric in the pack gets one row in a definitions table. Minimum fields: name, formula, numerator and denominator definition, unit, direction (higher-is-better or lower-is-better), source system/report, owner, frequency, target basis, RAG thresholds, and date last changed. Topic 2 (s05) develops the KPI definition sheet in depth.

KPI Formula Source Owner Direction Notes
EBITDA (MIS) Revenue from operations − COGS − employee cost − other operating expenses, pre-Ind AS 116 (rent deducted), excluding other income and exceptional items SAP FI, MIS mapping v3.2 FP&A Lead Higher Reconciled to reported EBITDA in A9
DSO Trade receivables (net of ECL, excluding GST) ÷ T3M revenue × 4 × 365 SAP FI-AR Head of Credit Control Lower Changed from YTD-annualised in Apr-26
Net debt (management) Borrowings + lease liabilities − cash − liquid investments (excl. restricted cash) Treasury Treasurer Lower Bank covenant definition excludes leases
OTIF Order lines delivered on time and in full ÷ total order lines SAP SD/TMS Head of Supply Chain Higher "On time" = within customer-requested date + 1 day

The definitions must be agreed with owners and frozen for the year. Changes require sign-off and are logged with an effective date; comparatives are restated.

Methodology notes

Short, dated notes on how numbers are produced. Topics to cover:

  • Basis of preparation: Ind AS ledger, MIS mapping version, close calendar, cut-off for late adjustments.
  • Accruals and estimates: provisional items in the month (e.g. scheme accruals estimated at 3.5% of secondary sales, true-up quarterly).
  • Allocation methods: keys for corporate costs, shared plant costs, freight; date of last change.
  • Forecast methodology: driver-based YTG (volume × price by segment), sources of assumptions (sales pipeline, S&OP volume plan, commodity forward curves), forecast date.
  • Variance methodology: PVM formulas (price at actual volume, volume at budget price, mix at budget margin), FX translation at budget rates for constant currency, timing classification.
  • Adjustments: definition of exceptional or one-off items and the list for the year.
  • Changes since last pack: re-mapping, restatements, KPI definition changes, budget re-phasing.

Example methodology note (one paragraph):

PVM method (from Apr-26). Price variance = (Actual price − Budget price) × Actual volume; volume variance = (Actual volume − Budget volume) × Budget contribution per unit at budget mix; mix variance = Actual volume × (Budget contribution per unit at actual mix − at budget mix). The three components reconcile to the contribution variance in Schedule A5 (check cell A5!K50 = 0).

Format and maintenance

  • Keep a contents list at the start of the appendix, with schedule numbers.
  • Use identical layouts each month (c05) so readers know where to look.
  • Version the definitions and methodology notes (v3.2, effective date) and keep a change log.
  • In Power BI, an appendix can be a separate report page group or a linked paginated report, with the definitions table sourced from a governed SharePoint list.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Appendix as a dumping ground: if appendix schedules are never referenced in commentary or questions over a quarter, remove them.
  • Sensitive schedules (salary detail, customer-level margins) may need restricted distribution; provide them on request rather than in the circulated pack.
  • Definitions conflicting with statutory terms: if "EBITDA" in the MBR differs from the investor-presentation EBITDA, either align or rename (e.g. "Operating EBITDA (MIS)") to avoid confusion.
  • Audit trail: methodology notes and definitions are often requested by internal audit and statutory auditors when they test management estimates; keep them dated and approved.

Key takeaways

  • The appendix holds detailed schedules, KPI definitions and methodology notes, in that order.
  • Schedules come from the same model, are numbered and are cross-referenced in commentary.
  • Each KPI definition states formula, source, owner, direction, frequency and change history.
  • Methodology notes record allocations, forecast and variance methods, adjustments and changes since last month.
  • Version and restate on change; prune schedules nobody uses.

Practice Questions

Q1. Classify pages [L1]

Shakti's current 32-page pack contains: executive summary; P&L; three segment pages; cost-centre opex for 46 cost centres; SKU-level sales for 180 SKUs; cash tracker; debt schedule; capex tracker; R&O log; action log; headcount by location; MIS-to-reported reconciliation; KPI scorecard. Allocate each to "front pack" or "appendix" and justify any that you would delete.

Q2. Write KPI definitions [L2]

Write full definition rows (formula, numerator, denominator, unit, direction, source, owner, frequency, RAG thresholds) for: gross margin %, DIO, fill rate, attrition % (annualised) and capex utilisation %.

💡 Hint: Be explicit about inclusions and exclusions, such as whether attrition counts contract staff or involuntary exits.

Q3. Methodology change note [L2]

From November, Shakti will move freight costs from "Freight & other opex" into COGS to align with the investor-presentation gross margin definition. YTD freight is ₹24 crore (budget ₹22 crore, PY ₹20 crore). Draft the methodology change note and show the restated YTD GM% for actual, budget and PY (YTD revenue ₹660 crore, budget ₹690 crore, PY ₹629 crore; GP ₹213.8 crore, ₹234.6 crore and ₹208.3 crore).

Q4. Definitions dispute [L3]

The Sales head argues DSO should exclude ₹18 crore of receivables from one modern-trade customer under dispute and ₹22 crore of GST included in receivables; the CFO's DSO includes both. Write the appendix note resolving the dispute, showing the effect on DSO (T3M revenue annualised ₹1,184 crore, receivables ₹212 crore) and recommending a definition with any supplementary metric.
