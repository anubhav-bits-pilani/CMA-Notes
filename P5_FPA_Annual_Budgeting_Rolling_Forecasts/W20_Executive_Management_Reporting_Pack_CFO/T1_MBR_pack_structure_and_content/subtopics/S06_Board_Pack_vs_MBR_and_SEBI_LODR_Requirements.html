Subtopic note

Board Pack vs MBR and SEBI LODR Requirements

Scope: Board pack vs MBR differences; SEBI LODR quarterly results and Audit Committee requirements for listed companies

Why it matters

The monthly MBR is a management document; the quarterly board pack is a governance document with legal consequences. In listed Indian companies, FP&A typically drafts the performance sections of the board and Audit Committee packs, and the numbers in them must reconcile to the Regulation 33 results the Board approves and files with NSE/BSE. Knowing what the law requires, what the Board needs, and how the MBR feeds both prevents two classic failures: management numbers that cannot be reconciled to reported results, and board papers that are either data dumps or omit matters that must be placed before directors.

Core notes

MBR vs board pack — key differences

Dimension MBR (monthly) Board / Audit Committee pack (quarterly)
Purpose Run the business: performance, LE, actions Oversight, approval of results, strategy, risk, compliance
Audience CEO, CXOs, BU heads Directors (incl. independent directors), committee members, statutory auditors (for AC)
Frequency and timing Working day 5–8 after month-end At least four meetings a year with not more than 120 days between two meetings (SEBI LODR Reg 17(2); Sec 173 Companies Act)
Basis Management definitions (adjusted EBITDA, segment contribution, constant currency) Ind AS reported results plus reconciled management KPIs
Detail BU, channel, cost centre, operating KPIs Entity and segment level, trends, strategic KPIs
Forward view Monthly LE, R&O, action log FY outlook vs AOP/guidance, strategic risks, capital allocation
Decisions Operational: pricing, spend, phasing Statutory and strategic: results approval, dividends, borrowing limits, capex above delegated authority, related-party transactions
Confidentiality Internal Contains UPSI; access controlled; subject to insider-trading code and structured digital database

A good practice is a single source: the board pack performance section is a condensed, quarter-end version of the MBR with the same definitions and page logic, plus governance-specific papers.

SEBI LODR — quarterly results (Regulation 33)

  • Timing: standalone and consolidated financial results for each quarter within 45 days of quarter-end; annual audited results within 60 days of financial year-end (Reg 33(3)).
  • Format: as prescribed by SEBI (Ind AS-compliant format aligned with Schedule III), with segment information for listed entities with reportable segments, and a statement of assets and liabilities and cash flow statement for the half-year (Reg 33(3)).
  • Assurance: quarterly results accompanied by a limited review report by the statutory auditor; annual results by the audit report with a statement on impact of audit qualifications where applicable (Reg 33(3)).
  • Certification: the CEO and CFO certify that the results do not contain false or misleading statements or figures (Reg 33(2)(a)); separately Reg 17(8) requires the annual CEO/CFO compliance certificate (Schedule II Part B) on financial statements and internal controls.
  • Approval: results are approved by the Board after review by the Audit Committee.
  • Prior intimation and outcome: prior intimation of the Board meeting to consider results to the stock exchanges (Reg 29 — check the current notice period, which has been amended over time); outcome disclosed within 30 minutes of the meeting's conclusion (Reg 30 and Schedule III Part A); newspaper publication of results (Reg 47).
  • SEBI has introduced integrated filing (financial and governance) formats from 2025; the filing mechanics are date-sensitive, so follow the current circulars.

Audit Committee — composition and role

Under Reg 18 (and Sec 177 of the Companies Act 2013):

  • At least three directors, at least two-thirds independent; all financially literate and at least one with accounting or related financial management expertise; chairperson an independent director.
  • Meets at least four times a year, gap not exceeding 120 days; quorum two members or one-third, whichever is higher, with at least two independent directors present.
  • Role (Schedule II Part C) includes: oversight of financial reporting; reviewing quarterly and annual financial statements before submission to the Board (changes in accounting policies, major judgements, significant audit adjustments, related-party transactions, qualifications); recommending auditor appointment and fees; reviewing auditor independence; approval and subsequent modification of related-party transactions (Reg 23); scrutiny of inter-corporate loans and investments; valuation of undertakings or assets; evaluation of internal financial controls and risk management; reviewing internal audit findings; reviewing the utilisation of loans/advances/investments in subsidiaries above the specified threshold; and reviewing the functioning of the whistle-blower mechanism.

Minimum information to be placed before the Board (Schedule II Part A)

Reg 17(7) lists information that must be placed before the Board, including: annual operating plans and budgets and any updates; capital budgets and any updates; quarterly results for the listed entity and its operating divisions or business segments; minutes of committee meetings; show-cause, demand and penalty notices that are materially important; fatal or serious accidents and pollution problems; material defaults in financial obligations; significant labour problems; foreign-exchange exposures; and non-compliance with regulatory requirements. This is the bridge from the MBR: AOP updates (LE), capex budget updates (capex tracker) and segment results all flow from the MBR into the board pack.

Typical quarterly board pack structure

  1. CEO/MD strategic update.
  2. Performance: quarter and YTD vs AOP and PY, segment results, FY outlook vs guidance, KPI scorecard, cash and net debt — condensed from the MBR.
  3. Draft Reg 33 results, Audit Committee recommendation, limited review report.
  4. Reconciliation of management (adjusted) EBITDA to reported profit.
  5. Capex updates and approvals above delegated authority.
  6. Risk management update (link to Risk Management Committee under Reg 21 for top 1,000 entities).
  7. Compliance certificates, related-party transactions, investor grievances.
  8. Approvals: dividends, borrowings (Sec 180 limits), allotments.

Audit Committee pack — finance content

The CFO's section typically includes: key accounting judgements for the quarter (ECL provisioning, inventory write-downs, revenue recognition for schemes and rebates under Ind AS 115, impairment testing, deferred tax recognition); summary of audit differences; internal financial controls status; tax litigation update; related-party transaction summary against omnibus approvals; and a bridge from MBR numbers to reported results so members can see no "management-only" number is unreconciled.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • UPSI management: monthly MBRs of a listed company contain unpublished price-sensitive information in the run-up to results. Restrict distribution, log recipients in the structured digital database as required by the SEBI PIT Regulations and observe the trading window.
  • Guidance revisions: material changes to outlook may need disclosure; decide with the Company Secretary and legal counsel whether information is material under Reg 30 and the company's materiality policy.
  • Non-GAAP measures: adjusted EBITDA in investor presentations must be defined and reconciled; avoid presenting it more prominently than reported numbers.
  • Debt-listed entities are governed by Reg 52 for results; "high value debt listed entities" have additional governance requirements.
  • Unlisted public companies follow the Companies Act (Sec 173, Sec 177 for prescribed classes) but not LODR; the MBR-to-board link still applies.

Key takeaways

  • MBR is monthly, managerial and detailed; the board pack is quarterly, statutory and condensed — but they must share definitions and reconcile.
  • Reg 33: quarterly results within 45 days (annual within 60), limited review/audit report, CEO/CFO certification, Audit Committee review then Board approval.
  • Reg 18 and Sec 177 set Audit Committee composition, meetings and a role list that FP&A content must support.
  • Schedule II Part A requires budgets, capex budgets and segment results to be placed before the Board — the MBR is the source.
  • Treat monthly packs of listed companies as UPSI and reconcile every management measure to reported Ind AS numbers.

Practice Questions

Q1. Results calendar [L1]

Shakti's Q3 FY27 ends on 31 December 2026. Prepare a calendar showing: MBR for December, Audit Committee meeting, Board meeting, latest date for filing Q3 results under Reg 33, outcome disclosure timing and trading window closure, given that Shakti targets filing 10 days before the deadline.

Q2. What goes where [L2]

Classify each item as MBR only, board pack only, Audit Committee pack, or more than one, and justify: (a) cost-centre travel variances; (b) draft Q3 results with limited review report; (c) LE vs guidance; (d) ECL provision methodology change; (e) SKU-level fill rates; (f) omnibus RPT approval utilisation; (g) capex above the CEO's delegated authority; (h) receivables ageing by customer.

Q3. Reconcile management to reported [L2]

Q2 MIS EBITDA ₹28.4 crore (pre-Ind AS 116, excluding other income and exceptional items). Lease rentals on Ind AS 116 leases ₹3.6 crore; other income ₹2.1 crore; exceptional item: VRS ₹3.0 crore; forex loss on ECB ₹0.8 crore (included in "other expenses" in reported results, excluded from MIS). Prepare the reconciliation to reported EBITDA (as defined in the investor presentation as profit before finance cost, depreciation, tax and exceptional items, including other income) for the Audit Committee pack.

💡 Hint: List each definitional difference and sign it relative to MIS EBITDA before summing.

Q4. Independent director challenge [L3]

An independent director on the Audit Committee says the MBR circulated to management shows Water Purifiers contribution of ₹12.2 crore YTD, but the segment results in the Reg 33 filing show segment profit of ₹9.8 crore. Draft the CFO's explanation, covering Ind AS 108 segment measure, allocations and what you would change in the MBR to prevent the query recurring.
