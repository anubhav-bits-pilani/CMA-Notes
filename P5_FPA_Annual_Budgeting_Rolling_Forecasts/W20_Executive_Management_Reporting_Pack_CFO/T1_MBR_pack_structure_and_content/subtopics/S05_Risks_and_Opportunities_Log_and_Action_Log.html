Subtopic note

Risks and Opportunities Log and Action Log

Scope: Risks and opportunities log, action log with owners and due dates

Why it matters

The LE is a single number, but the future is a range. The risks and opportunities (R&O) log quantifies what is not in the LE so management can see how wide that range is and whether guidance is safe. The action log turns MBR discussion into accountability: every corrective action has an owner, a due date and a ₹ value, and it stays on the log until closed. Together they are the forward-looking half of the pack. Without them, MBRs become a monthly recital of variances in which the same problems are "noted" every month.

Core notes

R&O log — what goes in it

An R&O is an uncertain future event that would change the LE if it happened, and which is not already in the LE. This rule is critical: if an item is in the LE, it is not a risk or an opportunity — it is the forecast. Double counting (a price increase both in the LE and in the opportunities list) is the most common error.

Standard fields:

Field Content
ID Unique, persistent (R-07, O-03)
Description Event, cause and affected line (e.g. "Copper LME above US 10,000 dollars/t for Q4 adds ₹4 crore to COGS")
Segment / line BU and P&L/cash line affected
Gross ₹ impact (FY) EBITDA impact if it occurs; also cash impact where different
Probability Banded: High (> 70%), Medium (30–70%), Low (< 30%); or a %
Weighted ₹ impact Gross × probability
Timing Month/quarter when it would hit
Mitigation / capture plan What is being done to reduce the risk or capture the opportunity
Owner One named person
Status / trend New, increasing, decreasing, closed; moved into LE

Illustration — Shakti, FY27 LE EBITDA ₹127.1 crore

ID Item Gross ₹ cr Prob. Weighted ₹ cr Owner
R-01 Copper/aluminium further +5% from Dec (4.0) 40% (1.6) Head of Procurement
R-02 Modern-trade customer delays ₹5 crore scheme settlement dispute; write-off (2.0) 30% (0.6) Head of Sales MT
R-03 Weak winter for geysers line (2.5) 50% (1.3) BU Head Kitchen
O-01 Price increase 2% on fans from 1 Dec (not yet in LE) 4.5 70% 3.2 Head of Sales Fans
O-02 Freight re-tender North region 2.0 60% 1.2 Head of Logistics
O-03 Early summer pre-buy by distributors in March 3.0 40% 1.2 National Sales Head
O-04 A&P re-phasing to Q1 FY28 2.5 80% 2.0 CMO
Net 3.5 4.1

Presentation on the executive summary:

\[ \text{Risk-adjusted LE} = \text{LE} + \sum (\text{Gross impact}_i \times p_i) = 127.1 + 4.1 = 131.2 \]

along with a downside (all risks, no opportunities: ₹127.1 − 8.5 = ₹118.6 crore) and upside (all opportunities, no risks: ₹127.1 + 12.0 = ₹139.1 crore). A tornado or a "LE range" bar showing guidance ₹150 crore against the range shows instantly that guidance is not achievable without further actions.

Probability-weighting caveats

The weighted sum is useful for the expected outcome but hides binary risks. A 10% chance of losing a ₹20 crore customer contract has a weighted value of ₹2 crore, but if it happens the impact is ₹20 crore. Show large binary items separately ("Top 3 risks" panel) and do not rely on the weighted total alone.

Action log — what goes in it

An action is a decision or task agreed in the MBR (or arising from a variance) that someone must do. Fields:

Field Content
ID and date raised A-14, raised 8-Oct-26 MBR
Action Specific, verb-first ("Re-tender North freight lanes covering 40% of volume")
Linked item Variance, KPI or R&O ID it addresses (O-02)
₹ value / KPI target ₹2 crore FY27 EBITDA; freight per unit ₹142 → ₹131
Owner One named individual, not a department
Due date A date, not "Q3" or "ASAP"
Status Not started / on track / at risk / overdue / closed (with evidence)
Update One line, dated

Rules for an effective log:

  1. One owner per action, at a level able to deliver it. "Sales team" is not an owner.
  2. Dates are dates. Overdue actions turn red automatically: =IF(Status="Closed","Closed",IF(TODAY()>DueDate,"Overdue",Status)).
  3. Carry forward until closed. Never delete; closed actions move to an archive tab with the closure evidence and the realised ₹ value.
  4. Link to value. Each action has a ₹ or KPI target so the log can be summed: "Actions identified ₹13.4 crore; in LE ₹0; at risk ₹2.0 crore."
  5. Review cadence. The MBR reviews new, overdue and at-risk actions only; on-track items are noted.

From R&O to LE to actions — the flow

  1. A risk or opportunity is identified and logged with ₹ impact and probability.
  2. A mitigation or capture action is logged with owner and date.
  3. When the event becomes probable and quantifiable (e.g. the price increase is announced to distributors), it moves into the LE and is closed on the R&O log, noting "moved to LE in Nov LE".
  4. Actual results then confirm or refute it, and the variance commentary refers back.

This flow avoids double counting and creates an audit trail for forecast changes.

Excel / Power BI build

Keep both logs as structured Excel tables (tblRO, tblActions) with data validation for status and probability bands, and a summary using SUMIFS(tblRO[Weighted],tblRO[Type],"Risk"). In Power BI, load the tables via Power Query from a SharePoint list so owners update their own rows; build a matrix with conditional formatting on status and a card for "overdue actions".

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Items partially in the LE: log only the incremental part beyond what the LE assumes, and state the LE assumption in the description.
  • Opportunities used to "plug" the gap: business heads may list optimistic opportunities to avoid an LE downgrade. Insist on evidence (customer confirmation, signed contract, price circular) before moving to the LE; apply probability bands strictly.
  • Non-financial risks (regulatory, ESG, cyber, plant safety) may belong in the enterprise risk register; include in the MBR only those with a quantifiable near-term P&L or cash effect, and cross-reference to the ERM register reviewed by the Risk Management Committee (SEBI LODR Reg 21 for the top 1,000 listed entities).
  • Disclosure sensitivity: a probable large risk to guidance may require consideration of disclosure obligations under SEBI LODR Reg 30 and the insider-trading code (UPSI). Flag to the Company Secretary.

Key takeaways

  • R&O log captures only items not in the LE, with gross ₹ impact, probability, weighted value, timing and owner.
  • Present LE, risk-adjusted LE and downside/upside range against AOP and guidance; flag large binary risks separately.
  • Action log: verb-first action, one named owner, a real date, ₹ value and status; carry forward until closed with evidence.
  • Move items from R&O into the LE when probable and evidenced — never double count.
  • Logs should be owner-updated tables feeding automated summaries and overdue flags.

Practice Questions

Q1. Risk-adjusted LE [L1]

LE EBITDA ₹127.1 crore. Risks: ₹6 crore at 25%, ₹3 crore at 60%, ₹10 crore at 10%. Opportunities: ₹4 crore at 75%, ₹2.5 crore at 50%. Compute the risk-adjusted LE, the downside and upside cases, and identify which item you would show separately as a binary risk.

Q2. Double-count check [L2]

The Fans BU submits an LE that already includes a 2% price increase from 1 December worth ₹4.5 crore. The R&O log also shows O-01 "price increase ₹4.5 crore, 70%". Volume elasticity analysis suggests a 3% volume loss on YTG fans revenue of ₹185 crore at 16% contribution margin. Correct the LE and the R&O log and write the one-line explanation for the pack.

💡 Hint: Decide what the LE should contain and what remains uncertain, then log only the uncertain part.

Q3. Action log rewrite [L2]

Rewrite these action log entries so they meet the five rules: (a) "Sales to improve collections — ongoing"; (b) "Look into freight costs — Logistics — Q3"; (c) "Reduce inventory — Supply chain/Finance — ASAP"; (d) "Price increase to be considered".

Q4. Excel status automation [L2]

Design the columns and write the formulas for an action log that: (a) flags overdue actions; (b) computes days overdue; (c) sums ₹ value by status; (d) counts actions per owner that are overdue. Use structured table references.

Q5. Guidance judgement [L3]

Guidance is ₹150 crore EBITDA; LE ₹127.1 crore; risk-adjusted ₹131.2 crore; identified actions not in LE ₹13.4 crore, of which ₹5 crore needs board approval of a price increase. The Q3 results call is on 5 February. Write a 200-word note to the CFO recommending whether to revise guidance now, at Q3, or hold, considering SEBI LODR disclosure obligations and investor credibility.
