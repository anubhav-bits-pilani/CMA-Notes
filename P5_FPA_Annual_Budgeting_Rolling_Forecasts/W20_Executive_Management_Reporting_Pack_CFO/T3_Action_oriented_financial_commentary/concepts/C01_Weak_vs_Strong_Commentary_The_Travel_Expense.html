Key concept

Weak vs Strong Commentary: The Travel Expense Example

Concept: Weak: "Travel expense increased by ₹4L"; strong: "Travel ₹4L (12%) over budget due to unplanned customer visits; FY impact ₹6L; Sales head to cap by Q3"

Statement

A strong variance comment contains, in one or two sentences:

  1. The variance with its base and scale — ₹ amount and % against a stated comparator (budget, PY or LE).
  2. The root cause — a specific business reason, not a restatement.
  3. The forward impact — full-year effect (and whether it is in the LE).
  4. The corrective action — what will be done, by whom (named owner), by when.

The weak comment ("Travel expense increased by ₹4L") has only a partial element 1 — an amount with no base — and none of elements 2–4. The strong comment ("Travel ₹4L (12%) over budget due to unplanned customer visits; FY impact ₹6L; Sales head to cap by Q3") has all four in 22 words.

Explanation

Deconstructing the strong version:

Element Weak Strong
Amount ₹4L ₹4L
Comparison base Missing ("increased" vs what? PY? last month?) "over budget"
Relative size Missing "(12%)" — tells the reader it is material relative to the line
Cause Missing "unplanned customer visits"
Forward impact Missing "FY impact ₹6L"
Action, owner, timing Missing "Sales head to cap by Q3"

Why each element matters: without a base, the reader cannot tell whether the spend is a problem (it may be in line with budget and up only versus PY); without the %, ₹4 lakh is unrankable; without the cause, no one knows whether the spend was valuable (customer visits that won orders) or wasteful; without FY impact, the reader cannot judge the effect on the year; without owner and date, nothing happens.

The strong version could be improved further for a full MBR: it could say whether the visits generated revenue (the "so what" — c04), and "by Q3" could be a specific date. In a Shakti context, for example: "Travel ₹4L (12%) over budget YTD due to 18 unplanned key-account visits for the modern-trade range reset; three accounts added ₹1.2 crore of orders. FY impact ₹6L (not in LE). Sales head to cap travel at ₹5L/month from 1 November." The cause now also explains the business benefit, which changes the decision: cap, or continue because the return is high?

This example also illustrates the scaling of commentary: a ₹4 lakh variance would not meet an entity-level threshold (s04) for a ₹1,200 crore company, but in a Sales function pack with a ₹33 lakh YTD travel budget, 12% is material.

Illustration

Apply the same transformation to a Shakti freight variance:

  • Weak: "Freight cost increased by ₹3 crore."
  • Strong: "Freight ₹3.0 crore (5%) over budget YTD due to part-load e-commerce dispatches (trips per unit +6%) and North lane rates (+3.5%); FY impact ₹5.2 crore (in LE). Head of Logistics to consolidate e-commerce dispatches and re-tender North lanes by 15 December; saving ₹1.5 crore in Q4."

Connections

This is the model for the five-part structure (s01): what (₹4L, 12%, over budget), why (customer visits), impact and outlook (FY ₹6L), action/owner/timeline (Sales head, Q3). Root cause comes from s02, quantification from s03, the threshold decision from s04 and c03, the wording from s05 and validation from s06. The "so what" test (c04) pushes it one step further.

Practice Questions

Q1. Transform weak comments [L1]

Rewrite each weak comment into a strong one using the data given: (a) "Power cost increased by ₹40L" — YTD ₹4.6 crore vs budget ₹4.2 crore, state tariff up 4% from July and Hosur line-1 running a third shift for 10 weeks; FY impact ₹65L; Plant head to shift 30% of load to off-peak hours from 1 December. (b) "Advertising lower by ₹2 crore" — Diwali campaign moved from October to November; FY impact nil.

Q2. Critique the "strong" comment [L2]

Identify two ways in which the roadmap's strong travel comment could still be improved for a CFO audience, and explain how each would change the decision the reader makes.

💡 Hint: Think about whether the visits created value and about the precision of the deadline.

Q3. Scale and threshold [L2]

Shakti's Sales function pack uses thresholds of ₹3 lakh and 10%; the entity MBR uses ₹1.5 crore and 5%. Explain where the travel comment appears in each pack, and draft the one-line roll-up that would appear in the entity pack if Sales travel, entertainment and event costs together were ₹1.8 crore (8%) over budget.
