Subtopic note

Model Integrity Checks: Balance, Cash, Variance and Error Flags

Scope: Model integrity checks: balance checks, cash reconciliation, variance reconciliation, error flags

Why it matters

A model that does not balance, or a pack whose bridge does not add up, destroys credibility in the first minute of a CFO review or an interview walkthrough. Integrity checks are the model's internal controls — the equivalent of the audit trail and reconciliations the learner already knows from statutory audit and TB finalisation. They catch broken links, sign errors, missing mappings and stale inputs before the numbers reach the MBR, and they let a reviewer trust the model quickly. FAST and ICAEW modelling guidance both treat a visible, aggregated check system as a minimum standard.

Core notes

Balance checks

The balance sheet must balance in every period, for every scenario:

\[ \text{Check}_t = \text{Total assets}_t - (\text{Equity}_t + \text{Liabilities}_t) = 0 \]

(concept c01). Implementation:

  • One check row under the balance sheet, one cell per month: =ROUND(G45-(G78+G60),2).
  • A tolerance of 0.01 (or ₹1,000 when the model is in ₹ lakh) to absorb floating-point noise; anything larger is an error, not rounding.
  • Opening balance sheet check: opening retained earnings plus FY profit less dividends equals closing retained earnings (the equity roll-forward), and opening balances equal the previous period's closing balances.
  • Trial balance check on actuals: sum of debits equals sum of credits in the imported TB, and every GL account is mapped (unmapped count = 0).

Common causes of imbalance: a new BS line not included in totals; P&L items not flowing to retained earnings; a cash flow item without a BS counterpart; sign flips on liabilities.

Cash reconciliation

\[ \text{Closing cash (CF statement)}_t = \text{Cash and cash equivalents (BS)}_t \]

and

\[ \text{Opening cash}_t + \text{CFO}_t + \text{CFI}_t + \text{CFF}_t = \text{Closing cash}_t \]

For actuals, reconcile model cash to the bank book and bank reconciliation statement, separating cash and cash equivalents (Ind AS 7.6–7) from other bank balances (fixed deposits over three months, earmarked balances, which Schedule III shows separately). Also reconcile: free cash flow on the cash tracker equals CFO less capex from the cash flow statement; net debt movement equals FCF less dividends, plus or minus other financing items (a net debt bridge).

Variance reconciliation

Every variance analysis must add back to its endpoints:

\[ \text{Budget} + \sum_i \text{Bridge step}_i = \text{Actual} \]

(concept c02). Checks to build:

  • EBITDA bridge: AOP EBITDA + price + volume + mix + cost + FX + other = Actual EBITDA, for the month, YTD and LE views.
  • PVM totals: sum of SKU-level price, volume and mix effects equals total revenue variance.
  • AvB tables: variance column = actual − budget, with sign convention consistent (favourable positive for income and negative cost variance shown as favourable).
  • Segment roll-up: sum of segment revenue and EBITDA plus unallocated/eliminations equals the consolidated P&L (mirrors the Ind AS 108.28 reconciliation requirement).
  • Forecast: LE = YTD actual + YTG forecast (c03) and LE in MBR = LE in forecast tab.
  • Deck tie-out: every headline number on the executive summary equals its source cell.

Error flags

Collect all checks into a single checks dashboard:

Check Formula logic Status
BS balances (all months) =MAX(ABS(BS_Check_Row))<0.01 OK / ERROR
CF closing cash = BS cash =MAX(ABS(CF_Close-BS_Cash))<0.01 OK / ERROR
TB mapped completely =COUNTIF(Map_Status,"Unmapped")=0 OK / ERROR
EBITDA bridge reconciles =ABS(AOP_EBITDA+SUM(Bridge)-Act_EBITDA)<0.01 OK / ERROR
Segments sum to total =ABS(SUM(Seg_Rev)+Elim-Total_Rev)<0.01 OK / ERROR
LE = YTD + YTG =ABS(LE-(YTD_Act+YTG_Fcst))<0.01 OK / ERROR
No negative cash without facility =MIN(Cash_Row)>=-Facility_Limit OK / WARNING
Covenant headroom =MAX(NetDebt_EBITDA_Row)<=Covenant OK / WARNING

A master flag =IF(COUNTIF(Check_Status,"ERROR")=0,"MODEL OK","CHECK ERRORS") sits on the cover page and on every output page header. Distinguish errors (mechanical failures that must be fixed) from warnings (business alerts such as covenant breaches or negative cash that must be discussed, not fixed).

Additional integrity practices:

  • Conditional formatting turns failing checks red; a sum of absolute check values gives a single number to monitor.
  • Sense checks: gross margin within a plausible range, DSO between 0 and 180, tax rate near 25.17%, depreciation not exceeding gross block.
  • Input validation (Data Validation lists, allowed ranges) to prevent bad inputs.
  • Formula consistency: one formula per row across all columns; check with FORMULATEXT or the Inquire add-in.

Illustration

After loading December actuals, the dashboard shows "BS balances: ERROR" from December onward with a difference of ₹37.4 lakh. Tracing shows a new GL account "Advance to gratuity trust" was unmapped; it sat outside the BS but its cash outflow was in CFO. Mapping it to "Other non-current assets" clears the check; the TB-mapped check would have flagged it first, which is why mapping checks sit at the top of the dashboard.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Rounding in outputs: presentation tables rounded to ₹ crore may not visually add; use a rounding line or disclose "totals may not add due to rounding" rather than forcing numbers.
  • Plugs: a "cash plug" or "balancing figure" hides errors; revolver logic is acceptable only if it is a modelled facility with limits and interest.
  • Switched-off checks: never disable a check to make the flag green; document any accepted exception.
  • Actuals versus model: actual BS from the TB may not match the modelled BS for opening months due to reclassifications; reconcile with a documented bridge.
  • FX and consolidation: translation differences in OCI must flow to equity, otherwise multi-currency models fail the balance check.

Key takeaways

  • Build balance, cash, variance, mapping and forecast checks, all aggregated into one master flag.
  • Tie model cash to the bank and to cash and cash equivalents as defined in Ind AS 7.
  • Every bridge and segment table must add back to its endpoints and to the consolidated P&L.
  • Separate mechanical errors from business warnings such as covenant headroom.
  • Never use plugs or disabled checks to force a model to balance.

Practice Questions

Q1. Diagnose an imbalance [L2]

Your balance sheet balances until September and then is out by ₹12.0 lakh every month from October onward. In October you added a ₹12.0 lakh security deposit paid to a landlord in the cash flow statement under investing activities. Identify the likely error, the fix, and the check that should have caught it earlier.

Q2. Build the checks dashboard [L2]

List eight checks you would include in the Project 6 checks dashboard, give an Excel formula for each using named ranges, and classify each as error or warning.

💡 Hint: Cover statements, mapping, variance bridges, forecast consistency and business alerts.

Q3. Bridge does not reconcile [L1]

The EBITDA bridge shows AOP ₹45.0 crore, price +₹3.2 crore, volume −₹4.1 crore, mix +₹0.8 crore, cost −₹2.6 crore, other +₹0.4 crore, and actual EBITDA ₹43.9 crore. Determine whether the bridge reconciles and, if not, list three likely causes.

Q4. Cash reconciliation for actuals [L3]

At 31 December, the model's cash is ₹28.6 crore; the bank book shows ₹31.1 crore including a ₹4 crore 6-month fixed deposit and ₹0.9 crore unpaid dividend account; the bank statement shows ₹33.4 crore with ₹2.3 crore of uncleared cheques issued. Explain how you would reconcile and which figure should appear as cash and cash equivalents on the balance sheet.
