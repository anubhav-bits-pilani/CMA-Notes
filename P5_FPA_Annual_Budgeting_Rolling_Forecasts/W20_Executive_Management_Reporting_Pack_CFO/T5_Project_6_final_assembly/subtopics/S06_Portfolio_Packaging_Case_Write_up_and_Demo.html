Portfolio Packaging: Case Write-up and Demo Walkthrough

Scope: Portfolio packaging for interviews: case write-up and demo walkthrough

Why it matters

For a CMA moving from audit and compliance into FP&A, the biggest hiring barrier is proof of modelling and business-partnering ability. A packaged Project 6 — a short case write-up, a clean model, the MBR deck and a rehearsed demo — gives interviewers concrete evidence and lets the candidate steer the interview toward strengths. Indian FP&A hiring (GCCs in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Gurugram, listed corporates, consulting firms) commonly includes a case study or a "walk us through a model you built" round; candidates who can share a portfolio link and present it in ten minutes stand out. Packaging also forces final quality: anything that would embarrass you in front of an interviewer must be fixed.

Core notes

Components of the portfolio

Component Format Length Purpose
Case write-up PDF 2–3 pages Context, problem, approach, results, learnings
Model Excel (and Power BI file if built) — Evidence of structure, checks, integration
MBR / executive deck PDF of slides 8–10 slides + appendix Evidence of storytelling and recommendations
Demo video (optional) 5–8 minute screen recording — Shows walkthrough skill asynchronously
One-page summary PDF or LinkedIn featured item 1 page Teaser with key visuals and skills

Host on a personal OneDrive/Google Drive folder with view-only links, or on GitHub for Power Query M scripts and DAX measures; share a single link in the CV. Name files clearly, for example Project6_FPA_Model_v3.xlsx.

Data and confidentiality

Use synthetic data or publicly available data (for example a listed company's annual report and investor presentation as a starting point, then a fictional "ABC Consumer Ltd"). Never use an employer's or audit client's data — it breaches confidentiality obligations, including the ICAI/ICMAI codes of ethics on confidential information, and potentially employment contracts. State in the write-up that data is illustrative.

Case write-up structure

  1. Context — company profile (fictional), industry, size (₹ revenue), FY basis, the business question.
  2. Objective — "Build an AOP, a 12-month rolling forecast, a PVM variance engine and a monthly MBR pack, and recommend actions to recover a ₹7 crore EBITDA gap."
  3. Approach — model architecture (inputs → calculations → statements → analysis → outputs), key drivers, variance methodology, KPI and RAG design, tools used (Excel, Power Query, Power BI, DAX).
  4. Key results — headline AOP, LE vs AOP, EBITDA bridge, three recommended actions with ₹ impact.
  5. Controls and quality — checks dashboard, documentation, version log.
  6. Learnings and extensions — what you would improve (for example scenario automation, driver-based headcount, SKU-level forecasting in Power BI).

Write in first person and active voice; quantify; keep each section short. Include two or three images (the architecture diagram, the EBITDA bridge, the KPI scorecard).

Demo walkthrough script (8–10 minutes)

Time Show Say
0:00–1:00 Cover and checks dashboard Business question, model scope, all checks green
1:00–2:30 Assumptions sheet Key drivers, sources, sensitivity ranks
2:30–4:00 Revenue and cost build, statements Driver logic, three statements linked, cash = BS cash
4:00–5:30 PVM engine and EBITDA bridge Methodology, reconciliation to actual
5:30–7:00 Rolling forecast and LE Actual/forecast switch; change last actual month live
7:00–8:30 MBR deck summary and decisions Story, three actions, risk-adjusted LE
8:30–10:00 Live sensitivity Change price or input cost; show impact on LE, KPIs, RAG

Practise the live change — it is the most persuasive moment of the demo and the riskiest. Keep a backup copy and a pre-recorded clip in case the live model misbehaves on a video call.

Linking the portfolio to the CV and interview

  • CV bullet example: "Built an integrated FP&A model (AOP, 12-month rolling forecast, PVM engine, MBR pack) with 32 automated checks; identified ₹6.5 crore of margin-recovery actions in a simulated MBR."
  • LinkedIn: featured section with the one-page summary and link.
  • Interview: when asked about experience, bridge from audit/TB finalisation to the model's integrity checks and documentation — reconciliation discipline is a genuine strength to emphasise.
  • STAR format for behavioural questions (Situation, Task, Action, Result) using the project.

Anticipating interviewer probes

Interviewers commonly ask: why this structure; how you handled circularity; how you would scale to 20 BUs; how you would move it to Power BI or an EPM tool; what the weakest assumption is; and how the PVM handles new products. Prepare answers alongside the s05 question bank.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Too much complexity: a 60-tab model impresses less than a clean 20-tab one; remove experimental tabs.
  • Macros: VBA may be blocked by employer security when the file is opened; make the model work without macros and describe any macro as optional.
  • File size and links: break external links, remove hidden sheets and named ranges with errors (#REF!), and check the file opens on a fresh machine.
  • Honesty: do not claim the case was real employer work; interviewers probe and the inconsistency is damaging.
  • Tailoring: for a GCC role, emphasise process, controls and tooling; for a corporate FP&A role, emphasise business partnering and recommendations; for a valuation-leaning role, extend with a DCF tab.

Key takeaways

  • Package a 2–3 page case write-up, clean model, deck and optional demo video behind one link.
  • Use synthetic or public data and say so; never use employer or client data.
  • Structure the write-up as context, objective, approach, results, controls, learnings.
  • Script a timed 8–10 minute demo with a live sensitivity change and a backup.
  • Link the project explicitly to CV bullets, LinkedIn and STAR interview answers.

Practice Questions

Q1. Case write-up draft [L2]

Draft the "Context", "Objective" and "Key results" sections (under 300 words in total) of your Project 6 case write-up for a fictional NSE-listed consumer appliances company with FY27 AOP revenue of ₹2,400 crore.

Q2. Demo under constraints [L2]

An interviewer gives you exactly five minutes to demo your model over a video call. Re-plan the walkthrough script, stating what you drop and why.

💡 Hint: Keep the checks, one driver change and the decisions slide; cut everything else to a sentence.

Q3. Portfolio quality checklist [L1]

Write a 12-point pre-release checklist for the portfolio files, covering data confidentiality, file hygiene, checks status, formatting and accessibility of links.

Q4. Interview probe [L3]

An interviewer says: "Your model is in Excel. Our team uses Anaplan and Power BI. Why is this relevant?" Prepare a structured answer that maps your model's components to an EPM and BI environment.
