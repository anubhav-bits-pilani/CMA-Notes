Key concept

Variance Bridge Reconciliation Check

Concept: Variance check: Budget + Σ bridge steps = Actual

Statement

\[ B + \sum_{i=1}^{k} \Delta_i = A \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad A - B - \sum_{i=1}^{k} \Delta_i = 0 \]

where \(B\) is the budget (or prior year, or prior LE) value of the metric being bridged (revenue, contribution, EBITDA, FCF), \(A\) the actual (or LE) value, and \(\Delta_i\) the \(k\) bridge steps — for an EBITDA bridge typically price, volume, mix, input cost, conversion cost, overheads, FX and one-offs — each signed so that favourable is positive for profit.

Explanation

A bridge is only credible if it adds up exactly. The check confirms that the decomposition is complete — no residual hidden in rounding or "other" — and that the bridge endpoints are the same numbers shown on the P&L page. Typical causes of a failing check:

  • Interaction terms dropped: in PVM, the joint price × volume effect must be assigned (usually to price, by computing price variance at actual volume) or shown separately.
  • Different endpoints: the bridge reads budget EBITDA from a stale copy or uses adjusted EBITDA while the P&L shows reported EBITDA.
  • Sign errors: cost variances entered as positive when adverse.
  • Missing categories: new SKUs, discontinued SKUs or FX not captured by any step.
  • Period mismatch: month bridge using YTD budget.

Good practice: compute an explicit "unexplained" step equal to \(A - B - \sum \Delta_i\) in the model; it must be zero (within tolerance), and the chart should not show it if it is zero. If management accepts a small residual for practical reasons, label it ("Other / rounding") and keep it below a materiality threshold such as 2% of the total variance. Build the same check for every bridge in the pack: month, YTD, LE vs AOP, and AOP vs PY.

Practice Questions

Q1. Reconcile or not [L1]

AOP revenue ₹520 crore; price +₹14 crore; volume −₹22 crore; mix +₹3 crore; actual revenue ₹517 crore. Test the bridge and state what you would investigate.

Q2. Interaction term [L2]

Budget: 10,000 units at ₹500. Actual: 11,000 units at ₹520. Compute price and volume variances (a) with price variance at actual volume and (b) with both at budget values plus a separate interaction term. Show that both reconcile and explain which convention you would document.

💡 Hint: The difference between the two conventions is exactly the change in price times the change in volume.

Q3. Endpoint mismatch [L2]

The executive summary shows actual EBITDA ₹61.2 crore; the bridge ends at ₹63.0 crore. The difference equals a ₹1.8 crore one-off legal settlement. Explain how to present the bridge so that it reconciles to reported EBITDA while still showing underlying performance.
