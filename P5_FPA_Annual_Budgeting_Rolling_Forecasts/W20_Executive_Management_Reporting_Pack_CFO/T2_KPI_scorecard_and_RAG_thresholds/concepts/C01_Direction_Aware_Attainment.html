Key concept

Direction-Aware Attainment %

Concept: Attainment % = Actual ÷ Target (higher-is-better); = Target ÷ Actual (lower-is-better)

Statement

\[ \text{Attainment} = \begin{cases} \dfrac{A}{T} & \text{if higher is better} \\[2ex] \dfrac{T}{A} & \text{if lower is better} \end{cases} \]

where \(A\) is the actual value of the KPI for the period and \(T\) is the target for the same period and basis. In both cases attainment ≥ 100% means the KPI is at or better than target; < 100% means it is worse.

Explanation

Attainment normalises KPIs with different units (₹, days, %, points) onto one scale so a single RAG rule and a single visual can cover the scorecard. The direction flag is what prevents the most common scorecard error: a DSO of 68 against 60 computes as 113% "attainment" on A/T and shows Green. Inverting to T/A gives 88% — correctly Red.

Conditions and limitations:

  • Both A and T must be positive and of the same basis (both YTD, both annualised). With zero or negative values the ratio is undefined or flips meaning; use a signed gap \(d \times (A - T)\) instead, where \(d = +1\) for higher-is-better and \(-1\) for lower-is-better.
  • Non-linearity of T/A: for lower-is-better KPIs, the inverted ratio is not symmetric — being 20% worse (A = 1.2T) gives 83.3%, but being 20% better (A = 0.8T) gives 125%. An alternative linear form is \(1 - (A - T)/T = 2 - A/T\), which gives 80% and 120%. Choose one convention and document it.
  • Percentage KPIs: attainment on percentages (GM 33.5% vs 34%) produces small ratio differences (98.5%) for economically large gaps; consider absolute bps bands.
  • Range KPIs: neither formula applies; use the absolute deviation \(\lvert A/T - 1 \rvert\).

Illustration

KPI Direction Actual Target Formula Attainment
OTIF HIB 88% 95% 88 ÷ 95 92.6%
EBITDA YTD (₹ crore) HIB 60.8 83.6 60.8 ÷ 83.6 72.7%
DSO (days) LIB 68 60 60 ÷ 68 88.2%
Freight per unit (₹) LIB 142 130 130 ÷ 142 91.5%
Attrition LIB 24% 18% 18 ÷ 24 75.0%
Net debt/EBITDA (×) LIB 1.28 1.50 1.50 ÷ 1.28 117.2%

Linear alternative for DSO: \(2 - 68/60 = 86.7\%\), slightly harsher than 88.2%.

Connections

Attainment is the input to the RAG rule (c02) and is implemented in Excel and DAX in s04; direction flags and bands are set in s03 and recorded in the KPI definition sheet (s05). In Power BI it is the Attainment measure using DIVIDE. It also underpins incentive scorecards in HR, where payout curves are often defined on attainment.

Practice Questions

Q1. Compute attainment [L1]

Compute attainment for: fill rate 93% vs 97% (HIB); DIO 80 vs 75 days (LIB); CCC 91 vs 80 days (LIB); NPS 41 vs 45 (HIB); cost per hire ₹62,000 vs ₹55,000 (LIB). For the LIB KPIs, also compute the linear alternative and comment.

Q2. When the formula breaks [L2]

Shakti's e-commerce unit has budget EBITDA of −₹2.0 crore (a planned loss) and actual EBITDA of −₹3.5 crore. A new BU has target CCC of −5 days and actual of +4 days. Show why A/T and T/A both mislead in these cases and propose the measure you would use, with values.

💡 Hint: Work out what a signed gap in the KPI's own unit tells you that a ratio cannot.
