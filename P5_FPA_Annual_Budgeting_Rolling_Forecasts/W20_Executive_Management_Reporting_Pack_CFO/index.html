Objective: Package budgets, rolling forecasts, KPIs, and variance narratives into a monthly board-level Management Information System (MIS) pack.

How this week fits

This is the closing week of Phase 5 (FP&A, Annual Budgeting, Rolling Forecasts & Variance Storytelling). Weeks 17–19 produced the engines: the driver-based master budget (AOP), the 12-month rolling forecast and Latest Estimate (LE), and the price-volume-mix (PVM) and EBITDA bridge engine. None of those is the product a CFO consumes. The product is the Monthly Business Review (MBR) pack: a fixed-format document that tells management, in fifteen minutes, where the business stands against plan, why, what the full year now looks like, and what decisions are needed.

Prerequisites you should already have: Ind AS 108 segment logic and Schedule III P&L presentation (Phase 1), ratio and working capital analysis (Phase 2), Power BI/DAX measures and conditional formatting (Weeks 11–12), and the Week 17–19 models. Week 20 converts these into communication: the MBR structure (Topic 1), the KPI scorecard with direction-aware RAG (Topic 2), action-oriented commentary (Topic 3), business partnering with non-finance heads (Topic 4) and the final assembly of Project 6 (Topic 5). Week 21 moves to capital budgeting, which feeds the capex tracker you build here; Weeks 23–24 reuse the Project 6 pack as a capstone and interview walkthrough.

Throughout the week, use one running case so every page ties: Shakti Home Appliances Ltd, an NSE-listed consumer durables company with three reportable segments (Kitchen Appliances, Fans & Lighting, Water Purifiers), FY27 AOP revenue ₹1,200 crore and publicly guided EBITDA of ₹150 crore (12.5% margin). The reporting month is October 2026 (Month 7 of FY27).

Refresh before starting

Week 17–19 outputs: master budget, rolling forecast, LE and PVM engine

Recall the chain of outputs you will plug into the pack. The master budget/AOP is the locked annual baseline: revenue by segment and channel built from volume × price, COGS from BOM and conversion cost, opex by cost centre, capex plan and the budgeted balance sheet and cash flow. The rolling forecast extends 12 months forward each month, driver-based, and is not locked. The LE for the fiscal year is YTD actual plus year-to-go (YTG) forecast. The PVM engine splits revenue and gross margin variance into price, volume, mix and (where needed) FX and timing effects, and the EBITDA bridge walks from budget EBITDA to actual through those steps plus cost variances. Check that your Week 19 bridge reconciles exactly (Budget + Σ steps = Actual) before you reuse it — every chart in the pack will inherit any break.

Working capital metrics and cash flow analysis

DSO = Trade receivables ÷ Revenue × days; DIO = Inventory ÷ COGS × days; DPO = Trade payables ÷ COGS (or purchases) × days; CCC = DIO + DSO − DPO. Use average or closing balances consistently, and annualise monthly revenue properly (trailing three months × 4 or trailing twelve months). Recall the indirect-method CFO under Ind AS 7: profit before tax adjusted for non-cash items and working capital changes, less taxes paid. Free cash flow = CFO − capex (including lease principal repayments if your definition treats leases as debt-like). Net debt = borrowings (+ lease liabilities if included) − cash and liquid investments. Recall that Ind AS 116 lifts EBITDA because rent moves below EBITDA into depreciation and interest — this affects Net debt/EBITDA covenants.

Power BI/Excel dashboard basics and conditional formatting

Excel: conditional formatting with formula rules (=$H5="R"), icon sets with "show icon only", custom number formats (#,##0.0;(#,##0.0);"–"), named ranges and XLOOKUP/SUMIFS from a data tab. Power BI: measures for Actual, Budget, PY and LE, a calendar table with fiscal-year columns (April start), field-value conditional formatting driven by a measure that returns a colour hex code or a status number, and bookmarks/page navigation. Keep visuals bound to measures, never to hard-typed numbers.

Executive communication frameworks (Pyramid, SCR)

Minto Pyramid: lead with the governing answer, support it with three to five mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive (MECE) arguments, then the data. SCR: Situation (shared context), Complication (what changed or what is at risk), Resolution (what we will do or what we need decided). In an MBR, the executive summary page is the top of the pyramid; variance pages are the supporting arguments; appendix schedules are the data. SCR is how each headline message is phrased.

Cross-cutting concepts

Consistency: same definitions, sign conventions and formats across all pages

A pack loses credibility the first time a director finds two different EBITDA numbers on two pages. Consistency has three layers. Definitions: EBITDA (pre- or post-Ind AS 116? pre- or post-other income? pre- or post-exceptional items?), net debt (leases in or out?), DSO (closing or average receivables, revenue gross or net of GST?) must be fixed in a KPI definitions appendix and applied on every page and every month. Sign conventions: decide once whether a variance is shown as "favourable positive" (F/A convention, where a cost overrun is negative) or arithmetic (Actual − Budget, where a cost overrun is positive) — the F/A convention is usually clearer for executives, and colour must follow favourability, not sign. Formats: units (₹ crore to one decimal throughout; never mix lakh and crore on one page), period labels (Oct-26, YTD Oct-26, FY27 LE), column order (Actual | Budget | Var | Var % | PY | Var vs PY), and chart styles (IBCS-style: actual solid, budget outlined, PY grey). Build consistency into the model: one mapping table, one measure library, one formatting template, and a reconciliation check that each page's totals equal the P&L master.

Storytelling: numbers to drivers to actions to decisions

The pack must move the reader up a ladder. Numbers: EBITDA YTD ₹61 crore vs budget ₹84 crore. Drivers: the ₹23 crore gap is volume −₹10 crore (Water Purifiers e-commerce slowdown and weak Fans season), adverse mix −₹3 crore, copper and aluminium inflation −₹7 crore, freight and other opex −₹3 crore, employee cost −₹1 crore, partly offset by A&P savings +₹2 crore. Actions: copper-indexed price increase of 2% on fans from 1 December (owner: Head of Sales, Fans); freight re-tender in the North (owner: Head of Logistics, by 15 December). Decisions: approve the price increase and accept a possible 3% volume risk; decide whether to defer the ₹25 crore Hosur line-2 capex by one quarter. Each page should be built so the reader can climb the ladder: chart (numbers), bridge (drivers), commentary box (actions), and the executive summary's "decisions required" section. A page that stops at numbers is a report; a pack that ends at decisions is management information.

Forward-looking emphasis: LE, risks/opportunities and corrective actions

By Month 7, most of the year's result is already banked; what management can still influence is the five months to go. The pack must therefore give at least equal weight to the LE (YTD actual + YTG forecast) and the gap to AOP and to external guidance as to the backward-looking variance. A risks and opportunities (R&O) log quantifies items not yet in the LE — each with ₹ impact, probability and owner — so management sees a range: LE ₹127 crore, LE plus probability-weighted R&O ₹131 crore, upside case ₹138 crore — all short of the ₹150 crore guidance. Corrective actions then close the remaining gap, each with an owner, due date and ₹ value, tracked in an action log carried forward month to month until closed. For a listed company with public guidance, this is also where finance flags whether guidance remains achievable, which feeds investor-relations and SEBI LODR disclosure judgements.

Business partnering lens: tailor insights to each stakeholder

The same variance means different things to different readers. The CEO wants the gap to guidance and the three actions that close it. The Sales head wants price realisation, discount leakage by channel and collections against target. The Supply Chain head wants inventory days by category, obsolescence risk, freight per unit and OTIF. The HR head wants headcount against plan, attrition cost and revenue per employee. The Audit Committee wants control matters, related-party transactions and the reconciliation of management numbers to reported results. A good pack has one consistent core (executive summary, P&L, cash, R&O) and stakeholder-specific deep-dive pages or appendices, each written in the operational language of its owner: "₹8 crore cash released if DSO in the modern-trade channel falls from 62 to 55 days" rather than "trade receivables decreased".

Exercise needs: complete MBR pack, KPI scorecard with RAG, PVM and EBITDA bridge pages, cash tracker, risk/action log and CFO commentary

Project 6 is assessed on the completeness and integrity of these components: (1) a complete MBR pack in standard page order; (2) a KPI scorecard of 12–15 financial and operational KPIs with direction-aware RAG computed by formula, not typed; (3) PVM and EBITDA bridge pages fed from the Week 19 engine and reconciled to the P&L; (4) a cash tracker covering CCC, receivables ageing, inventory days, net debt and covenant headroom; (5) a risk and action log with ₹ impact, probability, owner and due date; and (6) a one-page CFO commentary in SCR form ending with decisions required. Every number in the commentary must be traceable to a page and every page must reconcile to the master model. The Saturday simulation then tests whether you can present this pack and defend it.

Operational data: OTIF 88% (target 95%), fill rate 93% (target 97%), attrition 24% annualised (target 18%), NPS 41 (target 45).

Required tabs and pages

  1. Inputs and Assumptions — all raw data and drivers, with source and date.
  2. Map — GL-to-MIS line mapping and segment mapping.
  3. Calc_PL — month, YTD, FY LE for Actual, Budget, PY; variances in F/A convention.
  4. Calc_PVM — PVM by segment, EBITDA bridge Budget → Actual (YTD) and AOP → LE (FY).
  5. Calc_WC — DSO, DIO, DPO, CCC, ageing buckets, net debt, covenant headroom, FCF.
  6. Calc_KPI — attainment and RAG with direction flag per KPI.
  7. Pack pages: Executive Summary; P&L Month/YTD/LE; Segment pages (3); EBITDA bridge; KPI scorecard; Cash & Working Capital tracker; Capex tracker; R&O log; Action log; Appendix (definitions, methodology, schedules).
  8. Checks — master check cell that must read 0.

Required checks

  • Segment revenue totals = P&L revenue (month, YTD, LE).
  • Budget EBITDA + Σ bridge steps = Actual EBITDA; AOP + Σ steps = LE.
  • LE = YTD actual + YTG forecast for every line.
  • Net debt computed identically on cash tracker and covenant table.
  • Every RAG cell driven by formula; no hard-coded colours.
  • Commentary ₹ figures cross-referenced to page and cell.

Deliverable format

A 12–16 page PDF pack (landscape, IBCS-style), the Excel model with a ReadMe and version log, and a one-page CFO commentary (maximum 400 words) in SCR form ending with "Decisions required". Optionally, a Power BI version of the scorecard and cash tracker.

Saturday assessment paper

Roadmap assessment: Saturday 3-Hour Simulation: Present the Monthly Business Review pack and recommend 3 concrete margin-recovery actions to hit full-year EBITDA guidance.

Instructions. Time: 3 hours (30 minutes reading and preparation, 2 hours build and write, 30 minutes presentation and Q&A). Total: 100 marks. Use the Shakti Home Appliances data in the exercise brief plus the additional data below. Show all workings in Excel; written answers in a separate document. Assume FY27 guidance of ₹150 crore EBITDA was communicated to analysts in May 2026.

Q1. Executive summary page [L3] (20 marks)

Draft the one-page executive summary for October 2026: headline table (Revenue, GM%, EBITDA, EBITDA %, FCF YTD, Net debt) for Month, YTD and FY LE against AOP and PY; three to five key messages using the Pyramid Principle; LE vs AOP vs guidance; and a "decisions required" box with at least two decisions.

Q2. EBITDA bridge and LE gap [L2] (15 marks)

Construct the YTD EBITDA bridge from budget to actual (volume, price/mix, material inflation, freight, employee cost, A&P, other) and the FY bridge from AOP ₹150 crore to LE. Additional data: of the 1.6 pp GM shortfall YTD, 1.1 pp is copper/aluminium inflation and 0.5 pp is adverse mix towards entry-level fans. Show the reconciliation check.

💡 Hint: Compute volume effect at budget margin first, then layer the margin-rate effects on actual revenue so the steps add exactly.

Q3. KPI scorecard with direction-aware RAG [L2] (15 marks)

Build a 12-KPI scorecard covering revenue growth, GM%, EBITDA %, ROCE, DSO, DIO, CCC, OTIF, fill rate, attrition, NPS and capex utilisation. Define direction, target, tolerance band and formula for RAG; write the Excel formula you would use; justify one KPI where a tighter band than ±5% is appropriate.

Q4. Cash and covenant tracker [L2] (15 marks)

Compute DSO, DIO, DPO and CCC (use YTD annualised revenue and COGS), net debt on both the management definition (incl. leases) and bank definition (excl. leases), and covenant headroom on Net debt/EBITDA using LTM EBITDA of ₹128 crore (pre-Ind AS 116 EBITDA ₹116 crore for bank covenant). Quantify cash released if DSO returns to budget.

Q5. Commentary rewrite [L2] (10 marks)

Rewrite the following three draft comments into action-oriented commentary with root cause, ₹ and bps impact, FY impact, owner and timeline: (a) "Freight cost higher by ₹3 crore"; (b) "Water Purifier sales below budget"; (c) "Employee cost slightly over".

Q6. Three margin-recovery actions [L3] (15 marks)

Recommend three concrete actions to close the gap between LE and ₹150 crore guidance. For each: ₹ EBITDA impact in FY27, timing (month effective), owner, key risk and a leading KPI to monitor. Total identified value must be at least the gap; state the residual risk and whether guidance should be reaffirmed or revised.

💡 Hint: Think price (copper-indexed increase), mix (push premium purifiers), cost (freight re-tender, A&P phasing), and be honest about lead times with five months left.

Q7. Presentation and Q&A [L3] (10 marks)

Present the pack in 10 minutes. Be prepared for: "Why should I believe the YTG margin of 33.0% when YTD is 32.4%?"; "What happens to the covenant if LE slips another ₹10 crore?"; "Is the Water Purifier slowdown structural or timing?"; "Does the Audit Committee need to see anything from this month?"
