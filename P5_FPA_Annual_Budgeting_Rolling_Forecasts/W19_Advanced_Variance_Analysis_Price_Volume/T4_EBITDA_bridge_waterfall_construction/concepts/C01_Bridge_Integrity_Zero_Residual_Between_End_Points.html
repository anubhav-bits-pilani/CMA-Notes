Key concept

Bridge Integrity: Zero Residual Between End Points

Concept: Bridge integrity: Budget EBITDA + Σ bridge steps = Actual EBITDA (zero residual)

Statement

For any bridge from a base scenario B to a comparator scenario C with steps \(k = 1, \dots, n\):

\[ \text{EBITDA}^{B} + \sum_{k=1}^{n} \Delta_k = \text{EBITDA}^{C} \qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad R = \text{EBITDA}^{C} - \text{EBITDA}^{B} - \sum_{k=1}^{n} \Delta_k = 0 \]

where \(\text{EBITDA}^{B}\) is the base (budget) EBITDA, \(\text{EBITDA}^{C}\) is the comparator (actual) EBITDA, \(\Delta_k\) is the signed EBITDA effect of step \(k\) (positive raises EBITDA), and \(R\) is the residual. Integrity requires \(R = 0\) by construction of the formulas, not by adding a balancing step.

Explanation

A bridge is an exact decomposition of a difference. If the step formulas are chosen correctly — price at actual quantity, volume and mix at budget unit margin, cost at actual quantity, opex as budget minus actual — the algebra telescopes and the residual is identically zero for any data set. When the residual is non-zero, it is a symptom of one of a small number of methodology errors:

  • Joint (interaction) variance left out. Price computed at budget quantity and volume at budget price leaves \((P^{A} - P^{B}) \times (Q^{A} - Q^{B})\) unexplained.
  • Revenue volume used in an EBITDA bridge. Volume valued at budget price rather than unit margin overstates the bar by the budget unit cost of the incremental units.
  • Population mismatch. New SKUs (no budget price) or discontinued SKUs (no actual volume) dropped from the engine.
  • Sign errors. A cost step computed as Actual − Budget.
  • End-point mismatch. Actual EBITDA taken from the GL while the steps come from an MIS cube with different mapping or a later cut-off.

Integrity has to hold at every level that the pack reports: revenue sub-bridge, gross-margin sub-bridge and EBITDA bridge, and at every slice (region, channel, month) if the bridge is sliceable in Power BI. A rounding difference in displayed labels is acceptable if footnoted; an unrounded residual is not.

Illustration

Two SKUs (units in lakh, ₹ per unit, amounts ₹ lakh). Budget: A 60 units at ₹50 price, ₹35 cost; B 40 units at ₹80, ₹48. Actual: A 75 at ₹49, ₹37; B 35 at ₹84, ₹50. Budget opex 1,380, actual 1,330.

Item ₹ lakh
Budget EBITDA (2,180 GM − 1,380 opex) 800
Price: (49 − 50) × 75 + (84 − 80) × 35 +65
Volume: (110 − 100) × 21.8 budget avg margin +218
Mix: (75 − 66) × 15 + (35 − 44) × 32 −153
Cost: (35 − 37) × 75 + (48 − 50) × 35 −220
Opex: 1,380 − 1,330 +50
Actual EBITDA (2,090 GM − 1,330 opex) 760

Check: \(800 + 65 + 218 - 153 - 220 + 50 = 760\), so \(R = 0\). Had the analyst computed price at budget quantity (\(-1 \times 60 + 4 \times 40 = +100\)), the steps would sum to 795 and a −35 "balancing" bar would appear — that −35 is the joint price × volume effect, which belongs to price under this formula set.

Connections

Integrity is the first check in the reconciliation table (s06) and the reason the step valuation rules (c02) exist. It mirrors the CMA operating statement in which budgeted profit plus all sales and cost variances equals actual profit, and it extends to revenue PVM (topic 1, concept c04) and to the sign conventions of topic 5. In Power BI it is implemented as a check measure that should return zero for every slicer combination.

Practice Questions

Q1. Residual diagnosis [L2]

Budget: 1,00,000 units at ₹200, unit margin ₹60. Actual: 1,10,000 units at ₹210, unit cost unchanged. Opex unchanged. An analyst shows Price = (210 − 200) × 1,00,000 and Volume = 10,000 × ₹200, and adds a balancing bar. Compute the residual under the analyst's method, identify each error, and restate the bridge with zero residual.

💡 Hint: There are two separate errors: the quantity used for price and the unit value used for volume.

Q2. Integrity under slicing [L3]

Your Power BI bridge reconciles at total company but shows a residual of ₹3.4 crore when filtered to the Modern Trade channel. List four model or measure design issues that could cause a residual only at a filtered level, and the test you would run for each.
