Subtopic note

Full-Year Attainment vs AOP and Prior Year; Gap-to-Target

Scope: Full-year attainment vs AOP and vs prior year; gap-to-target identification

Why it matters

Leadership does not manage the LE number itself; it manages the gap between the LE and what was promised — to the Board (the AOP), to the market (guidance) and to employees (incentive targets). Attainment percentages drive bonus payouts, sales-team commission tiers and dealer schemes. Growth vs prior year is what analysts and the press compare. Identifying where the gap sits — which BU, which line, which months, price vs volume — determines the corrective actions ("gap-closure plan") the CFO will ask for in the MBR.

Core notes

Attainment and gap metrics

\[ \text{Attainment \%} = \frac{\text{LE}}{\text{AOP}} \times 100, \qquad \text{Gap to AOP} = \text{AOP} - \text{LE} \]

\[ \text{Growth vs PY \%} = \frac{\text{LE} - \text{PY}}{\text{PY}} \times 100 \]

where LE is the full-year Latest Estimate, AOP the approved annual plan and PY the prior-year actual for the same line.

Sign conventions. For revenue and profit lines, a positive gap (AOP − LE > 0) is a shortfall. For cost lines, presentation must be explicit: either keep the formula and label "positive = overspend avoided" or (more common) show favourable/(adverse) variance = LE − AOP for income and AOP − LE for costs. Pick one convention for the whole pack and state it in a footnote.

Negative bases. If AOP or PY is negative (a loss-making BU or a negative PBT), attainment and growth percentages become meaningless or sign-flipped. Report the absolute gap and mark the percentage "n.m." (not meaningful).

Attainment vs growth: why both

Metric Question answered Limitation
Attainment vs AOP Did we do what we committed to? Depends on AOP ambition; sandbagged AOPs make attainment easy
Growth vs PY Are we better than last year? Ignores the plan and market conditions
Gap to AOP (₹) How much must be found? Not scaled; compare with materiality
Gap vs Prior LE Is the outlook deteriorating? Needs stored prior versions

A BU at 104% attainment but 2% growth may have been under-planned; a BU at 92% attainment but 18% growth may have had a stretch AOP. Board discussions usually need both.

Gap-to-target identification

Decompose the total gap to find where it sits:

  1. By dimension: BU, region, product, channel, customer segment. Rank by ₹ contribution to the gap.
  2. By time: YTD gap (already lost, cannot be recovered except through YTG over-delivery) vs YTG gap (still addressable).
  3. By driver: for revenue, volume, price and mix (Week 19 variance techniques); for EBITDA, revenue effect at contribution margin, material cost, fixed-cost overruns.
  4. By controllability: market-driven (commodity, FX, demand) vs execution-driven (sales coverage, launches delayed).

A gap waterfall from AOP to LE shows these components; the gap-closure plan then lists actions (price increase, cost containment, deferral of discretionary spend, accelerated launches), each with ₹ value, owner, timing and confidence.

Excel implementation

Attainment   =IFERROR(IF(AOP<=0,"n.m.",LE/AOP),"n.m.")
Gap          =AOP-LE
GrowthPY     =IF(PY<=0,"n.m.",LE/PY-1)
FavAdv       =IF(AcctType="Income",LE-AOP,AOP-LE)

Use conditional formatting: attainment ≥ 100% green, 95–100% amber, < 95% red (thresholds set on the Control sheet). Rank BUs by gap with SORTBY(BUList,GapRange,-1).

Exports hold 71% of the net gap (12 of 17); Industrial over-delivers by 4. If the Exports YTD gap is 7 and YTG gap 5, only 5 plus any upside is addressable within the year.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Re-based AOP: if the Board approved a revised AOP (e.g. after a demerger or a major acquisition), state which AOP is the comparator and keep the original for incentive purposes if the scheme specifies it.
  • FX and commodity normalisation: compare LE with AOP at AOP exchange rates (constant currency) to separate performance from FX translation; show both.
  • Mix of growth bases: PY may include one-offs (e.g. a ₹40 crore insurance gain). Show "adjusted" growth excluding them.
  • Offsetting gaps: total attainment of 99% can hide one BU at 80% and another at 110%; always show the BU table.
  • Cost-line attainment: a cost LE below AOP is not necessarily good — underspend on maintenance or marketing may defer problems.
  • Incentive thresholds: small gaps near a payout threshold (e.g. 89.6% vs 90%) attract manipulation risk; finance should be aware when reviewing YTG.

Key takeaways

  • Attainment = LE ÷ AOP; Gap = AOP − LE; growth vs PY is the second lens — use both.
  • State the favourable/adverse sign convention clearly, especially for cost lines.
  • Mark percentages "n.m." for negative or zero bases.
  • Decompose the gap by BU, time (YTD vs YTG), driver and controllability.
  • Only the YTG portion of the gap is addressable; build a gap-closure plan with owners.
  • Normalise FX and one-offs before drawing conclusions.

Practice Questions

Q1. Attainment table [L1]

Revenue (₹ crore): AOP 2,800, LE 2,716, PY 2,450. Opex: AOP 640, LE 662, PY 590. Compute attainment, gap and growth for both lines and present favourable/(adverse) variances with a stated convention.

Q2. Gap decomposition [L2]

Total revenue gap to AOP is ₹84 crore. By BU: North 30, South −12 (ahead), West 46, East 20. YTD portion of the gap: North 18, South −5, West 21, East 9. Rank the BUs, separate addressable from lost gap, and propose which BU's gap-closure plan the CFO should prioritise and why.

Q3. Negative-base handling [L2]

A start-up BU has AOP EBITDA −₹15 crore, LE −₹22 crore and PY −₹30 crore. Explain why attainment % and growth % are misleading here, what you would report instead, and write the Excel formulas that handle these cases automatically across a 20-BU table.

💡 Hint: Absolute variance and a directional label carry the message without a percentage.

Q4. Constant-currency attainment [L3]

An IT services BU's AOP revenue was USD 120 million at ₹83 per USD (₹996 crore). The LE is USD 116 million at an average ₹86.5 per USD. Compute LE in ₹, reported attainment, constant-currency attainment, and explain which measure should drive the BU head's incentive and which the company's guidance commentary.
