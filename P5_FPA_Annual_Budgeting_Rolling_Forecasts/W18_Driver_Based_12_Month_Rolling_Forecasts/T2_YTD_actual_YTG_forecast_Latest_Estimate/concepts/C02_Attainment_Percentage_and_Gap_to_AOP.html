Key concept

Attainment Percentage and Gap to AOP

Concept: Attainment % = LE ÷ AOP; Gap to AOP = AOP − LE

Statement

\[ \text{Attainment \%} = \frac{\text{LE}}{\text{AOP}} \times 100, \qquad \text{Gap to AOP} = \text{AOP} - \text{LE} \]

where LE is the full-year Latest Estimate for a line (revenue, EBITDA, PAT, volume) and AOP the approved Annual Operating Plan value for the same line and fiscal year. For income lines, attainment below 100% and a positive gap denote a shortfall.

Explanation

Attainment scales performance against plan so BUs of different size can be compared and incentive curves applied; the gap states the absolute rupee amount that must be found (or that is ahead). Both are needed: a 2% shortfall in a ₹5,000 crore BU (₹100 crore) matters more than a 20% shortfall in a ₹50 crore BU (₹10 crore).

Conditions and conventions:

  • Positive denominators only: for negative or zero AOP (loss-making units), report "n.m." and the absolute gap.
  • Cost lines: attainment of a cost LE against AOP is ambiguous (105% of opex budget is bad). Present cost lines as favourable/(adverse) variances rather than attainment, or define "budget utilisation %" explicitly.
  • Same basis: compare at the same FX rates, accounting policies and perimeter (excluding unbudgeted acquisitions, or showing them separately). Constant-currency attainment isolates operational performance.
  • Which AOP: if a revised AOP exists, specify whether attainment is against original or revised; incentive schemes usually fix the comparator in the scheme rules.
  • Split of the gap: \( \text{Gap} = (\text{AOP}_{YTD} - A_{YTD}) + (\text{AOP}_{YTG} - F_{YTG}) \) — the first part is already lost; only the second is addressable through actions.

In Excel: =IF(AOP>0,LE/AOP,"n.m.") and =AOP-LE, with conditional formatting bands (e.g. ≥ 100% green, 95–99.9% amber, < 95% red). In DAX: Attainment = DIVIDE([LE],[AOP]), which returns BLANK on zero denominators.

Illustration

FY26 (₹ crore):

Line AOP LE Attainment Gap to AOP
Revenue 2,400 2,292 95.5% 108
EBITDA 360 318 88.3% 42
New BU EBITDA −20 −28 n.m. 8 (adverse)

YTD AOP revenue 1,050 vs actual 1,010 (gap 40, lost); YTG AOP 1,350 vs forecast 1,282 (gap 68, addressable). EBITDA attainment falls much further than revenue because of operating leverage: the revenue shortfall at a ~35% contribution margin explains about ₹38 crore of the ₹42 crore EBITDA gap.

Practice Questions

Q1. Attainment and gap by BU [L1]

BU revenue (₹ crore) AOP / LE: A 900 / 873, B 450 / 468, C 300 / 255. Compute attainment and gap for each BU and the total, and identify which BU contributes most to the total gap.

Q2. Splitting the gap [L2]

Revenue AOP ₹1,600 crore with H1 AOP ₹700 crore. H1 actual ₹672 crore; H2 forecast ₹858 crore. Compute LE, attainment, total gap, lost gap and addressable gap, and state the H2 forecast needed to reach 100% attainment.

Q3. Choosing the comparator [L3]

Mid-year the Board approved a revised AOP after a ₹300 crore acquisition, raising AOP revenue from ₹2,000 crore to ₹2,180 crore (the acquisition contributing ₹180 crore for the post-acquisition months). LE is ₹2,150 crore including ₹170 crore from the acquisition. Compute attainment on both comparators and on an organic basis, and recommend which should drive (a) the CEO's bonus and (b) the Board narrative.

💡 Hint: Organic attainment excludes the acquisition from both LE and AOP.
