Key concept

Indian Lender DSCR and Interest Coverage

Concept: Indian lender DSCR = (PAT + Depreciation + Interest) ÷ (Interest + Principal repayment); ICR = EBIT ÷ Interest

Statement

\[ \text{DSCR}_t = \frac{\text{PAT}_t + \text{Dep}_t + I_t}{I_t + P_t} \qquad \text{ICR}_t = \frac{\text{EBIT}_t}{I_t} \]

where \( \text{PAT}_t \) is profit after tax; \( \text{Dep}_t \) is depreciation and amortisation (non-cash add-back); \( I_t \) is interest on term debt (some lenders use total finance cost) for the year; \( P_t \) is scheduled principal repayment of term debt during the year (excluding revolver repayments and voluntary prepayments); and \( \text{EBIT}_t \) is earnings before interest and tax. Some agreements use EBITDA in the ICR numerator.

Explanation

The DSCR numerator approximates cash available for debt service: PAT plus non-cash depreciation gives a crude cash profit, and interest is added back because it is part of debt service in the denominator. The denominator is the contractual debt service in the year. This "PAT-based" definition is standard in Indian bank credit appraisal notes (term-loan appraisal, CMA data formats used for bank credit), typically requiring a minimum of 1.20–1.30× and an average over the tenor of around 1.30–1.50×.

Limitations to keep in mind:

  • It ignores working-capital investment and maintenance capex, so it overstates true debt-service capacity in growth years. Project-finance lenders therefore use CFADS-based DSCR: (EBITDA − tax − capex − ΔNWC) ÷ (interest + principal).
  • It ignores lease payments — for retailers, add lease principal and interest to the denominator (and rent-equivalent to the numerator) if the covenant is lease-inclusive.
  • Non-cash items beyond depreciation (deferred tax, impairment, ESOP cost) may be added back in some definitions.
  • Bullet repayments collapse DSCR in the maturity year; agreements often exclude refinanced bullets.
  • Interest in the formula should be the accrued amount including capitalised interest if the agreement says so (t2 s03).

ICR tests whether operating profit covers interest; EBIT-based ICR is stricter than EBITDA-based. Rating agencies (CRISIL, ICRA, CARE) also look at interest coverage and DSCR in rating rationales, along with Net debt/EBITDA and TOL/TNW.

Both ratios depend on interest, which depends on the debt schedule; with average-balance interest they are inside the circular loop — use breaker-consistent values when testing covenants.

Illustration

FY27E (₹ crore): PAT 340; depreciation 250; interest on term debt 160 (total finance cost 190); EBIT 640; scheduled principal 420.

\[ \text{DSCR} = \frac{340 + 250 + 160}{160 + 420} = \frac{750}{580} = 1.29\times \]

\[ \text{ICR} = \frac{640}{190} = 3.37\times \]

Against a 1.25× DSCR covenant, headroom is thin: a ₹30 crore fall in PAT reduces DSCR to \( 720 \div 580 = 1.24\times \) — a breach. A CFADS-based DSCR with maintenance capex of ₹150 crore and ΔNWC of ₹60 crore would be well below 1.25× on the same numbers, showing how flattering the PAT-based measure can be.

DSCR:  =(PAT+Dep+Int_Term)/(Int_Term+Sched_Principal)
ICR:   =IFERROR(EBIT/Total_Interest,"n.m.")

Practice Questions

Q1. Compute DSCR and ICR [L1]

FY26E: PAT ₹210 crore; depreciation ₹180 crore; interest on term loans ₹95 crore; total finance cost ₹120 crore; EBIT ₹400 crore; scheduled principal repayment ₹260 crore. Compute DSCR and ICR and assess against 1.25× and 2.5×.

Q2. PAT-based vs CFADS DSCR [L2]

Using Q1 data plus EBITDA ₹580 crore, cash tax ₹70 crore, maintenance capex ₹120 crore and ΔNWC ₹50 crore, compute CFADS-based DSCR. Explain the difference and which a project-finance lender would use.

💡 Hint: CFADS starts from EBITDA and deducts cash items before debt service.

Q3. Bullet year and covenant design [L3]

A company has ₹600 crore of NCDs maturing in FY28 that management intends to refinance. Its PAT-based DSCR excluding the bullet is 1.6×. Compute DSCR including the bullet if FY28 interest is ₹110 crore, scheduled term-loan principal ₹150 crore, PAT ₹300 crore and depreciation ₹200 crore. Explain how you would present DSCR in the model and what definition you would negotiate in a new facility.
