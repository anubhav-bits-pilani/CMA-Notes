Key concept

Cash Dividends: Prior-Year Final plus Current-Year Interim

Concept: Cash dividends_t = Final DPS (FY t−1) × Shares + Interim DPS (FY t) × Shares

Statement

\[ \text{CashDiv}_t = \text{DPS}^{\text{final}}_{t-1} \times N^{\text{final}}_t + \text{DPS}^{\text{interim}}_t \times N^{\text{interim}}_t \;\pm\; \Delta \text{Dividends payable}_t \]

where \(\text{CashDiv}_t\) is equity dividends paid in cash in financial year \(t\); \(\text{DPS}^{\text{final}}_{t-1}\) the final dividend per share relating to FY \(t-1\) (recommended after 31 March of \(t-1\), approved at the AGM held in FY \(t\)); \(N^{\text{final}}_t\) the shares eligible on the final dividend record date; \(\text{DPS}^{\text{interim}}_t\) interim dividend(s) per share declared in FY \(t\); \(N^{\text{interim}}_t\) shares eligible on the interim record date(s); and \(\Delta\)Dividends payable the change in declared-but-unpaid dividends (relevant only when an interim is declared near year-end and paid after it).

Explanation

Indian companies with a 31 March year-end follow a predictable cycle. The board recommends the final dividend with the annual results (April–May). Shareholders approve it at the AGM, which must be held within six months of year-end (Sec 96), typically July–September. Payment must be made within 30 days of declaration (Sec 127). Interim dividends are declared by the board during the year (Sec 123(3)) and also paid within 30 days. Hence the FY t cash outflow pairs last year's final with this year's interim.

Why it matters:

  • In the first forecast year, the prior-year final is often already known (recommended in the latest annual results) — use the actual DPS, not the payout assumption.
  • The final dividend relating to the last forecast year falls outside the forecast horizon; in a DCF or equity bridge it is not deducted from cash (it has not been paid), but analysts sometimes adjust net cash for a recommended-but-unpaid final dividend at the valuation date.
  • The share count to use is the count on the record date — opening shares for the final (unless allotments occurred between 1 April and the record date) and average or closing shares for interims.
  • Under Ind AS 10, the equity deduction follows the same timing as the cash (both in year \(t\)), so recognised dividends ≈ cash dividends except for dividends payable.

Excel:

=Final_DPS_Prev*Shares_Open + Interim_DPS*AVERAGE(Shares_Open,Shares_Close) - (Div_Payable_Close-Div_Payable_Open)

where Final_DPS_Prev is the prior column's final DPS (an OFFSET-free direct link to the previous year cell).

Illustration

Shares: FY27 opening 30.0 crore, closing 30.4 crore (ESOP allotments in Q3). FY26 final DPS ₹7.00 (recommended May 2026); FY27 interim DPS ₹3.00 declared October 2026 (record date shares 30.1 crore); FY27 final DPS ₹8.00 recommended May 2027; no dividends payable at either year-end.

  • FY27 cash dividends = 7.00 × 30.0 + 3.00 × 30.1 = 210.0 + 90.3 = ₹300.3 crore.
  • Dividends "relating to" FY27 = 3.00 × 30.1 + 8.00 × 30.4 (assuming no allotment before the record date) = 90.3 + 243.2 = ₹333.5 crore — the payout number a broker would quote.
  • FY28 cash dividends include ₹243.2 crore of FY27 final.

Connections

Practice Questions

Q1. Compute cash dividends [L1]

FY26 final DPS ₹5.50; FY27 interims of ₹2.00 (August) and ₹2.50 (January); shares constant at 22 crore; no dividends payable. Compute FY27 cash dividends and FY27 dividends relating to the year if the FY27 final is ₹6.00.

Q2. Late-March interim [L2]

A company declares a ₹4.00 interim dividend on 28 March 2027 on 50 crore shares, paid on 20 April 2027, in addition to its FY26 final of ₹6.00 paid in August 2026. FY28: FY27 final ₹5.00 paid August 2027; no interim. Compute recognised dividends and cash dividends for FY27 and FY28 and the dividends payable at 31 March 2027.

💡 Hint: Recognition follows declaration; cash follows payment.

Q3. Record-date share count [L2]

A 1:2 bonus issue (one bonus share for every two held) has a record date of 10 June 2027; the FY27 final dividend of ₹9.00 per pre-bonus share is approved at the AGM on 20 August 2027 with a record date after the bonus. Opening shares 12 crore. Explain how the DPS and share count should be expressed in the model and compute FY28 cash outflow for the final dividend.
