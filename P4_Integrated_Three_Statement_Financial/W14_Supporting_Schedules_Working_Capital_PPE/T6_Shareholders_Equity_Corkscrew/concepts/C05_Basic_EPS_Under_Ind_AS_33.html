Key concept

Basic EPS Under Ind AS 33

Concept: Basic EPS = PAT attributable to owners ÷ Weighted average shares (Ind AS 33)

Statement

\[ \text{Basic EPS}_t = \frac{\text{PAT}^{\text{owners}}_t - \text{Pref}_t}{\bar{N}_t} \]

where \(\text{PAT}^{\text{owners}}_t\) is profit for the period attributable to ordinary equity holders of the parent (consolidated PAT less NCI share, Ind AS 33.10, 12); \(\text{Pref}_t\) is the after-tax amount of preference dividends on preference shares classified as equity (and other adjustments such as differences on settlement of preference shares, Ind AS 33.12–18); and \(\bar{N}_t\) is the weighted average number of ordinary shares outstanding during the period, excluding treasury shares (Ind AS 33.19–20). EPS is presented for profit from continuing operations and for total profit, on the face of the statement of profit and loss (Ind AS 33.66).

Explanation

Numerator. Use the owners' share of PAT, not consolidated PAT and not total comprehensive income. Deduct dividends on preference shares classified as equity; dividends on preference shares classified as liabilities are already in finance costs. Cumulative preference dividends are deducted for the period whether or not declared (Ind AS 33.14).

Denominator. Time-weight every change in shares outstanding (Ind AS 33.20):

  • New shares from the date consideration is receivable (Ind AS 33.21).
  • Bonus issues, splits and consolidations retrospectively, as if from the start of the earliest period presented (Ind AS 33.26, 28, 64).
  • Rights issues: adjust for the bonus element (Ind AS 33.27(b), A2).
  • Buybacks from the date of buyback.
  • Treasury shares (e.g., held by a consolidated ESOP trust) excluded.

Diluted EPS adjusts both numerator and denominator for dilutive potential shares (options via treasury stock method at average price, convertibles via if-converted) — see s07. Diluted EPS ≤ basic EPS by construction; anti-dilutive instruments are excluded.

Modelling notes. Forecast basic EPS with the weighted average from the share-count schedule (s07); in annual models with no event dates use the mid-year average of opening and closing shares. EPS growth differs from PAT growth when share count changes — buybacks boost EPS, QIPs and ESOP allotments dilute it. Equity-research models compare forecast EPS to consensus, so getting the share count right is as important as getting PAT right.

Illustration

FY28E: consolidated PAT ₹1,150 crore; NCI share ₹85 crore; no equity preference shares. Opening shares 52.0 crore; ESOP allotment 0.6 crore on 1 October 2027 (183 days remaining of 366); buyback 2.0 crore on 1 January 2028 (91 days remaining).

\[ \bar{N} = 52.0 + 0.6 \times \frac{183}{366} - 2.0 \times \frac{91}{366} = 52.0 + 0.300 - 0.497 = 51.803 \text{ crore} \]

\[ \text{Basic EPS} = \frac{1{,}150 - 85}{51.803} = \frac{1{,}065}{51.803} \approx ₹20.56 \]

Using consolidated PAT by mistake would give ₹22.20 — overstated by 8%.

Practice Questions

Q1. Compute basic EPS [L1]

Consolidated PAT ₹640 crore; NCI share ₹40 crore; cumulative 7% preference shares of ₹100 crore classified as equity (dividend not declared this year); weighted-average shares 30 crore. Compute basic EPS.

Q2. Weighted shares and EPS growth [L2]

FY27 owners' PAT ₹900 crore, weighted shares 60 crore. FY28 owners' PAT ₹990 crore; opening shares 60 crore; buyback of 3 crore shares on 1 October 2027 (183 of 366 days remaining). Compute FY28 basic EPS, EPS growth, and decompose the growth into PAT growth and share-count effect.

💡 Hint: EPS growth ≈ (1 + PAT growth) ÷ (1 + change in weighted shares) − 1.

Q3. Restatement after bonus [L2]

FY27 basic EPS was reported as ₹36.00 on 25 crore weighted shares. On 15 July 2027 the company issues a 1:1 bonus. FY28 owners' PAT ₹1,050 crore, with no other share changes. Compute FY28 basic EPS and the restated FY27 EPS for the FY28 financial statements.
