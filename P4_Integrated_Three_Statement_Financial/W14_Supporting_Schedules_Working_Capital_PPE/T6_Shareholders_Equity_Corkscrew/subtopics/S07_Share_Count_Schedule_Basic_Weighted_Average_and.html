Subtopic note

Share Count Schedule: Basic, Weighted-Average and Diluted

Scope: Share count schedule: basic, weighted-average and diluted shares

Why it matters

EPS, DPS, book value per share and value per share all divide by a share count — and different measures need different counts. Basic shares outstanding at a date drive market capitalisation and the dividend record-date calculation; weighted-average shares drive Ind AS 33 basic EPS; diluted shares drive diluted EPS and per-share valuation. In Indian companies with active ESOP programmes, QIPs, rights issues, bonus issues and buybacks, these counts can differ by several percent. Equity-research models are judged on EPS accuracy against consensus, so an incorrect share count undermines the whole forecast.

Core notes

Three counts

Count Definition Used for
Basic shares outstanding (closing) Shares issued and outstanding at the date, net of treasury shares Market cap, book value per share, DPS × shares on record date
Weighted-average basic shares Shares outstanding during the period weighted by the fraction of the period they were outstanding (Ind AS 33.19–20) Basic EPS
Weighted-average diluted shares Weighted basic + dilutive potential ordinary shares (Ind AS 33.31–36) Diluted EPS
Fully diluted shares (valuation) Current basic + in-the-money options/convertibles at current price (treasury stock / if-converted) Equity value per share (Week 16)

Closing basic shares corkscrew

\[ N^{\text{close}}_t = N^{\text{close}}_{t-1} + \text{Issues}_t + \text{ESOP allotments}_t + \text{Bonus}_t - \text{Buyback}_t - \Delta\text{Treasury}_t \]

Bonus and split adjustments are applied retrospectively to all periods (including the historical columns) because they change the number of shares without changing resources (Ind AS 33.26, 28, 64).

Weighted-average shares

\[ \bar{N}_t = N^{\text{open}}_t + \sum_i \Delta N_i \times \frac{d_i}{D} \]

where \(\Delta N_i\) is the change in shares from event \(i\) (positive for issues, negative for buybacks), \(d_i\) the number of days from the event date to period end, and \(D\) the days in the period (365 or 366). Rules from Ind AS 33:

  • Shares issued for cash are included from the date cash is receivable (Ind AS 33.21).
  • Bonus issues and splits are treated as if they occurred at the start of the earliest period presented (Ind AS 33.28).
  • Rights issues at below fair value contain a bonus element: adjust prior-period shares by the factor fair value per share immediately before exercise ÷ theoretical ex-rights fair value per share (Ind AS 33.27(b), A2).
  • Shares bought back are excluded from the date of buyback.

In an annual model without dates, use a mid-year convention: \(\bar{N}_t \approx (N^{\text{open}}_t + N^{\text{close}}_t) \div 2\), unless the event date is known.

Diluted shares (Ind AS 33)

Potential ordinary shares are dilutive when their conversion decreases EPS from continuing operations (Ind AS 33.41).

Options and warrants — treasury stock method (Ind AS 33.45–46): assume exercise; proceeds are deemed used to buy shares at the average market price for the period; the difference is added:

\[ \Delta N^{\text{options}} = Q \times \left(1 - \frac{K}{\bar{P}}\right) \quad \text{for } K < \bar{P} \]

where \(Q\) is the number of options, \(K\) the exercise price (including, for unvested ESOPs, the fair value of future services yet to be recognised, Ind AS 33.47A) and \(\bar{P}\) the average market price. Out-of-the-money options (\(K \geq \bar{P}\)) are excluded.

Convertible instruments — if-converted method (Ind AS 33.49–50): add the shares on conversion and add back the after-tax interest (or preference dividend) to the numerator; include only if dilutive.

Contingently issuable shares: included when conditions are satisfied (Ind AS 33.52).

Illustration

FY28E: opening 60.00 crore shares; ESOP allotment 0.40 crore on 1 July 2027; QIP 3.00 crore on 1 December 2027; buyback 1.20 crore on 1 February 2028. Year has 366 days (FY28 includes 29 February 2028).

  • Days remaining: ESOP 275, QIP 122, buyback 60.
  • \(\bar{N} = 60.00 + 0.40 \times 275/366 + 3.00 \times 122/366 - 1.20 \times 60/366 = 60.00 + 0.3005 + 1.0000 - 0.1967 = 61.104\) crore.
  • Closing shares = 62.20 crore.
  • Options outstanding 1.50 crore at ₹300; average price ₹750 → TSM shares = 1.50 × (1 − 300/750) = 0.90 crore → weighted diluted ≈ 62.00 crore (ignoring the time-weighting of options granted in the year).

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Treasury shares held by ESOP trust: excluded from basic shares until transferred to employees.
  • Partly paid shares: treated as a fraction of a share to the extent entitled to dividends (Ind AS 33.A15).
  • Losses: potential shares are anti-dilutive when there is a loss from continuing operations; diluted EPS equals basic EPS.
  • Average vs closing price: Ind AS 33 uses average price for the period; valuation uses current price — so diluted EPS shares and valuation fully diluted shares differ.
  • Model-derived share price: using the model's own DCF value as the price for TSM creates a circularity; use the current market price or a fixed input.
  • Bonus after year-end but before approval of financial statements: per-share data is restated (Ind AS 33.64) — relevant when forecasting with a pending bonus announcement.

Key takeaways

  • Keep separate rows for closing basic, weighted-average basic and weighted-average diluted shares.
  • Weight issues and buybacks by days outstanding; treat bonuses and splits as if from the start of the earliest period.
  • Rights issues below fair value carry a bonus element requiring retrospective adjustment.
  • Use the treasury stock method at average market price for options; if-converted for convertibles; include only if dilutive.
  • Diluted EPS must never exceed basic EPS — add a check row.

Practice Questions

Q1. Weighted-average shares [L1]

Opening shares 45 crore. A preferential allotment of 2 crore shares on 1 November 2026 and a buyback of 0.9 crore shares on 1 March 2027. FY27 has 365 days. Compute weighted-average shares and closing shares.

Q2. Bonus and rights adjustment [L2]

FY27: opening shares 20 crore; 1:1 bonus on 1 August 2026; rights issue of 1 for 4 (on post-bonus shares) at ₹200 on 1 January 2027, when the cum-rights price was ₹300. FY26 EPS was reported as ₹24 on 20 crore shares. Compute FY27 weighted-average shares and the restated FY26 EPS.

💡 Hint: Compute the theoretical ex-rights price first and derive the adjustment factor.

Q3. Diluted shares with options and a convertible [L2]

Weighted basic shares 80 crore; owners' PAT ₹1,200 crore. Options outstanding 2 crore at ₹420 (average price ₹600). Compulsorily convertible debentures convertible into 4 crore shares, interest ₹36 crore a year, tax 25.168%. Compute diluted EPS, testing each instrument for dilution in the proper sequence.

Q4. Schedule design [L3]

Design a share-count schedule for a new-age tech company with quarterly ESOP allotments, an ESOP trust holding treasury shares, and a pending QIP. Specify rows, date logic, the formulas you would use for weighting, and how you would feed the valuation fully diluted count in Week 16.
