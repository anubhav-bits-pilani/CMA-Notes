Key concept

Current Tax After Loss Set-Off at 25.17%

Concept: Current tax = MAX(0, Taxable income − Loss set-off) × 25.17%

Statement

\[ \text{Current tax}_t = \max\left(0,\; TI_t - L_t\right) \times 25.168\% \]

\[ L_t = \min\left(\text{Loss pool}_{t}^{open} - \text{Expired}_t,\; \max(0, TI_t)\right) \]

where \(TI_t\) is taxable income before set-off (PBT adjusted for tax depreciation, 43B items, provisions and permanent differences — s03), \(L_t\) the brought-forward business loss and unabsorbed depreciation set off in year \(t\), and 25.168% the all-in Sec 115BAA rate (22% × 1.10 × 1.04), commonly shown as 25.17%.

Explanation

The MAX(0, …) captures the asymmetry of tax: profits are taxed, losses are not refunded — they are carried forward (s06). The MIN cap on \(L_t\) ensures losses are used only up to current positive income and never create a negative taxable income; unused losses roll to the next year's pool. When \(TI_t < 0\), current tax is zero and \(-TI_t\) is added to the pool (as business loss, except the depreciation component, which becomes unabsorbed depreciation).

Conditions and refinements:

  • Apply the rate only to business income at normal rates; capital gains and other special-rate income get their own rates.
  • For an old-regime company, current tax = MAX(normal tax, MAT) less MAT credit utilised; no MAT applies under 115BAA (Sec 115JB(5A)).
  • Surcharge in 115BAA is flat 10% regardless of income, so the rate is linear — no slab logic needed.
  • "Current tax" in Ind AS 12 also includes prior-year adjustments; a forecast usually sets these to zero.
  • Use 25.168% unrounded in the input cell; 25.17% is a display convention.

Illustration

Loss_Used   = MIN(Loss_Open - Loss_Expired, MAX(0, TI))
Current_Tax = MAX(0, TI - Loss_Used) * Tax_Rate
Loss_Close  = Loss_Open - Loss_Expired - Loss_Used + MAX(0, -TI)

Connections

Takes taxable income from the bridge (s03) and loss utilisation from the loss memo (s06); feeds the P&L current tax line, the cash tax computation (c05, s07) and the ETR reconciliation (c04). The 25.168% rate (s02) is the same marginal rate used in NOPAT and after-tax cost of debt in Week 16. If interest is modelled on average balances, current tax is inside the circularity loop of w14_t4.

A start-up expects losses for three more years and then profits. What tax rate should appear in (a) the P&L forecast, (b) the NOPAT for terminal value and (c) the after-tax cost of debt in WACC? Justify each.
