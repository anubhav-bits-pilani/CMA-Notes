Key concept

From EBIT to PBT: Other Income, Finance Costs, Exceptionals

Concept: PBT = EBIT + Other income − Finance costs ± Exceptional items (± share of associates per presentation)

Statement

\[ \text{PBT} = \text{EBIT} + \text{OI} - \text{FC} \pm X \;(\pm A) \]

where \( \text{EBIT} \) is operating profit after D&A (c01), \( \text{OI} \) is other income (treasury interest, dividends, MF gains, non-operating income), \( \text{FC} \) is finance costs (debt interest + lease interest + other borrowing costs − capitalised borrowing costs), \( X \) is exceptional items (income positive, expense negative; zero in forecast), and \( A \) is share of profit/(loss) of associates and JVs, included in PBT only if the company presents it above tax.

Explanation

The Schedule III Division II face runs: Revenue from operations + Other income = Total income; less Total expenses (including D&A and finance costs) = Profit before exceptional items and tax; ± Exceptional items = Profit before tax; less Tax = Profit for the period from continuing operations. The model re-orders the same lines analytically — EBITDA, EBIT, then the below-EBIT items — but must reconcile to the statutory "Profit before tax" in every historical year. A reconciliation row (=Model_PBT − Reported_PBT, which must be zero in history) is a cheap integrity check.

Why each sign:

  • Other income is added after EBIT because it is non-operating (s02).
  • Finance costs are subtracted net of capitalisation (s04).
  • Exceptional items carry their own sign; in forecast they are zero via an explicit input row (s05).
  • Associate share is already post-tax. Companies present it in different places: some before tax (as a line between exceptional items and PBT), many after tax just before "Profit for the year". The model should follow the company's presentation for comparability but compute tax on PBT excluding \( A \).

Key conditions: the circular items (interest income on average cash, interest expense on average debt) are part of this bridge, so PBT is the point where circularity enters earnings — the circuit breaker must be able to zero them.

Illustration

FY27E (₹ crore): EBIT 1,160; other income 256.5; finance costs 240.5; exceptional items 0; share of JV profit 60, presented after tax.

\[ \text{PBT} = 1{,}160 + 256.5 - 240.5 + 0 = 1{,}176 \]

Tax is computed on 1,176 (plus/minus tax-schedule adjustments). The JV share of 60 is added after tax to reach profit for the year. If instead the company presents the share before tax, the face shows PBT = 1,236, but the tax schedule's starting point must still be 1,176. A model that taxes 1,236 at 25.168% overstates tax by about 15.1.

Connections

Builds on c01 (EBIT) and the subtopics s02 (other income), s04 (finance costs) and s05 (exceptional items, associates). Feeds c03 (PBT to PAT) and c04 (why tax is on PBT). In the CFS, the Indian-format reconciliation starts from this PBT and reverses other income and finance costs (t2). Interest coverage (EBIT ÷ FC) and the Week 14 circularity loop both live on this bridge.

Practice Questions

Q1. Rebuild PBT and reconcile [L1]

FY26A reported figures (₹ crore): revenue 5,400; other income 140; total expenses 4,690 including finance costs 110 and D&A 230; exceptional items (60); profit before tax 790. Rebuild EBITDA, EBIT and PBT analytically and confirm the reconciliation to reported PBT.

Q2. Associate share presentation and tax [L2]

A company presents share of associates (₹75 crore) above tax. EBIT ₹2,100 crore, other income ₹180 crore, finance costs ₹320 crore, exceptional items nil, permanent disallowances ₹20 crore, tax rate 25.168%, no temporary differences. Compute PBT as presented, the correct tax expense, PAT and the ETR on reported PBT versus on PBT ex-associates.

💡 Hint: Remove the associate share from the tax base before applying the rate.

Q3. Circuit breaker effect on PBT [L2]

With the circuit breaker on, interest income and debt interest are set to zero; with it off, FY28E interest income is ₹64 crore and debt interest ₹210 crore (lease interest ₹35 crore is non-circular and always computed). EBIT is ₹1,450 crore. Compute PBT in both states and explain why the BS should balance in both states if the model is correctly linked.
