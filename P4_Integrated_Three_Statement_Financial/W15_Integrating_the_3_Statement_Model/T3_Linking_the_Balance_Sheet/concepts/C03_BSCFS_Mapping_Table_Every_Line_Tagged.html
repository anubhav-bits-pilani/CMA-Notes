Key concept

BS–CFS Mapping Table: Every Line Tagged

Concept: BS–CFS mapping table: each BS line tagged to its schedule or to "held constant"

Statement

A mapping table lists every forecast BS line \( i \) and, for each year, verifies:

\[ \text{Diff}_{i,t} = s_i \times \Delta B_{i,t} - \sum_{k \in K_i} \text{CFS}_{k,t} - \sum_{j \in J_i} \text{NC}_{j,t} = 0 \]

where \( \Delta B_{i,t} \) is the movement in BS line \( i \), \( s_i = -1 \) for assets and \( +1 \) for liabilities and equity, \( K_i \) is the set of CFS lines mapped to line \( i \), and \( J_i \) is the set of non-cash movements recorded against line \( i \) that have an offsetting BS entry elsewhere (new leases, SBP-to-premium transfer, OCI). Each line also carries a source tag: the schedule that drives it, or "held constant".

Explanation

The table is the model's double-entry ledger in summary. It answers two questions for every line: where does the closing balance come from? and where is its movement in the CFS? It is the practical form of the sign rule (t2/c06).

Typical columns:

BS line Source tag Opening Closing Δ CFS lines mapped Sum of CFS lines Non-cash offsets Difference
Trade receivables WC schedule 1,020 1,105 +85 CFO: Δreceivables −85 — 0
PPE + CWIP PPE/CWIP schedules 4,200 5,019 +819 CFI capex −1,020; CFF cap. interest −54; CFO dep +255 −819 — 0
ROU assets Lease schedule 400 418 +18 CFO ROU dep +57 +57 New leases −75 0
Lease liabilities Lease schedule 420 437.8 +17.8 CFF lease payments −95; CFO finance-cost add-back (lease interest) +37.8 −57.2 New leases +75 0
Goodwill Held constant 850 850 0 none 0 — 0

(For ROU and lease liabilities the new-lease offsets cancel across the two lines; the table proves it.)

Rules:

  • Every BS line appears, including those held constant.
  • Every CFS line is mapped at least once; an unmapped CFS line is a red flag (a flow with no BS counterpart).
  • PAT is split between retained earnings (owners) and NCI; group-level CFO starts from total PAT.
  • The sum of all difference cells equals the BS check (up to sign) — the table localises the imbalance to a line.

The table is built once and stays in the model (a Map or Checks sheet). It is also the reviewer's first stop and the backbone of the t4 line-by-line ΔBS vs CFS reconciliation block.

Illustration

A model shows a check difference of −36 in FY28E. In the mapping table, every line shows zero difference except "Share options outstanding (SBP reserve)": Δ = +36, CFS lines mapped = none (the add-back row was deleted when the CFS was reformatted), difference = +36. Restoring the CFO add-back clears both the table and the check.

Connections

Operationalises t2/c06 (sign rule) and supports s01–s06 of this topic: NWC and asset lines (s02), financing liabilities (s03), tax (s04), equity (s05) and held-flat lines (s06). Directly addresses the pitfall of holding a BS line constant while its CFS line moves. In t4 it becomes the line-by-line reconciliation block; in Project 4 it is a required deliverable alongside the scenario check table.

Practice Questions

Q1. Tag the lines [L1]

For a Project 4 BS with these lines — cash, other bank balances, receivables, inventories, PPE, CWIP, ROU, goodwill, associate investment, DTA, borrowings (current and non-current), lease liabilities, trade payables, provisions (long-term), current tax liability, share capital, other equity, NCI — assign each a source tag and list its mapped CFS line(s).

Q2. Localise an imbalance [L2]

A model's check is +40 in FY29E. Build the mapping rows for receivables (Δ +110, CFS −110), payables (Δ +65, CFS +65), DTL (Δ +18, CFS +18), dividends payable (Δ +40, CFS none) and borrowings (Δ −200, CFS −200). Identify the line responsible and explain the correct treatment.

💡 Hint: Dividends payable arises when declaration and payment fall in different years.

Q3. Design for maintainability [L3]

Explain how you would build the mapping table so that it updates automatically when rows are inserted in the BS or CFS (consider named ranges, INDEX/MATCH on labels, or a key column), and how you would surface any non-zero difference on the Cover sheet.
