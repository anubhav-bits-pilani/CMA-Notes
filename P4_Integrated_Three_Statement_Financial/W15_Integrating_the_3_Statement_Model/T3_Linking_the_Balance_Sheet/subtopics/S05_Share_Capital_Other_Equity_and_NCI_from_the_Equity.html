Subtopic note

Share Capital, Other Equity and NCI from the Equity Corkscrew

Scope: Share capital, other equity and NCI from equity corkscrew

Why it matters

Equity is where the P&L lands on the balance sheet. Every rupee of PAT attributable to owners enters retained earnings; every rupee of NCI share enters the NCI line; dividends, buybacks, ESOP charges and allotments, OCI and reserve transfers move the components of "other equity". A complete equity corkscrew is therefore the second half of the "P&L–BS" link (the first being cash via the CFS). Under Schedule III Division II, the BS shows only "Equity share capital", "Other equity" and, in consolidated statements, "Non-controlling interests"; the detail lives in the Statement of Changes in Equity (SOCIE). Modelling the SOCIE components — not just a single "reserves" number — lets the model handle ESOPs, buybacks with capital redemption reserve, OCI items and NCI dividends without plugs.

Core notes

Schedule III Division II structure

BS line Components (SOCIE) Forecast drivers
Equity share capital Face value × shares outstanding Share-count schedule: ESOP allotments, QIP/rights, buybacks, bonus, splits
Other equity Securities premium; retained earnings; general reserve; capital redemption reserve; share options outstanding (SBP reserve); OCI reserves (FVOCI equity, DB remeasurement, cash-flow hedge, FCTR) PAT to owners, dividends, SBP charge, allotments, buybacks, OCI
Non-controlling interests NCI share of net assets of subsidiaries NCI share of PAT and OCI, dividends to NCI, changes in ownership

The corkscrews

Share capital and securities premium:

\[ \text{SC}_t = \text{SC}_{t-1} + \text{FV} \times (\text{Shares issued}_t - \text{Shares bought back}_t) \]

\[ \text{SP}_t = \text{SP}_{t-1} + \text{Premium on issue}_t + \text{SBP transfer on exercise}_t - \text{Issue costs}_t - \text{Buyback premium utilised}_t \]

Retained earnings:

\[ \text{RE}_t = \text{RE}_{t-1} + \text{PAT}^{\text{owners}}_t - \text{Dividends declared}_t - \text{Transfers to reserves (CRR, general)}_t \pm \text{OCI reclassified}_t \]

DB remeasurements recognised in OCI are often transferred directly to retained earnings in Indian SOCIE presentation; follow the company's format.

SBP reserve:

\[ \text{SBP}_t = \text{SBP}_{t-1} + \text{ESOP expense}_t - \text{Transfer to premium on exercise}_t - \text{Lapses transferred to RE}_t \]

NCI:

\[ \text{NCI}_t = \text{NCI}_{t-1} + \text{NCI share of PAT}_t + \text{NCI share of OCI}_t - \text{Dividends to NCI}_t \pm \text{Ownership changes}_t \]

The rule: every cash movement in equity has a CFF line; every non-cash movement has an equal and opposite movement elsewhere on the BS (another equity component, a provision, an investment, a DTL) — never a CFS line.

Worked illustration (₹ crore, FY27E)

Opening: share capital 96.0 (48 crore shares of ₹2), securities premium 1,200, retained earnings 4,040, SBP reserve 60, OCI reserves (15), NCI 420. FY27E: PAT 940.1 (owners 871.5, NCI 68.6); final FY26 dividend declared and paid 260; dividends to NCI 25; ESOP expense 22; ESOP exercise 0.3 crore shares at ₹60 (cash 18), with SBP transfer of 9; actuarial loss net of tax (7.5) to OCI.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Dividend declared vs paid: if declaration and payment fall in different years, a dividend payable line (other current financial liability) is needed; otherwise RE and cash diverge.
  • Buyback: reduces share capital (FV) and securities premium/free reserves (premium); CRR created out of free reserves for the FV cancelled (Sec 69); post-1 October 2024 regime shifts buyback taxation to shareholders.
  • Bonus issue/split: capitalises reserves into share capital (bonus) or changes FV (split) — total equity unchanged; restate share counts for EPS.
  • NCI deficit: losses continue to be attributed to NCI even if it becomes negative (Ind AS 110.B94).
  • Changes in ownership without loss of control (buying out minorities) are equity transactions (Ind AS 110.23): cash in CFF, difference between price and NCI carrying amount in other equity.
  • Treasury shares held by ESOP trusts are deducted from equity; trust purchases are CFF outflows.

Key takeaways

  • Model equity at SOCIE-component level; the BS shows share capital, other equity (sum of components) and NCI.
  • Retained earnings take PAT to owners and dividends declared; NCI takes NCI share of PAT/OCI and dividends to NCI.
  • Every cash equity movement has a CFF line; non-cash movements (SBP charge, transfers, CRR, OCI) offset within the BS.
  • ESOP expense is a CFO add-back; exercise cash is CFF; the reserve transfer is non-cash.
  • Group CFO starts from total PAT, so NCI share of profit is already in cash; only NCI dividends leave in CFF.

Practice Questions

Q1. Build an equity corkscrew [L1]

Opening FY28E: share capital ₹100 crore (50 crore shares, FV ₹2), securities premium ₹1,500 crore, retained earnings ₹5,200 crore. FY28E: PAT to owners ₹1,050 crore; dividends declared and paid ₹330 crore; no other movements. Build the closing balances and list the CFS lines.

Q2. Buyback mechanics [L2]

On 1 December 2027 the company buys back 1.2 crore shares (FV ₹2) at ₹1,250 per share, utilising securities premium first and free reserves (retained earnings) for any excess; CRR is created for the FV cancelled out of free reserves. Opening securities premium ₹1,226 crore. Show the entries in each equity component and the CFF line.

💡 Hint: Separate the FV cancelled, the premium utilised and the CRR transfer.

Q3. NCI dividends omitted [L2]

A model's NCI corkscrew deducts dividends to NCI of ₹25 crore, ₹28 crore and ₹31 crore for FY27E–FY29E, but the CFS has no corresponding line. Predict the check-row differences (amount, sign and pattern) and explain why.

Q4. ESOP exercise and trust structure [L3]

A company's ESOP trust borrows ₹80 crore from the company to buy 0.5 crore shares in the secondary market, and later transfers them to employees on exercise at ₹100 per share. Explain the consolidated equity, CFS and BS treatment (treasury shares, loan to trust eliminated, exercise proceeds), and how you would model it in the equity corkscrew.
