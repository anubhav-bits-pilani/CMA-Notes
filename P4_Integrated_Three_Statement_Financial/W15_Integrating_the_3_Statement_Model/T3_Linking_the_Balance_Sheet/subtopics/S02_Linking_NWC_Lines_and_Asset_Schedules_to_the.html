Subtopic note

Linking NWC Lines and Asset Schedules to the Balance Sheet

Scope: NWC lines from WC schedule; PPE, CWIP, intangibles, ROU from asset schedules

Why it matters

Most of a manufacturing or consumer company's balance sheet is operating assets: trade receivables, inventories, advances, PPE, CWIP, intangibles and right-of-use assets; and operating liabilities: trade payables, other current liabilities, provisions. In the forecast, every one of these is the closing balance of a Week 14 schedule — the WC schedule driven by days and percentages, and the asset corkscrews driven by capex, depreciation and lease additions. Linking them correctly is what ties the BS to the CFS: the change in each linked balance must appear exactly once in the CFS (ΔNWC in CFO; capex in CFI; D&A in CFO; new leases as non-cash). A link to the wrong row (gross block instead of net block), the wrong column (prior year), or the wrong schedule (CWIP missed from capex) is a top-five source of imbalances.

Schedule III requires trade payables split between dues to micro and small enterprises and others, and ageing schedules for receivables and payables; the model need not forecast the split but must keep the total consistent.

NWC linking

The WC schedule (Week 14) produces closing balances by line; the BS links each one:

BS!F16  =WC!F20      ' Trade receivables (net)
BS!F15  =WC!F21      ' Inventories
BS!F18  =WC!F22      ' Other current assets (operating)
BS!F45  =WC!F30      ' Trade payables
BS!F47  =WC!F31      ' Other current liabilities (operating)
BS!F48  =WC!F32      ' Short-term provisions (operating)

The CFS ΔNWC lines must reference the same rows (=-(WC!F20-WC!E20) etc.). Best practice: the CFS references the BS lines rather than the schedule, so a BS link error shows up in both statements identically and the CFS still matches the BS movement. Either is fine as long as one source is used consistently.

Asset schedules

PPE. Link the net block (closing gross block − closing accumulated depreciation), or show gross and accumulated depreciation separately if the presentation requires — never gross alone. The corkscrew:

\[ \text{NB}_t = \text{NB}_{t-1} + \text{Transfers from CWIP}_t + \text{Direct additions}_t - \text{Dep}_t - \text{NBV of disposals}_t \]

CWIP. Closing CWIP links separately. Capex spend in CFI = spend into CWIP + direct additions (t2/s03). Transfers from CWIP to PPE are internal and have no CFS line.

Intangibles. Closing net from the intangibles corkscrew; amortisation in D&A; additions in CFI.

ROU assets.

\[ \text{ROU}_t = \text{ROU}_{t-1} + \text{New leases}_t - \text{ROU dep}_t \pm \text{Remeasurements}_t \]

New leases are non-cash; they increase ROU and the lease liability equally (s03). ROU depreciation is in D&A (CFO add-back).

Worked check (₹ crore, FY27E)

Running example: opening net PPE 3,600; opening CWIP 600; spend into CWIP 900; capitalised borrowing cost 54 (paid within interest in CFF); transfers to PPE 1,000; direct additions 120; PPE depreciation 255; no disposals.

Line Opening Movements Closing Δ
Net PPE 3,600 +1,000 transfers + 120 direct − 255 depreciation 4,465 +865
CWIP 600 +900 spend + 54 cap. interest − 1,000 transfers 554 −46
PPE + CWIP 4,200 5,019 +819

CFS side: capex in CFI (900 + 120) = −1,020; capitalised interest inside interest paid (CFF) = −54; depreciation add-back in CFO = +255. Net CFS effect = −819, equal and opposite to the combined asset increase of +819. ✓ Transfers of 1,000 appear in both corkscrews and cancel — they never touch the CFS.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • ROU presentation: some Indian companies include ROU within PPE, others show it separately; follow the company but keep the lease schedule separate for D&A and CFS mapping.
  • Capex creditors and capital advances must not be inside operating NWC; if modelled, they adjust cash capex.
  • Inventory and receivables net of provisions: link the net balance; ECL and write-downs flow through ΔNWC automatically.
  • Non-current portions of operating items (long-term trade receivables, retention money in EPC companies) can be driven by the WC schedule or held flat; whichever, the CFS must match.
  • Acquisitions: acquired NWC and PPE enter via the acquisition block, not ΔNWC or capex.
  • Linking to gross block rather than net block produces an imbalance equal to cumulative depreciation — a growing difference.

Key takeaways

  • Every operating BS line is the closing balance of a WC or asset schedule in the same column.
  • CFS ΔNWC lines reference the same rows (or the BS lines) so the movement is captured once.
  • Link net PPE (or gross and accumulated separately), CWIP, intangibles and ROU independently; CWIP must be in the capex reconciliation.
  • ROU additions are non-cash; ROU depreciation is in D&A.
  • Prove each asset family with a mini-reconciliation: Δ balance = cash flows ± non-cash items.

Practice Questions

Q1. Link and reconcile NWC [L1]

WC schedule closing balances FY28E (₹ crore): receivables 1,210 (opening 1,100); inventories 980 (opening 1,020); other CA 140 (opening 130); payables 890 (opening 840); other CL 260 (opening 250). Write the BS links (assume schedule rows 20–24 and 30–31) and compute each CFO working-capital line and ΔNWC.

Q2. PPE + CWIP reconciliation [L2]

FY28E: opening net PPE ₹4,465 crore, opening CWIP ₹554 crore; spend into CWIP ₹650 crore; transfers to PPE ₹900 crore; direct additions ₹70 crore; depreciation ₹310 crore; disposal of assets with NBV ₹12 crore for proceeds ₹20 crore. Compute closing net PPE and CWIP, and reconcile the combined change to the CFS lines.

💡 Hint: The gain on disposal sits in PAT; remove it in CFO and show proceeds in CFI.

Q3. Gross block link error [L2]

A model links BS PPE to closing gross block. Depreciation is ₹255, 290, 320, 350 and 380 crore for FY27E–FY31E. Predict the check-row pattern, and explain why historical years still balance.

Q4. Retention money in an EPC company [L3]

An EPC contractor's receivables include retention money (released 12–24 months after project completion) of 9% of revenue, presented as non-current trade receivables, plus contract assets under Ind AS 115. Design the WC schedule rows and BS links for these items, and explain how a sharp revenue increase in the Bull case affects CFO through each line.
