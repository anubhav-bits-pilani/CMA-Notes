Key concept

Cash Roll-Forward: Closing = Opening + CFO + CFI + CFF

Concept: Closing cash = Opening cash + CFO + CFI + CFF

Statement

\[ C_t = C_{t-1} + \text{CFO}_t + \text{CFI}_t + \text{CFF}_t + \text{FX}_t \]

where \( C_t \) is closing cash and cash equivalents per Ind AS 7 (cash on hand, demand deposits, and investments with original maturity of three months or less, net of bank overdrafts only if they form part of cash management), \( C_{t-1} \) is the opening balance (for the first forecast year, the last historical BS cash and cash equivalents), and \( \text{FX}_t \) is the effect of exchange-rate changes on foreign-currency cash (Ind AS 7.28), zero in forecasts.

Explanation

This is the cash corkscrew, and in an integrated model it is the only way the BS cash line is determined (t3/c02). Because every CFO, CFI and CFF line maps to a BS movement or a non-cash P&L item, the identity is equivalent to the balance sheet equation in changes:

\[ \Delta C_t = \Delta(\text{Liabilities} + \text{Equity})_t - \Delta(\text{Non-cash assets})_t \]

If the CFS captures every movement correctly, linking \( C_t \) to the BS makes assets equal liabilities plus equity. If any movement is missing or mis-signed, the check row shows the difference — which is why the identity is both the build mechanism and the diagnostic.

Conditions:

  • The opening link in year 1 must be BS cash and CE, matching the definition used in the forecast (overdraft treatment, other bank balances excluded).
  • Later years link to the prior forecast column's closing cash, never to an input.
  • Other bank balances (FDs of 3–12 months, earmarked accounts) are not part of \( C \); their movement is in CFI (Ind AS 7.7, Schedule III).
  • Ind AS 7.45 requires the components of cash and cash equivalents to be disclosed and reconciled to the BS.

Illustration

FY27E (₹ crore): opening cash and CE 1,200; CFO 1,183.0; CFI (748.5); CFF (822.7).

\[ C_{\text{FY27E}} = 1{,}200 + 1{,}183.0 - 748.5 - 822.7 = 811.8 \]

FY28E opening must be 811.8 (=CFS!F47), not 1,200 and not a typed number. If the FY28E net change is +260, closing is 1,071.8.

Connections

Implements s05; uses c02–c04 as components. Feeds t3/s01 (cash as sole balancing mechanism) and t3/c01 (balance check). The cash check (CFS closing − BS cash) is the first test in t4 debugging. Interest income on average cash (t1/s02) closes the circular loop through this identity.

Practice Questions

Q1. Five-year roll-forward [L1]

Opening cash FY27E ₹300 crore. Net change in cash FY27E–FY31E: +120, −80, +210, −340, +190 (₹ crore). Build the cash corkscrew and identify any year in which minimum cash of ₹100 crore is breached.

Q2. Broken opening link [L2]

In a model, FY29E opening cash is hardcoded at ₹650 crore while FY28E closing cash from the CFS is ₹612 crore. All other links are correct. Describe the check-row pattern across FY29E–FY31E and explain why.

💡 Hint: An opening-balance error persists as a constant difference.

Q3. Overdraft definition [L2]

A company's historical CFS defines cash and CE net of a ₹45 crore overdraft; its BS shows the overdraft in borrowings. Your forecast BS cash line is gross. State the consistent definition for the forecast cash corkscrew and the reconciliation disclosure you would add.
