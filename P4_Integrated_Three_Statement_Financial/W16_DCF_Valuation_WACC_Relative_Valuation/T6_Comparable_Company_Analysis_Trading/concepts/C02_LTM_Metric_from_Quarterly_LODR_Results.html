Key concept

LTM Metric from Quarterly LODR Results

Concept: LTM = Last FY + Current YTD − Prior-year YTD (SEBI LODR quarterly results)

Statement

\[ \text{LTM}_{q} = FY_{\text{last}} + YTD_{q,\,\text{current}} - YTD_{q,\,\text{prior}} \]

where:

  • \(FY_{\text{last}}\) is the metric for the most recent completed fiscal year (audited)
  • \(YTD_{q,\,\text{current}}\) is the cumulative metric from the start of the current fiscal year to the end of the latest reported quarter \(q\)
  • \(YTD_{q,\,\text{prior}}\) is the same cumulative period one year earlier, on a restated basis if comparatives were restated

Under SEBI LODR Regulation 33, listed entities publish quarterly results within 45 days of each of the first three quarter-ends, and annual audited results within 60 days of year-end. The YTD figures (for example "six months ended" or "nine months ended") are presented in the same filing.

Explanation

LTM gives the most recent full year of performance, so a multiple reflects up-to-date earnings without relying on forecasts. For an Indian March-FY company:

Latest results LTM period Formula
Q1 FY27 (June 2026) Jul-25 to Jun-26 FY26 + Q1 FY27 − Q1 FY26
Q2 FY27 (Sept 2026) Oct-25 to Sep-26 FY26 + H1 FY27 − H1 FY26
Q3 FY27 (Dec 2026) Jan-26 to Dec-26 FY26 + 9M FY27 − 9M FY26
Q4 FY27 (Mar 2027) FY27 FY27 (LTM = latest FY)

Conditions and adjustments:

  • Same basis in all three terms. Use consolidated figures, the same accounting policies, and exceptional items stripped consistently (s05).
  • Restated comparatives. If the prior-year YTD was restated (for a merger under common control, or a discontinued operation), use the restated figures.
  • Acquisitions and disposals. Pro-forma the LTM if the perimeter changed and EV reflects the new perimeter.
  • Balance-sheet items are not LTM'd. Use the point-in-time balance.
  • Per-share metrics. Build LTM EPS from LTM PAT attributable to owners ÷ current diluted shares, not by adding quarterly EPS figures, which have different share counts.

Where to get the YTD. The quarterly results format under the SEBI circulars shows the quarter, the corresponding prior-year quarter, the year-to-date figures and the previous year. Half-yearly filings also include the balance sheet and cash-flow statement.

Illustration

Peer Z (illustrative), consolidated EBITDA excluding other income and exceptional items, ₹ crore. Comps date 14 November 2026, after the Q2 FY27 results.

Item Amount
FY26 (audited) 640
H1 FY27 352
H1 FY26 (restated for a discontinued unit: originally 315, restated 300) 300
LTM Sep-26 640 + 352 − 300 = 692

Using the unrestated H1 FY26 (315) would give 677. That understates LTM, because FY26 is reported on the continuing basis while the prior YTD would include the discontinued unit. The three terms must share a perimeter.

Practice Questions

Q1. LTM at Q3 [L1]

A company reports FY26 revenue of ₹ 4,820 crore, 9M FY27 revenue of ₹ 3,960 crore and 9M FY26 revenue of ₹ 3,540 crore. 9M FY27 includes a ₹ 45 crore one-time export incentive true-up within other operating revenue. Compute the reported and normalised LTM revenue to December 2026.

Q2. Rolling four-quarter alternative [L2]

Write an Excel formula, or describe a Power Query step, that computes LTM EBITDA as a rolling sum of the last four quarters from a table of quarterly results (columns: Company, Quarter_End, EBITDA). Explain when the rolling sum and the YTD formula would give different answers.

💡 Hint: Consider restatements and Q4 figures derived as FY minus 9M.

Q3. LTM EPS construction [L2]

Quarterly diluted EPS figures for the last four quarters are ₹ 6.2, ₹ 5.8, ₹ 7.1 and ₹ 3.9 (the last after a 1:1 bonus). LTM PAT attributable to owners is ₹ 1,160 crore, and current diluted shares are 92.0 crore. Explain why summing the quarterly EPS is wrong, and compute the correct LTM EPS.
