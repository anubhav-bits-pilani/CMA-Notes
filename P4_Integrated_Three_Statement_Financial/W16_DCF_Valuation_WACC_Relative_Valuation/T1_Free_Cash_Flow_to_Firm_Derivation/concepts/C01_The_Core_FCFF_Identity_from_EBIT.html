Key concept

The Core FCFF Identity from EBIT

Concept: FCFF = EBIT × (1 − t) + D&A − Capex − ΔNWC

Statement

\[ \text{FCFF}_t = \text{EBIT}_t \times (1 - t) + \text{D\&A}_t - \text{Capex}_t - \Delta\text{NWC}_t \]

where:

  • \( \text{EBIT}_t \) = operating earnings before interest and tax in year \( t \), excluding other (non-operating) income and exceptional items;
  • \( t \) = marginal tax rate on operating income (25.17% for a domestic company under Sec 115BAA);
  • \( \text{D\&A}_t \) = depreciation and amortisation charged in arriving at EBIT (including ROU depreciation if the model is post-Ind AS 116);
  • \( \text{Capex}_t \) = cash spend on PPE, CWIP and intangibles (plus ROU additions in a post-116 model);
  • \( \Delta\text{NWC}_t = \text{NWC}_t - \text{NWC}_{t-1} \), operating working capital only.

Explanation

The identity converts operating profit into the cash that the operating business throws off for all capital providers. \( \text{EBIT}(1 - t) \) is NOPAT: tax is computed on EBIT as if the firm were unlevered, so the interest tax shield is not in the cash flow — it lives in the after-tax cost of debt inside WACC. D&A is added back because it was deducted to reach EBIT but is non-cash. Capex and ΔNWC are the reinvestments needed to sustain and grow NOPAT.

Grouping the last three terms gives net reinvestment:

\[ \text{FCFF} = \text{NOPAT} - (\text{Capex} - \text{D\&A} + \Delta\text{NWC}) = \text{NOPAT} \times (1 - \text{Reinvestment rate}) \]

This is the bridge to c05 and to the value-driver terminal formula in t3.

Conditions for correct use:

  • Operating only. Other income from treasury assets is excluded; those assets are added in the EV bridge.
  • Marginal, not historical, tax. Historical ETR includes exempt income, deferred tax remeasurement and prior-year items.
  • Consistent lease basis. Post-116 requires ROU additions in capex; pre-116 requires rent deducted in EBIT.
  • Sign conventions. An increase in NWC is a deduction; a decrease (release) adds to FCFF.

The formula sometimes starts from EBITDA: \( \text{FCFF} = \text{EBITDA} - \text{Taxes on EBIT} - \text{Capex} - \Delta\text{NWC} \), where taxes on EBIT = \( t \times \text{EBIT} \). The two are algebraically identical — useful as a cross-check when D&A is large.

Illustration

A Gujarat-based specialty chemicals company, FY27E (₹ crore):

Line Amount
Revenue from operations 3,600
EBITDA 756
D&A 144
EBIT (excl. other income ₹48) 612
Tax at 25.17% (154.0)
NOPAT 458.0
Add: D&A 144
Less: Capex (maintenance 160 + growth 240) (400)
Less: Increase in NWC (66)
FCFF 136.0

EBITDA route: \( 756 - 154.0 - 400 - 66 = 136.0 \). Net reinvestment = \( 400 - 144 + 66 = 322 \); reinvestment rate = \( 322 / 458 = 70.3\% \), typical of a company in a capacity-expansion phase.

Connections

  • t1/s01 (NOPAT and the 115BAA rate) and t1/s02 (D&A, capex split, ΔNWC from schedules) supply each term.
  • t1/s03 adjusts capex and EBIT for Ind AS 116; c06 states the post-116 variant.
  • c02 cross-checks the same number from PAT; c03 extends it to FCFE.
  • t3 (terminal value) uses terminal-year FCFF from this identity, normalised.
  • Weeks 14–15 schedules (PPE corkscrew, WC schedule, CFS) are the source of every line.
  • FP&A practice: the same identity underlies "free cash flow" KPIs in board packs, though corporate definitions often deduct actual interest and lease payments — always reconcile definitions.

Practice Questions

Q1. Compute FCFF two ways [L1]

FY26E (₹ crore): EBITDA 2,100; D&A 380; other income included in reported EBIT 95; tax 25.17%; capex 640; NWC 31-Mar-25 1,240 and 31-Mar-26 1,318. Compute FCFF via the EBIT route and the EBITDA route and show they agree.

Q2. Diagnose a model [L2]

A DCF sheet computes FCFF as =EBITDA-Tax_Expense_IS-Capex-dNWC, where Tax_Expense_IS is the P&L tax charge. List the two conceptual errors embedded in this formula and rewrite it correctly.

💡 Hint: Consider what the P&L tax charge is computed on and what it includes besides current operating tax.

Q3. Negative FCFF interpretation [L2]

A renewable IPP shows FCFF of ₹-1,200, ₹-800, ₹150, ₹600 and ₹900 crore for FY26–FY30 as it builds 2 GW of capacity. Explain to a non-finance board member why negative FCFF in early years does not imply the project destroys value, and what the DCF needs to show to confirm value creation.
