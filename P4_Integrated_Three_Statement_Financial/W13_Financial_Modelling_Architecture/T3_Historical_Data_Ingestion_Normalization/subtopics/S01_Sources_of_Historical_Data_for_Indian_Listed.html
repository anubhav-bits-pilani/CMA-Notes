Subtopic note

Sources of Historical Data for Indian Listed Companies

Scope: Sources: annual reports, BSE/NSE XBRL filings, SEBI LODR Reg 33 quarterly results, investor presentations, concall transcripts

Why it matters

A model's historicals are only as good as their source. Each source differs in completeness (full notes vs summary), timeliness (quarterly vs annual), assurance (audited vs limited review vs unaudited management data), format (PDF vs machine-readable XBRL) and comparability (restated comparatives vs as-originally-reported). An analyst who knows which source to use for which line — and which source wins when two disagree — loads historicals faster and avoids the classic errors of mixing restated and unrestated years or standalone and consolidated figures. In interviews, being able to say "I take FY24 from the FY25 annual report's comparative column, segment KPIs from the investor presentation, and guidance from the Q2 concall transcript" signals real practice.

Core notes

1. Annual reports

The audited annual report is the primary source for full-year statements and notes. Under SEBI LODR Reg 34, a listed entity submits its annual report to the exchanges no later than the day it commences dispatch to shareholders; under Sec 129 and Sec 134 of the Companies Act 2013, the financial statements (standalone and consolidated) with the Board's report are approved and laid before the AGM.

What it gives that no other source does:

Content Model use
Standalone and consolidated statements with full notes Line-item mapping (s02)
Notes on PPE, CWIP ageing, intangibles, ROU assets Gross block, D&A/opening gross block (s07), capex
Borrowings note (secured/unsecured, current maturities, rates, repayment terms) Debt schedule
Lease note (Ind AS 116 maturity analysis, cash outflow) Lease normalisation (s04)
Tax note (current, deferred, ETR reconciliation under Ind AS 12.81(c)) ETR (c04)
Segment note (Ind AS 108) Segment revenue builds
Employee benefits note (Ind AS 19) Gratuity, leave, actuarial assumptions
Related party note (Ind AS 24) Royalty, inter-group transactions
Schedule III additional disclosures (2021 amendment): receivables and payables ageing, key ratios with explanations for > 25% variance Ready historical ratios, ageing for DSO/DPO checks
Management Discussion & Analysis (Reg 34(3) and Schedule V) Operating KPIs, industry context
Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report Energy, workforce data (headcount)

Comparatives rule: use the latest annual report's comparative column for the prior year (s05) — it reflects reclassifications and restatements.

2. BSE/NSE XBRL filings

Listed entities file financial results and several other disclosures in XBRL (eXtensible Business Reporting Language) format on the exchanges' portals (BSE Listing Centre, NSE Electronic Application Processing System — NEAPS / NSE Digital Exchange). Separately, MCA requires certain classes of companies (including listed companies) to file financial statements in XBRL with Form AOC-4 XBRL.

Advantage Limitation
Machine-readable; tagged by element (e.g., RevenueFromOperations) Quarterly result XBRL holds the results format (P&L, segment, half-yearly BS and CFS), not full notes
Consistent taxonomy across companies — ideal for Power Query ingestion Tags may be used inconsistently ("other expenses" catch-all)
Fast multi-company pulls for comps Units and signs vary (absolute ₹ vs ₹ lakh; expenses sometimes negative — topic t1, c05, c06)

Workflow: download XBRL/Excel from NSE "Corporate Filings → Financial Results" or BSE, load via Power Query (Phase 3), unpivot periods, map tags to the model chart of accounts with a mapping table (s02).

3. SEBI LODR Reg 33 results

Reg 33 requires listed entities to submit quarterly and year-to-date standalone and consolidated financial results within 45 days of the end of each quarter (other than the last), and annual audited results within 60 days of the financial year end. Quarterly results carry an auditor's limited review report (or audit report). Half-yearly results must include the statement of assets and liabilities and the cash flow statement (Reg 33(3)).

Model use:

  • Q4/annual results (available by end-May) let you update the model before the annual report (often July–August).
  • Quarterly data supports seasonality analysis, run-rate checks and LTM (last twelve months) metrics for comps.
  • Results include segment information (Reg 33 format) and exceptional items.

Caution: Reg 33 results are a summary; notes, ageing, lease and tax details arrive only with the annual report. Q4 standalone figures are often derived as annual minus nine-month figures, so they absorb year-end adjustments.

4. Investor presentations

Uploaded under Reg 30 (disclosure of material information; Schedule III Part A Para A of LODR covers schedules of analyst/investor meets and presentations). They provide operating KPIs not in the financial statements: store counts and area by format (Trent: Westside, Zudio stores, net additions, area in mn sq ft), underlying volume growth (Pidilite: UVG by segment), price changes, capacity, utilisation, raw-material trends (VAM prices), management-defined EBITDA.

Unaudited and management-defined — reconcile any financial figure to the statements; treat KPIs as management data with source references.

5. Concall transcripts

Since the 2023 LODR amendments, listed entities must upload audio/video recordings of earnings calls promptly (within 24 hours) and transcripts within five working days (Reg 30 and Reg 46; check the current timelines when relying on them). Transcripts give guidance (growth, margin bands, capex plans, store-addition targets), explanations of one-offs, and answers to analyst questions that explain anomalies in the numbers.

Model use: forecast driver sources in the assumption log (with call date and speaker), qualitative support for normalisation decisions (e.g., "inventory write-down of ₹45 crore is one-time").

Source hierarchy and conflicts

Data type Preferred source Fallback
Full-year statements Latest audited annual report (comparatives) Reg 33 audited annual results
Latest quarter Reg 33 results Investor presentation
Notes and breakdowns Annual report Prior annual reports for older years
Operating KPIs Investor presentation / MD&A Concall transcript
Guidance Concall transcript Investor presentation
Multi-company comps XBRL (exchange) Data vendors (Capitaline, Ace Equity, Prowess, Bloomberg)

Record the source for each historical block (document, page, date) in a Sources column on Historicals.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Vendor databases (Screener, Capitaline, Prowess) reclassify lines to their own templates; useful for speed, but tie key totals to the annual report.
  • Reg 33 vs annual report differences: post-results audit adjustments are rare but possible; the annual report prevails.
  • Restated quarterly comparatives after an Ind AS 105 discontinuance or common-control merger: use the latest filing.
  • Ind AS transition years (FY2016-17 to FY2018-19 depending on phase) mean older Indian GAAP data is not comparable; limit history to Ind AS years.
  • Insider information: use only published information in public models; management-provided data requires confidentiality controls.

Key takeaways

  • Annual report = full audited statements and notes; use the latest report's comparative column.
  • XBRL = machine-readable, ideal for Power Query and comps, but limited detail and inconsistent tags/units.
  • Reg 33 results arrive within 45 days of quarter end (60 days for annual), with limited review; half-yearly BS and CFS included.
  • Investor presentations and concall transcripts supply KPIs and guidance — unaudited, so reconcile and cite.
  • Keep a source hierarchy and record document, page and date for every historical block.

Practice Questions

Q1. Pick the source [L1]

For a Pidilite model being updated on 20-Nov-2025, state the best source for: (a) FY24 consolidated revenue; (b) Q2FY26 EBITDA; (c) FY25 UVG for Consumer & Bazaar; (d) FY25 gross block of plant and machinery; (e) management's FY26 margin guidance; (f) H1FY26 cash flow from operations.

Q2. Timeline of availability [L2]

Map out, for an FY ending 31-Mar-2025, the latest dates by which Reg 33 Q4/annual results, the annual report and the Q1FY26 results must be available, and explain which model updates you would make at each point.

💡 Hint: Use the 45-day, 60-day and Reg 34 annual report timing rules.

Q3. Reconcile a KPI [L2]

Trent's investor presentation reports "Revenue from fashion concepts" of ₹X crore while the consolidated P&L shows revenue from operations of ₹Y crore, higher by about 4%. List the likely reconciling items and how you would decide which figure drives the store-level revenue per sq ft calculation.

Q4. Build an ingestion pipeline [L3]

Design a Power Query-based ingestion pipeline that pulls five years of consolidated P&L and BS from exchange XBRL/Excel downloads for Trent and three peers, converts units to ₹ crore, applies sign rules, maps tags to the model chart of accounts, and flags unmapped tags. Specify the query steps, the mapping table columns and the tie-out checks.
