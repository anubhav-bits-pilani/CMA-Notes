Key concept

Historical Balance Check: Assets Equal Equity Plus Liabilities

Concept: Balance check: Total Assets − (Total Equity + Total Liabilities) = 0.00 for every historical year

Statement

For every historical year \( t \in \{\text{FY21A}, \ldots, \text{FY25A}\} \):

\[ \text{BC}_t = \sum_{i \in A} a_{i,t} - \left( \sum_{j \in E} e_{j,t} + \sum_{k \in L} l_{k,t} \right) = 0.00 \]

where \( a_{i,t} \) are the model's mapped asset lines, \( e_{j,t} \) the mapped equity lines (share capital, other equity, NCI), \( l_{k,t} \) the mapped non-current and current liability lines, all in ₹ crore. The check is computed from the model chart of accounts, not from reported totals, and must equal zero (within an absolute tolerance of 0.001) in every year.

Explanation

Why every year. Forecasts open from the last actual year, so one might think only FY25A matters. But historical ratios (DSO, DIO, capex/sales, ROCE) use every year; an error in FY22 distorts averages and trends. Moreover, a failure in an earlier year often reveals a systematic mapping error (a caption mis-mapped in all years but offset by coincidence in the latest).

Why model-mapped totals. If the Historicals tab types reported "Total assets" and "Total equity and liabilities" and subtracts them, the check is always zero and proves nothing. The test must sum the model lines, which proves that every reported line has been captured once, with the right sign and unit.

Companion checks.

Check Purpose
Σ model assets − reported total assets Mapping completeness on the asset side
Σ model equity + liabilities − reported total Completeness on the funding side
Balance check (above) Internal consistency
Rounding line (c06) Absorbs genuine ₹ crore rounding in the published statements, explicitly

Equity includes NCI. In consolidated statements total equity = equity attributable to owners + NCI. Omitting NCI produces a failure equal to the NCI balance.

Signs. Contra items (e.g., treasury shares held by an ESOP trust, which reduce other equity) must follow the declared convention (topic t1, c05).

Illustration

FY23 mapped BS (₹ crore):

Side Lines Sum
Assets PPE 3,210; CWIP 240; ROU 1,860; goodwill 120; investments (non-current) 310; other NCA 280; inventories 1,140; receivables 380; cash 210; other bank balances 150; current investments 690; other CA 310 8,900
Equity Share capital 36; other equity 4,520; NCI 85 4,641
Liabilities Borrowings NC 420; lease liabilities NC 1,780; other NCL 160; borrowings C 260; lease liabilities C 210; payables 980; other CL 449 4,259

BC = 8,900 − (4,641 + 4,259) = 0.00 ✓. Had NCI (85) been omitted, BC = 85.00; had current lease liabilities been mapped into "other CL" and "lease liabilities C", BC = −210.00.

Practice Questions

Q1. Locate the failure [L1]

A historical balance check shows FY24 = 412.00, all other years zero. The FY24 reported "other current liabilities" is ₹412 crore. List two mapping errors that would produce this exact difference.

Q2. Prove the check is meaningful [L2]

A colleague's Historicals tab computes the balance check as =H60-H95 where H60 and H95 are typed reported totals. Explain why this is inadequate and design the replacement check rows.

💡 Hint: The check must be able to fail if a single line is mis-mapped.

Q3. Sign of a contra item [L2]

An ESOP trust holds treasury shares of ₹140 crore, presented as a deduction within other equity. Your mapping has "Treasury shares" as a separate equity line stored positive (Convention A). Explain how the check behaves if the line is summed rather than subtracted, and the fix.
