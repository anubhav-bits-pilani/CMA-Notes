Key concept

Operating Net Working Capital: What to Exclude

Concept: Operating NWC excludes cash, current investments, current maturities of debt, current lease liabilities and dividends payable

Statement

\[ \text{Operating NWC}_t = \underbrace{\left( \text{CA}_t - \text{Cash}_t - \text{OBB}_t - \text{CI}_t - \text{Non-op CA}_t \right)}_{\text{operating current assets}} - \underbrace{\left( \text{CL}_t - \text{STD}_t - \text{CMLTD}_t - \text{CLL}_t - \text{DivP}_t - \text{Non-op CL}_t \right)}_{\text{operating current liabilities}} \]

where \( \text{CA}_t \) and \( \text{CL}_t \) are total current assets and liabilities; \( \text{Cash}_t \) cash and cash equivalents; \( \text{OBB}_t \) other bank balances; \( \text{CI}_t \) current investments; \( \text{STD}_t \) short-term borrowings; \( \text{CMLTD}_t \) current maturities of long-term debt (within current borrowings under Schedule III after the 2021 amendment); \( \text{CLL}_t \) current lease liabilities; \( \text{DivP}_t \) dividends payable (and unpaid dividends); and "Non-op" captures other financing, capex-related or tax items (interest accrued on borrowings, capital creditors, current tax assets/liabilities if forecast in the tax schedule, assets held for sale).

Explanation

Operating NWC is the investment in receivables, inventories and other operating current items, net of trade payables and operating accruals, required to run the business. Its change is a component of FCFF. Anything that is cash, financing or investing in nature must be excluded, because it is captured elsewhere:

Excluded item Why Where it goes
Cash, other bank balances, current investments Treasury, not operating Net debt / EV bridge
Short-term borrowings, current maturities of LTD Financing Debt schedule
Current lease liabilities Financing under Ind AS 116 Lease schedule; net debt if post-116
Dividends payable / unpaid dividend Distribution to owners (offset by earmarked bank balance) Equity schedule
Interest accrued on borrowings Financing Debt schedule
Capital creditors (payables for capex) Investing PPE schedule / capex
Current tax assets/liabilities Tax timing Tax schedule
Derivative assets/liabilities on borrowings Financing Debt
Assets held for sale Disposal Bridge

Typical inclusions: trade receivables, inventories, contract assets, advances to suppliers, prepaid expenses, GST input credit receivable (operating), trade payables, contract liabilities (advances from customers), employee-related payables, statutory dues (GST, TDS payable), short-term provisions for employee benefits and warranties.

Consistency with days. Receivables include GST billed while revenue is net of GST, so DSO computed on net revenue is slightly higher than "true" collection days — fine, as long as the forecast inverts the same formula.

Why this matters. Leaving current maturities of debt in NWC makes NWC swing with repayment schedules; leaving current lease liabilities in NWC double-counts leases if the liability is also in net debt; leaving cash in makes NWC grow with cash, so FCFF falls as the company accumulates cash — circular and wrong.

Illustration

FY25 (₹ crore): current assets 4,200 (inventories 1,650; receivables 640; cash 300; other bank balances 420; current investments 780; other CA 410, of which current tax asset 40); current liabilities 3,050 (current borrowings 380, of which current maturities of LTD 150; current lease liabilities 230; trade payables 1,520; other financial liabilities 520, of which capital creditors 90, unpaid dividends 8, interest accrued 12; other CL 270; provisions 130).

Operating CA = 1,650 + 640 + (410 − 40) = 2,660. Operating CL = 1,520 + (520 − 90 − 8 − 12) + 270 + 130 = 2,330. Operating NWC = 2,660 − 2,330 = ₹330 crore. Reported (CA − CL) = 4,200 − 3,050 = ₹1,150 crore — overstates operating NWC by ₹820 crore, mostly treasury.

Practice Questions

Q1. Classify the items [L1]

Classify as operating NWC or excluded (with destination): advance from customers; GST input credit; interest accrued on NCDs; current maturities of term loans; security deposits paid to landlords (current); capital advances; provision for gratuity (current); unpaid dividends; liquid mutual funds; derivative liability on a USD loan.

Q2. Compute operating NWC [L2]

From FY25 data (₹ crore): inventories 980; receivables 410; cash 120; FDs 3–12 months 250; other CA 190 (incl. advance tax net 25, assets held for sale 30); current borrowings 300 (CMLTD 100); current lease liabilities 140; trade payables 760; other financial liabilities 260 (capital creditors 45, dividends payable 15, employee dues 80, others 120); other CL 150 (statutory dues 90, contract liabilities 60). Compute operating NWC and NWC as % of revenue of ₹6,400 crore.

💡 Hint: Work line by line, excluding treasury, financing, capex-related, tax and disposal items.

Q3. Lease double count [L2]

A model includes current lease liabilities in NWC and also includes total lease liabilities in net debt. Explain the effect on FCFF in years when lease liabilities grow, on equity value, and the correction.
