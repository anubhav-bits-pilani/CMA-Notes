Subtopic note

Segment Revenue Builds: Trent and Pidilite Case Structures

Scope: Segment builds: Trent (Westside, Zudio, Star) or Pidilite (Consumer & Bazaar vs B2B; domestic vs international)

Why it matters

Consolidated revenue is the sum of businesses with very different growth drivers, margins and capital intensity. Trent's value-fashion Zudio stores grow store count at a pace the premium Westside format does not; Pidilite's Consumer & Bazaar (C&B) adhesives and waterproofing behave differently from its B2B industrial adhesives and pigments business, and its international subsidiaries carry FX and country risk. Forecasting one consolidated growth rate averages away exactly the information an analyst is paid to have an opinion on. A segment build also anchors on audited disclosure: Ind AS 108 Operating Segments requires segment revenue and results as reviewed by the chief operating decision maker (CODM), so segment-level history is reconcilable to the financial statements, while format-level and geography-level detail comes from investor presentations and concalls.

This subtopic is the bridge between the generic methods (s01–s05) and the Week 13 exercise.

Core notes

Step 1 — define the segment tree and reconcile to reported revenue

Before forecasting, define the hierarchy and prove it adds up to reported revenue from operations for each historical year:

\[ \text{Revenue from operations} = \sum \text{Segment revenue} - \text{Inter-segment eliminations} + \text{Other operating revenue} \]

Keep a reconciliation row per year (target difference = 0, or an explicit "unallocated/other" line disclosed by the company). Choose consolidated vs standalone deliberately (w13 t3 s03): the entity basis determines which businesses even appear in revenue.

Trent — format-led retail build

Structure. Trent's fashion business is run under Westside (premium-mid, in-house brands, larger stores), Zudio (value fashion, smaller stores, rapid Tier-2/3 expansion) and other lifestyle concepts (Utsa, Samoh, Misbu and beauty). The Star grocery business is operated through Trent Hypermarket Pvt Ltd, a joint venture with Tesco — in Trent's consolidated financial statements it is equity-accounted under Ind AS 28, so Star's revenue does not sit in consolidated revenue from operations; Trent's share of its profit appears below EBITDA in "share of profit/(loss) of joint ventures and associates". Modelling Star's stores and revenue is still useful for valuing the JV stake (sum-of-the-parts), but it must not be added to consolidated revenue.

Driver tree per format (from s02 and s03):

Driver Westside Zudio Source
Opening stores, gross adds, closures Moderate adds (~25–40 a year) High adds (150–250 a year) Annual report store counts, concall guidance
Average new-store area Larger (~20,000+ sq ft) Smaller (~7,000–10,000 sq ft) Investor presentation area disclosures
Mature revenue per sq ft Higher Lower (value pricing) Derived: format revenue ÷ average area
LFL Single-digit to low-teens Single-digit to low-teens Concall commentary
Maturity curve Slower (mall-dependent) Faster (high street, price-led) Calibrated assumption

Other revenue lines. Online sales (Westside.com, partner platforms), franchise/royalty income, and the international Zudio stores (first stores outside India opened in Dubai) should be separate rows with their own drivers or modest growth assumptions. Illustrative magnitude (not actuals): if Zudio's average area grows 35% and revenue per sq ft 4%, Zudio revenue grows about 40%; if Westside's area grows 10% with 7% density growth, Westside grows about 18%; the blended rate depends on the shifting weights — mix in reverse (s01).

Checks specific to Trent. Total area and store count by format reconcile to the latest disclosed figures; blended revenue per sq ft falls as Zudio's share rises (mix), which is not a deterioration; store adds tie to the Ind AS 116 lease addition schedule and fit-out capex in Week 14.

Pidilite — category and channel build

Structure. Pidilite reports operating segments under Ind AS 108 as Consumer & Bazaar (C&B: Fevicol, Fevikwik, M-Seal, Dr. Fixit, Araldite consumer range, art and craft) and Business-to-Business (B2B: industrial adhesives, synthetic resins, pigments and construction-chemical project sales), with a small "others" segment. C&B is the large majority of revenue. Geographically, standalone (India) is the bulk; consolidated adds international subsidiaries (South Asia, Middle East, Africa, Americas, South-East Asia) and domestic subsidiaries/JVs.

Driver tree:

Level Driver Source
C&B domestic UVG × price/mix (s01); sub-categories: adhesives & sealants, waterproofing, art & craft Quarterly UVG and price commentary
B2B domestic Industrial volume growth tied to IIP/packaging/textiles demand × price Segment disclosure, concall
International Local-currency growth × INR/FCY translation Subsidiary financials (AOC-1), concall
Eliminations Inter-segment sales (e.g., resins supplied to C&B) Segment note

UVG is the anchor: Pidilite discusses C&B UVG separately from value growth, and price changes follow VAM and other raw material cycles — when VAM falls, price cuts can make value growth lag volume growth (Topic 5 links this to gross margin). International revenue in rupees:

\[ \text{Revenue}_{\text{INR},t} = \text{Revenue}_{\text{LC},t-1} \times (1 + g_{\text{LC}}) \times \text{FX}_t \]

so currency depreciation in, for example, Bangladesh or Egypt can cut reported rupee revenue even with healthy local growth. Use average exchange rates for revenue (Ind AS 21 translation of income items).

Step 2 — segment outputs and validation

For each segment produce: revenue, growth decomposition (volume/area, price/density, mix), and share of total. Validate against: (a) historical segment growth distribution; (b) management guidance ranges; (c) the top-down check (s07). Where segment EBIT is disclosed (Ind AS 108), build segment margins as well — this supports a sum-of-the-parts valuation later in the course.

Implementation notes

  • One block per segment, same column structure as every other sheet (w13 t1 s04).
  • Inputs per segment on the Assumptions sheet, grouped so the scenario switch (Topic 6) flexes each segment's drivers independently.
  • Segment total feeds the 3-statement P&L through a single link row (link once, reference locally).

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Segment re-organisation. Companies re-cast segments (Pidilite has re-classified products between C&B and B2B in the past); use the latest comparatives and re-cast history (w13 t3 s05).
  • JVs and associates. Star (Trent) and Pidilite's JVs are equity-accounted; do not add their revenue.
  • Acquisitions. Pidilite's acquisitions (e.g., Araldite consumer business) add inorganic revenue — separate them for UVG purposes in the first year.
  • Disclosure gaps. Trent does not publish audited revenue by format; derived densities are estimates — show them as assumptions, not facts.
  • Transfer pricing between segments. Inter-segment pricing can shift margins between segments without affecting consolidated results.

Key takeaways

  • Build revenue at the segment/format level and reconcile every historical year to reported revenue.
  • Trent: format builds for Westside and Zudio; Star is an equity-accounted JV, not consolidated revenue.
  • Pidilite: C&B and B2B domestic builds on UVG × price, plus international in local currency × FX.
  • Use Ind AS 108 segment data as the audited anchor, investor materials for drivers.
  • Keep segment inputs grouped so scenarios can flex each segment.
  • Re-cast history when segments are reorganised.

Practice Questions

Q1. Segment reconciliation [L1]

A company reports segment revenues of ₹6,420 crore (Segment A) and ₹1,880 crore (Segment B), inter-segment revenue of ₹140 crore included in Segment B, and revenue from operations of ₹8,195 crore. Build the reconciliation and identify what the remaining difference could represent.

Q2. Trent consolidated revenue [L2]

A junior analyst adds Star's store count × revenue per store to Trent's consolidated revenue forecast. Explain the error with reference to Ind AS 28 and Ind AS 110, where Star should appear in the consolidated P&L and valuation, and how to correct the model.

Q3. Pidilite international translation [L2]

Pidilite's Bangladesh subsidiary earns BDT 900 crore in FY25 at an average rate of ₹0.71 per BDT. Local growth for FY26 is expected at 12% and the average rate at ₹0.66 per BDT. Compute FY26 revenue in ₹ crore and the growth in rupee terms, and explain how you would present the FX effect in the segment bridge.

Q4. Exercise design — segment driver map [L3]

For either Trent or Pidilite, design the full segment driver map for a five-year forecast: list each segment, its drivers, the data source for each driver, the historical years needed, and the checks that prove the segment build reconciles to reported revenue.

💡 Hint: Start from the Ind AS 108 note and the latest investor presentation, then work down to the drivers.
