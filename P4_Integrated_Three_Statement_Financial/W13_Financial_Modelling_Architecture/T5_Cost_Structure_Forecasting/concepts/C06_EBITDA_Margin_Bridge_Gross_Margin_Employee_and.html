Key concept

EBITDA Margin Bridge: Gross Margin, Employee and Other Opex Steps

Concept: Margin bridge: prior EBITDA margin ± gross margin Δ ± employee cost Δ ± other opex Δ = current margin

Statement

\[ m_t = m_{t-1} + \left(\text{GM}_t - \text{GM}_{t-1}\right) - \left(e_t - e_{t-1}\right) - \left(o_t - o_{t-1}\right) \]

where \(m_t\) is EBITDA margin (EBITDA ÷ revenue), \(\text{GM}_t\) is gross margin (gross profit ÷ revenue), \(e_t\) is employee benefits expense ÷ revenue and \(o_t\) is other operating expenses ÷ revenue. Because \(m_t = \text{GM}_t - e_t - o_t\) and all terms share the same denominator, the bridge is exactly additive with no residual, and is usually expressed in basis points.

Explanation

The bridge answers "why did margin change?" in the language management and investors use. A rise in a cost ratio reduces margin, so each cost step enters with a negative sign: if employee cost falls from 11.0% to 10.5% of revenue, the bridge shows +50 bps.

Interpretation of each step:

  • Gross margin Δ — price vs input cost, mix, pass-through lag (s02). Often the largest and most volatile item for commodity-exposed businesses.
  • Employee cost Δ — increments vs revenue growth and headcount vs volume. Negative (adverse) when increments exceed revenue growth or headcount is added ahead of growth.
  • Other opex Δ — A&P intensity, freight and power per unit, store opex per sq ft vs density, fixed overhead leverage, step costs.

Conditions:

  • Define EBITDA consistently (excluding other income, exceptional items, D&A and finance costs) and state pre- or post-Ind AS 116.
  • Use the same cost groupings every year; re-cast history if the company reclassifies.
  • For deeper analysis, split each step further (e.g., GM into price, cost and mix), but keep the three-step version as the headline.
  • The additive property holds for ratio changes; a rupee bridge (EBITDA change in ₹ crore by driver) is a different exhibit with volume and price effects, and must not be mixed with the bps bridge in one table.

Illustration

Line FY25A FY26E
Revenue (₹ crore) 12,000 13,200
Gross margin 51.5% 53.0%
Employee cost ratio 10.8% 10.4%
Other opex ratio 17.2% 17.8%
EBITDA margin 23.5% 24.8%

Bridge: 23.5% + 150 bps (GM) + 40 bps (employee) − 60 bps (other opex) = 24.8%. Narrative: input-cost easing expanded gross margin; increments below revenue growth gave employee leverage; higher A&P intensity (reinvestment of gross-margin gains, typical in Indian consumer companies) and a new DC absorbed part of the gain.

Q3. Bridge vs rupee walk [L2]
