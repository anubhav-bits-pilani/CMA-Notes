Subtopic note

Single Master Timeline Row and Period Flags

Scope: Single master timeline row: FY labels (FY25A, FY26E…), 31-March period ends, period counter, actual/forecast flags

Why it matters

The timeline is the spine of the model. Every time-series formula, every flag, every cross-sheet link and every DCF discount factor depends on it. If timelines are typed independently on each tab, sooner or later one tab starts a year later or skips FY24, and links silently connect FY27E revenue to FY26E receivables. A single master timeline — defined once, linked everywhere — removes that class of error and lets you roll the model forward a year by changing one input. In Indian practice it must also encode the April–March financial year correctly, because FY labels, Income-tax "previous year" and SEBI quarter conventions all depend on it.

Core notes

Inputs that drive the timeline

Only three inputs are needed on the Inputs tab:

Named range Example Meaning
First_Period_End 31-Mar-2021 Period end of the first column
Last_Actual_End 31-Mar-2025 Last reported (audited) year
Periodicity 12 Months per period

Everything else is a formula on a master Timeline block (at the top of Inputs or a dedicated Timeline tab), then linked to row 1–6 of every calc sheet.

The master rows

Row Label First column formula Subsequent columns Example (col H, I)
1 Period start =EDATE(H2,-Periodicity)+1 same 01-Apr-2020, 01-Apr-2021
2 Period end =First_Period_End =EOMONTH(H2,Periodicity) 31-Mar-2021, 31-Mar-2022
3 Fiscal year =YEAR(H2) same 2021, 2022
4 Actual flag =--(H2<=Last_Actual_End) same 1, 1
5 Forecast flag =1-H4 same 0, 0
6 FY label ="FY"&RIGHT(H3,2)&IF(H4=1,"A","E") same FY21A, FY22A
7 Period counter =H5*(SUM($G5:G5)+1) or =N(G7)+H5 same 0, 0 → 1, 2… in forecast
8 First forecast flag =H5*(1-G5) same 1 only in FY26E

Because 31 March falls in the calendar year after 1 April, YEAR(Period end) gives the Indian FY number: the year ending 31-Mar-2026 is FY2025-26, labelled FY26. Always label by the year of the period end, and state the convention on the Cover ("FY26 = year ending 31 March 2026").

EOMONTH is preferred to adding 365 days because it handles leap years (FY24 ended 31-Mar-2024 after a 29-Feb) and keeps the period end on the month end.

Period counter uses

The forecast period counter (1 for FY26E, 2 for FY27E…) is used for:

  • DCF discount factors: \( DF_t = \frac{1}{(1+WACC)^{t}} \), or with mid-year convention \( DF_t = \frac{1}{(1+WACC)^{t-0.5}} \), where \( t \) is the counter.
  • Compounding inflation from a base year: \( \text{Cost}_t = \text{Base} \times (1+\pi)^{t} \).
  • Driver curves indexed by forecast year (e.g., store maturity year).

A stub-period valuation (valuation date 30-Sep-2025) replaces the integer counter with a year fraction: =YEARFRAC(Valuation_Date,H2,1).

Flags: switching logic without breaking row consistency

The actual/forecast flags let one formula handle both regions:

=H4*Historicals!H20 + H5*(G22*(1+H15))

In actual years the formula picks the historical link; in forecast years it applies the growth driver. This preserves the "one row, one formula" rule (topic t2) across the actual/forecast boundary. An alternative used by many banks keeps historical columns as pure links and forecast columns as a different formula, with a visual boundary — acceptable, but the flag approach makes roll-forward automatic.

Rolling the model forward

When FY26 results are published, change Last_Actual_End to 31-Mar-2026, load the FY26 actuals on Historicals, and every flag, label and counter updates: FY26E becomes FY26A, the counter restarts at FY27E, and DCF discounting shifts. If labels were typed, every tab would need manual edits.

Linking to calc sheets

Each calc sheet's rows 1–8 are direct links (=Timeline!H2) — green font because they are cross-sheet — in the same columns as the master. Never re-derive the timeline locally with its own EOMONTH; one source means one truth.

Quarterly and half-yearly variants

SEBI LODR Reg 33 quarterly results end on 30 June, 30 September, 31 December and 31 March. A quarterly timeline uses Periodicity = 3, labels like ="Q"&(INT(MOD(MONTH(H2)-4,12)/3)+1)&"FY"&RIGHT(YEAR(H2)+(MONTH(H2)>3),2) (30-Jun-2025 gives Q1FY26, 31-Mar-2026 gives Q4FY26) and an annual flag =--(MONTH(H2)=3) for roll-ups via SUMIFS on the FY number.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Non-March year ends. A few Indian subsidiaries of foreign groups had December year ends before Sec 2(41) of the Companies Act 2013 required a uniform April–March financial year (with Tribunal exemptions for entities consolidated abroad). For comps calendarisation, convert peers to March year ends.
  • Partial first actual year. A company that changed its year end has a 15- or 9-month period; flag it and annualise ratios only with explicit adjustments.
  • Unaudited latest year. If only Reg 33 audited annual results (without the full annual report) are out, label FY25A but note "per Reg 33 results; notes pending".
  • Stub forecast periods. When valuing mid-year, add a stub flag and fraction rather than shifting the whole timeline.
  • Label-driven lookups. Avoid MATCH on text labels like "FY26E" — they change on roll-forward. Look up on the period-end date or FY number.

Key takeaways

  • Build the timeline once from two or three inputs; link it to every sheet.
  • Label FY by the year of the 31-March period end and state the convention on the Cover.
  • Use EOMONTH for period ends; YEAR(period end) gives the Indian FY number.
  • Actual/forecast flags and a forecast counter let one formula span the whole row.
  • Rolling forward should require changing one input plus loading the new actuals.
  • Look up by date or FY number, never by the A/E text label.

Practice Questions

Q1. Build the timeline [L1]

Write the formulas for rows 1–8 of a master timeline starting with the year ending 31-Mar-2021, last actual 31-Mar-2025 and forecasts to 31-Mar-2030, in columns H to Q. State the values each row shows in column M.

Q2. Discount factor with a stub [L2]

The valuation date is 30-Sep-2025 and WACC is 11.5%. Using the master timeline (FY26E ends 31-Mar-2026), design the rows needed to compute mid-period discount factors for FY26E (half-year stub) through FY30E, and specify the formula for each row.

💡 Hint: Separate the stub fraction from the period counter before combining them in the exponent.

Q3. Roll-forward test [L2]

Describe step by step what a reviewer should do to test that a model rolls forward correctly from Last_Actual_End = 31-Mar-2025 to 31-Mar-2026, and list four specific things that typically break if labels or counters were hardcoded.

Q4. Quarterly to annual roll-up [L3]

An FP&A team maintains a quarterly forecast (Q1FY26 to Q4FY27) on one tab and an annual 3-statement model on another. Design the timeline rows on the quarterly tab and the SUMIFS formula that populates FY26E and FY27E revenue in the annual model, ensuring the annual tab does not need editing when the quarterly tab is extended.
