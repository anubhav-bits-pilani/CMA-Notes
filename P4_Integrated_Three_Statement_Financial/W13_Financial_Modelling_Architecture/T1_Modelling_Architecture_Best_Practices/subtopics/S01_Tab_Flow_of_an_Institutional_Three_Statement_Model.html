Subtopic note

Tab Flow of an Institutional Three-Statement Model

Scope: Tab flow: Cover → Model map → Inputs/Assumptions → Historicals → Schedules (WC, PPE, Debt, Tax, Equity) → 3-Statement → DCF/WACC → Comps → Outputs

Why it matters

A reviewer opening your model for the first time — an investment committee member, a Big 4 model auditor, a lender's technical advisor or the hiring manager running a modelling test — judges it in the first ninety seconds by its tab order. A model whose tabs follow the direction of calculation can be audited by reading left to right; a model whose tabs were added in the order the analyst happened to think of them forces the reader to jump back and forth, and every jump is a place where an error hides. In Indian equity research and corporate FP&A, the same skeleton is reused across companies, so a disciplined tab flow is also a productivity tool: once the structure is fixed, each new company is mostly a data-loading and driver exercise.

Core notes

The principle: tabs in the order of dependency

Excel recalculates by dependency, not by tab order, but humans read by tab order. The rule is that a tab should only take inputs from tabs to its left (with the unavoidable exception of feedback loops such as interest on average debt, which are handled by an explicit circularity switch). This makes the workbook a directed flow: inputs → calculations → outputs.

The standard tab sequence

# Tab Role Typical contents Feeds
1 Cover Identity and control Company, entity basis, units, sign convention, version, author, change log, master check flag, scenario selector (sometimes) Nothing (display only)
2 Model map Navigation Tab list with purpose, colour legend, flow diagram, hyperlinks Nothing
3 Inputs / Assumptions All hardcoded drivers Timeline settings, scenario blocks, growth/price/store drivers, cost drivers, WC days, capex, tax rate, WACC inputs All calc tabs
4 Historicals Reported data 3–5 years of P&L, BS, CFS mapped to the model chart of accounts, with balance and cash checks Schedules, 3-statement
5 Revenue / Opex build (operating schedules) Driver calculations Volume × price, store rollout, headcount × CTC 3-statement P&L
6 Working Capital NWC schedule DSO, DIO, DPO, other current items BS and CFS
7 PPE (Fixed assets) Capex and depreciation Gross block roll-forward, depreciation, CWIP, ROU assets BS, P&L, CFS
8 Debt Financing schedule Term loans, NCDs, revolver, lease liabilities, interest BS, P&L, CFS
9 Tax Tax schedule Current tax (115BAA at 25.168%), deferred tax, MAT credit if legacy P&L, BS
10 Equity Equity roll-forward Share capital, buybacks, dividends, NCI BS, CFS
11 3-Statement Integration P&L, BS, CFS pulling from schedules; balance check DCF, outputs
12 DCF / WACC Valuation FCFF, terminal value, WACC build, EV to equity bridge Outputs
13 Comps Relative valuation Peer multiples (EV/EBITDA, P/E), calendarisation Outputs
14 Outputs / Dashboard Presentation Summary financials, charts, scenario table, football field Nothing
15 Checks Integrity All check rows rolled into one master flag Cover

Some practitioners place the Checks tab immediately after the Cover so that it is the second thing a reviewer sees; others place it at the end. Either is acceptable provided the master flag is displayed on the Cover (see s06).

Why each block sits where it does

  • Cover and Model map first because they describe the file; a reviewer reads these before anything else.
  • Inputs before Historicals is a choice: some houses put Historicals first because forecast drivers are often derived from historical ratios. The key is that both sit before the schedules, since schedules use both.
  • Schedules before the 3-statement tab because the 3-statement tab should be a thin integration layer — mostly links, sums and a handful of subtotals. If the P&L tab contains a 40-term formula for depreciation, the PPE schedule is in the wrong place.
  • Order of schedules: WC, PPE, Debt, Tax, Equity mirrors the order in which a cash flow statement is built — operating, investing, financing — with tax after debt because interest drives taxable income, and equity last because dividends depend on PAT.
  • Valuation after the 3-statement because DCF consumes FCFF derived from the integrated statements, and Comps uses forecast EBITDA/EPS.

Inputs, calcs and outputs as tab "zones"

Many teams colour tab names: e.g., yellow tabs for inputs, grey for calculations, green for outputs, red for checks. Excel allows right-click → Tab Color. This gives the reader a visual cue matching the cell colour code in topic t2.

Granularity: when to split or merge tabs

A schedule deserves its own tab when it has its own timeline logic and more than about 30–40 rows, or when a different person maintains it. Small schedules (e.g., equity roll-forward with five rows) can sit as a block on the 3-statement tab. The opposite failure — one tab per line item, such as "Rent", "Power", "Freight" as separate sheets — fragments the model (pitfall in topic.md).

Mapping to an Indian listed company build

For Trent Ltd the operating schedule tab would contain the store rollout (Westside, Zudio, Star) and an opex build; for Pidilite a volume × price × mix build for Consumer & Bazaar and B2B. Both models share the same downstream tabs. The Historicals tab follows Schedule III Division II line items (topic t3), so the 3-statement tab's historical columns are links to Historicals and its forecast columns are links to schedules — one row structure across the boundary.

Illustration: dependency trace for one number

Forecast FY27E interest cost (₹ crore) flows: Inputs (interest rate 8.5%, repayment schedule) → Debt tab (opening balance ₹1,200 crore, repayment ₹200 crore, closing ₹1,000 crore, average ₹1,100 crore × 8.5% = ₹93.5 crore) → 3-Statement P&L finance costs → Tax tab (deductible) → PAT → Equity tab (dividends) → BS. Every step moves right. If the Debt tab's opening balance pulled from the BS on the 3-statement tab (to its right), that is a backward link; it is acceptable only if the BS line is itself a pure link to the prior year's Debt-tab closing balance — better practice is to roll the balance within the Debt tab and have the BS link to it.

Edge cases & judgement calls

  • Circular references. Interest on average cash/revolver creates a loop between Debt, 3-Statement and CFS. Keep the loop explicit and switchable with a Circ named range; do not let the tab flow disguise it.
  • Segment-heavy companies. A conglomerate may need one revenue tab per segment; keep them adjacent and identical in layout, then consolidate on a single "Revenue summary" tab.
  • Monthly or quarterly models. An FP&A rolling forecast may add a quarterly tab feeding the annual model; keep the annual roll-up formula-driven (SUMIFS on the FY label), never pasted.
  • Lender models (project finance) add CFADS, DSCR and reserve account tabs between Debt and Outputs.
  • Comps from external data. Comps often link to a Capitaline/Bloomberg/Ace Equity export; isolate the raw export on its own tab and flag external links (red/purple font, topic t2).

Key takeaways

  • Tabs should follow the direction of calculation: identity → inputs → historicals → schedules → integration → valuation → outputs → checks.
  • The 3-statement tab should be thin; heavy logic belongs in schedules.
  • Schedule order (WC, PPE, Debt, Tax, Equity) mirrors operating → investing → financing logic.
  • Colour tabs by zone and provide a model map so a reviewer can navigate in seconds.
  • Backward links should exist only for genuine, switchable circularity.
  • Split a schedule into its own tab when its size or ownership justifies it, not per line item.

Practice Questions

Q1. Order the tabs [L1]

You inherit a workbook with tabs in this order: DCF, P&L, Inputs, Debt, Cover, Comps, BS, WC, Historicals, CFS, Tax, PPE, Checks. Re-sequence them into an institutional tab flow and justify the position of Tax relative to Debt and of Checks relative to Cover.

Q2. Identify backward links [L2]

On the Debt tab, opening FY26E term loan balance is ='3-Statement'!H45 (the FY25A balance sheet borrowings line), and FY26E interest is =Debt!H20*Inputs!$E$30. The 3-Statement tab sits to the right of Debt. Explain whether each link is a problem, and redesign the FY26E opening balance so that the dependency runs left to right.

💡 Hint: Think about where the FY25A actual should first enter the model.

Q3. Thin integration tab [L2]

A colleague's P&L tab computes depreciation as =SUMPRODUCT(PPE_Additions_Row,1/Useful_Life)*... directly on the P&L line. List three reasons this should move to the PPE tab, and describe what the P&L depreciation line should contain after the change.

Q4. Design a tab map for Trent Ltd [L3]

Design the tab list and model map for a 5-year Trent Ltd model (FY21A–FY25A historicals, FY26E–FY30E forecast) with separate Westside, Zudio and Star store builds, an employee-cost schedule, Ind AS 116 lease schedule, debt, tax and DCF. For each tab state its zone (input/calc/output/check), its main sources and its main consumers, and decide whether the lease schedule belongs on PPE, Debt or its own tab.

Q5. Interview: why not one sheet [L1]

An interviewer says, "I prefer everything on one long sheet so I can see it all." Give a two-minute answer covering when a single-sheet model is acceptable and why a modular tab flow is preferred for a listed-company valuation model.
