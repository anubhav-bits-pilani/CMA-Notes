Six Portfolio-Grade Capstone Projects

Project 01 · Institutional Month-End Closing & Ind AS Financial Accounting Workbook

Phase 1 (Weeks 1–4) · 45 Hours

Scenario: You are the Financial Accountant for a multi-branch Indian manufacturing & distribution company transitioning from unadjusted Tally trial balances to audit-ready monthly Ind AS financial statements under a strict T+5 close deadline.

Inputs provided: Unadjusted multi-branch Trial Balance, Bank Statements, GST/TDS ledgers, 8 Commercial Lease contracts (Ind AS 116), Term Loan agreement with processing fees (Ind AS 109), Fixed Asset register, and Gratuity actuarial summary.

Skills applied: Ind AS 1, 12, 16, 19, 109, 115, 116; R2R close checklists; bank/GST/TDS reconciliations; EIR & lease amortization schedules; Schedule III Division II mapping.

Required output: Multi-tab Excel Close Workbook featuring: (1) T+5 Close Control Checklist, (2) Bank & Statutory Reconciliations, (3) Fixed Asset, ROU Lease, Debt EIR & Deferred Tax Schedules, (4) Adjusting Journal Entry Log, and (5) Auto-linked Schedule III Balance Sheet, P&L, and Cash Flow Statement with zero-error check flags.

Acceptance criteria: 100% of trial balance accounts mapped; Balance Sheet check = 0.00; Cash Flow net change ties exactly to cash movement; every adjusting journal cross-referenced to a supporting schedule.

Project 02 · Three-Company Listed Peer Financial Statement & Forensic Analysis Report

Phase 2 (Weeks 5–8) · 45 Hours

Scenario: An investment and corporate strategy committee requires a rigorous 3-year comparative financial health, earnings quality, and capital efficiency evaluation of three competing NSE-listed Indian companies (e.g., Titan, Kalyan Jewellers, Senco Gold OR Asian Paints, Berger, Kansai Nerolac).

Inputs provided: 3 consecutive years of published Annual Reports (Standalone & Consolidated P&L, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, Notes to Accounts, Auditor's Report, CARO, and MD&A) downloaded from BSE/NSE filings.

Skills applied: Common-size & trend analysis, EBITDA normalization, 5-step DuPont ROE decomposition, Cash Conversion Cycle, ROIC vs WACC spread, Beneish M-Score, and Auditor KAM/contingent liability forensics.

Required output: Standardized 3-Company Comparative Excel Engine + an Executive Analytical Report (8–10 pages) containing normalized statements, ratio scorecards, DuPont bridges, accounting red-flag assessment, and a ranked capital efficiency conclusion.

Acceptance criteria: All reported numbers reconciled back to published annual report pages; non-recurring items explicitly adjusted; clear differentiation between standalone and consolidated leverage.

Project 03 · Automated Excel, Power Query & Power BI Management Reporting System

Phase 3 (Weeks 9–12) · 50 Hours

Scenario: The CFO wants to eliminate 4 days of manual Excel copy-pasting every month by automating raw ERP/Tally CSV exports into an interactive self-refreshing P&L, Balance Sheet, and Working Capital dashboard.

Inputs provided: 12 monthly raw GL transaction CSV files, horizontal departmental budget spreadsheets, Chart of Accounts mapping master, and customer/product master tables.

Skills applied: Power Query ETL (folder ingestion, unpivoting, M transformations, anti-join reconciliations), Star-Schema data modelling, Financial DAX (CALCULATE, TOTALYTD, SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR, P&L subtotal switching), and Power BI executive visualization.

Required output: End-to-end automated pipeline: Raw CSV folder → Power Query ETL → Power Pivot / Power BI Star-Schema Model → 3-page Interactive Power BI Dashboard (Executive P&L & Variance, Balance Sheet & Cash Conversion, Drill-through Expense Ledger).

Acceptance criteria: Dropping a 13th month CSV into the source folder and clicking 'Refresh' updates all YTD, YoY, and Budget variance visuals with zero manual intervention and verified GL tie-out.

Project 04 · Five-Year Integrated Three-Statement Financial Model

Phase 4 (Weeks 13–15) · 55 Hours

Scenario: You are building the core corporate planning and equity research model for an NSE-listed Indian company to project 5-year financial performance across Base, Bull, and Bear economic scenarios.

Inputs provided: 3–5 years of historical financial statements, segment volume/price disclosures, historical capex and depreciation rates, debt repayment schedules, and corporate tax rates.

Skills applied: Institutional model architecture, color-coding standards, granular revenue & cost drivers, Working Capital/PPE/Debt/Tax corkscrew schedules, Revolver cash sweep, interest circularity breaker, and dynamic 3-statement integration.

Required output: Institutional-grade Excel 3-Statement Model with Cover/Control tab, Scenario Switcher, Assumptions, Historicals, 4 Supporting Schedules, Integrated P&L, Balance Sheet, and Indirect Cash Flow Statement.

Acceptance criteria: Balance Sheet balances (Assets - L&E = 0.00) across all 5 forecast years under Base, Bull, and Bear scenarios; zero hardcoded constants inside formula cells; zero #REF!/#VALUE! errors.

Project 05 · DCF Valuation, WACC & Relative Multiples Valuation Suite

Phase 4 (Week 16) · 35 Hours

Scenario: Extending Project 4, the Board / Investment Committee needs an intrinsic valuation range and peer trading multiple benchmark to evaluate whether the company's current market valuation is justified.

Inputs provided: 5-year projected NOPAT, D&A, Capex, and ΔNWC from Project 4; Indian 10-year G-Sec yield; peer betas and capital structures; current share prices and diluted share counts for 5 NSE peers.

Skills applied: FCFF calculation, WACC build (unlevered/relevered beta, cost of debt, target weights), Gordon Growth & Exit Multiple terminal value, EV-to-Equity bridge, 2D Data Tables, Trading Comps, and Football Field charting.

Required output: Integrated DCF & Comps Valuation tabs appended to Project 4 featuring WACC derivation, 5-year FCFF discounting, WACC vs Growth & Exit Multiple sensitivity tables, 5-peer Comps table, and a Football Field valuation chart.

Acceptance criteria: Terminal growth rate logically bounded; EV-to-Equity bridge accurately accounts for cash, debt, and non-controlling interest; sensitivity tables recalculate dynamically.

Project 06 · Annual Operating Budget, 12-Month Rolling Forecast & CFO Reporting Pack

Phase 5 (Weeks 17–20) · 55 Hours

Scenario: As FP&A Lead for a multi-product Indian enterprise, you must construct the upcoming fiscal year Annual Operating Plan (AOP), transition it into a 6+6 Latest Estimate rolling forecast, and deliver a monthly CFO variance commentary pack.

Inputs provided: Historical 24-month SKU-level sales volumes, pricing, standard BOM costs, department headcount roster, Capex requests, and 6 months of actual YTD results.

Skills applied: Bottom-up Revenue, Payroll, ZBB Opex & Capex budgeting; Master Budget consolidation; 12-month Rolling Forecast; Price-Volume-Mix (PVM) variance decomposition; EBITDA waterfall bridges; executive narrative writing.

Required output: Integrated FP&A Workbook (Departmental Budgets + Master Budget + Rolling Forecast + PVM Variance Bridge) paired with an 8-slide Monthly Business Review (MBR) CFO Deck.

Acceptance criteria: Price, Volume, and Mix variances reconcile 100% of total Gross Margin variance; Rolling Forecast dynamically blends Actuals + Forecast based on cutoff date; commentary identifies root causes and action owners.
