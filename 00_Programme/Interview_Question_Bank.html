Technical & Case Interview Question Bank (30 Questions)

Questions only — answer them aloud and in writing, then check against your notes.

Financial Accounting

Q1. Conceptual & Practical

Walk me through your month-end close process from T+1 to T+5. How do you ensure completeness of accruals and zero suspense balances?

Q2. Numerical / Linkage

A company buys ₹100 of inventory on credit in Month 1, sells 80% of it for ₹130 on credit in Month 2, and collects ₹100 cash while paying ₹70 to the supplier in Month 3. Trace all three financial statements in Month 2 and Month 3 (ignore tax).

Q3. Practical Case

During GL scrutiny before year-end finalisation, you notice a large debit balance sitting in the 'GR/IR (Goods Receipt / Invoice Receipt) Clearing Account'. What causes this and how do you resolve it?

Ind AS

Q4. Conceptual & Numerical

Under Ind AS 116, how does capitalizing an operating lease change EBITDA, EBIT, PAT, Operating Cash Flow, and Free Cash Flow?

Q5. Practical Case

Explain the 5-step model under Ind AS 115 with an example of a software company selling a 3-year software license plus 3 years of post-contract support (PCS) and custom implementation for a bundled ₹60 Lakhs.

Q6. Conceptual

How does Ind AS 109 Expected Credit Loss (ECL) differ from the old incurred-loss bad debt provision, and how do you compute it for trade receivables?

Q7. Numerical

A company buys machinery for ₹100L. Book depreciation is 10% SLM (₹10L/yr), while Income Tax depreciation is 15% WDV (₹15L in Year 1). Corporate tax rate is 25.17% (Sec 115BAA). Compute Year 1 Deferred Tax.

Q9. Practical Case

When reviewing an Indian listed company's annual report, what 4 items do you inspect first in the Auditor's Report and Notes to Accounts?

Q10. Conceptual

Why might a profitable company with growing PAT report negative Operating Cash Flow for three consecutive years?

Advanced Excel

Q11. Practical Formula

Why is XLOOKUP preferred over VLOOKUP in financial models, and when would you still use INDEX-MATCH or SUMIFS?

Q12. Conceptual & Practical

Explain the difference between NPV and XNPV, and why using standard NPV on monthly project cash flows causes valuation errors.

Q13. Practical Workflow

You receive 12 monthly trial balance Excel files where months are arranged horizontally across columns. How do you automate consolidating them into a single P&L report?

Financial Statement Analysis

Q14. Numerical & Conceptual

Company A and Company B both generate 18% ROE. Company A has a 12% Net Profit Margin, 1.0x Asset Turnover, and 1.5x Equity Multiplier. Company B has a 4% Net Profit Margin, 1.5x Asset Turnover, and 3.0x Equity Multiplier. Which business is higher quality and why?

Q15. Numerical

Calculate the Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC) if Annual Revenue is ₹365 Cr, COGS is ₹219 Cr, Average Receivables are ₹45 Cr, Average Inventory is ₹36 Cr, and Average Payables are ₹30 Cr.

Q16. Conceptual

Why do analysts use ROIC (Return on Invested Capital) instead of ROE when comparing capital-intensive businesses?

Financial Modelling

Q17. Conceptual & Practical

What causes circularity in an integrated three-statement financial model, and how do you prevent it from crashing your workbook?

Q18. Practical Debugging

You finished linking your 3-statement model, and your Balance Sheet is out of balance by ₹14.5 Cr in Forecast Year 1, ₹29.0 Cr in Year 2, and ₹43.5 Cr in Year 3. What is the single fastest clue here?

Q19. Conceptual

In a 3-statement model, how does a ₹10 Cr increase in Depreciation (assuming a 25% tax rate) flow through all three financial statements?

Q20. Practical Architecture

How do you model a Revolving Credit Facility (Revolver) so a company never runs into a negative cash balance during a downturn scenario?

DCF Valuation

Q21. Conceptual & Numerical

Q22. Conceptual & Practical

How do you estimate Beta for an unlisted company or a newly listed Indian division when computing Cost of Equity in WACC?

Q23. Practical Sanity Check

In your DCF model, Terminal Value accounts for 85% of total Enterprise Value, and the Perpetuity Growth Rate is set at 7.5% for a mature Indian FMCG company. What is wrong with this?

Q24. Numerical

A company's DCF Enterprise Value is ₹1,200 Cr. It holds ₹150 Cr in cash & mutual funds, ₹320 Cr in total debt (including lease liabilities), ₹30 Cr in Minority Interest, and has 20 Cr diluted shares. Compute Implied Share Price.

Variance Analysis

Q29. Numerical & Practical

Q30. Conceptual & Practical

Total Gross Margin ₹ increased by 8% vs Budget, yet Gross Margin % dropped by 180 bps even though raw material costs and selling prices per SKU remained exactly on budget. How is that possible?
