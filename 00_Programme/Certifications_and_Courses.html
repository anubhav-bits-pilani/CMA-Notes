Certifications & Structured Courses

CFI — Financial Modeling & Valuation Analyst (FMVA®)

Provider: Corporate Finance Institute (CFI)

Phases supported: Supports Phases 3, 4, 5 & 6

Cost: Paid (~$497/year Self-Study subscription; verify current pricing on CFI)

Priority: Optional (High Practical Value)

What it teaches: End-to-end 3-statement financial modeling, DCF valuation, comparable company analysis, budgeting/forecasting, Excel shortcuts, and presentation design through hands-on template builds.

Verdict for a CMA: Since you already hold the ICMAI CMA credential, skip CFI's introductory accounting modules and focus strictly on their 3-Statement Modeling, DCF, M&A, and FP&A case courses if you want structured guided templates.

CFA Institute — Financial Modeling Practical Skills Module (PSM)

Provider: CFA Institute

Phases supported: Supports Phases 4 & 6

Cost: Included with CFA Program registration (Paid exam registration)

Priority: Optional (Only if pursuing CFA)

What it teaches: Building a clean three-statement financial model from scratch in Excel, modeling debt/interest schedules, and applying institutional formatting conventions.

Verdict for a CMA: Do not register for the CFA exam solely for this module—the curriculum in Phases 4–6 covers identical 3-statement mechanics alongside Indian Ind AS context.

Microsoft Certified: Power BI Data Analyst Associate (PL-300) Learning Path

Provider: Microsoft Learn

Phases supported: Supports Phase 3 (Weeks 10–12)

Cost: Free self-paced learning modules on Microsoft Learn (Official certification exam is paid, ~$165 USD / regional India pricing)

Priority: Essential (Learning Path Free; Exam Optional)

What it teaches: Power Query data preparation, star-schema data modeling, DAX time-intelligence measures, and interactive executive dashboard deployment.

Verdict for a CMA: Complete the free Microsoft Learn modules during Weeks 10–12. Combining CMA + PL-300 data modeling skills strongly differentiates you for FP&A and Management Reporting roles.

ICAI Ind AS Certificate / Educational Materials & ICMAI Technical Resources

Provider: ICAI & ICMAI

Phases supported: Supports Phases 1 & 2 (Weeks 1–8)

Cost: Free official standards, educational materials, and guidance notes on ICAI/ICMAI portals

Priority: Essential

What it teaches: Authoritative Indian accounting standards (Ind AS 115, 116, 109, 12, 103, 36), Schedule III Division II disclosures, and CARO/audit reporting nuances.

Verdict for a CMA: Mandatory reference foundation for Indian Financial Accounting, Controllership, and Financial Reporting interviews.

A Simple Model — Integrated Financial Modeling & Valuation Curriculum

Provider: A Simple Model

Phases supported: Supports Phases 4 & 6 (Weeks 13–16, 22)

Cost: Free foundational modules / Paid full access (~$36–$100+ depending on tier; verify current pricing)

Priority: Essential (Core 3-Statement & DCF Walkthroughs)

What it teaches: Step-by-step construction of integrated financial statements, corkscrew schedules, DCF models, and LBO/M&A mechanics with downloadable Excel templates.

Verdict for a CMA: One of the clearest, most practical resources for mastering how Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow link dynamically.
