Indian Statutory Reporting vs. International Financial Modelling Standards

Dimension Indian Financial Reporting (Ind AS / Schedule III) International Financial Modelling & FP&A Practice
Primary Format MCA Schedule III Division II (Balance Sheet vertical: Non-Current/Current Assets → Equity → Liabilities; P&L by nature of expense). Functional P&L (Revenue → COGS → Gross Profit → SG&A → EBITDA → D&A → EBIT → EBT → Net Income) for operational margin analysis.
Lease Accounting Ind AS 116: Right-of-Use (ROU) asset + Lease Liability on BS; Depreciation + Finance Cost in P&L. Models often track EBITDA pre-Ind AS 116 vs post-Ind AS 116, adjusting Enterprise Value (EV) consistently with EBITDA definition.
Tax & Deferred Tax Ind AS 12 Balance Sheet liability approach; MAT credit entitlement & Section 115BAA/115BAB corporate tax regimes. Effective tax rate forecasting paired with a separate Deferred Tax Asset/Liability schedule reconciling book vs tax depreciation.
Model Architecture Audit workpapers emphasize historical tie-outs, voucher traceability, and statutory disclosures. FAST / ICAEW / Wall Street standards: strict separation of Inputs (blue font), Calculations (black font), and Links (green font); zero hardcoded constants inside formulas.

  • Phase 2: Financial Reporting & Listed Company Analysis (Weeks 5–8 (192 Hours)): Develop institutional equity/credit analyst skills to dissect Indian listed company annual reports, evaluate earnings quality, and benchmark peers across 3+ years.

  • Phase 4: Integrated Three-Statement Financial Modelling & Valuation (Weeks 13–16 (192 Hours)): Master institutional Wall Street / corporate finance modelling architecture: building dynamic 5-year 3-statement forecasts, resolving circularity, and executing DCF & Relative Valuation.
  • Phase 5: FP&A, Annual Budgeting, Rolling Forecasts & Variance Storytelling (Weeks 17–20 (192 Hours)): Leverage your CMA costing/variance roots and elevate them into enterprise FP&A: driver-based budgeting, 12-month rolling forecasts, and executive board packs.
