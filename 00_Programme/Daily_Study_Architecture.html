Daily & Weekly Study Architecture (48 Hours / Week)

Daily & Weekly Study Architecture (48 Hours / Week)

Designed as a full-time analytical apprenticeship (8 hours/day, Monday to Saturday; Sunday rest and cognitive recovery).

Standard Daily Execution Timetable (Monday – Friday)

Time Block Duration Activity Category Execution Protocol for a CMA Professional
09:00 – 11:00 2.0 Hours Conceptual Learning & Technical Standards Read ICAI Ind AS Educational Material, IFRS standards, or corporate finance technical notes. Focus on edge cases (e.g., ECL staging, variable consideration, WACC beta unlevering), skipping basic bookkeeping already mastered in CMA.
11:15 – 13:15 2.0 Hours Practical Excel / Power Query / DAX Exercises Replicate mechanics in Excel without mouse shortcuts. Build dynamic schedules, Power Query M transformations, or ratio engines using raw NSE financial exports.
14:00 – 16:00 2.0 Hours Modelling & Listed-Company Case Studies Dissect annual reports of Indian listed companies (e.g., Titan, Asian Paints, Tata Motors, Infosys, Trent). Trace footnotes into model drivers.
16:15 – 17:15 1.0 Hour Capstone Project Development Incremental daily build on the active Phase deliverable (Month-End Close Pack, 3-Company Report, Power BI Dashboard, 3-Statement Model, DCF, or Annual Budget).
17:15 – 18:15 1.0 Hour Revision & Interview Articulation Document formula logic, update technical flashcards, and practice explaining financial conclusions out loud as if presenting to a CFO or Audit Committee.

Saturday Weekly Assessment & Sunday Recovery Protocol

Day Block Focus Deliverable / Standard
Saturday 3.0 Hours (Morning) Timed Technical & Modelling Assessment Complete the week's closed-book case test (e.g., building a debt/tax schedule from scratch in 90 mins or solving Ind AS numerical adjustments).
Saturday 3.0 Hours (Afternoon) Weekly Project Milestone Audit Run error checks (`#REF!`, `#VALUE!`, balance sheet check row = 0, cash flow tie-out) on the week's project workpaper.
Saturday 2.0 Hours (Evening) Peer/Mentor Review & Next-Week Setup Download annual reports, clean raw CSVs, and prepare data templates for Monday morning.
Sunday Full Day Mandatory Rest & Recovery Zero screen-based financial modelling. Prevents analytical burnout across the 24-week marathon.
